## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding the pending closure of their account and the canceled withdrawal from their investment account. Operational review confirmed that the account was queued for pending closure due to policy violations, which automatically triggered the cancellation of outbound transfer requests and necessitated the liquidation of taxable brokerage assets. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding the closure process, clarifying that direct deposit streams would either post or return to the originator depending on settlement timing, while remaining brokerage funds would be liquidated and disbursed via check. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining restricted in pending closure status while final asset liquidation and check issuance are finalized.
