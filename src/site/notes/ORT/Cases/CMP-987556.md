## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a potential account compromise, stating an unauthorized individual deposited an unrecognized check. The member emphasized that they never authorized the deposit, do not want their account closed, and do not want to be penalized. Additionally, the member voiced deep concern regarding the final resolution and its potential impact on their credit score. The member requested to speak with a supervisor, and a supervisor callback was scheduled.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist engaged with the customer via a phone call to address their complaint regarding an unauthorized check deposit, concerns over account termination, and potential credit reporting impacts. Operational review confirmed that the determination regarding the fraudulent check deposit was final and could not be administratively reversed. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that a remaining balance check was disbursed with an expected arrival by the second to last week of November, provided the formal mailing address and instructions for disputing any potential credit reporting inaccuracies, and scheduled a supervisor callback per the customer's escalation request. The customer's account remains subject to final review decisions, pending the arrival of the disbursed funds check and the scheduled supervisory follow-up.
