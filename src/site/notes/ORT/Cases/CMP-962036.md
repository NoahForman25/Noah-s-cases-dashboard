## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi regarding his smart card account to ask whether closing the account would erase the reporting on his credit bureau records. The member stated he was unaware that opening a smart card account would impact his credit reporting and requested that SoFi delete the reported records.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding credit bureau reporting resulting from opening a Smart Card account and his request to delete the inquiry and account history. Operational review confirmed that the inquiry was accurately submitted as part of the formal application process, precluding the removal or deletion of valid credit bureau records. To resolve the complaint, the specialist delivered operational transparency explaining that closing the account would not erase prior reporting and could further affect his score, advising that maintaining the card with consistent on-time payments is the most effective approach to positive credit building. The complaint is resolved, with the credit reporting maintained as accurate and the Smart Card account remaining open and active in good standing.
