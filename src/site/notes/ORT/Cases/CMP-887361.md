## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction with the personal loan application process, stating they originally selected an offer that did not include an origination fee and were surprised to discover a fee applied to their finalized loan terms. The member requested an immediate escalation to a manager to adjust the terms. The agent explained that because the loan had already advanced to the funding stage, the underlying offer could no longer be modified. The agent attempted a warm transfer to a supervisor, but after queue wait times exceeded 20 minutes, the member agreed to receive a manager callback instead. Support documented the origination fee dispute, noted the funding stage constraints, and submitted an urgent escalation ticket to Lending Operations Leadership to schedule a supervisor follow-up call.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on his strong dissatisfaction regarding a loan origination fee that was applied to his finalized loan terms. The member had previously stated that he selected an offer without an origination fee during the application process and requested a supervisor escalation to adjust the terms, ultimately agreeing to a callback after a line transfer attempt timed out while the loan proceeded through funding. The specialist authenticated the member, who explained his selection of a fee-free offer and expressed frustration over the unexpected charge applied after funding. The specialist acknowledged his concerns but explained that because the loan had already been fully funded, the executed contract and origination fee could no longer be modified or removed. The member reiterated his dissatisfaction with the outcome and noted that he would consider filing a formal complaint regarding the application process.
