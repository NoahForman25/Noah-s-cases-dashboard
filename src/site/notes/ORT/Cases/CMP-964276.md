## Complaint
- The member requested a reimbursement for a $30 instant transfer fee, stating that the option was selected by mistake due to a confusing button layout. The member noted that no reconfirmation prompt appeared before the transaction processed and requested that the funds be sent via the standard two to three business day transfer instead.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a $30.00 instant transfer fee incurred due to a confusing interface layout and the absence of a secondary confirmation prompt. Operational review determined that the transaction had processed immediately under the selected instant delivery speed, which applies standard transfer fees, while standard two- to three-business-day transfers remain available without a surcharge. To resolve the complaint, the specialist issued an apology for the customer's experience, credited 3,000 reward points (valued at $30.00) to the customer's profile to offset the fee, and provided guidance on selecting the standard transfer method for future transactions. The complaint is resolved, with the fee effectively offset via points credit and the customer's account active and in good standing.
