## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after being notified of a $100.57 remaining balance on a personal loan they believed was fully paid off and settled. The member stated that a previous agent assured them their payoff payment—sent by their credit union on July 10, 2026—would be backdated to the July 10 quote date once received, covering the full loan balance. The agent clarified that because the payment posted on July 17, daily interest accrued, leaving an outstanding balance. The agent documented the member's dispute and escalated the account for follow-up within 3 to 7 business days.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the customer via phone call to address their grievance regarding an unexpected remaining balance on a personal loan they believed was settled. Operational review confirmed that a previous representative promised to lock the payoff rate, but a delay in check posting allowed $102.47 in daily interest to accrue after the quote date. To resolve the complaint and address the representative's error, the specialist credited 12,000 rewards points to the customer's profile to cover the accrued interest along with additional compensation for the inconvenience. The complaint is resolved, with the fee offset applied and the personal loan balance fully satisfied.
