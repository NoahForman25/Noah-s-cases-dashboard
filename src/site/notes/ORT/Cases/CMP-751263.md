## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their concerns regarding recent denied dispute decisions under ticket numbers 8088843, 8086278, and 8082682, as well as their stated intent to involve law enforcement and a union attorney to file an affidavit regarding the contested transactions. The specialist acknowledged the member's plan to seek external assistance and confirmed that an open complaint was already on file to ensure the matter is monitored smoothly. The member was advised that a formal appeal requires the submission of new, additional documentation or information not previously evaluated during the initial investigation, and that if no new details are available, the prior decisions will stand. Furthermore, the specialist provided instructions for how the member's legal or law enforcement representatives can route official correspondence to the institution, while concluding the communication with a standard debt collection disclosure.
