## Complaint
- The member and her spouse expressed severe dissatisfaction and distress regarding ongoing difficulties completing the identity verification process and a perceived lack of support. The spouse took over the call in extreme frustration, utilizing profanity and indicating that the account restriction was causing severe personal hardship, specifically stating they were facing imminent homelessness and food insecurity, before disconnecting the call prematurely.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address the severe dissatisfaction and distress expressed by the member and her spouse regarding ongoing identity verification failures and account restrictions. The specialist acknowledged the personal hardship and frustration conveyed during the previous call—which had escalated to profanity and explicit concerns regarding homelessness and food insecurity before a premature disconnection—and apologized for the difficulties experienced with the platform. To help resolve the account restriction, the specialist provided practical photographic tips to ensure a successful verification attempt, such as placing the physical ID flat on a solid surface rather than holding it, and offered direct contact details and office hours to support their next attempt.
