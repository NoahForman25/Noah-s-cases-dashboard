## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an extended wait time to reach a representative, noting that the system prompt estimating a five-minute wait was inaccurate as they waited longer than five minutes.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding an extended wait time to reach a representative and an inaccurate five-minute phone queue estimate. Operational review confirmed that IVR system wait times are automated estimates generated from general call volume data rather than real-time queue positioning, causing actual wait times to fluctuate based on live agent availability. To resolve the complaint, the specialist apologized for the delay and provided clear transparency regarding how queue time estimates are generated during peak call volumes. The complaint is resolved, with the operational explanation provided and standard support contact channels re-established for future member needs.
