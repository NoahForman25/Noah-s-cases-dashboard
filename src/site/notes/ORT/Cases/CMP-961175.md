## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi regarding an account closure resulting from an internal risk review and inquired about the status of her remaining funds ($580.00). After being told $500.00 was reversed back to USAA Federal Savings Bank, the member sought clarification on what "eligible funds" meant regarding the remaining $80.02. The representative was unable to provide specific eligibility criteria and subsequently informed the member that the $80.02 was deemed ineligible to be paid out and would instead be returned to the originator of the deposited check. The member expressed strong frustration, stating that returning funds from a deposited check made no sense, accused SoFi of taking her money, and called the situation a major inconvenience
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding an unexpected account closure and the handling of her remaining funds, specifically an $80.02 balance deemed ineligible for direct payout. Operational review confirmed that following the account termination, $500.00 was reversed to USAA Federal Savings Bank, while the remaining $80.02 was returned directly to the originating institution of the deposited check pursuant to account eligibility and risk review policies. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding the fund disposition, directed the customer to coordinate with the check originator to retrieve the $80.02, and offered to supply the transaction trace ID number to facilitate that recovery. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's account remaining permanently closed and the remaining funds successfully returned to their respective source institutions.
