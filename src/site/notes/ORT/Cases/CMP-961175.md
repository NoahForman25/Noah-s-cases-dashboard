## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi regarding an account closure resulting from an internal risk review and inquired about the status of her remaining funds ($580.00). After being told $500.00 was reversed back to USAA Federal Savings Bank, the member sought clarification on what "eligible funds" meant regarding the remaining $80.02. The representative was unable to provide specific eligibility criteria and subsequently informed the member that the $80.02 was deemed ineligible to be paid out and would instead be returned to the originator of the deposited check. The member expressed strong frustration, stating that returning funds from a deposited check made no sense, accused SoFi of taking her money, and called the situation a major inconvenience.
