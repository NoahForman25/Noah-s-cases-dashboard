## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction, stating this is the second time they were cheated out of a direct deposit promotional bonus and previously robbed of $300 in 2024. Records indicate the direct deposit was coded as a "deposit," and the member previously received three direct deposit bonuses (May 2024, July 2024 for $50, and January 2025). The agent educated the member that promotional bonuses are paid out only once per member provided all requirements are met.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist engaged with the customer via a phone call to address his complaint regarding disqualification from a direct deposit promotional bonus and disputed transaction classifications. Operational review confirmed that the customer had previously received multiple direct deposit promotions, rendering the account ineligible under terms limiting members to a single direct deposit promotional bonus, while the transaction in question was coded as a standard deposit rather than an eligible direct deposit. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding program eligibility rules, submitted an internal ticket to investigate the transaction coding, and offered courtesy reward points as a goodwill gesture. The customer's account remains open, active, and in good standing, pending final findings from the deposit coding investigation.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
- SoFi called the member to discuss a direct deposit promotion and verify the member's eligibility for the current promotion. Key Events The agent authenticated the member and confirmed their identity. The member expressed confusion about being disqualified from the current promotion due to a previous direct deposit. The agent explained that the member could only qualify for one direct deposit promotion and confirmed the date of the previous promotion. The member argued that previous deposits should also qualify as direct deposits based on the definition provided by SoFi. The agent acknowledged the member's frustration and submitted a ticket for further investigation into the coding of the deposits. The agent offered additional points as a goodwill gesture but could not grant the full bonus due to policy restrictions.
