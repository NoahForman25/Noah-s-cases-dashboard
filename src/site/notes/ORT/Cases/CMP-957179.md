## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an undelivered debit card originally ordered on September 30. The member stated they had called over three times about the issue, including on October 19 when an agent promised tracking information that was never received. After being offered a replacement card with shipping, the member stated they do not believe what representatives say, doubted the card would ever be sent, and demanded an explanation for why the card was not ordered following the October 19 call. The member then requested to speak with someone else.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding an undelivered debit card ordered on September 30, repeated follow-up calls, and missing tracking information from prior representative interactions. Operational review verified that previous orders and tracking updates failed to fulfill as promised due to internal communication and fulfillment lapses. To resolve the complaint, the specialist apologized for the service breakdowns and placed a new replacement debit card order with expedited shipping on October 26. The complaint is resolved, with the new debit card successfully ordered and actively in transit via expedited delivery.
