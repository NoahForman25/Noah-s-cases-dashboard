## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration over being unable to close their checking and savings accounts due to a remaining balance of $0.46, calling the situation ridiculous and explaining that they have no way to transfer the funds out. The chatbot informed the member that the balance must be brought to $0 prior to account closure and provided instructions on how to close the account through the app once the balance is cleared.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the inability to close their checking and savings accounts due to a remaining balance of $0.46. Operational review confirmed that accounts with no external bank linked require a $0 balance to close self-service via the app, preventing automatic closure while residual funds remain. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided clear instructions explaining that support can manually execute the account closure and disburse the remaining $0.46 balance via a check mailed to the customer's verified address on file. The complaint is resolved, with clear instructions established to finalize the account closure and issue the remaining funds upon customer request.
