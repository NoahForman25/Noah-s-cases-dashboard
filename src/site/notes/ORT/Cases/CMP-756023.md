## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding recurring difficulties with the platform, stating that SoFi occasionally complicates their ability to pay bills. The member disconnected the chat session before the issue could be further addressed.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding recurring difficulties with the platform and their statement that the institution occasionally complicates their ability to pay bills. The specialist apologized for the challenging experience and noted that the member's previous chat session had disconnected before the issue could be fully addressed. To provide a seamless alternative for managing and transferring finances, the specialist outlined a step-by-step guide on how to initiate an outbound wire transfer directly within the mobile app, emphasizing the need for camera permissions to complete required identity verification steps. Additionally, the member was provided with a direct link to a detailed support article for further guidance on sending and receiving wire transfers.

### Second Contact
- Hi Marc,   
  
This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. We are reaching out regarding the recent chat session where frustration was expressed about difficulties with the platform and paying bills. We completely understand how important it is to have a seamless experience when managing finances, and we are happy to provide clear steps to help resolve this today.  
  
We sincerely apologize that this process has been difficult, and we noted that the chat disconnected before we could fully assist. To make sending funds easier, we have outlined the exact steps to initiate a wire transfer directly from the mobile app:  
  
Navigate to the Banking Tab: From the main menu, tap the Banking tab to get started.  
Choose the Transfer Option: Once in the Banking tab, select Transfer.  
Select Wire Transfer: After clicking Transfer, choose the option to send a wire transfer.  
Complete the Wire Transfer Form: Fill out all necessary fields. We recommend following any specific wire transfer instructions provided by the receiving institution.  
Verify Identification: During the process, the system will ask for identification verification. When prompted, allow camera permissions to complete this step smoothly.  
For additional guidance on how to send and receive a wire transfer, please review our detailed support article here: https://support.sofi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360062532471-How-to-Send-and-Receive-Wire-Transfers-with-SoFi  
  
To recap, we have provided the step-by-step instructions and a helpful link to complete a wire transfer. Please let us know if there are any further questions at all—we are here to help.
