## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi support regarding a missing 2,000-point referral bonus, expressing frustration and stating the situation was "unacceptable" and "unprofessional." The member mentioned being a part owner of Kirkland's and reconsidering plans to move their business account to SoFi. The agent explained that the referral did not qualify for the promotion, but granted 2,000 reward points as a one-time courtesy for the inconvenience while noting on the account that no additional points could be credited in the future. The member expressed satisfaction, and the agent confirmed the points were added and advised the member on future referral eligibility requirements.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a missing 2,000-point referral bonus and frustration with the referral process. Operational review confirmed that the referral did not meet promotional eligibility requirements because the referred individual opened their account prior to using the referral link. To resolve the complaint, the specialist confirmed that 2,000 reward points had already been credited to the customer's account as a one-time courtesy and clarified the eligibility guidelines required for future referral bonuses. The complaint is resolved, with the courtesy reward points successfully posted to the customer's account and the referral policy established for future promotional tracking.
