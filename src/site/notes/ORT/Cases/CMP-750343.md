## Complaint
- Member contacted to demand an immediate escalation to a supervisor ("higher than agents") due to their inability to receive a One-Time Password (OTP) via email. The member expressed frustration after being advised that representatives do not have an administrative option to manually route the OTP to an email address. When questioned if there had been a recent change to their phone number that might be disrupting text delivery, the member confirmed that no such change had been requested.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed to address their previous difficulties receiving a One-Time Password (OTP) and their request to speak with a supervisor. The specialist acknowledged the high stress caused by losing a phone and validated the importance of smooth account access. A positive resolution was provided, confirming that the team successfully updated the two-factor authentication settings while keeping the member's current phone number on file. The specialist verified that the login issues have been sorted out and full account access has been successfully restored.
