## Complaint
- The member expressed anger and frustration upon discovering that their SoFi checking and savings accounts had been closed without understanding the reason for the closure. The member was particularly upset because a direct deposit had posted that same day, leaving them distressed with no accessible funds.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding the sudden closure of her checking and savings accounts and the lack of access to a direct deposit that posted on the same date. Operational review confirmed that the accounts were definitively closed pursuant to internal security policies, which automatically restricts profile access and triggers standard balance liquidation procedures. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided formal written notice explaining the security basis for the closure and confirmed that the funds from the newly posted direct deposit were promptly disbursed via check mailed to the address on file. The complaint is resolved, with the checking and savings accounts remaining permanently closed pending the customer's receipt of the disbursement check.
