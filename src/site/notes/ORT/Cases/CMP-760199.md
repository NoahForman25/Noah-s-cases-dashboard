## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction regarding the closure of their account and the delay in returning their remaining funds. Due to their frustration with the situation, the member stated that they intend to consult with their attorney regarding the matter.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed the member to address their severe dissatisfaction regarding the closure of their account, the delay in returning their remaining balance, and their stated intention to consult with an attorney. The specialist conducted a comprehensive review of the account and provided a final resolution, confirming that the funds in question were successfully returned directly to the original sender (the originator). The specialist recommended that the member contact the originating institution to track the routing details and confirmation of the returned deposit on their end.
