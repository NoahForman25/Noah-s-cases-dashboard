## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction because an agent that helped them on the 5th of August was rude and disrespectful towards them. They also explained that the action that they asked for was not made properly. Which was an stop payment and a dispute. The dispute was not submitted and the stop payment was submitted with a wrong merchant.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding a negative experience with a previous representative on August 5th, as well as an ongoing account restriction caused by an active wire recall. The member reported that the prior agent was rude and disrespectful, and failed to correctly process their requests by omitting a transaction dispute and filing a stop payment under the wrong merchant name.The specialist apologized for the prior agent's conduct and confirmed that the dispute for the Subway transaction has now been successfully filed on the member's behalf. Addressing the active account restriction, the specialist explained that wire recalls automatically trigger mandatory security protocols to protect transaction integrity, and reassured the member that their case has been escalated to Risk Operations for priority review to expedite restoring full account access.
