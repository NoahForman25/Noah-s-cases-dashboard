## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction regarding a significant delay experienced when attempting to withdraw $6,000.00 from their Invest account to an external Chase bank account. Due to the extended processing timeframe and frustration with the transfer process, the member stated that they intend to discontinue all future use of SoFi's services.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their severe dissatisfaction regarding a significant delay experienced when attempting to withdraw $6,000.00 from their Invest account to an external Chase bank account. The specialist acknowledged the member's frustration over the extended processing timeframe as well as their stated intent to discontinue using SoFi's services. Delivering a clear resolution, the specialist apologized for the delay, confirmed that the $6,000.00 withdrawal was successfully processed and completed on July 20, 2026, and assured the member that their feedback regarding the transfer process was documented to help improve future system performance.
