## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration and confusion regarding where funds went when transferred into their crypto account, stating they do not understand how to navigate the platform or read the value associated with the purchased coin.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their confusion and frustration regarding the tracking of funds transferred into their crypto account and navigating coin valuations. Operational review confirmed that the transferred funds were successfully converted and reflected within the platform's cryptocurrency ledger, which requires distinct account navigation from standard banking balances. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, clarified how cryptocurrency transactions and balances appear on the platform, and offered direct access to follow-up support or specialized crypto assistance when the customer is ready to review the details later in the week. The complaint is resolved, with the funds securely accounted for in the crypto account and a clear path established for further guidance upon the customer's callback.
