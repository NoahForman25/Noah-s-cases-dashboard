## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding a pending $345 Zelle transfer that processed as a 2–3 business day ACH transaction instead of being delivered instantly. They questioned why the transfer was classified as ACH if it could not be canceled like typical ACH transactions, called the delayed funds availability and inconsistent processing unreasonable, and abruptly disconnected the chat after being informed of the multi-day timeline.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong dissatisfaction regarding a $345.00 Zelle transfer that processed over a 2-to-3 business day timeframe rather than instantly, as well as the disconnection during their previous chat interaction. The specialist acknowledged the member's frustration regarding the processing delay and their confusion over why the transfer was classified as ACH yet subject to different cancellation protocols. Clarifying the mechanics, the specialist explained that certain Zelle transfers utilize ACH networks and may require multiple business days to complete without traditional ACH cancellation options, but provided resolution by confirming system records show the $345.00 transaction has since been fully reversed.
