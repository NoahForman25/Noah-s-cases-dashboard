## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding a funds availability hold on a recently posted Mobile Check Deposit, requesting an immediate release of the funds due to urgent need. Support apologized for the inconvenience and explained standard mobile deposit processing and hold timeframes, but the member disagreed with the policy and demanded a supervisor escalation. Support informed the member that a supervisor review callback takes 24 to 48 business hours, documented their immediate financial need, and submitted an urgent escalation ticket to Deposit Operations and Management Leadership to review the check for potential hold release.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her strong dissatisfaction regarding a funds availability hold placed on a recent Mobile Check Deposit and her request for an immediate release due to urgent financial need. The member had previously challenged the standard processing hold timeframes explained by support and requested a supervisor escalation after being advised that the funds could not be released immediately. The specialist reviewed the account and confirmed that the information provided by the previous agent was accurate, explaining that mobile check deposit holding periods are automated and cannot be manually overridden by support or management. The specialist apologized for the inconvenience caused by the delay and provided clear expectations by confirming that the deposited funds will become fully available in her account on September 8, 2026.
