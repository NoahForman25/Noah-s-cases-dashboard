## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding unauthorized activity on their account, reporting that their card was stolen and used for ATM withdrawals at a Circle K on Finley Boulevard, resulting in approximately $690 taken from their SoFi account. They stated their email address was changed without authorization, their phone passcode was compromised and altered by an unknown party, and multiple external bank accounts (Truist, Regions, and Chase) were accessed without consent. Additionally, the member reported that their payroll check intended for Truist was redirected to SoFi without their permission. The member emphasized that they had not used the card, had not shared their credentials, and had not authorized any of the transactions.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding account takeover, approximately $690 in unauthorized ATM withdrawals, and the redirection of their payroll direct deposit. Operational review clarified that financial institutions receive incoming deposits passively, confirming that SoFi does not control or redirect payroll allocations from external employers. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided instructions on filing formal dispute claims for the unauthorized charges through phone support or the mobile application, emphasizing the critical need to submit these claims immediately prior to account closure. The complaint is resolved, with the banking accounts remaining in pending closure status while awaiting the customer's initiation of the formal dispute process.
