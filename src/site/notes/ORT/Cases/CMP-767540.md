## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding a wire transfer on their account and the current status of the corresponding investigation update. Due to their dissatisfaction with the progress of the investigation, the member requested an escalation and a supervisor callback has been arranged.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint and investigation update regarding a wire transfer delay, addressing their previous request for a supervisor callback. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's dissatisfaction with the processing delay and documented their feedback concerning the negative impact it had on their medical situation, as well as the need for greater compassion during stressful business interactions. The agent provided mixed feedback review, acknowledging the member's appreciation for one helpful employee while documenting their concerns regarding another representative's lack of knowledge on wire transfer policies. Ultimately, the agent apologized for the delay, thanked the member for their feedback, and reassured them that comprehensive email updates would be sent if further phone contact could not be established.
