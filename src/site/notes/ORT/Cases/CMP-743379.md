## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding the enhanced verification process and requested to use an expired identification card to complete the verification, which they were informed could not be accepted.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member to address their frustration regarding the enhanced verification process and their request to use an expired identification card. The specialist clarified the institution's verification guidelines, explaining that an expired ID can only be accepted if the expiration date is within the past 90 days. The member was advised that if the ID exceeds this 90-day timeframe, a valid, updated form of identification will be required to proceed with the verification process.
