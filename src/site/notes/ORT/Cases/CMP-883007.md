## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding significant delays in their loan application process and an ongoing technical error related to clearing the FS 12 condition. Emphasizing that the processing time has extended a week longer than usual and noting that they hold competitive loan offers from other lenders, the member requested a definitive underwriting status update indicating whether the application has been approved or declined. Support acknowledged the member's frustration regarding the extended timeline and technical issue, documented the competitive pressure, and submitted an urgent priority escalation ticket to Underwriting Operations and Lending Leadership to immediately clear the FS 12 blocker and render a decision on the application.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on a personal loan application that had previously experienced significant processing delays and an ongoing technical error related to clearing the FS 12 condition. The member had previously expressed strong dissatisfaction after processing extended a week longer than expected, emphasizing that they held competitive loan offers from other lenders while demanding a definitive underwriting approval status. The specialist authenticated the member, followed up on the prior technical escalations submitted to Underwriting Operations, and confirmed that the complications and FS 12 condition blocking the account were fully resolved. During the call, the member acknowledged that the personal loan has since been fully approved, funded, and received in their account, and the specialist apologized for the prior technical friction and service delays while confirming that the loan is now properly set up.
