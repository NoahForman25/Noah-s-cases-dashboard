## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction upon learning they were not eligible to reopen their account or reapply, noting they had contacted the bureaus to remove a security alert and stated they had done nothing wrong. The member sought clarity on whether the restriction was permanent or temporary and requested to speak with a supervisor. The agent apologized, confirmed the documentation indicated the member was ineligible to reopen or reapply, and attempted to transfer the call to a supervisor. Because the supervisor was unavailable on another call, the agent confirmed the member's phone number on file, arranged a supervisor callback, and concluded the interaction.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an account closure and the extended review timeline for two dispute claims involving Blue Guardian. Operational review confirmed that following an evaluation by the member security team, the account closure was upheld under institutional risk policies, rendering the customer permanently ineligible to reopen the account or reapply. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency confirming that the termination is final and non-reversible based on internal review findings, while an attempted phone escalation concluded with scheduling a supervisor callback to address any outstanding dissatisfaction. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's account remaining permanently closed and future application eligibility formally restricted.
