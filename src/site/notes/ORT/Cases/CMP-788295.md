## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding a delay in receiving a full balance withdrawal from their Robo Investing account. The member is frustrated that the liquidated funds have not yet been deposited into their external bank account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding a delay in receiving a full balance withdrawal from their Robo Investing account. The specialist addressed the member's frustration that the liquidated funds had not yet been deposited into their external bank account. Delivering a clear update, the specialist explained that the standard timeframe for these transfers is 4 to 6 business days, a processing period required to sell the shares, allow the trades to settle, and subsequently transfer the cash. Finally, providing a definitive resolution, the specialist confirmed that a review of system records shows the funds have already been successfully sent out to the member's SoFi bank account.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
- The resolution specialist attempted to contact the member regarding their complaint. The specialist did not receive the member's voicemail. The specialist will send a detailed email.
