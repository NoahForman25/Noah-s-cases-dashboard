## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding their ineligibility for a referral bonus after referring their daughter for a SoFi Checking and Savings account. The member explained that they applied a month ago without sending the referral link to their daughter first, resulting in no records of the referral on file, and insisted on creating a new account now to qualify. The agent explained that the referral link must be sent prior to registration and application to be eligible, making them ineligible for the referral bonus, and further educated them that they did not meet the $1,000 direct deposit requirement for the $50 welcome bonus. The member insisted on speaking with a supervisor or escalating the issue, and was advised that they would be contacted within 24 to 48 hours.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding ineligibility for a referral bonus after assisting their daughter in opening a SoFi Checking and Savings account without utilizing a referral link. Operational review confirmed that referral tracking policy strictly requires the referral link to be generated and used prior to registration, and verified that the associated $50 welcome bonus was not triggered due to the required $1,000 direct deposit threshold not being met. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided clear transparency regarding program terms, explained why retroactively applying the referral was not permitted, and issued 1,500 reward points to the customer's account as a goodwill gesture for participating in the referral program. The complaint is resolved, with the terms of the referral and welcome bonus programs fully clarified and the gesture points applied to the customer's profile.
