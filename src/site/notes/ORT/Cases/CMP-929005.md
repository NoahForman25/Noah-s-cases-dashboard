## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding difficulties depositing a $21,335.86 check made payable to Brock Jamison, Rachel Jamison, and JP Morgan Chase into their account. The check is being rejected due to a missing stamped endorsement from Chase. The member noted that they already contacted JP Morgan Chase and were advised that the bank does not stamp checks for mobile deposit.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding a rejected $21,335.86 mobile check deposit payable to multiple parties, including JP Morgan Chase. Operational review confirmed that mobile deposit policies strictly require an official stamped endorsement from co-payee financial institutions when multiple parties are named on the instrument to ensure valid negotiation and prevent check alterations. To address the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, acknowledged their friction with Chase's stamping policies, and explained the regulatory endorsement requirements needed to process the check. The complaint is closed, with the customer declining further assistance after securing an alternative method to negotiate the funds independently.
