## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a review hold on their account and the one-business-day timeline required for resolution. The member was frustrated with their experience because the funds were not made available immediately after being transferred to their SoFi account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a review hold placed on their account and the delay in accessing newly transferred funds. Operational review confirmed that the account hold was instituted due to pending security profile verification requirements that must be completed prior to the release of funds. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding the hold's security origin and instructed the customer to contact support to be connected with a member security agent for live identity verification. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining under review pending the completion of identity verification by the member security team to release the funds.
