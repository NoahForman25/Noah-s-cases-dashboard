## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding a denied dispute, explaining they believed the claim had been resolved in their favor when provisional credit was issued. The member noted that a recent miscellaneous debit related to dispute #8061250 resulted in a negative balance they cannot afford, emphasizing that this outcome causes financial strain and negatively impacts their personal finances.

## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
- Dear Favian,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. Thank you for reaching out regarding the denied dispute #8061250 and the resulting negative balance from the reversed provisional credit. We acknowledge your dissatisfaction regarding the financial strain this outcome has caused and your frustration that the claim was denied after the provisional credit was issued.

A review of your account and dispute #8061250 was conducted. Provisional credit is a temporary measure provided during the investigation process. Because the dispute investigation concluded that no error occurred, the provisional credit was reversed, which resulted in the miscellaneous debit and the current negative balance on your account.

The original denial remains in effect unless new supporting information is provided for review. If no new information is submitted, the case will remain closed. If new documentation is provided and an appeal is reviewed but denied, you will be notified of the final decision, and the ticket will be resolved. 

To recap, the provisional credit was reversed following the conclusion of the dispute investigation, and the original denial remains in effect unless new supporting information is provided. Please let us know if you have any further questions.
