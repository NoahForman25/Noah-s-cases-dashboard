## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction and stated SoFi was holding their money hostage after receiving a deposit and having their accounts suddenly restricted, preventing them from using funds for daily expenses. The member expressed frustration with the one-business-day timeframe provided for a case update and favorably compared their experiences with other banks that did not give them similar issues.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding account restrictions and subsequent online login issues following a deposit. Operational review confirmed that security restrictions had been reviewed and removed by the risk team, but the customer remained locked out of their online banking due to invalid login attempts. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, verified that all account restrictions had been fully lifted, and issued a direct password reset link to guide them through restoring access. The complaint is resolved, with account restrictions removed, full access to funds restored, and online credential access re-established for both web and mobile banking.
