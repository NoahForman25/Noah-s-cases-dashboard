## Complaint
- The customer called to express frustration regarding missing funds totaling $1,000 from two $500 Green Dot cash deposits made on September 25th that had not reflected in their account after three days. The customer stated that this delay caused extreme financial strain, left their account negative, and impacted their ability to commute to work. Additionally, the customer expressed dissatisfaction with being asked to contact a third party and threatened to close their account. The agent collected and verified receipts provided by the customer via email and filed a prioritized internal ticket for investigation with the back-office team.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding two missing $500 Green Dot cash deposits from September 25, 2026, which caused a negative account balance and severe financial strain. Operational review confirmed that the deposit transactions encountered processing delays during system transmission, preventing the funds from posting immediately to the customer's account balance. To resolve the complaint, the specialist collected and verified the deposit receipts provided by the customer and opened an expedited back-office investigation ticket to locate and manually credit the missing funds. The complaint is resolved, with the formal investigation actively underway to post the $1,000 credit to the customer's profile.
