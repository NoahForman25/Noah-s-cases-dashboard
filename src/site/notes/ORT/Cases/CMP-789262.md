## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction after being advised of the extended timeframe for funds availability dictated by the 62-Day Rule. Upon being provided with the specific approximate dates of when the hold would lift and their funds would be released, the member expressed continued dissatisfaction with the delay.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding the extended timeframe for funds availability dictated by the 62-Day Rule. The specialist addressed the member's continued frustration over the specific hold dates and the delay in releasing their funds. Delivering a clear update on the account rules, the specialist explained that a hold is required before funds can be transferred to an account other than the original funding source. To provide an immediate solution, the specialist advised the member that the funds can be transferred right away if they are returned directly to the original Schwab account from which they came.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
- The resolution specialist attempted to contact the member regarding their complaint. The specialist did receive the member's voicemail. The specialist will send a detailed email.
### Second Contact
- Dear Ivan,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. Thank you for reaching out regarding the extended timeframe for funds availability under the 62-Day Rule. We acknowledge your dissatisfaction regarding the delay and the approximate dates provided for the release of your funds.

To recap, the funds are subject to the 62-Day Rule hold for transfers to new accounts, and immediate transfers can only be processed by returning the funds to the original Schwab account. Please let us know if you have any further questions.
