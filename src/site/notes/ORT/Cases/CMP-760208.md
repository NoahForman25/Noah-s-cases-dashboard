## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding missing funds from a recent deposit. The member noted that while the transaction is visible within their account activity, the funds are not being reflected in their available balance. The issue concerns the following transfer: a JPMorgan Chase ACH deposit (ACHDDIN) executed on June 24, 2026, in the amount of $175.11.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address concerns regarding missing funds from a JPMorgan Chase ACH deposit of $175.11 on June 24, 2026. The specialist conducted a comprehensive review of the transaction history and delivered a clear resolution, confirming that the deposit was successfully credited and the account balances are calculating accurately. A detailed breakdown was provided, verifying that the account balance correctly updated from $55.86 to $230.97 upon receipt of the deposit, before a subsequent vault transfer adjusted the total balance to $230.86.
