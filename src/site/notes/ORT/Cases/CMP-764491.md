## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction and distrust regarding an email they received concerning their Annual Percentage Yield (APY). The member objected to the email's contents, which instructed them to transfer funds to avoid losing their current APY rate. Due to this communication, the member stated that they feel they have been scammed and explicitly expressed that they no longer trust the institution.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on an inquiry regarding their account and address their severe dissatisfaction and distrust concerning an email they received about their Annual Percentage Yield (APY) rate. During the interaction, the agent attempted to verify the member's identity; however, the member declined to proceed with the full verification process, explicitly stating that they had already withdrawn all the money from their account.
