## Complaint
- The member called SoFi after receiving a text notification stating that a $2,000 transaction to "Action Advocacy" via ACH/AFP was restricted and her account was locked. The agent confirmed that the transaction was processed, but the account had been placed under restriction by SoFi's investigation team.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding account restrictions and a blocked $500.00 transaction following a $2,000.00 ACH transfer to Action Advocacy. Operational review confirmed that an internal security investigation placed a temporary restriction on the account, which successfully allowed the $2,000.00 charge to post but prevented the subsequent $500.00 attempt from processing. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, confirmed that the risk team had fully removed the account hold, and issued courtesy reward points to compensate for the inconvenience caused by the failed transaction. The complaint is resolved, with all security restrictions lifted and the customer's account restored to full, unrestricted functionality.
