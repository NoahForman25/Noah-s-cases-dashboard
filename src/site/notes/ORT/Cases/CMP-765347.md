## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe frustration regarding difficulties cashing their account closure check, noting that both Walmart and a local credit union refused to accept it without providing a specific reason. The member further reported that when they attempted to cash it at Chase, they were informed the check could only be deposited into an active account rather than cashed directly. Due to their ongoing inability to access these funds and frustration with the situation, the member requested an immediate escalation to a supervisor.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their severe frustration regarding difficulties cashing an account closure check at multiple locations, which had prompted a previous request for a supervisor escalation. The specialist acknowledged the member's interactions with various institutions, including Chase's requirement that the check be deposited into an active account rather than cashed directly. Delivering a final resolution, the specialist confirmed that the member recently connected with an agent who successfully provided the necessary instructions to deposit the check, and noted that the team remains standing by to assist if any further issues arise.
