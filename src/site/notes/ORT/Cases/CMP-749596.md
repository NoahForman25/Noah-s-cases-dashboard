## Complaint
- Member contacted to express embarrassment and extreme frustration after their card was repeatedly declined at a merchant location. The member initially believed the declines were due to a malfunctioning PIN, prompting them to retrieve their spouse's card from home, which was also declined. Upon review, the agent explained that the second card attempted was an old, deactivated card. The member became highly agitated, used profanity, and disconnected before the agent could clarify that their primary card simply required activation. During the interaction, the member stated, "I can't believe they/you're holding my money from me."
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their embarrassment and extreme frustration regarding multiple card declines at a merchant location, which led the member to believe their funds were being withheld. The specialist provided necessary clarity on the situation, explaining that the secondary card attempted by the member's spouse was an older, deactivated card. Crucially, the specialist resolved the core issue by confirming that the primary debit card simply required activation, which was successfully completed on June 23, making the card fully active and ready for immediate use.
