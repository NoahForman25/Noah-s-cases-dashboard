## Complaint
- Member contacted to express extreme frustration regarding excessively long hold times, stating they had been on the phone for two hours—and nearly the entire day—attempting to reach the dispute team without success. The member inquired about the optimal time to call back to secure an immediate connection. They were provided with accurate, realistic scheduling information regarding department availability, ensuring no unrealistic expectations were set. Additionally, the member requested to speak with a supervisor, and this escalation request was noted and processed.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address a complaint regarding long hold times and an escalation request to speak with a supervisor, while following up on a pending transaction dispute. After completing identity verification using the member's full name, date of birth, and a phone verification code, the agent addressed the member's extreme frustration over waiting on the phone for two hours to reach the dispute team. Providing a clear resolution, the agent delivered realistic scheduling information regarding department availability to set accurate expectations for future calls, processed the supervisor escalation request, and successfully initiated a formal dispute for an unauthorized $11.00 posted transaction after gathering details confirming the member had not shared their debit card or PIN.

### First contact
