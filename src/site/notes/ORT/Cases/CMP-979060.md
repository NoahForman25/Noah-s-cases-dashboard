## Complaint
- The member complained regarding their APY, stating they expected a 4.40% rate after receiving direct deposit but saw it increase to only 3.30%. The member noted they were previously told by a SoFi agent that setting up direct deposit would increase their rate to 4.40%, which proved incorrect because the account lacked the APY Boost offer. The member requested to have the APY Boost offer applied to make their account eligible for the 4.40% APY, and requested to escalate the case further.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email and outbound phone calls to address his complaint regarding receiving conflicting representative guidance and experiencing an interest rate shortfall between an expected 4.40% APY and the active 3.30% rate. Operational review confirmed that direct deposit alone only triggered the baseline 3.30% rate because the account lacked the promotional APY Boost offer, and a previously promised referral link had failed to connect due to deposit criteria discrepancies. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding APY boost tiers and member benefits, conducted agent coaching for the representative who provided inaccurate details, and credited 7,500 reward points to the customer's profile alongside 2,500 reward points to his referee's profile to cover the earned referral and accommodate the misinformation. The complaint is resolved, with the reward points credited and the customer's account remaining active, fully accessible, and in good standing.
