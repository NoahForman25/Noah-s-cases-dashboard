## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding marketing materials that mention Trump, interpreting the content as an indication that SoFi is politically aligned with him.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong dissatisfaction regarding marketing materials referencing Trump, which the member interpreted as SoFi taking a political stance. The specialist apologized for the concerning wording and clarified that the reference pertained strictly to the official name of a federal account program, rather than an endorsement or political alignment with any party or figure. The specialist reassured the member that their feedback had been documented for internal review and offered assistance with updating their marketing communication preferences if desired.
