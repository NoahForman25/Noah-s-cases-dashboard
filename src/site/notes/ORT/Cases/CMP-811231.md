## Complaint
- The member is frustrated that the app prompts users to open additional Self-Directed Invest accounts, yet only permits opening Robo Invest accounts, which they view as misleading and non-functional functionality. Additionally, the member expressed strong dissatisfaction with customer support for repeatedly offering generic troubleshooting advice—such as uninstalling and reinstalling the app—rather than resolving the underlying system issue.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding the application prompting them to open additional Self-Directed Invest accounts while only permitting Robo Invest account creation, as well as their frustration with customer support offering repetitive, generic troubleshooting advice. The specialist acknowledged the member's perception of misleading functionality and apologized for the unhelpful support experience involving repeated app reinstallation requests. Providing clarity on the feature, the specialist explained that opening multiple Self-Directed Invest accounts is part of a newly rolling out system capability and outlined specific refresh steps—including updating the device operating system, clearing app cache, and re-logging into the profile—to enable the new functionality on the account dashboard.
