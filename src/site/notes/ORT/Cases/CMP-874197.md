## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with the adverse decision on their application and requested to speak directly with an Account Manager to discuss potential alternative loan options or paths for reconsideration. Support acknowledged the member's dissatisfaction, explained the automated underwriting guidelines and policy parameters surrounding application decisions, and submitted an urgent escalation ticket to Lending Operations for an Account Manager callback to review alternative credit options.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an inquiry regarding their declined personal loan application and to discuss paths for future reapplication. After authenticating the member, the agent addressed their dissatisfaction with the adverse application decision and their request to speak with an Account Manager regarding alternative lending options or reconsideration. While support had previously explained automated underwriting guidelines and escalated the case to Lending Operations for a manager callback, the specialist clarified the exact eligibility timeline for submitting a new request. The specialist informed the member that platform policy requires a 30-day waiting period following a decline, making them eligible to reapply on or around September 27, 2026. After the member expressed a desire to ensure the next submission is completed accurately, the specialist advised them to call support directly when ready so an agent can assist them through the application process.
