## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction and frustration regarding the closure of their account and the subsequent arrival of a new debit card. The member questioned why the account was closed, whether it was due to an internal decision or an unauthorized third-party request, and demanded to know why it was shut down despite recent activity. The member requested to reapply for an account and demanded an immediate escalation to a supervisor, stating:

"So how can y'all send me a ******* card after y'all close the ******* account? Like and why and did the account get closed? Because someone requested it, or did it get closed because you guys closed it? And if there was activity on the account, why it get closed? Like, I don't understand this ****. Y'all allow somebody to access my account."

During the interaction, the member became repeatedly verbally abusive and used profane language. Despite multiple behavior warnings provided by the agent, the member's abusive conduct continued.

### Second Contact
- Hi Christina,  
  
This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. We are reaching out regarding the recent concerns raised about the account closure and the subsequent arrival of a new debit card. We completely understand why receiving a card after an account closure, along with learning about activity on the profile, would be alarming, and we are here to provide clarity on this matter.  
  
We have completed a comprehensive review of the account. Based on this review, the decision to close the account is final.   
  
To provide clear expectations, we are unable to reopen or reinstate the account, and we cannot disclose the specific internal review criteria or transactions that led to this decision. Additionally, based on this determination, there is no eligibility to reopen the prior account or apply for new SoFi bank products.  
  
We have accurately documented the concerns regarding the receipt of the debit card and the dispute of unauthorized activity. We are ensuring these details are fully recorded on the profile to reflect the exact concerns raised.  
  
To recap, the account closure is final, and we have fully documented the disputes regarding the recent account activity and debit card delivery. Please let us know if there are any further questions regarding the available communications or next steps—we are here to help.
