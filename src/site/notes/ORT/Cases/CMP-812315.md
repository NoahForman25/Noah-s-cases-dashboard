## Complaint
- The member expressed anger and frustration regarding eight unauthorized debit card charges of $50.00 each from "Google ADS1376696023" on July 25, 2026, totaling $400.00 in pending transactions. Citing serious security concerns over these unwanted charges, the member demanded immediate account closure.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to address a complaint regarding multiple unauthorized transactions on their account, where the member reported eight pending debit card charges of $50.00 each from "Google ADS1376696023" totaling $400.00, alongside additional posted transactions totaling $172.00. Expressing severe anger and security concerns over the unwanted charges and a prior scam call, the member requested assistance with disputing the transactions and demanded immediate account closure. The agent authenticated the member, gathered transaction details, confirmed that account credentials and PINs were not shared, and explained that posted charges must follow standard dispute workflows while pending transactions settle. To reassure the member, the agent confirmed that the dispute information was documented and that a specialized dispute agent would follow up via email to process the claims.
