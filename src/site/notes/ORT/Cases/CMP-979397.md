## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a delayed tax refund sent by the IRS to their closed SoFi checking and savings account, which had been closed on September 17, 2026, due to an unpaid negative balance. The member's primary concern was that the funds were not returned to the IRS within the expected three-business-day timeframe, and they repeatedly asked the agent to confirm whether the deposit had been received and returned. As frustration escalated, the member raised the possibility of legal action, stated that the agent was not listening, repeatedly demanded that the agent "stop saying the account was closed," and requested to speak with someone else, citing difficulty with the agent's language comprehension.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding extended account restrictions, communication delays, and unaccepted incoming transactions. Operational review confirmed that two incoming ACH transfers attempted on September 24 and October 23—identified as recurring IRS tax refunds—could not be accepted or credited because the profile was already closed, causing the funds to automatically return to the sender. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency explaining that funds directed to a terminated account are automatically rejected by system controls, advising that the customer must update direct deposit information directly with the IRS to redirect payments to an active account. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining permanently closed and the incoming transactions rejected back to the originating agency.
