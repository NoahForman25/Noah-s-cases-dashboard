## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a discrepancy between the APR and the fixed interest rate on their loan, stating it differed from what was previously provided. The agent clarified that the offered interest rate was lower than the APR mentioned (7.12% APR, or 6.86% after the autopay discount). The member stated they did not wish to proceed with the loan and disconnected the call.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a perceived discrepancy between the fixed interest rate and the higher Annual Percentage Rate (APR) on their loan offer. Operational review confirmed that the rates were calculated accurately according to standard lending formulas, with the 4.77% fixed interest rate representing the simple ongoing interest charge and the 7.12% APR reflecting the total cost of borrowing inclusive of the $1,950 origination fee. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency explaining how finance charges and origination fees cause the APR to exceed the base interest rate, while confirming the rate figures generated for the application were correct. The complaint is resolved, with the loan explanation provided and the application remaining withdrawn per the customer's choice not to proceed.
