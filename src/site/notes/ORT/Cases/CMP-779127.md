## Complaint
- Member is upset about acat out fee and ira close out fee
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding an Automated Customer Account Transfer (ACAT) out fee and an IRA close out fee applied during their recent account transfer. The specialist delivered a clear update, confirming that the transfer was successfully completed and explaining that both charges were standard fees applied in accordance with the institution's official fee schedule to finalize the transaction.
