## Complaint
- The member inquired about a pending $100.00 refund from Walmart, expressing strong dissatisfaction with the processing timeline and alleging that SoFi is holding their money after Walmart confirmed the credit was issued. Highlighting the urgency of the situation, the member explained that the $100.00 was urgently needed to purchase an inhaler for their child, which they cannot afford while the funds remain unavailable. Citing severe frustration with SoFi's handling of the situation, difficulties in getting matters resolved quickly, and a lack of trust, the member stated their intention to switch banks once their direct deposit and the refund post. Support acknowledged the urgent medical necessity, detailed standard merchant credit processing frames—noting a separate partial merchant credit of $22.83 from WALMART.COM—and submitted an urgent priority escalation to Deposit Operations to track and expedite the incoming $100.00 merchant credit.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to address her concerns regarding a delayed $99.99 refund from Walmart following the issuance of a new debit card, as well as a separate pending $100.00 merchant credit needed urgently to purchase an inhaler for her child. During the call, the member expressed strong dissatisfaction with the processing timeline, alleging that SoFi was holding her money after Walmart confirmed the credit was issued, and stated her intention to switch banks due to a loss of trust and ongoing difficulty getting issues resolved. The specialist authenticated the member, explained that Mastercard automatically updates merchant records with new card details so incoming refunds route properly, and confirmed that the formal $99.99 dispute remains actively in progress. Acknowledging the critical medical necessity of the funds, the specialist explained standard merchant processing timeframes, confirmed a separate partial credit of $22.83 from WALMART.COM, and submitted an urgent priority escalation ticket to Deposit Operations to locate, track, and expedite the release of the incoming $100.00 merchant credit as quickly as possible.
