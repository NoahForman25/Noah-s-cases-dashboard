## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an ongoing restriction on their Crypto account that has remained unresolved for weeks, stating, "wtf this is ridiculous" in reaction to the situation.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a prolonged, unresolved restriction placed on their Crypto account. Operational review confirmed that the hold stemmed from an extended internal review process that experienced an unexpected administrative delay. To resolve the complaint, the specialist coordinated directly with the Crypto team to expedite the case, secured confirmation that all review criteria were met, and successfully removed the restriction from the profile. The complaint is resolved, with the hold lifted and the customer's Crypto account fully restored to active status.
