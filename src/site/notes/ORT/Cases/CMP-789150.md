## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding multiple system barriers blocking them from opening a Robo-advisor account. The member explained that they recently relocated and need to update their address; however, their new address is not recognized or verified by Lob.com (the third-party address verification system), which prevents the application from accepting it. Additionally, the member reported recurring technical issues with uploading their government-issued ID, leaving them unable to complete the identity verification (IDV) process and finalize their account setup.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member to address their frustration regarding multiple system barriers blocking them from opening a Robo-advisor account following a recent relocation. The specialist addressed the member's technical issues with uploading their government-issued ID and the fact that their new address is not recognized by the third-party verification system, Lob.com. Delivering a clear next step, the specialist advised the member that if the institutional system indicates their address is invalid, they must contact the United States Postal Service (USPS) to ensure it is fully updated in the postal network. The specialist instructed the member that once the address is validated by USPS, or if it is already verified as correct on their end, they should reach back out to the institution so they can successfully update their profile address, complete the identity verification process, and finalize the setup of their Robo account.
