## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi support while in the hospital to report an unauthorized $16.08 Domino's Pizza charge on their newly issued debit card. The agent explained that because the charge was pending, it could not be disputed immediately, and instructed the member to call back or chat once the charge posts. The agent issued a replacement debit card, but the member expressed frustration, stating they cannot add the card to Apple Pay because the individual who compromised the account knows how to access their Apple Pay. The member requested a supervisor and expressed dissatisfaction with having to call back to report the charge, noting that the same individual had previously taken $2,400 from the account. The member stated they can no longer bank with SoFi and requested additional account security. The agent arranged a callback from an account manager within 24 hours.
