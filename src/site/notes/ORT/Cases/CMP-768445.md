## Complaint
- Member contacted to express concern regarding a missing referral bonus, claiming they utilized a referral link when opening their active Invest account. However, the member expressed uncertainty regarding the exact name of the individual who referred them.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their concern regarding a missing referral bonus for an active Invest account, noting the member's uncertainty regarding the exact name of the individual who referred them. The specialist conducted a comprehensive review of the profile and provided a final resolution, confirming that there is no referral link attached to the member's account. Furthermore, the member was educated on standard promotional guidelines, which dictate that new member referral bonuses apply exclusively to an individual's first account; because the member already possesses an active banking account, the profile is ineligible for new member promotions. Finally, the specialist informed the member that they remain eligible to earn promotional rewards by sharing their own unique referral link with others in the future.
