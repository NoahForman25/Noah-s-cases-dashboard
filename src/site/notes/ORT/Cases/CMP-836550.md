## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding the security verification process, noting that this was their third or fourth attempt to complete it. Additionally, the member raised urgent concerns about processing delays, explaining that they are scheduled to travel to Italy on Thursday morning and need immediate access to their account and funds. Support verified the previous verification attempts, escalated the case for expedited priority review to Fraud/Account Operations, and documented the member's imminent travel timeline to ensure rapid resolution before their departure.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an inquiry regarding a wire transfer and ongoing security verification issues. After authenticating the member, the agent discussed the repeated difficulties encountered with the member's driver's license photos, which had stalled the verification process across three or four prior attempts. The member expressed strong dissatisfaction with the repeated delays and raised urgent concerns regarding account access, explaining that they are scheduled to travel to Italy on Thursday morning and require immediate access to their funds. Recognizing the critical departure timeline, the agent placed the member on hold and successfully transferred them directly to a member security agent. Support verified the prior verification attempts, documented the member's imminent international travel schedule, and escalated the case for expedited priority review with Fraud and Account Operations to ensure rapid resolution prior to departure.
