## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding a hold placed by SoFi on a $4,527.02 Royal Caribbean transaction (ANTHEM OF THE SEAS) processed on September 22, 2026. The member requested that the funds be released immediately so they could pay their home mortgage.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a $4,527.02 pre-authorization hold from Royal Caribbean and their request for an immediate release of funds to pay a home mortgage. Operational review confirmed that the merchant documentation submitted by the customer was insufficient to meet early release criteria ahead of established risk policy schedules. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided clear transparency regarding the documentation review outcome and confirmed that the $4,527.02 hold is set to release automatically on October 26, 2026. The complaint is resolved, with the authorization hold remaining active under standard processing timelines until the scheduled release date when full funds will be returned to the available balance.
