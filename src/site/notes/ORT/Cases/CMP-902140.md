## Complaint
- The member called regarding discrepancies between their expected interest rate, APR, and payment amounts on their in-progress personal loan agreement. A loan specialist and supervisor explained that while the fixed interest rate is 3.71%, the listed APR of 5.68% includes the $1,000 origination fee, and the document payment amount of $21.64 does not reflect the 0.25% autopay discount (which lowers the payment to $21.59). The member requested written confirmation via email explaining how the $2,958.80 finance charge was calculated and which rate was used before signing the agreement.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their concerns regarding discrepancies in their personal loan disclosures, specifically regarding interest rates, annual percentage rates (APR), and monthly payment amounts. The specialist clarified that the overall finance charge and the higher listed APR accurately incorporate both the estimated interest at the fixed rate of 3.71% and the $1,000 loan origination fee, while explaining that initial contract payment amounts do not display the active 0.25% AutoPay discount until setting activation is complete. To resolve the complaint and provide the requested transparency, the specialist detailed the exact mathematical calculation behind the $2,958.80 finance charge and verified that the lower monthly payment will automatically reflect once AutoPay takes effect. The complaint is resolved, with the loan agreement remaining active and ready for final signing by the customer.
