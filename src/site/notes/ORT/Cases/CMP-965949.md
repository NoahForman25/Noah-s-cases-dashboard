## Complaint
- The member contacted support in distress after their account was restricted by the security department, leaving them unable to use their card, pay their phone bill, make calls, or access their funds. The member explained they are deaf and physically unable to make phone calls, presenting an inaccessible barrier because the security department only conducts business via phone and not chat. Prior to this, the agent had attempted an unanswered call to the member and subsequently suggested borrowing someone else's phone, failing to recognize that the member's condition is permanent. The member stated, "you're holding my money" with a heartbreak emoji, asking if the bank intended to hold their funds indefinitely. The agent acknowledged the member's frustration but stated the banking department does not manage account restrictions, leaving the member without a clear path forward to access their funds.
## Resolution
-  A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding severe accessibility barriers preventing her from resolving an account restriction over chat, which left her unable to access her funds or pay bills. Operational review confirmed that following an internal fraud review, the accounts were permanently closed on November 4 due to deposit fraud, initiating standard balance liquidation procedures. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency by confirming that the remaining funds would be disbursed via a mailed check to the verified address on file within 10 to 15 business days, while establishing an accommodation option to connect with the fraud team via phone with a translator present. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining permanently closed pending the delivery of the balance disbursement check.
