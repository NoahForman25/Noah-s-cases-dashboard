## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction after their debit card was canceled due to a fraud dispute filed for unauthorized transactions. Despite being advised of security protocols and given instructions on using a virtual card as an immediate alternative, K refused the workaround, stating that the specific physical card number was required for a pending application they were waiting on. The member insisted that the original physical card be reactivated and, upon being informed that canceled cards cannot be unblocked, requested a supervisor escalation.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on a complaint regarding the cancellation of their physical debit card following a fraud dispute, which threatened to disrupt a pending government application tied to that specific card number. The member strongly objected to standard security protocols, rejected using a virtual card alternative, and demanded the original card be reactivated or the matter escalated to a supervisor. After authenticating the member, the agent reiterated that once a card is canceled due to security measures, it cannot be unblocked, and offered 2,000 goodwill reward points to compensate for the inconvenience. The member declined the points, emphasizing that the issue was ensuring government access to their funds rather than financial compensation, and threatened account closure over the disruption. In response, the agent confirmed that the underlying unauthorized transactions were actively being processed under an open dispute, with updates to be provided directly via email as the investigation proceeds.
