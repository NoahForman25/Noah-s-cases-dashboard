## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding the inability to negotiate a lower interest rate on a Personal Loan Refinance application, stating that it feels disingenuous given their long-standing membership with SoFi. The member voiced frustration over the lack of rate flexibility and personal consideration for loyal members seeking relief to lower their monthly payments, reflecting a feeling that SoFi is not doing enough to assist existing members in difficult financial situations. Support explained the automated underwriting and fixed risk-based pricing policies that govern personal loan rates, documented the member's feedback regarding member loyalty recognition and rate negotiation options, and provided information on alternative account features or payment options that might help lower monthly obligations.
