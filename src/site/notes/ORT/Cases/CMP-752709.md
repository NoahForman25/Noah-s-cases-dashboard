## Complaint
- Member contacted to request that a formal complaint be filed against a previous agent who promised their account would be unrestricted within one business day, noting that the restriction remains in place past the stated timeline. The member also requested additional financial compensation due to the extended delay and a perceived lack of adequate security measures on their account. The member emphasized the urgency of the situation, stating that they require immediate access to their funds in order to transfer them to Vanguard.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their previous difficulties experienced with their Crypto account and the transition process. The specialist issued an apology for the friction encountered during the transition, noting that the process should have been significantly smoother, and thanked the member for the feedback to aid in future system improvements. Ultimately, a final resolution was provided, confirming that the profile was reviewed and the Crypto account has been officially closed per the member's request.
