## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction with the dispute process, asserting that permanent credit should be granted while the investigation is underway.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding the dispute handling and her demand for permanent credit during the ongoing investigation of a $237.48 transaction. Operational review confirmed that the charge is not governed by Regulation E requirements, precluding the issuance of provisional or early permanent credit while the dispute undergoes standard inquiry. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct written guidance explaining these regulatory constraints and clarified that any credit determination is strictly contingent upon the investigation's final outcome. The complaint is resolved, with the formal dispute review actively proceeding and the customer's account pending the final investigative findings.
