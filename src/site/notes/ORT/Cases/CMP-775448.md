## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding a delay in receiving their funds, noting that a full balance withdrawal initiated from their Robo Roth IRA has not yet been reflected in their linked external bank account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on an inquiry regarding a full balance withdrawal initiated from their Robo Roth IRA, addressing the member's dissatisfaction over the delay in the funds reflecting in their linked external bank account. During the call, the agent attempted to verify the member's identity using their full name and date of birth; however, the member expressed confusion and stated they had not initiated any recent inquiries. Consequently, after confirming the member had no other immediate concerns, the agent concluded the call.
