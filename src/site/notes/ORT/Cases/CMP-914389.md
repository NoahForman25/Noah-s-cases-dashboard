## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding an inability to receive money to their debit card, despite being able to send funds and use services like Cash App or Apple Pay. The member requested to speak with a manager, asked for a callback, and hung up.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding technical failures preventing incoming fund transfers to their debit card from third-party services like Cash App. Operational review confirmed a temporary debit card system processing defect affecting multiple account holders, which blocked inbound card deposits while leaving outbound transactions operational. To resolve the complaint, the specialist informed the customer of the ongoing system fix, advised periodic re-attempts as technical teams work to restore functionality, and credited 1,500 rewards points to the customer's profile as goodwill for the service disruption. The complaint is resolved, with the technical issue actively being remediated by engineering and promotional points issued to the customer's account.
