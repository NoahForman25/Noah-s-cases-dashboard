## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with SoFi after a loan offer was rescinded after they attempted to fix an error in the agreement they had been sent to sign. The member requested a formal complaint to be filed and a written explanation for the decision, noting they were considering ending their banking relationship with SoFi entirely. The chatbot informed the member that the loan was still open and pending signature, but stated it could not file a formal complaint or provide a written explanation. After offering to connect the member to a live agent, the chatbot indicated that no live agents were available.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the rescission of their personal loan offer during an agreement correction and the subsequent difficulties experienced when attempting to submit a grievance through the automated chatbot. Operational review confirmed that the loan offer was formally rescinded following a mandatory application re-evaluation triggered during the document revision process. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided the requested written explanation detailing the review decision, clarified that new applications submitted within 30 days face automatic decline, and outlined alternative pathing including co-borrower options or application revival eligibility if an error occurred. The complaint is resolved, with the original loan offer remaining rescinded and the customer provided clear criteria for future re-application.

We recognize the frustration caused by the offer being rescinded during that process, as well as the difficulty you experienced when attempting to file a formal complaint and receive a written explanation through the chatbot.

We reviewed the matter and confirmed that the loan offer was rescinded. Regarding your request for a written explanation of the decision, we are providing that explanation here. When a loan offer is rescinded, the application is reviewed based on the information available at the time of the decision. The offer was withdrawn based on the outcome of that review.

Regarding next steps, you may submit a new Personal Loan application; however, a new application submitted within 30 calendar days of the decline will be automatically declined. If your circumstances change after 30 days, you may submit a new application at that time. For eligible situations, you may also apply with a co-borrower. If the decline appears to have been made in error, the application may be eligible for review and revival, but only if it was incorrectly declined, is within 30 days, and was not an automatic soft-credit-pull decline.
