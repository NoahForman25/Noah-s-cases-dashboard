## Complaint
- Member contacted to express significant dissatisfaction with an ongoing, unresolved merchant complaint regarding a $64.98 charge from Bloom and Bond. The member explained they have been going back and forth with the institution without a resolution after ordering an item that was never received despite the funds being deducted from their account, and they warned that they intend to file a formal complaint with the Better Business Bureau (BBB) due to their extreme frustration with the situation. Empathy was extended to the member regarding the impact of the issue, and they were informed that a formal dispute can be filed for the charge since the item was never delivered and the merchant is no longer cooperating with them.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their significant dissatisfaction and extreme frustration regarding an unresolved merchant complaint involving a $64.98 charge from Bloom and Bond for an item that was never received. The specialist addressed the member's warning regarding their intent to file a formal complaint with the Better Business Bureau (BBB) due to the ongoing back-and-forth communication issues. Delivering a clear resolution, the specialist confirmed that because the merchandise was never delivered and the merchant is no longer cooperating, a formal dispute has been successfully filed on the member's behalf and is currently under active investigation by a specialized team to recover the deducted funds.
