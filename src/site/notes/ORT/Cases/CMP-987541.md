## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding a delay with their cryptocurrency deposit being stuck as pending due to a known outage. The agent informed the member that the issue is being worked on and that delays will continue. The member alleged that the company is holding the funds until the price of crypto rises, and stated they will be contacting the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB).
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding an extended pending status on a cryptocurrency deposit and concerns of intentional fund holds. Operational review determined that the processing delay stemmed from a known system outage alongside standard security reviews designed to verify transaction legitimacy. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified account records, issued an apology for the service disruption, and provided operational transparency confirming that the underlying technical issues had been remediated. The complaint is resolved, with all pending crypto trades successfully posted and executed on November 5, leaving the customer's account active and in good standing.
