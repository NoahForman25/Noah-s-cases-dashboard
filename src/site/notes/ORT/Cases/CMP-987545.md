## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an active pending transaction from APPLE.COM/BILL of approximately $44.00 after being informed that pending charges cannot be canceled or disputed until they officially post. The member also noted concerns regarding automated system alerts for multiple attempted charges on October 28, 29, and 30, several of which appeared to have been declined.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding recurring authorization attempts from Apple and the inability to dispute a pending charge of approximately $44.00. Operational review confirmed that multiple attempted charges occurred between October 28 and October 30, and explained that standard network processing rules require transactions to fully settle before a formal dispute can be initiated. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the $44.00 transaction had officially posted, confirmed eligibility to initiate the dispute claim immediately, and advised the customer to report future alert notifications promptly to help intercept unauthorized postings. The customer's account remains active, open, and in good standing, pending intake to finalize the dispute for the settled charge.
