## Complaint
- The member expressed significant frustration and emotional distress regarding an ongoing issue over approximately three months, stating it negatively impacted their ability to consolidate debt, lower monthly payments, and prepare for a mortgage application. The member raised concerns that their request for a Personal Loan Refinance was incorrectly processed as a regular Personal Loan. The agent reviewed the situation and explained that the recent application was denied due to underwriting factors, including debt-to-income ratio and recent loan activity. The agent filed a complaint, submitted an MTS ticket to the tech engineering team to investigate why the refinance application converted to a regular personal loan, and escalated the member's concerns regarding the extended timeframe, repeated contacts, financial hardship, and emotional distress.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the processing of their Personal Loan Refinance application as a standard Personal Loan, which contributed to an unexpected denial and set back their financial plans. Operational review confirmed that while the loan application was declined due to underwriting guidelines—specifically debt-to-income ratio and recent credit activity—a system error occurred during intake that converted the refinance request into a standard loan application. To resolve the complaint, the specialist escalated a technical support ticket to the engineering team to investigate the system conversion error and documented the customer's financial hardship and extended resolution timeline. The complaint is resolved, with the technical investigation actively under review by engineering while the application remains declined under current underwriting criteria.
