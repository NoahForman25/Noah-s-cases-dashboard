## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction with the outcome of a dispute investigation for a $90.00 debit card transaction at V & J LIQUOR STORE on July 9, 2026. The member felt the claim was unfairly denied despite providing all available information, as they were unable to provide the physical receipt requested by the review team due to it being lost or damaged.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding a denied dispute for a $90.00 debit card transaction at V & J LIQUOR STORE on July 9, 2026. The specialist acknowledged the member's feeling that the claim was unfairly denied simply because they could not provide a physical receipt due to it being lost or damaged. Delivering next steps for resolution, the specialist outlined the dispute appeal process and advised the member to contact the dispute team directly if they wish to initiate a formal appeal for the transaction.
