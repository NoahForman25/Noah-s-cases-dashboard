## Complaint
- Member contacted to express significant frustration regarding a previous interaction, stating that the prior agent was highly rude and disconnected the call abruptly. The member emphasized that they were hung up on before receiving full assistance and while still seeking clarification on matters they did not understand.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address their significant frustration regarding a previous call during which they felt an agent was highly rude and abruptly disconnected them before they could receive full assistance or clarify matters they did not understand. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's feeling of being unsupported and their frustration over being unable to reset their password. Delivering a supportive resolution, the agent apologized for the poor experience, explained that the disconnection was likely accidental rather than intentional, and successfully offered the member 2,000 promotional points (equivalent to $20.00) as compensation for the inconvenience. The member expressed their appreciation for the follow-up, and the call was successfully concluded.
