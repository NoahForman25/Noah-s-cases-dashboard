## Complaint
- The member called to inquire about the status of an unposted **$25,000.00** check payment intended for their personal loan account. The member explained that they mailed the **$25,000.00** check together with a separate **$330.12** check in the same envelope, but the larger payment has not yet posted to their account, prompting them to request an immediate transfer to a manager. Support verified the status of incoming payments on the account, documented the missing check details and mailing specifics, and submitted an urgent supervisor callback ticket to Loan Operations to investigate and locate the unposted **$25,000.00** check.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their concerns regarding an unposted $25,000.00 check payment intended for their personal loan account and their request for a manager escalation. The member had previously called support after noticing that a smaller $330.12 check mailed in the exact same envelope had already processed, while the larger $25,000.00 payment remained unposted, causing significant concern and prompting an urgent supervisor callback request to Loan Operations. Following an account review and investigation by Loan Operations into the incoming check status, the specialist confirmed that the $25,000.00 check payment has now been successfully located, processed, and posted to the member's personal loan account. The specialist apologized for the delay in processing and the resulting anxiety, confirming that the payment is fully reconciled and offering continued support if any further questions arise.
