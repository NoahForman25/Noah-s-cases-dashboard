### Second Contact
- Dear Kyle,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. We acknowledge your complaint regarding frustration with the process of adding a bank account on SoFi. We recognize the difficulty you experienced when attempting to complete this process.

We reviewed your account and confirmed that a previous representative walked you through the steps for making future payments. We are providing those steps again below for your reference.

To make a payment on your personal loan electronically through your bank account, follow these steps:

Log in to your SoFi account and select "View Details" on your personal loan.
Select "Make a 1-Time Payment," then select "Make Payment."
Select the "Payment Type" from the drop-down menu:
Scheduled Payment: Advances your due date if paid in full.
Payoff: Pays off the remaining loan balance.
Select the "Bank Account" drop-down menu. To add a new bank account, select "Add new bank account," enter the required information, and select "Save."
Select the "Payment Amount":
Next due amount
Past due amount, if applicable
Other amount: Enter a custom amount.
Select the "Payment Date" using the calendar icon. Payments must be scheduled by 5:59 PM PST, or 8:59 PM EST, for same-day processing.
Check the box to acknowledge that the one-time payment will be pulled in addition to any autopay payment that has already been authorized.
Select "Continue."
To remove a bank account from your list of active accounts, select "Remove an Account" from the "Bank Account" drop-down menu, select the account to be removed, and select "Remove Bank Account."
