## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a denied fraud claim involving a $2,900.00 wire transfer and requested the associated report or claim number. They contacted SoFi seeking to speak directly with the fraud department and became frustrated when the agent attempted to clarify the transaction's status rather than immediately connecting them to an account manager, insisting on being transferred to speak with a specialist.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint and inquiries regarding a pending $2,900.00 wire transfer and dissatisfaction with prior customer service interactions. Operational review confirmed that the inbound wire transfer remains in a pending status awaiting processing and release from the sending financial institution, which operates without a fixed external settlement deadline. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, clarified that the request remains open pending the sender's institution review, provided proactive guidance on handling an upcoming $5,000 transfer to mitigate potential fund holds, and offered a referral to a credit specialist to explore product options. The complaint is resolved, with the wire transfer actively monitored for receipt and clear guidance provided to prevent future transaction holds.
