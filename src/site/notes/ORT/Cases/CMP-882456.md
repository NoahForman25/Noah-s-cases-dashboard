## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with the SoFi Plus subscription model, stating that paying a recurring fee solely to receive a loan rate discount or related benefits does not make financial sense to them. Support acknowledged the member's perspective regarding the cost-benefit trade-offs of the premium tier, explained the broader ecosystem features and rewards included with SoFi Plus, and documented their feedback for Management and Product Strategy to review pricing structures and member benefit options.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her complaint regarding the SoFi Plus subscription model and her concern that paying a recurring membership fee strictly to access loan rate discounts or associated perks does not align with her financial goals. The specialist acknowledged her feedback regarding the cost-benefit structure of the premium tier and confirmed that her perspective was formally documented for Management and Product Strategy to evaluate future pricing structures and benefit offerings. To help alleviate her concerns, the specialist credited enough reward points to her profile to cover one full month of the membership fee, allowing her to evaluate the complete tier features at no additional expense.
