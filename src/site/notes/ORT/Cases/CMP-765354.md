## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction and concern regarding a lock on their account, which is preventing them from using their funds to pay bills. The member strongly objected to the delay, refusing to fall behind on their bills, and accused the institution of holding their money hostage. The member stated that they would never keep their money with SoFi again, expressed disbelief that they have to wait to access their own paycheck, and threatened to file formal complaints with both the FTC and the FDIC. Due to their dissatisfaction, the member requested an escalation and agreed to receive a callback from a supervisor.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on their severe dissatisfaction regarding an account lock that has prevented them from paying bills, addressing their previous escalation request and threats to file complaints with the FTC and FDIC. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's specific concern regarding a pending fraudulent transaction from Belden Jewelers that has remained on hold for over a week. The agent delivered a clear resolution, explaining that the dispute is still actively being investigated and that the hold is scheduled to expire in two days. The member was informed that if the merchant does not officially post the charge by that expiration date, the held funds will automatically be released back into their account balance.
