## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding marketing communications they received after working with SoFi, which they attributed to data sharing practices. The member's primary concern was that their information had been shared, resulting in unwanted marketing contacts.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding third-party marketing communications received after working with SoFi. The member had previously expressed concern that their personal information had been shared with external parties, attributing a surge of unwanted solicitations directly to SoFi's data handling practices. The specialist apologized for the inconvenience caused by the unwanted contacts and reviewed the account's communication settings. The specialist confirmed that a previous support representative had already updated the profile to fully disable all marketing communications from SoFi. Additionally, the specialist requested that the member reply if any unwanted promotional messages persist so that technical support can investigate further and ensure all marketing channels remain deactivated.
