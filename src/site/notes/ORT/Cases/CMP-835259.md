## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding the handling of an Apple transaction dispute initially filed in July 2026. After their first claim was denied, the member promptly submitted appeal reasoning and required supporting documentation; however, a representative failed to attach the paperwork, resulting in the appeal never being properly submitted. Upon following up this week, the member learned that a new dispute had to be initiated from scratch—restarting the entire timeline through operational error despite the member fulfilling all requirements in a timely manner. The member emphasized that they are well past the standard 10-day provisional credit window, have immediate financial obligations to meet today, and should not suffer continued delays due to agent processing errors. Given the failure to submit documentation and the improper reset of the dispute timeline, the member requested an immediate escalation. Support logged the operational intake error, captured all previously omitted documentation, and submitted an urgent high-priority complaint for immediate risk management and executive dispute review regarding provisional credit issuance.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong dissatisfaction regarding an operational error that mishandled an Apple transaction dispute filed in July 2026. The specialist acknowledged the member's frustration after learning that a representative had previously failed to attach their timely submitted appeal documentation, improperly forcing the dispute timeline to reset from scratch despite the member being past the standard 10-day provisional credit window and facing immediate financial obligations. The specialist confirmed that the dispute team has now successfully received all required supporting documentation and is actively reviewing the appeal. Additionally, the specialist confirmed that the dispute team has been formally notified of the member's urgent request for provisional credit, which will be evaluated in accordance with standard procedures while an escalated high-priority complaint undergoes executive risk management review. The specialist reassured the member that they will be notified directly via email as soon as a final decision is reached.
