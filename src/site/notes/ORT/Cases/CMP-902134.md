## Complaint
- The member called to express frustration after their new application was declined due to "too many credit inquiries in the last 60 days." The member explained that they had accidentally selected the co-borrower option on their original application (which had shown a pre-approval) and could not undo it due to a site error, forcing them to withdraw and reapply. They disputed the denial reason, stating they had only one prior credit inquiry. The agent explained that the system cannot revert an application once the co-borrower option is selected, clarified that soft checks are processed through Experian, and advised that the member can dispute the inquiries with Experian or reapply anytime with an eligible co-borrower.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the customer via email to address their grievance regarding the decline of their loan application resulting from excessive credit inquiries following a forced application withdrawal. An internal review confirmed that system limitations prevent an application from removing a co-borrower once selected, necessitating a complete reapplication that triggered additional soft credit checks through Experian. To resolve the complaint, the specialist clarified how soft credit inquiries are evaluated, provided instructions on how to dispute inaccurate inquiry counts directly with Experian, and suggested submitting a new application alongside an eligible co-borrower to improve approval prospects. The complaint is resolved, with the application status under active operational review for any further updates.
