## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration because loan funds were disbursed directly to their checking account after they were previously informed that funding would be held until they requested it.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist reached out to the customer via email to address their grievance regarding the premature disbursement of loan funds into their checking account after being previously informed that funding would remain on hold. Operational review confirmed that the loan proceeds were automatically disbursed to checking upon final approval processing, and system constraints prevent the disbursement from being reversed or adjusted retroactively. To resolve the complaint, the specialist logged formal agent feedback regarding the miscommunication on funding holds to prevent future occurrences and provided clarity on managing the disbursed funds. The complaint is resolved, with the loan active and the disbursed funds remaining in the customer's checking account.
