## Complaint
- Member contacted expressing frustration regarding conflicting information about a provisional credit. The member was previously advised by a representative that sending an email would expedite a provisional credit for their ongoing dispute. After following these instructions and not receiving the expected funds, the member was informed by the current agent that their claim does not qualify for provisional credit, causing significant dissatisfaction.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding conflicting information received about a provisional credit for an ongoing dispute. The specialist acknowledged the member's frustration after previously being advised that sending an email would expedite the provisional credit, only to be informed later that their claim was ineligible. Delivering a detailed regulatory explanation, the specialist apologized for the miscommunication and clarified that provisional credit eligibility is governed by federal Regulation E guidelines—which apply strictly to specific electronic transaction errors such as unauthorized debits, duplicate charges, or missing ATM funds. The specialist confirmed that because the member's specific claim does not meet these federal criteria, provisional credit cannot be issued while the investigation remains underway.

- Dear Adam,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. Thank you for reaching out regarding the conflicting information provided about your provisional credit. We acknowledge your dissatisfaction regarding the previous advice that an email would expedite the credit, and your frustration upon learning that your claim does not qualify.

A review of your dispute and the previous communication was conducted. We apologize for the miscommunication regarding the provisional credit process. To qualify for a provisional credit, a dispute must involve a Regulation E (Reg E) transaction. A Reg E dispute applies to issues with electronic transactions, such as an unauthorized debit, a duplicate charge, an incorrect amount, missing ATM funds, or a missing expected credit. 

Claims are reviewed under federal Regulation E guidelines. Provisional credit may be provided while an investigation is in progress, depending entirely on the specific dispute type and its current status. Based on these regulatory guidelines, your specific claim does not meet the criteria for a provisional credit.

To recap, provisional credit eligibility is determined by federal Regulation E guidelines, and your current dispute does not qualify for this credit. Please let us know if you have any further questions.
