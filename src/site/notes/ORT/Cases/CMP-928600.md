## Complaint
- The member, Samantha Gurcsik, called on behalf of account holder Matthew, who was present, to appeal a denied dispute for a $12,000.00 transaction to Lynisha McGee on 10/04/2026. She expressed dissatisfaction that the dispute was denied before all evidence was submitted, noting that a police report was filed and an initial receipt provided. She stated that fraud specialist Jose had advised that morning that the investigation would remain open pending the full police report. Additionally, she voiced concern that the account is unrestricted, preventing Matthew from verifying his identity and leaving their funds at risk, and requested that the account be restricted. She was informed that only the fraud department could restrict the account and that she must contact Jose directly, as the call could not be transferred. An appeal was submitted, and the complaint remains unresolved.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the initial denial of a disputed $12,000.00 transaction to Lynisha McGee and their concern that the account remained unrestricted. Operational review confirmed that a formal dispute appeal was submitted on the customer's behalf, and that account security restrictions fall strictly under the authority of the specialized fraud department rather than the resolution team. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the claim appeal is actively under review pending the submission of the full police report, and provided instructions for contacting the fraud department directly to apply security restrictions to the account. The complaint is resolved, with the $12,000.00 dispute appeal actively pending documentation review and clear contact pathways established for fraud prevention actions.
