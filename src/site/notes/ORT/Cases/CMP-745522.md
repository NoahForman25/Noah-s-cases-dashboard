## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration after being informed they are ineligible for Zelle services despite maintaining a direct deposit, which they noted was their primary reason for opening the account. Due to this eligibility restriction, the member indicated they are now considering closing their account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist reached out to the member  via email to provide clarity regarding Zelle feature eligibility and direct deposit requirements. This outreach followed an initial phone contact where the member expressed concern about providing personal identification over the phone, prompting the agent to send a detailed email instead. The specialist clarified that the system is actively monitoring the account's transactional history and typically updates Zelle eligibility upon receiving the next one to two qualifying direct deposits. The member was informed that the current inquiry is being closed while awaiting these upcoming deposits to process and update the account status, with an invitation to call back for further assistance if needed.
