## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding check security, stating that his wife was able to draw a line under the signature area and successfully cash a check. The member voiced concern that SoFi's check verification procedures feel insecure and demanded an upgrade to automated signature-recognition systems to ensure checks are verified against actual signature records before processing. Support explained standard check processing and image verification protocols, documented the member's security concerns, and submitted a formal product feedback ticket regarding check signature validation procedures for Risk and Operations review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on their concerns regarding security protocols for mobile check deposits. After authenticating the member using their full name and date of birth, the specialist addressed the member's frustration regarding check verification procedures, which arose after his wife was able to draw a line under the signature line and successfully deposit a check.The member had expressed concern that SoFi's verification controls feel insecure and demanded the implementation of automated signature-recognition systems to validate checks against official signature records prior to processing. The specialist explained standard check processing and image verification protocols, confirming that the member's security concerns and product feedback were formally documented and submitted into the system for Risk and Operations review. During the call, the member also briefly mentioned needing $50,000 returned to EverBank, but stated they did not have time to discuss the funds transfer further during this interaction.
