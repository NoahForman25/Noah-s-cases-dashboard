## Complaint
- The member expressed severe frustration and accused the platform of lying regarding the security of his profile, citing ongoing access restrictions, verification blocks, and a lack of transparency as reasons for his distrust while attempting to resolve his account issues.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their severe frustration, ongoing account access restrictions, and accusations that the platform was providing false information regarding profile security. The specialist acknowledged the member's deep distrust stemming from repeated verification blocks and a perceived lack of transparency while trying to resolve their account issues. Reassuring the member regarding safety protocols, the specialist clarified that current restrictions represent standard security measures rather than misleading practices, and confirmed that the technical support ticket for the ID verification failure has been escalated to the engineering team for further investigation and resolution.
