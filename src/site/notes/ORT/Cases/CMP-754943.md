## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding conflicting information provided by multiple representatives about the timeline for resolving a Zelle transaction error. The member objected to the shifting turnaround times, noting they were sequentially advised the correction would take 24 hours, then 3 days, and now 5 days.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed the member to address their frustration regarding conflicting information provided by multiple representatives about the resolution timeline for a Zelle transaction error. The specialist acknowledged the member's objection to the shifting turnaround times—having been sequentially advised the correction would take 24 hours, then 3 days, and finally 5 days—and explained that while standard holds typically take 1 to 3 business days, certain cases require an extended review period. A positive resolution was provided, confirming that the Zelle transaction in question has been successfully canceled and that the funds in the account now accurately reflect the correct balance.
