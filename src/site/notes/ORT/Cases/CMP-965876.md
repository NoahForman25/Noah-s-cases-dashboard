## Complaint
- The member called SoFi to follow up on a missed call from the member security team regarding a $5,000 wire transfer, confirming it was authorized for bills and urgent medical issues. The member expressed frustration that the wire remained pending five days after being assured it would go through by Friday afternoon, and was dissatisfied with receiving no answers when calling twice on Friday other than being told everyone had gone home. The agent asked standard security verification questions regarding potential scams, which the member answered in the negative. The agent approved the wire transfer, explained the delay was due to necessary fraud verification, and apologized for the communication lapse and delay on Friday. The member accepted the explanation and concluded the call.
## Resolution
- An internal review was conducted on members' profile and found mule fraud with 3rd party fraud confirmed as well.
