## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding a lack of communication, stating they had called multiple times about their personal loan application without any representative notifying them that the application had been denied. The member voiced concern and discomfort with the inconsistent information provided during previous interactions, noting that no agent had proactively clarified their application status. Support acknowledged the member's frustration, verified the final application outcome, and explained the adverse action review process. Support logged a formal service complaint regarding the previous miscommunications and submitted a request to Lending Operations to re-send the formal written decision notice containing the specific denial reasons.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her strong dissatisfaction regarding a lack of proactive communication and inconsistent updates concerning her personal loan application. The member had previously expressed significant frustration after calling multiple times without receiving clarity or being informed that her application had actually been denied. The specialist apologized for the communication failures and confirmed that the personal loan application had been denied. The specialist verified that a formal service complaint regarding the miscommunications was logged for internal review, and confirmed that a request was submitted to Lending Operations to re-send the official written decision notice detailing the specific denial reasons and adverse action disclosures.
