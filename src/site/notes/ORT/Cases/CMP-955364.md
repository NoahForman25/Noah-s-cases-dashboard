## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration stating her account was breached after receiving a suspicious call from someone impersonating Apple support. She reported the incident to SoFi and was instructed to change her password, but was not informed of an account restriction. The member was expecting a deposit that was not received due to the restriction, causing her to use profanity and express disappointment and frustration over not being informed earlier. An apology was offered to the member.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding an unnotified account restriction and a blocked deposit following an account breach involving an Apple support impersonation scam. Operational review confirmed that an account takeover had occurred, requiring the immediate placement of security restrictions that inadvertently prevented the expected incoming deposit from posting before notification was delivered. To resolve the complaint, the specialist formally acknowledged the lack of timely communication, provided transparency regarding the necessary fraud mitigation protocols, and issued an apology for the disruption caused to her funds. The complaint is resolved, with the account secured under protective review while the customer receives ongoing profile status updates.
