## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding an unresolved two-factor authentication issue. The member stated that the experience felt discriminatory, remarking that "Sofi is being inconsiderate to the member for being gay by not resolving their 2 factors authentication problem."
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone but reached there voicemail. Then emailed the member to address their dissatisfaction and concerns of discrimination regarding an unresolved two-factor authentication (2FA) issue. The specialist reassured the member of the institution's commitment to equal and respectful service and offered two actionable pathways to resolve the login trouble. The member was advised that they can either call in directly to have an agent disable and re-enable the feature over the phone, or request a new technical support ticket to replace a previous ticket that had expired while awaiting requested troubleshooting screenshots.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
-  The resolution specialist attempted to contact the member regarding their complaint. The specialist received the member's voicemail and left a message. The specialist will send a detailed email.

- Hi Abram,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. Thank you for reaching out and sharing your experience regarding your two-factor authentication (2FA) issue. To get your 2FA working properly, we can try disabling and re-enabling the feature on your account. For immediate assistance with this step, please give us a call so our agents can process this update for you right over the phone. Alternatively, we can submit a new technical support ticket, as the previous ticket expired while waiting for the requested screenshots from you.

To recap, we can resolve the 2FA issue by either calling in to reset the feature or by submitting a new technical support ticket and follow up with screenshots to better assist them. Please let us know how you would like to proceed or if you have any further questions at all—we are here to help.
