## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction and frustration regarding the complexity of the card activation and PIN setup process, specifically citing difficulties with logging in and password management. They characterized these operational security requirements as fraudulent practices by the institution.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed the mmeber to address their frustration regarding the complexity of the card activation, PIN setup, and password management processes, which the member had characterized as fraudulent. The specialist acknowledged the difficulties encountered but shared a positive update, noting that the debit card is now successfully active. Additionally, the specialist clarified that these complex operational requirements are standard security measures designed strictly to protect the account, and suggested writing down new passwords in the future to make the login process easier.
