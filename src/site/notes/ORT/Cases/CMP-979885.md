## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction after discovering unauthorized activity involving an inactive SoFi account, including two unauthorized transfers from Wells Fargo totaling $2,400 and $1,540, and an initiated $3,000 payment to Credit One Bank that the member canceled. Wells Fargo opened a claim with an estimated 10-business-day timeframe, leaving the member's account negative by several hundred dollars and unable to cover business expenses. The member reported their computer was compromised after opening a suspicious DocuSign email that downloaded screen-mirroring software, noted unauthorized login activity from Boca Raton, and observed their mouse moving independently during the call. The member voiced frustration over having spent 45 minutes on the phone prior to being transferred and felt misled and trapped after initially being told funds could be reversed to Wells Fargo following identity verification, only to be informed that an account restriction blocked all ACH activity until a new account could be established one business day after November 9. Emphasizing that they did not cause the restriction and were left without funds at either bank, the member requested leniency, used profanity, and asked to speak with a supervisor. A supervisor callback was scheduled.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist engaged with the customer via a phone call to address their complaint regarding an account takeover stemming from a device compromise, resulting in unauthorized inbound transfers and restrictive security holds. Operational review confirmed that automated protective restrictions had frozen account activity following suspicious external logins, requiring the creation of a secure new checking and savings profile before funds could be safely accessed or transferred. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the replacement account had been established, confirmed the remaining balance of $50.79, and initiated an outbound transfer of the residual funds to the customer's external Wells Fargo account per their request. The compromised profiles were formally closed, and the customer's funds were routed back to their external institution to finalize the security remediation.
