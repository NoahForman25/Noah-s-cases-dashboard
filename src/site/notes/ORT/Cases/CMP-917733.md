## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction because they were not informed that net income would be used for evaluation and felt that SoFi should have confirmed more details. The member requested to have their application reopened.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding loan evaluation criteria and the rejection of their request to reopen their application. Operational review confirmed that income verification documents established a verified annual income of $25,373.50, which failed to match the higher figure reported on the initial application. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding the underwriting income discrepancies and explained that the Complex Income Review could not be approved based on the submitted documentation. The complaint is resolved, with the loan application remaining formally declined and closed.
