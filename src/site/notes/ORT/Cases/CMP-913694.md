## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration because an $80 ACH transfer initiated yesterday to their Chime account has not gone through. They noted that this issue occurs consistently whenever they attempt to transfer funds to another account and stated they are considering closing their account as a result.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a missing $80 ACH transfer to their Chime account and recurring outbound transfer failures. Operational review confirmed that no $80 transfer request to Chime exists in the system records, though an outbound transaction to The Bancorp Bank was identified instead. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding the absence of the Chime transaction record, clarified the verified destination of the recorded transfer, and invited the customer to contact support for live assistance if future transfer issues occur. The complaint is resolved, with the transaction history clarified and support channels available to address any ongoing transfer difficulties.
