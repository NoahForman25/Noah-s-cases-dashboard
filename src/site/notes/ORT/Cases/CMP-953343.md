## Complaint
- The member called to dispute two unauthorized Lyft transactions: a $17.95 temporary authorization hold and a $56.38 charge, stating she did not authorize them and wants her money back as soon as possible. The agent explained that their department creates disputes and routes them to the back office, who will contact her if further information is needed via email reply. The agent confirmed they would submit the dispute and place a stop payment for the merchant.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding two unauthorized Lyft transactions totaling $74.33 and dissatisfaction with the dispute handling process. Operational review confirmed that claims require formal submission to the back-office dispute team for investigation, and that authorization holds and posted charges cannot be resolved with guaranteed outcomes or fixed completion timelines prior to full review. To resolve the complaint, the specialist formally submitted an appeal on the customer's behalf for both transactions, placed a merchant stop payment on the account to prevent future charges, and clarified that the back-office team will request additional documentation via email if needed. The complaint is resolved, with the merchant successfully blocked and the disputed charges actively pending back-office investigation.
