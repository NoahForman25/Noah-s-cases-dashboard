## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction regarding an internal processing error where SoFi incorrectly disputed and canceled an Apex Trader Funding transaction from June 12, 2026 (-$80.00), instead of the requested Anthropic* Claude subscription from June 14, 2026 (-$200.00, ID 613-27801001). As a direct result of this error, the merchant restricted the member's account, causing the member to incur $75.00 in total collateral fees ($45.00 merchant reinstatement fee and $30.00 SoFi wire transfer fee) to rectify the issue. The member stated verbatim:

"I only attempted to dispute 1 transaction with ID 613-27801001 , SoFi cancelled a wrong charge, from Apex, now I have to fix the issue with Apex - they charged me $45 fee for reinstating the charge and restoring my account, and they requested a wire transfer which now SoFi charges $30 for |How in the world could a mistake like this happen - I requested to cancel a transaction by its ID, yet somehow completely different transaction got cancelled. | what do I do with the damage $75 in fees that it's costing me now to fix with Apex ?"
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding an internal processing error where the institution incorrectly disputed and canceled an Apex Trader Funding transaction instead of the requested Anthropic* Claude subscription, resulting in $75.00 in collateral fees. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's severe dissatisfaction with the error and the associated merchant restrictions. To resolve the issue, the agent issued an apology for the confusion and applied a credit of 10,000 rewards points (valued at $100.00) to the member's profile as compensation for the inconvenience. Additionally, the agent confirmed that the active dispute regarding the Claude charge is still being reviewed and that updates will be provided directly via email.
