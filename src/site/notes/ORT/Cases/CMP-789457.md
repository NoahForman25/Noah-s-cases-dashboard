## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction and frustration regarding a $100.00 IRA close-out fee that was assessed against their account following a full balance withdrawal. The member noted that after the fee was applied, they only received a remaining balance of $0.33. The member explicitly stated that they would not have proceeded with the full withdrawal of their funds had they known or clearly understood that a close-out fee would be charged.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction and frustration regarding a $100.00 IRA close-out fee that was assessed against their account following a full balance withdrawal, which left them with a remaining balance of only $0.33. The specialist addressed the member's statement that they would not have proceeded with the withdrawal had the fee been clearly understood beforehand. Delivering a clear resolution, the specialist apologized for the experience and confirmed that a full reimbursement of $100.00 has been processed and credited directly to the member's Invest account to offset the close-out fee.
