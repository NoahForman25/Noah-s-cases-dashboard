## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after trying to update their information multiple times throughout the day with various agents. The member is attempting to update their country of birth from Israel to Palestine and completed the persona verification with a work permit; however, Palestine is not included in the system's list of countries.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the inability to update their country of birth from Israel to Palestine across multiple contacts. Operational review confirmed that the customer successfully completed identity verification, but Palestine is currently unavailable as a selectable option within the profile intake system. To resolve the complaint, the specialist logged formal system feedback to escalate the missing country option for future system updates, advised the customer to maintain the default selection in the interim, and issued 1,500 reward points ($15 value) to compensate for the frustration and repeated contacts. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's profile verification fully satisfied and the system enhancement request submitted for internal review.
