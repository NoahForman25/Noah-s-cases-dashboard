## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a crypto withdrawal of XRP submitted on October 4 that has not fully processed. The member stated that the delay in processing the withdrawal resulted in a loss of $4,000.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a delay in processing an XRP cryptocurrency withdrawal submitted on October 4, 2026, which the customer stated caused a $4,000 loss. Operational review confirmed that the digital asset transfer encountered a delay during network processing before the order could be fully executed. To resolve the complaint, the specialist oversaw the manual push of the transaction to ensure the trade was fulfilled and verified that the XRP funds were successfully transferred. The complaint is resolved, with the cryptocurrency withdrawal completed and the asset transfer fully finalized on the account.
