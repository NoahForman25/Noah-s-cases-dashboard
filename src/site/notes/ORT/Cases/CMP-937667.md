## Complaint
- The member submitted a formal complaint expressing extreme distress and frustration after their bank accounts were restricted twice in a single week due to routine purchases. The member emphasized that these security holds occurred during an active cross-country relocation following a twelve-hour workday, causing significant anxiety and nearly leaving them stranded without access to personal funds. The member strongly criticized repetitive system glitches, requested compensation for the disruptions, and expressed serious doubts about maintaining a banking relationship with the institution.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding repeated account restrictions placed following routine purchases during a cross-country relocation. Operational review confirmed that an Account Takeover review was initiated and completed, determining that no unauthorized activity occurred and allowing the temporary security flags to be cleared. To resolve the complaint, the specialist apologized for the disruption caused during the customer's move, provided transparency regarding the security review outcome, and verified that all restrictions were officially removed on October 16, 2026. The complaint is resolved, with security flags removed and full, unrestricted access to the customer's accounts and funds completely restored.
