## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding an inability to access or utilize the funds within their account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding an inability to access or utilize the funds within their account due to ongoing identification requirements. The specialist delivered a clear update, explaining that standard security protocols require a physical ID to complete the mandatory verification process and safeguard the profile against unauthorized activity. To fully resolve the restriction, the specialist advised the member to obtain a physical ID and contact support once it is in their possession so that the verification can be completed and account access restored.
