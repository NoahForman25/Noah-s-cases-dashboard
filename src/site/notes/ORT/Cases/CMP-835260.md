## Complaint
- The member requested to expedite the issuance of provisional credit on their account. Support advised the member that there is no option or manual override available to expedite the provisional credit processing timeline. Unsatisfied with this response, the member requested to speak with a supervisor to escalate the matter.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding a request to expedite provisional credit on their account and a subsequent request for a supervisor escalation. The specialist explained that in accordance with standard banking policies, there is no manual override available to shorten or bypass the provisional credit processing timeline. The specialist confirmed that the standard review period of up to 10 business days must be adhered to in order to evaluate eligibility, and reiterated that the timeframe cannot be expedited.
