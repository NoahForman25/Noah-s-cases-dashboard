## Complaint
- The member called to express frustration that their student loan refinance interest rate increased (from ~5.8% to ~6.37%) at the time of signing. The member stated that they had already selected and been approved for the lower rate within the promotional window, and disputed the increase since their credit had already been pulled for the original offer. The agent explained that the rate lock expired after passing the 30-day window due to delays in receiving requested documentation, causing the system to automatically update to current rates. The agent submitted a formal complaint on the member's behalf for management review, with expected follow-up via phone or email within 24 to 48 hours.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding an interest rate increase from 5.8% to 6.37% on their student loan refinance application prior to signing. Operational review confirmed that the initial promotional rate lock expired because the application exceeded the standard 30-day window while awaiting required documentation, causing the system to update automatically to current prevailing rates. To resolve the complaint, the specialist reviewed the applied Military Lending Act benefits, provided alternatives to reduce the rate—such as adjusting the loan term or exploring post-payment options—and filed a formal grievance for further management review. The complaint is resolved, with the application remaining active and available for signing under the updated rate terms until October 22nd.
