## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with SoFi holding their funds, stating that the delay is causing them to incur late fees and negative impacts on their credit. The member indicated they will advise others against opening accounts with SoFi and requested a manager callback.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a held cryptocurrency deposit, reported late fees, credit impacts, and a request for a manager callback. Operational review confirmed that the 0.00811 BTC deposit was placed on temporary hold to complete a routine security review required for account integrity. To resolve the complaint, the specialist apologized for the disruption, provided direct transparency regarding security review protocols, and clarified that the held cryptocurrency will be released for withdrawal or use immediately once verification completes. The complaint is resolved, with the deposit actively undergoing security review and clear operational parameters established for fund availability upon completion.
