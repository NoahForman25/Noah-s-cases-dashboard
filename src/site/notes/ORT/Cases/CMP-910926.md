## Complaint
- The member expressed significant frustration and dissatisfaction regarding the loan disbursement process after learning that their funds could not be located, as they were initially unaware that a personal checking or savings account was required instead of a wire routing number. Although the member initially requested to cancel the loan application without internal escalation due to these delays, they ultimately agreed to proceed after the root cause was identified and provided a copy of a voided check to correct the account details for re-processing.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted follow-up phone calls with the customer to address their complaint regarding significant delays in personal loan disbursement caused by misrouted funding details. Operational review confirmed that the initial funding attempt failed because a wire routing number was submitted rather than standard ACH checking or savings details, which required a member technical support ticket to update the account information. To resolve the complaint, the specialist assisted the customer in processing a voided check to correct the disbursement details, escalated the technical ticket to re-route the funds, and confirmed that the underlying delivery issue was resolved. The complaint is resolved, with the technical issue successfully corrected and the loan proceeds actively in transit to the customer's account.
