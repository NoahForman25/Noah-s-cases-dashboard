## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a pending $214.57 Amazon transaction on their SoFi Smart Card account. The member stated that Amazon had released the charge, provided an authorization number, and argued that the pre-authorization hold should not remain pending, requesting that SoFi release the funds. The agent explained that SoFi cannot manually override the pre-authorization hold, which can last up to 8 business days. The member requested escalation to an Account Manager.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist engaged with the customer via a phone call to address their complaint regarding a pending $214.57 pre-authorization hold from Amazon remaining on their Smart Card account despite a merchant release. Operational review confirmed that while Amazon processed the refund and released the charge, pre-authorization holds are system-managed across standard network timeframes lasting up to eight business days and cannot be manually overridden or dropped prematurely. To address the member's concerns, the specialist verified that the merchant refund was properly processed, issued an apology for the negative experience, and escalated the case to an Account Manager per the customer's request. The customer's account remains active, open, and in good standing, pending standard expiration of the pre-authorization hold or further management review.
