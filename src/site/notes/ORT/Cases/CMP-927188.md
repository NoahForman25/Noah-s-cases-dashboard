## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding the closure of their account due to a violation of the member agreement. The member requested the specific reason for the account closure and requested to speak with a supervisor to obtain this information.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding the decision to close their account and the lack of specific details provided for the action. Operational review confirmed that account agreements and internal risk policies restrict customer service representatives from disclosing specific operational criteria or backend security indicators behind involuntary account closures. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, acknowledged their frustration, and clearly communicated company disclosure policies regarding account termination decisions. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining permanently closed in accordance with compliance guidelines and bank agreement terms.
