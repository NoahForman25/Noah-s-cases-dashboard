## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration that SoFi is holding his funds and preventing him from using them, even after he completed all requested actions. Additionally, the member stated he was told SoFi would contact him with updates regarding his account, but no one reached out.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's complaint regarding account restrictions, an active hold on a recent deposit, and a lack of follow-up communication from the fraud team. Operational review confirmed that the customer's accounts are pending closure following an internal investigation, which placed an administrative hold on a $5,000 deposit scheduled to expire on the twenty-eighth. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, apologized for the communication delays, and provided transparent details regarding the closure status and funds disbursement timeline. The complaint is resolved, with the accounts set to finalize closure and the $5,000 deposit scheduled to automatically transfer to the customer's verified external bank account once the hold clears on the twenty-eighth.
