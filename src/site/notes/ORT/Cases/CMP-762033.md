## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction and deep disappointment regarding the unauthorized closure of their account and their inability to reach a successful resolution through standard dispute channels. The member emphasized that they have followed all proper procedures but continue to face denials, stating that the level of frustration they are experiencing cannot be understated and that the treatment received falls far short of acceptable service standards. The member demands that a member of management contact them directly at 208-392-5577 to ensure this situation is taken seriously and resolved promptly.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their severe dissatisfaction and deep disappointment regarding the closure of their account, their inability to reach a successful resolution through standard channels, and their demand for direct management contact. The specialist conducted a comprehensive review of the account and the dispute decisions, providing a final resolution that confirms the account was permanently closed due to a violation of the Member Services Agreement. Furthermore, the member was informed that the investigations into the denied disputes have been completed and remain final, serving as the official, permanent escalated review from the resolution team.
