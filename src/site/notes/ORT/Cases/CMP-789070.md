## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding their inability to withdraw a remaining cash balance of $100.97 from their Roth IRA without closing the account. The member expressed strong dissatisfaction with automated system constraints that enforce a $100 minimum threshold and block partial transfers below that amount, which effectively prevented them from completing a partial withdrawal of their funds while keeping the account active.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding system constraints that prevented them from making a partial withdrawal of a remaining $100.97 cash balance from their Roth IRA without closing the account. The specialist delivered a clear update on the available paths forward, confirming that the mandatory $100.00 withdrawal fee is a standard, non-waivable institutional assessment. The specialist reiterated that the member must either leave the funds in the active account or proceed with the withdrawal and accept the fee. However, to help mitigate the cost and resolve the complaint, the specialist confirmed that 2,000 rewards points (equivalent to $20.00) have been credited directly to the member's profile.
