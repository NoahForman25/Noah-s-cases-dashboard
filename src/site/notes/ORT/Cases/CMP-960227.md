## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration and threatened to contact the Better Business Bureau (BBB) if her account is not unrestricted today and subsequently closed as requested.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding delays in processing her account closure and her intent to report the issue to the Better Business Bureau. Operational review determined that standard back-office processing workflows prevented the closure from being executed on the exact date requested, resulting in the prolonged closure timeline. To resolve the complaint, the specialist formally acknowledged the processing delay, issued an apology for the service experience, and confirmed in writing that the account was officially terminated on October 30. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's account remaining permanently closed.
