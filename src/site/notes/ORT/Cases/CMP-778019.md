## Complaint
- Member contacted to express significant frustration and dissatisfaction after falling victim to a fraudulent merchant on July 7, 2026, which resulted in three unauthorized PayPal transactions totaling $1,814.97 ($1,800.00 principal plus three separate $4.99 fees). Highlighting their seven-year tenure as a loyal SoFi customer, the member felt entirely let down upon being informed that their dispute was denied and classified as a civil matter, leaving them with no internal recourse. Consequently, the member threatened to close all of their SoFi banking, investing, and loan accounts if a satisfactory resolution is not provided, and explicitly requested an immediate escalation to the Customer Retention Team.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address their significant frustration and dissatisfaction after falling victim to a fraudulent merchant on July 7, 2026, which resulted in three unauthorized PayPal transactions totaling $1,814.97 ($1,800.00 principal plus three separate $4.99 fees). During the call, the specialist delivered the final determination that the platform is unable to provide internal recourse as the issue is classified as a civil matter, advising the member that they may pursue resolution through legal channels. The member acknowledged the explanation, and the specialist awarded them 3,000 courtesy rewards points due to the institution's inability to assist further before concluding the interaction.
