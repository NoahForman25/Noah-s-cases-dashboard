## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding their inability to log into their account, as well as frustration over an email address that was not updated properly. Support acknowledged the login issue and the email record error, verified the member's correct contact information, and escalated the technical login blocker and profile update failure to Account Operations for immediate review and resolution.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on a reported technical login blocker caused by an incorrect email address on their profile. After authenticating the member, the agent addressed their strong dissatisfaction regarding their inability to log into their account and the prior failure to update their contact details properly. The member confirmed that a previous agent had successfully corrected the email record, resolving the login issue, though they noted that their loan application remains under active review. The specialist apologized for the inconvenience caused by the profile error and added $15.00 worth of reward points (1,500 points) to the member's profile as a gesture of goodwill, confirming that all contact details are now properly aligned.
