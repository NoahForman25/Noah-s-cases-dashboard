## Complaint
- Member contacted via chat to express intense dissatisfaction after being informed that the institution is unable to manually adjust or increase their Mobile Check Deposit (MCD) limits. The member became highly upset regarding the lack of limit flexibility and explicitly stated their intention to close their account and switch their business to Fidelity Bank to take advantage of a competing offer. A formal complaint has been filed on behalf of the member to document their dissatisfaction with the firm's strict MCD policies and the resulting attrition risk.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a formal complaint (CMP-784819) regarding their dissatisfaction with the mobile check deposit (MCD) limits. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's ongoing frustration surrounding the restriction. Delivering a clear update on institutional policy, the agent re-advised the member of their current deposit limits and explained that while these caps cannot be manually adjusted by an agent, the limits are subject to automatic system evaluations and may increase or decrease in the future based on account activity. The member expressed understanding of the criteria, and the call was successfully concluded.
