## Complaint
- The member expressed confusion and frustration after being informed that their account was identified for closure following an internal review, stating they had barely used the account and had recently deposited a check from their mother. The agent apologized, explained that the account closure decision was final following a comprehensive review, stated the account would remain restricted until closure, and advised that any eligible remaining funds would be mailed to the address on file. The agent placed the member on hold to check for an available supervisor for escalation support, but the member became disconnected while waiting.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding an unexpected account closure following an internal review and a dropped call during an attempted supervisor escalation. Operational review confirmed that the account closure was officially finalized on October 29 pursuant to standard review protocols, meaning the decision cannot be overturned. To address the customer's concerns, the specialist confirmed the closure status in writing and clarified the fund disbursement process, explaining that any remaining eligible balance will be issued via check to the address on file. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining permanently closed pending the mailing of any eligible residual funds.
