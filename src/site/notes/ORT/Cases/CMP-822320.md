## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction, stating that an unacknowledged fraud alert led to the disconnection of their internet service, preventing them from working. Support advised the member that the service disruption occurred because the fraud alert went unacknowledged, triggering security protocols on the card used for the billing transaction.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding a blocked transaction and the resulting disconnection of their internet service, which impacted their ability to work. The specialist apologized for the disruption and explained that the payment was blocked because the automated fraud alert went unacknowledged at the time of processing. Confirming that rewards points had already been credited to the account to help resolve the issue, the specialist advised the member to respond promptly to all future security notifications to prevent similar service interruptions.
