## Complaint
- The member contacted support to express significant dissatisfaction and frustration regarding an ongoing restriction on their checking account. The member emphasized that they never authorized or requested a fraud investigation and voiced intense frustration over the prolonged resolution timeline. They explained that the lack of access to their funds is creating a severe financial hardship, leaving them unable to pay pending bills. Additionally, the member noted that they have spent hours on the phone without receiving clear answers or a resolution, and expressed heightened concern after being informed by a previous representative that they would be unable to receive their upcoming direct deposits due to the restriction.
## Resolution
- The representative contacted the member to address their ongoing frustration regarding fraudulent activity and the resulting checking account restriction, following up on their severe dissatisfaction over losing access to their funds to pay bills and concerns regarding upcoming direct deposits. During the interaction, the representative checked to see if the member had been able to connect with the Member Security Team (MST) to address the unauthorized fraud investigation and account block. The member informed the representative that they had already spoken with an MST agent, confirming that everything was fully taken care of.
