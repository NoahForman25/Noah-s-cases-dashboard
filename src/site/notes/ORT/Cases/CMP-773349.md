## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding ongoing transaction declines, noting that the issues persist regardless of whether they attempt to use the account directly or their associated card. The member emphasized the urgency of the situation, stating that if the current charge fails to go through, they will incur an additional $25 fee for late payment.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding ongoing transaction declines affecting both their account and associated card, which threatened an additional $25 late payment fee. The specialist delivered a clear resolution, confirming that the member was ultimately able to successfully complete the urgent payment via Zelle. Additionally, the specialist addressed the member's frustration regarding the notification process, explaining that the security declines were triggered by automated standard protocols designed to protect their funds, and informed the member that the resolution team stands ready to investigate further if any alternative transaction issues arise in the future.
