## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding the process for provisional credit, stating that they want to receive the credit right away.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding the provisional credit process and their demand for immediate credit for unrecognized activity. Operational review confirmed that two disputed transactions totaling $51.25 remained in a pending status without posting to the ledger, making them ineligible for provisional credit issuance, while a $142.55 transaction was verified as authorized by the customer. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, validated the legitimized transaction, and clarified that pending authorization holds expire automatically without deducting funds from the balance. The complaint is resolved, with the pending holds confirmed to drop off without financial impact and the account status verified as accurate.
