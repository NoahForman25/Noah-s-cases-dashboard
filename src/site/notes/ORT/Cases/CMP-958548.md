## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding the closure of his checking account ending in 8065 and what he described as misleading communication from SoFi. Although an email sent that morning stated the account was temporarily restricted due to unusual activity and offered a 14-day window to respond, an agent informed him on the call that the account was already permanently closed following a comprehensive review. The member disputed the legitimacy of the review, arguing that his negative balance was only $31 and that an upcoming recurring Amazon direct deposit would have brought the account positive. Furthermore, he alleged that SoFi blatantly lied in its email by characterizing an already finalized closure as a temporary restriction with a 14-day response period. The member's concerns regarding communication discrepancies, lack of transparency, and closing the account over a small overdraft remained unresolved at the end of the call.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding the closure of his checking account ending in 8065 and conflicting communications concerning a temporary restriction versus finalized closure. Operational review confirmed that the account had remained in a continuous negative balance since August 28, and an investigation into outbound records found no record of the correspondence offering a 14-day response period. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency explaining that the account was definitively closed pursuant to internal review procedures prompted by the extended negative balance. The complaint is resolved, with the checking account remaining permanently closed.
