## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding the standard 10-business-day timeline required to issue a provisional credit and requested that the funds be released to their account sooner.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding the standard 10-business-day timeline required to issue a provisional credit and their request to have the funds released sooner. The specialist validated the importance of quick access to the funds but clarified that the standard processing timeframe cannot be expedited to ensure a thorough and accurate investigation of the dispute. The member was reassured that the dispute team is actively working on the claim and that the provisional credit will be applied to the account by the tenth business day if the review is still ongoing at that time.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
- Hi Stephen, This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. Thank you for reaching out regarding the timeline for the provisional credit on the recent dispute. We completely understand how important it is to have access to these funds quickly, and we are here to provide clarity on this process today. To ensure a thorough and accurate review, the standard time frame for issuing a provisional credit is up to 10 business days. While we are unable to expedite this specific timeline, please know that our dispute team is actively working on the claim. The provisional credit will be applied to the account by the 10th business day, provided the dispute has not been fully resolved before then. To recap, we are actively monitoring the dispute, and the provisional credit will be issued by the 10th business day if the review is still ongoing. Please let us know if there are any further questions at all—we are here to help.
