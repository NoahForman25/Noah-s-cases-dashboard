## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an inability to register their SoFi profile and complete required loan document signatures. The issue stemmed from technical barriers during their work shift: the member's personal phone was blocking access to the registration portal on the corporate network, while their work computer automatically defaulted to an unassociated professional email address rather than their registered SoFi email. When support advised the member to attempt the process from home on a personal device and network, the member voiced dissatisfaction with their restrictive work schedule and the technical friction preventing profile registration. Support acknowledged the member's frustration regarding the login blocker and time constraints, provided guidance on adjusting browser autofill settings to override the default email on their desktop, and submitted an internal support ticket to Technical Operations and Lending Operations to monitor the application status and offer alternative verification options if needed.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on prior technical issues regarding one-time passcodes, profile registration barriers, and loan document signature delays. The member had previously expressed frustration over login blockers occurring during their work shift, caused by their personal phone blocking portal access on a corporate network and their work computer defaulting to an unassociated professional email address. The specialist authenticated the member, who confirmed that the technical issue with receiving one-time passcodes was successfully rectified and resolved. The specialist expressed satisfaction that the access barriers were resolved, verified that the member was able to proceed with their profile and loan documentation, and offered further assistance as needed.
