## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after being unable to complete identity verification via SMS because they could not access or follow the link sent to their device. When support advised that an internal ticket would be submitted for review and that they would need to wait for a follow-up, the member described the process as "ridiculous." Support acknowledged the member's dissatisfaction with the verification friction and handling delays, documented the failed SMS link issue, and submitted an urgent review ticket to Account Operations and Member Security to explore alternative verification methods and expedite the account review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address his frustration regarding identity verification friction and delays caused by inaccessible SMS verification links. The member had previously expressed significant dissatisfaction with the multi-step verification process and the extended wait time required for an internal support review, describing the handling as ridiculous after being unable to follow the device link. The specialist apologized for the technical barrier and delay, confirming that the initial SMS link issue was reviewed by support. The specialist noted that the verification issue was successfully resolved by closing the impacted application and initiating a new application for the member, and confirmed that resolution specialists remain available to provide direct assistance should any further issues arise during the new application process.
