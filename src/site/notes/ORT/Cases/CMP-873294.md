## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding a lack of communication, stating they had called multiple times about their personal loan application without any representative notifying them that the application had been denied. The member voiced concern and discomfort with the inconsistent information provided during previous interactions, noting that no agent had proactively clarified their application status. Support acknowledged the member's frustration, verified the final application outcome, and explained the adverse action review process. Support logged a formal service complaint regarding the previous miscommunications and submitted a request to Lending Operations to re-send the formal written decision notice containing the specific denial reasons.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong dissatisfaction regarding a lack of proactive communication and inconsistent updates concerning their $35,000.00 personal loan application. The member had previously expressed deep frustration after calling multiple times without any representative informing them that their application had actually been denied, raising significant concerns about the transparency and consistency of support interactions. The specialist apologized for the communication lapses and confirmed that the application was denied due to identity verification issues, which require the member to verify their identity directly with the credit bureaus as part of standard adverse action procedures. The specialist confirmed that a formal service complaint regarding the miscommunications was logged for internal review, and a request was submitted to Lending Operations to re-send the official written decision notice detailing the specific denial reasons and instructions for completing credit bureau verification.
