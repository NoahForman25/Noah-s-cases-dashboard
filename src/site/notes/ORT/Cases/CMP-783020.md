## Complaint
- Member contacted to express deep frustration regarding multiple operational and systemic issues, noting that their interactions with the automated AI system failed to provide any meaningful solutions. The member reported a breakdown in the debit card delivery process; they initially received automated notifications to activate a card they had not yet received, but those notifications have since stopped, and the physical card remains undelivered. This delay has caused significant concern, as the member is worried about potential fraud exposure regarding the missing card. Additionally, the member expressed strong dissatisfaction with the lack of integration across company systems, pointing out that marketing campaigns appear completely uncoupled from actual account records. To illustrate this, they noted receiving promotional materials to apply for a loan just one day after they had already submitted a loan application. Finally, the member expressed frustration over the institutional requirement to maintain a debit card for a checking account they have no operational interest in using.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their deep frustration regarding unhelpful automated AI interactions and multiple systemic issues, specifically a breakdown in the debit card delivery process that left a physical card undelivered and caused significant anxiety over potential fraud exposure. Delivering a clear path forward, the specialist advised the member to call customer support to verify their address, order a replacement card, and review their history to file any necessary disputes. The specialist also addressed complaints regarding poorly integrated marketing systems—explaining that promotional campaigns operate on separate distribution schedules—and clarified that while maintaining a debit card is an institutional requirement for all checking accounts, the member can safely freeze and destroy the card if they do not intend to use it.

## Notes

### Debit card not received and fraud concern

- Confirm that the member has not received the physical debit card.
    
- Acknowledge the security concern: “I understand why a missing debit card would raise concerns about possible unauthorized use.”
    
- Reassure the member that you will review the card status, delivery status, and any recent activity on the account.
    
- If appropriate after review, discuss available next steps such as:
    
    - Blocking or closing the missing card.
        
    - Reissuing a replacement card.
        
    - Confirming the correct mailing address.
        
    - Reviewing account activity for any unauthorized transactions.
        
- Avoid guaranteeing fraud did or did not occur until account activity has been reviewed.
    

### Automated notifications and AI experience

- Acknowledge that receiving activation reminders before receiving the card created confusion and concern.
    
- Validate the member’s frustration with automated support: “It sounds like the automated system did not give you a clear path to resolution, and I understand how that made the experience worse.”
    
- Explain that you are now reviewing the issue directly and will focus on actionable resolution instead of repeating automated guidance.
    

### Marketing campaign concern

- Acknowledge the disconnect the member described: “I can understand why it felt frustrating to receive loan marketing shortly after already applying.”
    
- Explain carefully that promotional communications may not always update immediately based on recent application activity.
    
- Avoid overexplaining or defending the system.
    
- Position the feedback as valid: “Your point about marketing communications needing to better reflect actual account activity is understandable, and I’ll make sure this feedback is captured.”
    

### Debit card requirement concern

- Acknowledge that the member does not intend to use the checking account operationally and is frustrated by the debit card requirement.
    
- Explain that debit card issuance may be tied to the structure of the checking account product, but do not frame this as the member’s fault.
    
- Offer to review available account options or preferences where applicable.
    
- Keep the tone empathetic: “I understand that receiving or maintaining a debit card you don’t plan to use can feel unnecessary.”
    

### Closing points

- Summarize the action items:
    
    - Review the missing debit card status.
        
    - Address card security concerns.
        
    - Confirm whether a replacement or card restriction is needed.
        
    - Capture feedback about automated support and marketing/account system alignment.
        
- End with accountability: “I appreciate you bringing all of this forward. My priority is to help resolve the immediate card concern and make sure your feedback about the broader experience is documented.”
