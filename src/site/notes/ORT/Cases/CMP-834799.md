## Complaint
- The member expressed strong frustration regarding the denial of their transaction dispute, stating that they were given no opportunity to submit supporting documentation to substantiate their claim. The member criticized the dispute filing process, attributing the adverse outcome to institutional negligence, and insisted that the matter could have already been successfully resolved had their evidence been properly collected and reviewed. Support documented the member's dissatisfaction regarding the filing process and initiated an appeal intake request to allow submission of the member's supporting documents for secondary dispute review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong frustration regarding the denial of a transaction dispute, which the member attributed to institutional negligence after not being given an opportunity to submit supporting documentation during the initial filing. The member maintained that the dispute could have been successfully resolved had their evidence been properly collected and reviewed at the outset. The specialist confirmed that an appeal intake request was initiated on the member's behalf to allow for a secondary dispute review. To advance the case, the specialist explained that the dispute team sent an email requesting supporting documentation, instructing the member to reply directly to that email with their evidence and any additional information for formal evaluation.
