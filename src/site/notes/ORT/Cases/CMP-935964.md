## Complaint
- The customer reported multiple pending unauthorized charges (primarily from "MCLUCK", "APPLE CASH BALANCE ADD", and "PNM*McLck") appearing on an unactivated debit card. The transactions resulted in a negative balance, causing severe financial distress, an inability to pay an electricity bill due today, and a risk of utility shutoff. The customer expressed frustration that the charges could not be immediately disputed or credited while in a pending status. Noting that this is a recurring issue, the customer stated a loss of trust and an intent to close the account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding multiple unauthorized charges appearing on an unactivated debit card, which resulted in a negative account balance and financial distress. Operational review confirmed that the pending transactions posted to the account, allowing them to be formally submitted for dispute investigation. To resolve the complaint, the specialist confirmed that the dispute claim is actively under back-office review and credited 1,500 reward points to the customer's profile for immediate redemption to help alleviate their financial strain. The complaint is resolved, with the unauthorized charges actively undergoing formal dispute review and the courtesy reward points deposited for customer access.
