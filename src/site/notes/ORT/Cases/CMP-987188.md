## Complaint
- The member called to resolve an issue with their Zelle account being blocked (CON 113), expressing frustration over the lack of resolution after more than an hour on the phone. The member stated that Zelle confirmed there was no block on their end, insisted the issue originated with SoFi, and requested escalation as well as a call transcript. The agent informed the member that Zelle is currently unavailable for their account without a set end date for the restriction. The agent then filed an open complaint, created an escalation ticket to a specialist with a 24-to-48-hour contact window, and included the member's transcript request in the escalation email.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an inability to access Zelle, prolonged phone wait times, and requests for escalation and call transcripts. Operational review confirmed that Zelle access remains restricted on the account under system eligibility parameters, which carry no predetermined expiration date and cannot be manually overridden. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency confirming that the service restriction cannot be lifted at this time, while noting that eligibility is subject to periodic review in the future. The complaint is resolved, with Zelle remaining disabled per institutional policy and the customer's account remaining active and in good standing.
