## Complaint
- The member inquired about opening a new account within 2 business days. Following an internal review escalation, the request was denied because active pending disputes must be finalized first. Upon learning the new account number could not be provided today, the member became extremely aggravated, expressing severe dissatisfaction over ongoing account handling and stating that a supervisor had previously promised the account would open today. The member claimed their funds were being held against their will, announced plans to cancel their direct deposit, transfer their banking to another institution, and threatened legal action against the bank.
