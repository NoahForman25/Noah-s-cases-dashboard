## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding delays in establishing their new account, stating that the situation has left them feeling "in limbo." The member emphasized an urgent need to make payments today and voiced frustration over being forced to wait without immediate access to functional account features. Support explained the current processing timeline for new account setup, documented the member's operational urgency, and escalated the case to Account Operations for priority processing and an expedited callback.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong dissatisfaction regarding delays in establishing their new account, which had left the member feeling "in limbo" and unable to make urgent scheduled payments. The member had previously voiced intense frustration over waiting for functional account features to become accessible without clear visibility into processing timelines. While support had previously documented the operational urgency and escalated the case to Account Operations for priority processing, the specialist confirmed that the review is now complete and the new account profile has been successfully established and fully activated. The specialist apologized for the delay experienced during onboarding, reassured the member that all features are now in working order, and provided direct contact information should any further assistance be required.
