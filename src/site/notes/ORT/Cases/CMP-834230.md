## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding a restriction placed on their account due to an active wire recall, emphasizing that the account hold prevents them from accessing vital funds needed for their basic livelihood. Support explained that wire recalls trigger mandatory security protocols to investigate and protect transaction integrity, and escalated the case to Risk Operations for priority review to resolve the restriction as quickly as possible.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an account restriction stemming from an active wire recall investigation. After authenticating the member, the agent confirmed that while the underlying wire transfer had successfully gone through, standard protocols required a mandatory security review. The member expressed strong dissatisfaction, emphasizing that the resulting account hold and frozen debit card severely impacted their livelihood by blocking access to vital funds. The agent reassured the member that the case was being looked into and confirmed that an escalation was submitted to Risk Operations for priority review to expedite resolving the restriction.
