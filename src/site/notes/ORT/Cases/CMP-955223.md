## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding not receiving their debit card and being ineligible for expedited delivery. Although offered alternatives and instructions on using a digital debit card, the member was dissatisfied with these options, insisted on having the card expedited, repeatedly voiced frustration, and requested to speak with an account manager.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's complaint regarding non-receipt of their debit card and previous issues securing expedited shipping. Operational review confirmed that standard automated card orders follow routine delivery windows, but manual verification of address details allows for prioritized fulfillment. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer using their full name and date of birth, verified the correct mailing address on file, and placed an expedited order for a replacement debit card. The complaint is resolved, with the new debit card successfully ordered and scheduled to arrive within two to three business days.
