## Complaint
- The member expressed intense frustration and anger regarding the handling of their transaction dispute, stating they received no communication or status updates prior to discovering today that the claim had been denied. Highlighting a complete lack of transparency throughout the review process, the member criticized the lack of notification and requested a full explanation for the decision. Support acknowledged the member's dissatisfaction regarding the breakdown in communication, documented the lack of status updates, and escalated the dispute file to Fraud Operations and Dispute Advocacy for a supervisor review and formal case re-evaluation.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her intense frustration and anger regarding the handling and sudden denial of her dispute for a $113.49 transaction with PAYPAL *MAURICEBLAZE63 on August 2, 2026. The member had previously criticized the total breakdown in communication and complete lack of transparency after discovering the denial on her own without receiving prior status updates or an explanation of the decision. The specialist apologized for the communication breakdown, documented her concerns, and confirmed that the dispute file was escalated to Fraud Operations and Dispute Advocacy for a supervisor review and formal case re-evaluation. The specialist explained that while the original denial currently remains in effect, the member may submit new supporting documentation to reopen the appeal, noting that any provisional credit previously provided may be subject to reversal per standard dispute timelines if the denial is upheld upon final review.
