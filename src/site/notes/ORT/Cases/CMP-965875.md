## Complaint
- The member requested a discretionary management review for an exception to manually reconcile, credit, or return 0.98193087 SOL, acknowledging Section 3 regarding incompatible address transfers. The member stated the funds were returned over the Solana network to the exact SoFi-controlled originator address from their documented withdrawal and noted the transactions are traceable on-chain. Additionally, the member requested confirmation of receipt before close of business, a case/escalation number, confirmation no further details are needed, a review timeline, and an estimated date/method for a written decision, stating an intention to file a CFPB complaint if the request is denied.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an uncredited transfer of 0.98193087 SOL returned to an internal originator address rather than his individual crypto wallet, as well as delays in reaching a resolution. Operational review evaluated the on-chain transfer details alongside terms governing incompatible address transactions, confirming that the assets could not be manually recovered or routed into the member's wallet. To resolve the complaint, the specialist issued a formal apology for the inconvenience and multiple contacts, and credited 5,000 reward points directly to the customer's profile as a courtesy accommodation. The complaint is resolved, with the cryptocurrency remaining unrecoverable on-chain and the customer's account active and in good standing with the courtesy points applied.
