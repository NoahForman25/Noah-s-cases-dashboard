## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction and embarrassment after their transactions were declined 10 times in a single day, stating they intended to transfer their funds to another bank and close their account. The agent apologized and explained that the transactions were flagged for security reasons requiring manual recognition, advising the member to reprocess the payment. The member continued to complain about the experience, noting that they are a long-time, loyal SoFi customer and that their physical card had not yet been delivered. The member used profanity, requested to speak with a manager, and disconnected the call while waiting on the line.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding ten declined debit transactions in a single day and an undelivered replacement card. Operational review revealed that the debit card ordered on October 28 was impacted by a fulfillment system failure that disrupted card shipments, while the multiple point-of-sale declines were caused by heightened security authorization protocols that temporarily required increased manual verification. To resolve the complaint, the specialist offered to expedite a new replacement debit card upon direct member contact and provided operational reassurance that transaction authorization parameters would normalize as security processes stabilize. The customer's account remains open, secure, and active in good standing pending inbound contact to confirm details and initiate the expedited card shipment.
