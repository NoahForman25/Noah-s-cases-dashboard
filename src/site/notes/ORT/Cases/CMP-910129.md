## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration that a Citi Bank credit card is displaying incorrect account numbers, though they confirmed the correct last four digits are 8289. The member stated that a payment of $7,158.36 has not been credited to their bank account, while Citi Bank confirmed three separate times that no payment has been received on their end. Additionally, the member voiced strong dissatisfaction with SoFi regarding a lack of communication, stating they have called multiple times and were repeatedly promised follow-up and resolution, yet no one from SoFi has contacted them to address the issue.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding uncredited direct loan payoff funds of $7,158.36 to their external credit card and inconsistent account display numbers. Operational review confirmed that the disbursement was successfully processed to the correct destination account ending in 8289, though processing delays between financial institutions delayed the post date. To resolve the complaint and enable external tracing, the specialist verified the correct account details, issued a transaction trace ID for the customer's bank to locate the funds, and re-sent documentation confirming the transaction breakdown. The complaint is resolved, with the loan funds disbursed and the customer equipped with the trace ID to finalize processing with their external lender.
