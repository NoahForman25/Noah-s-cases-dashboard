## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding ongoing collection calls, stating SoFi would continue to call and harass her despite a full loan payoff check being sent by her financial institution (Megastar Financial) and expected to arrive by Thursday or Friday. The member declined to make a manual $2,200 payment, stated she would pay any remaining interest balance once the check posts, and voiced frustration with repeating her payoff details during the call.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding ongoing collection calls despite an incoming loan payoff check sent by Megastar Financial. Operational review confirmed that while the payoff check had not yet posted to SoFi's processing systems as of October 6, 2026, standard automated collection workflows continued to trigger outreach. To resolve the complaint, the specialist extended an apology for the continued phone contacts, clarified that the loan will be fully satisfied upon check arrival, and confirmed that all collection calls will automatically terminate once the funds are processed. The complaint is resolved, with the account awaiting final posting of the pending payoff check to close the balance and stop further collection activity.
