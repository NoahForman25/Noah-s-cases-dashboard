## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding a fraud restriction that prevented them from moving their money during a prime cryptocurrency period, which they stated resulted in a loss of funds.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an account fraud restriction that delayed a cryptocurrency transfer during a critical market window and resulted in perceived financial loss. Operational review confirmed that the restriction was instituted as a mandatory security measure to protect account funds and verify transactional activity, which postponed an outbound transfer of $23,077 in Bitcoin. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the security review was completed and the full transfer finalized on October 31, while issuing an apology acknowledging the disruption and market timing impact caused by the hold. The complaint is resolved, with the cryptocurrency transaction fully processed and the customer's account active and in good standing.
