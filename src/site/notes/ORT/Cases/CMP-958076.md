## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi to regain online account access after attempting identity verification over several days with multiple representatives, expressing frustration, nervousness, and feeling upset and disgusted with the process. The member encountered numerous technical obstacles, including expiring links, an unfamiliar QR code prompt, ID recognition failures, failing selfie captures, unexpected browser errors across Safari, Edge, Firefox, and Brave, Apple account password and payment method prompts, and outdated phone numbers blocking verification codes. The agent verified the member's identity, acknowledged their frustration, and provided step-by-step guidance by resending verification links via text and email, recommending clearing tabs and changing browsers, advising a device restart if issues persisted, and committing to remain on the line to exhaust all available methods until verification was successfully completed.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's ongoing inability to complete identity verification and restore online access amid identity theft concerns. Operational review established that automated security protocols and repeated mobile browser compatibility errors had repeatedly invalidated verification attempts and hindered access restoration. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer using full name and date of birth, delivered live technical troubleshooting by issuing fresh verification links, and provided formal instructions on appealing disputed charges and filing reports with Verizon and the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3). The complaint is resolved, with the specialist remaining on the line to guide the customer through the technical workflows to achieve identity verification and restore account access.
