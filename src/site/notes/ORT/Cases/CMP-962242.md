## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding the permanent closure of their account without prior notice or explanation, as well as an unfriendly automated system that provided misleading status messages. Specifically, the member stated the AI greeting sounded impersonal when repeatedly requesting the last six digits of their Social Security number, suggesting a friendlier phrasing instead. The agent confirmed the closure decision was final, informed the member that a check for $76,045.77 would be mailed to the address on file within 14 to 30 days, and documented the member's feedback and dissatisfaction for internal review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an unnotified permanent account closure, dissatisfaction with the funds disbursement process, and negative experiences with an impersonal automated phone system. Operational review confirmed that the account closure was upheld as final following internal review protocols, which trigger standard balance liquidation procedures. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that a disbursement check in the amount of $76,045.77 will be mailed to the customer's address on file within 14 to 30 days, issued an apology, and formally submitted the customer's feedback regarding the automated greeting to the internal review team. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining permanently closed pending the delivery of the balance check.
