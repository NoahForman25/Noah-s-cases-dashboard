## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding their current loan application because the initial preapproval offer could not be approved and requested an explanation for why the offer changed.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the modification of their initial personal loan preapproval terms and to provide a formal explanation for the decision. Operational review confirmed that personal loan preapproval rate locks carry a strict 7-day expiration window, and because the application was not finalized before this period elapsed, the initial rates expired and mandated a new underwriting review. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding the rate lock expiration rules and explained how elapsed processing windows alter loan term offers upon re-evaluation. The complaint is resolved, with the updated loan application terms remaining active for the customer's review.

### Second Contact
- Dear Jake,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. We acknowledge your complaint regarding your current loan application and your request for an explanation as to why the initial preapproval offer could not be approved. We recognize the frustration caused by the change in the offer from what you initially received.

We reviewed the matter and confirmed that for Personal Loan applications, the rate lock duration is 7 days. The rate lock begins when it is created for the application. If the application is not completed and the rate lock expires after that 7-day period, the original offer terms are no longer valid. When this occurs, the application is subject to a new review, and the offer terms may differ from the initial preapproval.

This process explains why the initial preapproval offer could not be approved at the original terms. The offer changed because the rate lock period had elapsed before the application was finalized.
