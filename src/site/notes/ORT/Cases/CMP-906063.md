## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction, stating that the information provided by the agent was misleading compared to what was displayed in the app.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding misleading information received from a representative about loan payment allocations compared to the displays within the mobile app. Operational review confirmed that personal loan payments follow a strict, standardized hierarchy where incoming funds apply first toward daily accrued interest based on the APR and remaining principal, followed by delinquent balances, and finally the principal amount. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding the daily interest accrual calculation and explained how standard payment waterfall logic accounts for the discrepancy between prior agent statements and the app display. The complaint is resolved, with the loan account remaining active and current under standard payment application terms.

### Second Contact
- Dear Fransico,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. We acknowledge your complaint regarding the information provided during your previous contact, which you indicated was misleading when compared to what was displayed in your app. We recognize the frustration caused by the discrepancy between the information you received and what you observed in the app.

We reviewed your account and confirmed how your loan payments are allocated. When a payment is made, it is applied first toward the interest that has accrued on your loan, and then toward any delinquency amount on the account. This allocation order is the standard process for personal loan payments.

Regarding daily interest, interest on your loan accrues each day based on your remaining principal balance and your annual percentage rate (APR). This means that a portion of interest is added to your account daily. When a payment is received, the amount that has accrued in interest is satisfied first before any remaining portion is applied to the delinquency amount or principal balance. This daily accrual is why the interest portion of your payment may differ from what was communicated during your previous contact.
