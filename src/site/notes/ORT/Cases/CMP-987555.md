## Complaint
- The member expressed concern and confusion after receiving a fraud alert and learning their account was pending closure, explaining that home internet DNS resets by AI agents, a bounced $561.40 check after funds were spent, and duplicate Google charges from an AI program contributed to the issue. The member was also worried about an incoming direct deposit expected that night, stated the account should not be closed, and asked how to prevent the closure and resolve the situation. The agent acknowledged the member's concerns, reviewed the account and transactions, and informed the member that the account was restricted and pending closure following a comprehensive review. The agent explained that transaction investigations are handled by the dispute intake team, offered assistance with filing a dispute for the Google charges, and checked internally for options to stop the closure, confirming the closure decision was final and irreversible.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an account fraud alert, pending account termination, disputed Google charges, and concerns over an incoming direct deposit. Operational review determined that the account was restricted and slated for permanent closure following a comprehensive risk investigation, a final decision that cannot be halted or reversed. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency explaining that the account cannot accept incoming funds and that the direct deposit would automatically be returned to the sender, while offering referral assistance to the dispute intake team should the customer wish to dispute the duplicate merchant charges. The customer's account remains restricted and pending final closure, with no further administrative reversals available.
