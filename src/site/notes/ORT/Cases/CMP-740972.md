## Complaint
- Member contacted to request an escalation to a supervisor regarding an expedited provisional credit. They expressed frustration that the standard ten-business-day timeframe to receive a resolution or credit is too long to wait, noting an urgent financial need for the funds. 
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on their dispute and their request to escalate for an expedited provisional credit due to urgent financial need. After completing authentication, the agent explained the dispute resolution timeline and addressed the member's frustration regarding the standard timeframe and communication gaps. The specialist clarified that provisional credit is standardly issued after ten business days if the investigation is not resolved sooner, ensuring the member has clear expectations regarding when the funds could be applied to the account.
