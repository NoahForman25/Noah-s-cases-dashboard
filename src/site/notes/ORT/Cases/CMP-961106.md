## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration that SoFi migrated crypto accounts to Blockchain in 2023 and closed open accounts, noting that SoFi now offers crypto accounts again. The member stated that SoFi should be responsible for recovering their migrated crypto account, explaining that they cannot access their assets on Blockchain, have made little progress with Blockchain's customer support, and feel their assets are lost if SoFi cannot recover them.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding the inability to access cryptocurrency assets migrated to Blockchain in 2023 and her demand for the institution to recover the funds. Operational review confirmed that all eligible crypto assets were fully transferred to independent accounts hosted on the Blockchain.com platform during the 2023 transition, leaving the institution with no custody, access, or management authority over the relocated holdings. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding third-party asset ownership and directed the customer to coordinate directly with Blockchain.com support to locate the balances and recover login credentials. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's prior crypto profile remaining closed and external recovery resting entirely with Blockchain.com.
