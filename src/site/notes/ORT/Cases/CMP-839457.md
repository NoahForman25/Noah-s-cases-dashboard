## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction after their debit card was repeatedly declined while attempting to make a purchase at a store, stating the experience made them feel embarrassed. The member noted that they had called support previously and were assured everything was resolved, and directly demanded to speak with a manager. Support validated the member's frustration, issued an apology for the negative experience, and completed an immediate warm transfer to a Banking Operations manager for real-time assistance.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an inquiry regarding persistent debit card declines and issues with their smart card functionality. After authenticating the member, the specialist addressed their strong dissatisfaction and embarrassment following repeated transaction rejections at a retail store, which occurred after a previous support agent had assured them the issue was fully resolved. While support had previously validated the member's frustration and completed a warm transfer to a Banking Operations manager for immediate assistance, this follow-up call focused on technical troubleshooting to restore full card access. The member noted that while their primary debit card began functioning after a PIN reset, their smart card was still failing to prompt for a PIN. The specialist walked the member step-by-step through updating their PIN directly within the SoFi app, which was completed successfully. The specialist apologized for the inconvenience, and both parties agreed that a replacement smart card would be reissued if any further technical performance issues arise.
