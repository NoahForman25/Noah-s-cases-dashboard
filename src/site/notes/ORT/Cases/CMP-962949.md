## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with the company, stating that it "sucks," due to being unable to receive a One-Time PIN. The agent provided basic troubleshooting to reset the text message method, but the member was still unable to receive the PIN.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding the inability to receive a one-time passcode for authentication and the frustration caused by the service disruption. Operational review confirmed that SMS passcode delivery issues were occurring on October 30, but subsequent profile activity recorded multiple successful logins, indicating that passcode transmission functionality had been restored. To resolve the complaint, the specialist issued an apology for the login experience, verified that system access had normalized, and offered to initiate a technical support ticket if further authentication failures occur. The customer's account remains active, secure, and in good standing with login access functioning normally.
