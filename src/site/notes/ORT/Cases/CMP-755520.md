## Complaint
- Member contacted to express anger and frustration regarding the security procedures required to update their phone number and deactivate two-factor authentication (2FA). The member provided a copy of their driver's license, which was subsequently declined by the system, leaving them unable to complete the security updates. Due to their dissatisfaction with the process, the member demanded an immediate escalation to a manager.

## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their severe dissatisfaction regarding a denied dispute outcome and the security hurdles preventing them from updating their phone number and deactivating two-factor authentication. The specialist acknowledged the frustration surrounding the lack of immediate access to funds and the system's rejection of their submitted driver's license during identity verification. The member—who utilized profanity throughout the initial interaction and threatened to escalate the matter to the FBI while retaining legal counsel—was informed that the requested security updates remain pending. To restore full access and update the profile, the specialist clarified that the member must submit a new, valid identification document, reassuring them that the team is ready to complete the verification as soon as the new ID is acquired.
