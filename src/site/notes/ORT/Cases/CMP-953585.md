## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction after disputes for allegedly unauthorized transactions were denied and their account was closed following the loss or theft of their card. The member repeatedly questioned why the account was closed, voiced frustration that no specific explanation was provided, and alleged that SoFi was the "fraud" for denying the claims and closing the account without justification. Additionally, the member threatened legal action over the handling of the disputes and the account closure.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the denial of disputes for unauthorized card transactions, resulting account closure, and allegations of bank misconduct. Operational review confirmed that the disputed charges were initiated externally following the reported loss or theft of the card, verifying that the institution did not originate or participate in the transactions. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided transparency regarding the operational findings, formally refuted the allegations of internal fraud, and extended an apology for the customer's overall experience throughout the dispute and closure process. The complaint is resolved, with the formal dispute denial standing and the customer's account remaining closed.
