## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding account takeover fraud in which their SoFi account was hacked, resulting in unauthorized transactions, a Bitcoin purchase, and an unauthorized $20,000 personal loan application. The member reported a negative checking balance of $28 and $250 still missing from savings, expressing concern that upcoming automatic payments for electric and telephone bills would be declined or incur overdraft fees. Additionally, the member voiced frustration with delays in returning the funds, stating they expected resolution within 24 to 48 hours, and threatened to notify the Federal Trade Commission if the issue is not resolved immediately.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding account takeover fraud involving unauthorized transactions, a Bitcoin purchase, a $20,000 personal loan application, and missing funds threatening upcoming utility bill payments. Operational review confirmed that account security protocols were successfully executed to resolve the mobile wallet issue during a follow-up support call. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the mobile wallet fix was completed, extended an apology for the delays in resolving the broader security incident, and provided direct instructions for the customer to reconnect with support should any further unauthorized activity or balance discrepancies persist. The complaint is resolved, with the immediate mobile wallet functionality restored and clear contact protocols established for ongoing security monitoring.
