## Complaint
- Member contacted to express strong frustration regarding an inability to withdraw $100,000.00 from their account to a SoFi bank account to cover immediate housing expenses. The member noted that the funds originated from an account transfer initiated from E*TRADE on July 2, 2026, and made claims that the institution is intentionally preventing them from accessing their money and showing a disregard for the difficult financial position this restriction has caused.

Internal consultation with the Fraud team confirmed that due to security protocols on recent large-dollar transfers, the withdrawal is currently restricted. The member must wait for the standard 30-day holding period to elapse before a manual request to release and transfer the funds can be processed.
