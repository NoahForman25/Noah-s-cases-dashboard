## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding the timeline for the payout of their direct deposit promotional bonus, stating that the delay has prevented them from making essential purchases, specifically necessary medication.

The member was advised of the standard evaluation and processing timeline but is demanding that the bonus be credited immediately. Account review confirms the initial direct deposit was received on May 28, 2026, initiating the 25-day evaluation window which concluded on June 12, 2026. Because the member's qualifying direct deposits totaled $3,617.00 during this period, they are eligible for a $50.00 bonus under the promotional terms and conditions, which currently remains pending payout.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed the member to address their frustration regarding the timeline for the payout of their direct deposit promotional bonus and their demand for the immediate credit of these funds to purchase necessary medication. The specialist conducted an audit of the account deposits, clarifying that the member's initial deposit on May 28, 2026, initiated the 25-day evaluation window. Because the qualifying direct deposits totaled $3,617.10 during this period, the automated system approved a $50.00 bonus rather than the higher tier. Ultimately, a final resolution was provided, informing the member that the payout timeline cannot be manually adjusted, but confirming that the $50.00 bonus is pending and scheduled to officially post to the account on or before July 2, 2026.
