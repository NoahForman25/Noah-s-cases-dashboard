## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding recurring account restrictions after funds were reportedly released, and reported receiving inconsistent information across multiple interactions. Specifically, the member stated that representatives told them the funds were released and the account was unlocked, only for the account to show as restricted again when attempted to be used. Additionally, the member expressed frustration with undergoing repeated identity verification, stating that representatives act as though they cannot see the information on their screen and stating they did not want to go through verification again due to the trouble it caused.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding recurring account restrictions, inconsistent representative guidance, and the frustration caused by repeated identity verification requests. Operational review confirmed that despite previous indications that funds were released, the account triggered automated protective holds that strictly require live identity verification to satisfy security standards and release the restriction. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct phone contact details and clear instructions for completing the mandatory verification with the support team to definitively clear the security flags. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining in restricted status pending the customer's completion of phone-based identity verification to restore full profile access.
