## Complaint
- The member called to update their phone number and resolve a loan application blocker caused by an Account Take Over fraud alert (FR-55471) on their credit report. The member explained that their old, inaccessible phone number remained on file, which triggered the fraud alert during the application process, and expressed frustration over the resulting delay. Support empathized with the member's situation and attempted identity verification via email; however, the member was unable to access the link sent to their email address. Support then consulted with Member Security and warm-transferred the caller directly to the Fraud Prevention team to complete identity verification, update the phone number, and evaluate removing the Account Take Over flag from the application.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist followed up with the member regarding their complaint about identity verification issues and a loan application blocker triggered by an Account Take Over fraud alert (FR-55471) on their credit report. The member had previously called to update an old, inaccessible phone number that caused the fraud alert during the application process, expressing frustration over the resulting processing delays and difficulty accessing the email verification link provided by support. While support had previously consulted with Member Security and transferred the member to the Fraud Prevention team to complete identity verification and address the account flag, the specialist confirmed during the follow-up outreach that all outstanding issues have been fully resolved. The member stated that they no longer require further assistance, and the case has been closed.
