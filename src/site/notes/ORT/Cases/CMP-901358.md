## Complaint
- The member requested a manager because they were unaware that they needed to send at least 50% of their loan funds directly to creditors to receive a 0.25% direct pay discount, and because SoFi could not pay one of their non-partner banks. The member expressed frustration over their loan rate, alleged SoFi misinformed them, and requested to have the rate lowered or the discount applied. The manager explained that because the loan is already in repayment, the rate cannot be adjusted, and offered to submit a formal complaint or allow the member to return the loan funds while paying accrued interest.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the direct pay discount requirement and their inability to apply the 0.25% interest rate reduction to their personal loan. An operational review confirmed that the direct pay discount requires at least 50% of total loan proceeds to be disbursed directly to third-party creditors at initial origination, and because the loan had already entered active repayment, retroactive rate adjustments or interest modifications could not be executed. To resolve the complaint, the specialist clearly outlined the fixed terms of the loan contract, apologized for the miscommunication during the application process, and provided the customer with options to either lodge a formal service grievance or return the full principal amount alongside accrued interest. The complaint is resolved, with the loan remaining in active repayment under its original contract terms.
