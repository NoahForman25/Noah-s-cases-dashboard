## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after calling to request a letter containing their name, last four digits of their account number, and May and June deposit stubs needed immediately for a car application. The agent advised the member to obtain a blank verification of deposit form from the requester and sign it to authorize SoFi to release the details, but the member insisted SoFi provide the form; after the agent explained that the form originates from the requester, the member demanded a supervisor and hung up while on hold. The agent subsequently attempted a callback, which went to voicemail, and left a message directing the member to call back for further assistance.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address a complaint regarding a previous interaction concerning a request for a verification of deposit document needed immediately for a car application. During the initial call, the member requested a document containing their name, the last four digits of their account number, and May and June deposit details; after being advised that the requesting party must provide the blank verification form for SoFi to complete, the member demanded a supervisor and hung up while on hold. After authenticating the member on the follow-up call, the agent discussed the documentation request, and the member explained that they had independently gathered the required banking history through screenshots. The agent reassured the member regarding standard support protocols, and the member confirmed the issue was resolved and noted no complaints regarding the previous agent's performance.
