## Complaint
- The member called SoFi to dispute a fraudulent charge of $16.04 on their account. The member refused to provide personal information for authentication, insisting they were the account owner, demanded that the agent dispute the charge, and responded with hostility when the agent attempted to address their concerns.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding a fraudulent $16.04 charge and his frustration with mandatory identity authentication requirements during customer service calls. Operational review confirmed that identity verification is an essential security control required to protect member accounts and verify authorization before claims can be processed or account changes initiated. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding authentication mandates, offered a self-service alternative to submit the fraud report directly through his online profile, and provided direct phone contact details to complete verbal verification. The customer's account remains open, secure, and active in good standing, pending inbound contact or self-service submission to officially initiate the dispute for the $16.04 charge.
