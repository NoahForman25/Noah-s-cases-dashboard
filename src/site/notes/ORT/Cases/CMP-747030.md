## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration after being informed that they are no longer eligible for the Pay in 4 installment program.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address their frustration and confusion regarding their sudden ineligibility for the Pay in 4 installment program. After completing identity verification, the agent explained that eligibility criteria fluctuate dynamically based on direct deposit amounts and general account activity. Although the member asserted that they maintain consistent activity on their account, the specialist advised them to monitor their status and recheck their program eligibility at the beginning of the month.
