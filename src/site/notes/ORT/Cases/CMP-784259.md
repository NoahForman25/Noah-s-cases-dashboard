## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding an inability to deposit a settlement check, reporting that the mobile application consistently crashes whenever they attempt to complete the deposit process.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding issues with the mobile check deposit process. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's extreme frustration regarding their inability to deposit a large settlement check due to the bank's established limits and repeated app crashes. Delivering a clear update, the agent validated the member's experience and explained that their feedback has been formally documented to help drive future platform and policy improvements. While the member expressed subsequent dissatisfaction with an earlier suggestion to take their business to another institution, they thanked the agent for the direct outreach and follow-up.
