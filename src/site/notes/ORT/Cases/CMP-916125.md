## Complaint
- The member expressed severe frustration regarding an inability to link a bank account or transfer funds into their SoFi Invest account, as the app repeatedly displayed a "please try again" error. The member noted that their long-linked personal savings account balance was still recognized in their net worth, indicating the issue was on SoFi's end. After experiencing repeated automated responses from the AI virtual assistant, the member expressed dissatisfaction, threatened to switch to another investment platform, and disconnected from the chat.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding application errors preventing bank account linking and fund transfers into their SoFi Invest profile. Operational review confirmed that a temporary system integration defect caused recurring "please try again" error messages within the mobile app despite existing external account connections. To resolve the complaint, technical teams remediated the backend transfer error, restoring full functionality to account linking and deposit processing within the app. The complaint is resolved, with the technical issue successfully fixed and normal transfer capabilities fully restored.
