## Complaint
- The member contacted support to appeal closed dispute ticket 8279540 regarding a $500.00 transaction on May 17, 2026 (`POSDD PAYPAL *CGARCIA8`). The member explained that the case was erroneously closed on July 20 after SoFi cited an unrelated PayPal merchant credit, which was then reopened on July 22 after the member provided evidence that the credit belonged to a different user (`kglaser78`). Pointing out that SoFi phone support and the PayPal app confirmed on August 16 that the merchant's documentation was denied and the case was decided in their favor, the member demanded immediate release of the $500.00 dispute credit and stated they would file a complaint with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) if the issue is not promptly resolved. Support acknowledged the member's frustration regarding the misapplied merchant credit and conflicting case closures, documented the timeline and previous resolution confirmations, and submitted an urgent priority escalation ticket to Dispute Operations, Fraud Management, and Compliance Leadership to review ticket 8279540, issue the $500.00 credit, and send a direct written confirmation to the member.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address his dissatisfaction regarding the status and handling of his dispute appeal for a $500.00 debit card transaction with "PAYPAL *CGARCIA8" on May 17, 2026. The member had previously raised concerns over misapplied merchant credits and conflicting case closures, highlighting that prior communications indicated the merchant's evidence had been denied in his favor, and stated an intent to file a Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) complaint if the $500.00 credit was not released. The specialist reviewed the case details and confirmed that his appeal is actively pending review with the dispute team. The specialist clarified that the formal evaluation is ongoing without a guaranteed completion timeframe or outcome, noting that no further action is required from the member while the file remains under re-evaluation, and assured him that direct notification will be provided once a final determination is reached.
