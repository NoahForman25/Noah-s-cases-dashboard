## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding the denial of their dispute for transactions categorized as ATM withdrawals. They argued that the disputed amounts could not be legitimate ATM withdrawals because ATM machines only dispense $20 bills. Additionally, the member expressed skepticism regarding how the company handled their case.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the denial of a dispute for approximately $400.00 in electronic fund transfers that the customer argued could not be legitimate ATM withdrawals due to non-standard bill increments. Operational review confirmed that the initial dispute investigation was conducted in accordance with claim policies and determined that the denial decision remains in effect based on available account data. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided transparency regarding the review outcome, explained that provisional credits may be reversed if previously issued, and outlined the requirement for new supporting documentation to initiate a formal appeal. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute remaining closed under the initial denial determination unless new supporting evidence is provided for review.
