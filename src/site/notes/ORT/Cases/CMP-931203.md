## Complaint
- The member expressed severe dissatisfaction regarding their declined personal loan application, stating an underwriter previously informed them the decline was not due to income. According to the underwriter, the application was system-declined because the co-borrower had another open application, reaching the $100,000 personal loan limit. The member noted they were never informed that multiple ongoing applications were prohibited, requested an alternative to the 30-day waiting period, and inquired if the application could be revived for either $100,000 or $35,820.13. Additionally, the member reported that Supervisor Belinda and another unnamed supervisor were rude during previous calls.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding account security disruptions while traveling, inconsistent customer support communications, and dissatisfaction over a declined personal loan application. Operational review confirmed that the personal loan decline decision is final due to an inability to verify income according to policy guidelines, and verified that the application cannot be revived or re-evaluated. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding the final status of the loan application while confirming that the secondary operational tickets for the customer's account compromise and pending deposits remain under active review. The complaint is resolved regarding the loan decision, with the loan application remaining closed and active investigation ongoing for the remaining account access and transaction disputes.
