## Complaint
- The member expressed intense frustration after a **$2,500 Zelle transaction** was flagged and held, complaining that similar transfers from a business account at another bank are never flagged and demanding immediate access to the funds. Support explained that automated fraud and risk monitoring systems evaluate transfers dynamically on a platform-by-platform basis—which can trigger security reviews on high-value activity regardless of history at other institutions—and submitted the transfer to risk operations for expedited review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an inquiry regarding a flagged $2,500 Zelle transfer. After authenticating the member, the agent discussed the held transaction, which had previously prompted intense frustration and demands for immediate release due to contrast with unflagged transfers at another bank. The member explained that after the Zelle transfer failed to complete, they resolved the matter by using PayPal instead. The member also shared frustration regarding a separate fraudulent transaction and dispute experience at Citibank, leading the agent to clarify how Zelle policies and risk monitoring criteria vary across different financial institutions. The member confirmed that the transfer issue was fully resolved and thanked the agent for following up.
