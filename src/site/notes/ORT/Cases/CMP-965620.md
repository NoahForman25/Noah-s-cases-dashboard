## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction and frustration regarding their accounts being frozen for five days without a clear explanation, stating they had been calling every hour to learn the reason for the hold and wanted to speak directly with the security team. The member reported submitting proof of wallet ownership approximately an hour prior, noting the review team cited it as the final requirement to resolve the issue, though they questioned if the submission was acceptable and voiced strong reluctance to share sensitive wallet information. Additionally, the member disputed a claim that the internal review team had attempted to call them at 10:38 AM, insisting they had their phone in hand all day and received no call.
## Resolution
- Closing complaint with no contact as an ORT agent has already reached out to the member on Nov 7th with a different complaint with the same issues/disatisfaction stated in this complaint. Got approval from Lori Williams to proceed as so.
