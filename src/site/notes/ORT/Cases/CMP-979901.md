## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a pending crypto transfer that had been delayed for approximately two days. The member requested escalation, asked why the funds could not be manually released after receiving over 5,000 confirmations, questioned why no estimated resolution time was available, and asked when SoFi is required to make the funds available, noting that other platforms were not experiencing this issue. The agent acknowledged the member's frustration, apologized for the inconvenience, and explained that an ongoing outage with an outside vendor was causing delays for crypto deposits and withdrawals with no estimated resolution time currently available.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding a two-day delay on an outbound Litecoin transfer despite blockchain network confirmations, as well as the inability to manually expedite the release. Operational review confirmed that the processing interruption stemmed from an external vendor outage affecting cryptocurrency deposits and withdrawals across the platform rather than an isolated account restriction. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding third-party dependency timeframes, issued an apology for the service disruption, and verified that the transaction successfully cleared on November 10 once vendor systems normalized. The complaint is resolved, with the cryptocurrency transfer fully executed and the customer's account active, operational, and in good standing.
