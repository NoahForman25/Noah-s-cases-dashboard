## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi after a wire transfer was denied and their balance was placed on hold, despite verifying their identity and having confirmed funds. They expressed deep frustration that this occurs repeatedly with every wire transfer attempted at SoFi, noting that Chase Bank never imposes such holds. The member disputed SoFi's claim that an outreach call was attempted, stating their phone records showed no calls or voicemails from SoFi and demanding proof of the attempt. Additionally, they were upset about waiting on hold for 36 minutes, stated their money was being held hostage for six additional days without justification, and threatened to cancel all of their SoFi accounts.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's complaint regarding repeated wire transfer holds, extended phone wait times, and reported lapses in proactive communication. Operational review confirmed that the wire transfer was successfully processed through the network but remained subject to an automated administrative hold set to clear the following business day. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, apologized for the previous communication breakdowns and service inconvenience, and provided clear transparency regarding the hold parameters and release timeline. The complaint is resolved, with the wire transfer processed and scheduled for final release upon the expiration of the hold the following day.
