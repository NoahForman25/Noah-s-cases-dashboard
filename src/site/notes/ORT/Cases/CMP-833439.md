## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding the referral bonus because they were with their prospect when they used their referral link July 7, 2026 but until today they have not received referral bonus, but the system did not recognize the prospect using the link. The member is speaking in Spanish.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email (translated from Spanish) to address their dissatisfaction regarding a missing referral bonus from July 7, 2026. The member noted they were physically present when their prospect used the referral link, but the system failed to automatically register the referral. The specialist apologized for the technical issue and confirmed that, to resolve the matter and honor the referral, 1,500 rewards points were directly credited to the member's profile.
