## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding an inability to access their account via both the website and mobile application for an extended period of approximately forty minutes. The member noted that this is a recurring issue and emphasized that the repeated technical failure has directly prevented them from managing their account, specifically hindering their ability to set a PIN for their new debit card. The member reported receiving an error message upon attempting to log in and demanded a timely, effective resolution to the system outage.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding a recurring technical issue that prevented account access via both the website and mobile application for approximately forty minutes, which had hindered their ability to set a PIN for their new debit card. The specialist delivered a final resolution, confirming that the member recently connected with a technical support agent who successfully restored account access and resolved the login error. To prevent future service interruptions, the specialist recommended standard troubleshooting practices, including keeping their iPhone's operating system updated and routinely clearing the browser's cache and cookies.
