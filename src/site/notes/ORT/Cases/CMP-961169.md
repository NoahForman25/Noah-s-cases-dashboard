## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding a lengthy delay in receiving their check for $286.06 from account closure, describing the wait as outrageous.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding the extended delay in receiving a $286.06 disbursement check following her account closure. Operational review confirmed that a previous address change request ticket was closed because institutional policy strictly requires disbursement checks to be mailed only to an established address already verified on the profile. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding address verification requirements and directed the customer to contact support directly to connect with the member security team to explore potential delivery destination options. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining permanently closed pending customer contact with member security to finalize check disbursement arrangements.
