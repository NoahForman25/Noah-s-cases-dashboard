## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with SoFi's refusal to accept a mobile check deposit made out to their previous legal last name following a mortgage fee refund. The member stated that they possess a court-ordered final judgment validating their legal name change, asserted there is no fraud involved, and voiced frustration when advised that SoFi could not accept the document to process the check and that the check would need to be reissued by the mortgage company.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding SoFi's refusal to accept a mobile check deposit made out to their previous legal last name following a legal name change. Operational review confirmed that check processing policies require paper instruments to strictly match the legal name on the active account, and court orders cannot bypass mobile deposit name-matching criteria to accept checks issued to prior names. To resolve the complaint, the specialist documented the customer's operational feedback regarding the six-month name change rule and interest rate adjustments, while clarifying that the check must be reissued by the originating mortgage company under their current legal name. The complaint is resolved, with clear check reissuance guidelines established and internal feedback recorded regarding SoFi's mobile deposit policies.
