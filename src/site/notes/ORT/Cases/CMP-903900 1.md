## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration because loan funds were disbursed directly to their checking account after they were previously informed that funding would be held until they requested it.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the unexpected disbursement of loan funds into their checking account after believing the funds were to be held until requested. Operational review, including a thorough examination of call recordings, confirmed that the representative did not advise that funds would be held, and system processing automatically disbursed the approved proceeds directly to checking. To resolve the complaint, the specialist delivered official clarification regarding the recorded interaction, provided transparency around loan disbursement protocols, and offered an apology for the overall member experience. The complaint is resolved, with the loan active and no additional action available to reverse the disbursed funds.
