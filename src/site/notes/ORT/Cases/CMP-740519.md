## Complaint
- Member called regarding an account restriction placed on multiple recent ACH transfers (including $10,000 and $1,000 deposits), expressing frustration that the account was frozen without proactive communication and stating they would not have deposited the funds had they known a hold would occur. They initially refused to provide identity verification, arguing that sufficient documentation was already provided at account opening, and grew highly dissatisfied with the additional verification questions and the two-to-three business day resolution timeframe. Describing the situation as "horrible customer service," the member emphasized that the originating banks already debited the funds, they do not want the money returned, and they expect a callback within two business days as promised.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed to address their frustration regarding an unnotified account restriction placed on recent large ACH deposits and their dissatisfaction with the additional identity verification requirements and resolution timeframe. The specialist shared a positive update, confirming that the account review was completed and the restriction was successfully lifted on the 15th, making the deposited funds fully available for use. Additionally, the specialist clarified that fresh identity verifications are standard security steps required to protect the account and safely process larger transfers, even if documentation was previously provided at account opening.
## Case Intake & Investigation

### First contact
- The resolution specialist attempted to contact the member regarding their complaint. The specialist received the member's voicemail and left a message. The specialist will send a detailed email.
