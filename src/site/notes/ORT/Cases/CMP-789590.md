## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding a recurring buy order that failed to execute and was returned due to insufficient funds in their account. The member is dissatisfied with the transaction failing rather than processing as scheduled.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding a scheduled recurring buy order that failed to execute and was voided by the system due to insufficient funds. The specialist addressed the member's dissatisfaction with the transaction being returned rather than processing as scheduled. Delivering a clear resolution, the specialist explained that the system voided the transaction because the linked external account lacked a sufficient balance at the time of execution. To prevent future issues and ensure accurate balance reporting, the specialist recommended that the member reconnect their external account via Plaid to refresh the connection, apologizing for the inconvenience caused by the voided order.
