## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction following the denial of their fraud dispute for ticket #8516738. The member detailed that an unauthorized party compromised their account, transferred funds from savings to checking, attempted a Zelle transfer, and executed multiple fraudulent transactions between August 26 and August 28. Expressing confusion and frustration over why SoFi deemed only one transaction unauthorized while denying the rest, the member emphasized that the unusual activity and internal account transfer clearly indicate account compromise, and they urgently requested a full re-investigation and refund of their stolen savings. Support acknowledged the member's frustration, logged the additional account compromise details, and submitted an urgent dispute appeal ticket to Fraud Operations and Dispute Advocacy for a manual secondary review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her strong dissatisfaction following the partial denial of her fraud dispute under ticket #8516738. The member had previously challenged the decision, expressing confusion as to why only one transaction was deemed unauthorized while remaining fraudulent charges were denied, despite evidence showing an unauthorized party compromised her account, transferred funds from savings to checking, attempted a Zelle transfer, and executed multiple unauthorized transactions between August 26 and August 28, 2026. The specialist acknowledged the member's concerns and confirmed that the additional account compromise details were fully documented and attached to the file. The specialist verified that an urgent dispute appeal ticket was submitted to Fraud Operations and Dispute Advocacy for a comprehensive, manual secondary review of the entire transaction sequence, noting that the appeal remains actively under review and inviting the member to submit any further supporting documentation while the investigation is ongoing.
