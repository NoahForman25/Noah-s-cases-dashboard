## Complaint
- The member contacted support regarding an inability to access their account after the mobile app repeatedly rejected their physical state ID during an mandatory enhanced identity verification prompt. The member expressed intense frustration that their identity was being rejected by the platform automated tools and became increasingly alarmed upon being advised that access would remain restricted until they obtained a replacement physical driver's license from the DMV—a process that could take up to three weeks. Emphasizing that this extended delay felt entirely unreasonable and was causing severe hardship, the member offered alternative government-issued documentation, including a valid passport, to verify their identity. Support logged the technical verification failure, captured the alternative identity documents for secondary operational review, and escalated the access block for urgent manual verification.
## Resolution
- No Contact Exception: Did not contact the Member due to confirmed ATO on profile.  
  
Justification: member had discussion with agent on July 30th about information they are needing to help member get access to funds (ID).  
  
Action Taken: Closing complaint with no contact
