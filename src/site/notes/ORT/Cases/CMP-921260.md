## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an inability to log in due to not receiving two-factor authentication (2FA) codes. The member also expressed frustration that agents are unable to transfer calls directly to a manager and can only schedule a callback.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding two-factor authentication (2FA) login code delivery failures and the policy requirement of scheduling manager callbacks rather than providing direct transfers. Operational review confirmed that login issues stemmed from authentication code delivery delays, while call-handling procedures restrict direct live transfers to management in favor of scheduled callback queues. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the authentication issue had been successfully resolved by another support team member, documented the customer's service feedback, and submitted a formal internal complaint to review agent transfer protocols and login troubleshooting processes. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's 2FA access fully restored and their account functioning normally.
