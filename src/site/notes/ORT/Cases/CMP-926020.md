## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding not receiving the $50 Direct Deposit Welcome Bonus after setting up payroll with SoFi via ADP, describing the promotion as misleading and heavily gated by fine print. The member expressed frustration that their 25-day evaluation window closed three days prior to a $514.71 payroll deposit posting on September 30th, leaving their cumulative total at $907.17 and short of the required $1,000.00 threshold to trigger the bonus.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding a missed $50 Direct Deposit Welcome Bonus after their promotional evaluation period expired before a final payroll deposit posted. Operational review confirmed that the customer's 25-day evaluation window closed three days prior to a $514.71 deposit posting on September 30th, resulting in a cumulative total of $907.17 that fell short of the required $1,000.00 promotional threshold. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, explained the timing criteria for the evaluation window, and credited $15.00 worth of reward points to their account as goodwill remediation for the timing shortfall. The complaint is resolved, with the promotional requirements fully clarified and the reward points credited to the customer's profile.
