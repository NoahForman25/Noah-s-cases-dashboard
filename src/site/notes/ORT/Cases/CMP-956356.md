## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after receiving a notification stating that no fraud was determined, despite being a victim of a scam and identity theft where a personal loan was opened in his name.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a notification stating that no fraud was found following an identity theft incident and fraudulent personal loan. Operational review confirmed that an account takeover had indeed occurred, overturning the initial finding and necessitating specialized fraud mitigation procedures. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified the account breach, provided direct contact details for the member security team, and outlined the mandatory security steps required to secure the profile and address the unauthorized loan. The complaint is resolved, with the case escalated to the member security team pending the customer's direct phone contact to finalize identity theft remediation.
