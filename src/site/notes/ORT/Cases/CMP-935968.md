## Complaint

Additionally, the member raised concerns about a $110 dispute filed on September 11, 2026, for an Apex Arch LLC charge, expressing dissatisfaction that the investigation could take up to 90 days to finalize despite already receiving provisional credit on September 20. The member also inquired about an expected $80 refund from Amazon—which the agent confirmed was not yet showing as pending—and asked how to transfer $20 out of an Active Invest account, for which the agent advised calling back after 8:00 AM EST to speak with the Invest team.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding an extra debit card, a $110 dispute for an Apex Arch LLC charge, a pending $80 Amazon refund, and a $20 transfer from an Active Invest account. Operational review confirmed that processing and posting timelines accounted for the ongoing updates across the respective transactions and account features. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the $110 dispute was officially approved with funds applied, confirmed the successful completion of the $20 Invest transfer, verified that the $80 Amazon refund posted to the account on October 18, and addressed the extra debit card status based on the customer's freezing preferences. The complaint is resolved, with all disputed and transferred funds fully accounted for and the account remaining open for standard operations.
