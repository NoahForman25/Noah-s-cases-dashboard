## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding an ongoing $318.00 dispute filed on August 11, 2026, stating they contacted the merchant directly and received confirmation that the chargeback was accepted on September 1, 2026. The member voiced frustration over SoFi's lack of resolution, delayed refunds, and failure to provide provisional credit or follow up as promised. When informed that SoFi was awaiting a merchant response and could not expedite the process or issue a credit, the member demanded to speak with an immediate higher-level manager. Upon learning that an escalation would take 24–48 hours, the member refused to accept the timeframe or disconnect the call, prompting the agent to end the interaction to assist other members.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted follow-up phone calls with the customer to address their complaint regarding a delayed $318.00 refund dispute for a Foot Locker transaction. Operational review confirmed that while the refund request had not originally posted from the merchant, the ongoing dispute was reviewed and processed to resolve the customer's financial loss. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, clarified the status of the funds, and the dispute team finalized the dispute and credited $318.00 directly to their account. While resolution specialists credited points to address the prior delays and communication gaps. The complaint is resolved, with the $318.00 credit successfully posted to the customer's account and the dispute closed.
