## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding an ongoing $318.00 dispute filed on August 11, 2026, stating they contacted the merchant directly and received confirmation that the chargeback was accepted on September 1, 2026. The member voiced frustration over SoFi's lack of resolution, delayed refunds, and failure to provide provisional credit or follow up as promised.
