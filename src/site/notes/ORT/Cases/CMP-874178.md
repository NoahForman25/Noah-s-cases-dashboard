## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after failing to receive the One-Time Passcode (OTP) text messages required to verify their identity and complete the document upload process. Support assisted with basic SMS troubleshooting steps, verified the contact details on file, and initiated an alternate verification procedure to help the member successfully submit their documents.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their ongoing frustration regarding technical barriers experienced during identity verification and document submission. The member had previously been unable to receive required One-Time Passcode (OTP) text messages, which prevented them from completing mandatory identity validation and uploading necessary loan documentation. The specialist confirmed that a Member Technical Support ticket had been submitted to resolve the technical barrier, and an agent subsequently generated and sent a secure, alternate verification link directly to the member's messages. The specialist advised the member to access the newly provided link to complete their identity verification and document upload, confirming that technical support will remain available to ensure the process completes smoothly.
