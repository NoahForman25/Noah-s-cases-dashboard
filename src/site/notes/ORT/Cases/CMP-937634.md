## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration over being unable to access Zelle as a self-employed individual without access to direct deposit. The member stated that the direct deposit requirement discriminates against and prevents individuals who cannot set up direct deposits from using Zelle.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an inability to access Zelle as a self-employed individual who does not receive standard direct deposits. Operational review confirmed that qualifying direct deposits are a mandatory institutional eligibility requirement for Zelle activation, meaning the feature cannot be enabled without eligible recurring payroll or benefit transfers. To resolve the complaint, the specialist issued an apology for the service frustration and provided operational guidance recommending alternative peer-to-peer payment platforms that do not require direct deposit, such as PayPal, Venmo, or Cash App. The complaint is resolved, with Zelle remaining disabled per institutional policy and the customer's account remaining active, open, and in good standing.
