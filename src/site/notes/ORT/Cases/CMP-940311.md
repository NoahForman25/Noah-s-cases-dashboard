## Complaint
- The member expressed significant frustration during a chat session to dispute four unauthorized debit card transactions—a $444.00 ATM withdrawal, $59.38 at Safeway Fuel, $16.81 at Burlington, and $63.00 at Luxury Massage—following the loss of their wallet. The member criticized the intake process as repetitive and inefficient, insulted the agent by calling them a "moron," "incompetent," and an "idiot," and stated SoFi provides the "worst service ever." The member repeatedly requested a supervisor and was provided with SoFi's phone number. The agent collected the necessary dispute details, confirmed the member's mailing address to issue a replacement debit card, and submitted the dispute.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding four unauthorized debit card charges following a lost wallet and their dissatisfaction with the dispute intake process. Operational review confirmed that disputes were formally submitted for all four transactions—a $444.00 ATM withdrawal, $59.38 at Safeway Fuel, $16.81 at Burlington, and $63.00 at Luxury Massage—and subsequently denied under initial investigation guidelines. To resolve the complaint, the specialist formally acknowledged the intake process feedback, apologized for the frustrating experience, and provided explicit instructions on submitting new documentation to appeal the denied claims. The complaint is resolved, with the disputes remaining closed under the initial denial pending the submission of new supporting evidence for an appeal review.
