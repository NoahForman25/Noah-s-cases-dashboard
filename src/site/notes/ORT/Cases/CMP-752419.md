## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding ongoing difficulties linking their external bank account via Plaid to their Invest account. Due to the persistent technical issues and the inability to establish the link, the member decided to proceed with closing their account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their previous difficulties attempting to link an external bank account via Plaid to their Invest account. The specialist apologized for the confusion and persistent technical issues encountered during the account linking process and provided alternative funding options to assist the member. Specifically, the specialist clarified the institution's wire transfer policies, informing the member that inbound wire transfers are free to receive, while outbound wire transfers incur a $30 fee.
