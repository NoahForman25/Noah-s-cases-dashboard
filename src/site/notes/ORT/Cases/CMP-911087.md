## Complaint
- The member reached out to dispute two unauthorized debit card transactions: a $4.99 charge from CASH APP*GOOGLE on September 17, 2026, and a $4.00 charge from JACK IN THE BOX on July 3, 2026. The member expressed frustration that money was being taken immediately upon hitting their account, stated that they had previously been promised replacement cards that never arrived, and asked for a phone number to report the issue to management. The agent apologized, clarified that the bank was not at fault for the unauthorized charges, and provided the support phone number ((855) 456-7634) so the member could call to check card tracking and file a dispute. The agent subsequently closed the chat due to inactivity after receiving no further response from the member.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding two unauthorized debit card transactions totaling $8.99 and unreceived replacement physical debit cards. Operational review confirmed that a new replacement debit card was successfully ordered and dispatched under standard 7-to-10 business day postal delivery timeframes, while the disputed $4.99 Cash App charge had not yet been formally filed in the system. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct instructions on how to initiate the formal dispute intake over the phone, confirmed the dispatch details of the replacement card, and provided tracking follow-up protocols. The complaint is resolved, with the replacement card actively in transit and clear steps provided for the customer to finalize the transaction dispute.
