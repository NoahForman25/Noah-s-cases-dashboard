## Complaint
- The member requested to speak with a supervisor regarding information provided by a previous representative concerning an additional $10,000.00 loan amount.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address his request to speak with a supervisor regarding guidance provided by a previous representative about an additional $10,000.00 loan amount. The member had previously sought clarification and leadership oversight concerning how the requested funds impacted his personal loan application. The specialist reviewed the application history and noted that a representative had already sent an email requesting supplemental details required to advance the underwriting process. The specialist requested that the member complete and return the requested information so the processing team can move forward with evaluating his loan application.
