## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding a $25 inactivity fee charged to their account and the liquidation of stock to cover it, stating they received no prior notice. 
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a $25 inactivity fee and the unnotified liquidation of stock to cover the balance. Operational review confirmed that the fee was assessed in accordance with the standard SoFi Invest fee schedule due to account inactivity, and automated system protocols execute necessary asset liquidations to cover outstanding fees without separate advance warning when cash balances are insufficient. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding the governing fee terms and automated liquidation mechanisms while logging the lack of advance notification for internal process improvements. The complaint is resolved, with the fee policy and liquidation rationale fully explained to the customer.

We reviewed the matter and confirmed that the inactivity fee is a standard fee assessed on SoFi Invest accounts. The fee is applied when an account has not met the required activity level within the applicable period. The specific terms of the fee, including the amount and the conditions under which it is assessed, are outlined in the SoFi Invest fee schedule. When an account balance is insufficient to cover the fee in cash, the system may liquidate securities to cover the amount owed. This process is automated and does not require separate advance notice before the liquidation occurs.
