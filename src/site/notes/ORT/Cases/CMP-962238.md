## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding the handling of an inquiry for an $800.00 ACH transfer from Capital One, following a previously reversed transfer that caused a negative balance of $299.00. The member emphasized the urgency of releasing the funds to cover imminent automatic debits and avoid financial strain, but the agent reiterated standard protocol that transfers take one to three business days and cannot be expedited. As a result, the member expressed frustration and felt unsupported.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an extended hold placed on an $800.00 incoming ACH transfer from Capital One and the urgency of accessing those funds to prevent overdrafts from scheduled debits. Operational review confirmed that the transfer had been placed on a temporary administrative hold pursuant to standard security protocols designed to evaluate incoming transactions following a prior reversed transfer and mitigate potential fraud risks. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the review had successfully concluded, verified the release of the $800.00 hold to make the balance available for pending debits, and issued a formal apology for the inconvenience caused by the delay. The complaint is resolved, with the funds fully posted and the customer's account returned to active standing with no further restrictions in place.
