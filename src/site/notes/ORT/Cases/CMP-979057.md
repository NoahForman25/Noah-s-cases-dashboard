## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction that their account remained restricted for several days despite completing identity verification. The member stated they had previously called to update their information, completed the ID and selfie verification, and were told the request was complete with an update expected the next day following an investigation. Upon calling back, the member was informed the investigation was still ongoing and felt they were getting the runaround after uploading their ID and scanning their face three separate times without regaining access to their funds. The member voiced significant concern over being unable to access their money, expressed frustration at being repeatedly prompted to verify, and encountered an "unexpected error" when attempting to open the initial verification link sent to their phone during the call.
## Resolution
- P&D have been in contact with member due to ATO being confirmed on the profile and investigation is still continuing in the matter
