## Complaint
- The member contacted support after a recent transaction attempt was declined, expressing strong dissatisfaction over the denied activity and emphasizing an urgent need to access and use their funds immediately.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to address a security restriction placed on their account following a recently declined transaction, which caused strong dissatisfaction due to their urgent need to access and use their funds immediately. After authenticating the member, who expressed additional frustration about managing the call while busy at work, the agent informed them that the account was currently under review. The agent explained the next steps, clarifying that a dedicated fraud agent would follow up within one business day to handle the identity verification process, and provided the general security contact number when the member requested a direct call-back line.
