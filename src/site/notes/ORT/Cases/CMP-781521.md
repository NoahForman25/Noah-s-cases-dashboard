## Complaint
- Member contacted to request an immediate escalation to a supervisor, explaining that they need to speak with management to have an active restriction lifted from their account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction and claim that an unauthorized transaction on their account was the fault of the institution due to a data breach. The specialist delivered a clear update regarding the status of the account, informing the member that the profile is currently under review and pending closure with no specific timeframe for completion. Finally, the specialist advised that once the review is finalized, any funds the member is entitled to receive will be officially issued via check and mailed directly to the address on file.
