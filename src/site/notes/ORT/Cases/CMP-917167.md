## Complaint
- The member called to express frustration regarding a wire transaction initiated the previous day. They stated that an individual claiming to be a SoFi agent called them but failed to provide proof of their identity before requesting the member's personally identifiable information (PII) for verification. The member was extremely dissatisfied with SoFi's security and verification procedures regarding agent identification.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a wire transfer initiated on September 30, 2026, and security concerns surrounding an unverified outbound call requesting personally identifiable information. Operational review confirmed that the wire transfer was successfully processed through standard settlement channels. To resolve the complaint and address the service disruption, the specialist credited 2,000 reward points to the customer's account to fully reimburse the wire transfer fee and recommended Zelle as an alternative option for future transfers. The complaint is resolved, with the wire fee fully offset by reward points and clear guidance provided for future money movements.
