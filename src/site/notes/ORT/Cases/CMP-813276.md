## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration and strongly objected to having their debit card replaced, stating they did not authorize a replacement. They questioned SoFi's processes and emphasized that customer authorization and preferences should be respected.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong objections and frustration regarding the reissuance of their debit card without explicit customer authorization. The specialist acknowledged the member's concerns about customer preferences being respected and clarified the necessity of the action, explaining that standard security protocols require the immediate cancellation and replacement of any debit card involved in unauthorized activity to prevent further compromise. Confirming that the new debit card had been processed, the specialist informed the member that the updated card details were available in the mobile app for immediate digital use while awaiting the delivery of the physical card.
