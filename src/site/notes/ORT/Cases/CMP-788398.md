## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding a recurring deposit of $500.00 that was processed on their Robo Investing account. The member is frustrated by the inconvenience and administrative delay of having to manually initiate a withdrawal and wait for the funds to settle before they can be transferred back to their external bank account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on their frustration regarding a recurring deposit of $500.00 that was processed on their Robo Investing account, which forced them to manually initiate a withdrawal and wait for the funds to settle before transferring them back to their external bank. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's dissatisfaction regarding the administrative delay and their concern that an automatic transfer had not been canceled as previously requested. Delivering a clear resolution, the agent confirmed that the recurring deposits have now been successfully canceled to prevent future occurrences. To apologize for the inconvenience and compensate the member for the frustrating transferring process, the agent credited 2,000 promotional points to the member's profile.
