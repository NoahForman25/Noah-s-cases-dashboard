## Complaint
- The member expressed high frustration regarding an ongoing restriction on their account, demanding it be lifted immediately due to an urgent medical need, and threatened to switch to Bank of America and pursue a lawsuit if it is not resolved. The member used profanity, stated they are contacting FOX News and emailing CNN to publicize the issue, and expressed dissatisfaction that SoFi failed to deliver their debit card, leaving them dependent on their phone for transactions. Additionally, the member demanded an immediate transfer to a supervisor and stated they would record the call.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding an ongoing account restriction, debit card delivery failures, and technical difficulties with Zelle. Operational review confirmed that the account hold was instituted pending security clearance, which was finalized upon completion of the member security team's review. To resolve the complaint, the specialist confirmed that the restriction was officially removed, restored full profile functionality, and provided a direct phone number to collaboratively troubleshoot and resolve the remaining Zelle issues. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's account returned to active, unrestricted status and direct outreach arranged to finalize Zelle configuration.
