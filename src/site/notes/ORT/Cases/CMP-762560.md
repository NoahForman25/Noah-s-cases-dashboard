## Complaint
- Member contacted to express strong frustration and dissatisfaction regarding a $400 direct deposit promotional bonus offer. The member stated they opened their account based on a SoFi advertisement promising a $400 bonus for maintaining direct deposits over a six-month period. However, upon opening the account, they found that the active promotional terms required $5,000 in direct deposits within a 25-day window—a threshold they are unable to meet. The member reported being unable to locate the original promotion they signed up under and expressed feeling misled by the institution, explicitly describing the marketing tactics as a "bait and switch" and a "scam."
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong frustration and dissatisfaction regarding a $400 direct deposit promotional bonus, noting the member's feeling of being misled by what they described as a "bait and switch" and a "scam." The specialist conducted a comprehensive review of historical and current offers and delivered a clear resolution, confirming that the institution has not offered a $400 bonus tied to a six-month timeline, clarifying instead that the separate Annual Percentage Yield (APY) promotion utilizes the six-month framework. The member was informed of the correct active requirements for the $400 direct deposit bonus, which mandates receiving $5,000 or more in qualifying direct deposits within a 25-day evaluation window.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
- Reason for Call SoFi called the member to discuss their account. Key Events The agent introduced themselves and asked to speak to the member. The member requested a call back after four thirty due to being at work. The agent offered to send a detailed email instead, which the member accepted.
### Second Contact
- Dear Jennifer,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. Thank you for reaching out regarding your concerns with the $400 direct deposit promotional bonus. We completely understand how frustrating it is to feel misled by promotional terms, and we appreciate the opportunity to provide clarity on this matter today.

To ensure your concerns were thoroughly addressed, a comprehensive review of our historical and current promotional offers was conducted. We can confirm that SoFi has not offered a $400 direct deposit welcome bonus based on maintaining direct deposits over a six-month period. The current $400 direct deposit promotion requires receiving $5,000 or more in qualifying direct deposits within a 25-day evaluation period or $1,000 for a $50 payout.

We recognize that the differing terms caused a sense of a "bait and switch," which is certainly never our intention. To help clarify the timeline you mentioned, we do offer an Annual Percentage Yield (APY) promotion that extends over a six-month period. It is highly likely that the details of the APY promotion and the direct deposit bonus were inadvertently combined, leading to the confusion regarding the requirements.

To recap, the $400 direct deposit bonus requires $5,000 in deposits within a 25-day window, and the six-month timeframe applies to our separate APY promotion. Please let us know if you have any further questions at all—we are here to help.
