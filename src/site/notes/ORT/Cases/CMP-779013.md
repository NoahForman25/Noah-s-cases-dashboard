## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding a Bill Pay transaction that was reprocessed without prior notification from the institution. It was explained to the member that because the initial Bill Pay was canceled after the funds had already been deducted from the account, a manual refund had to be processed. The member was further advised that the reprocessed Bill Pay will take 8 to 10 business days to be received by the payee. Dissatisfied with this timeline and the lack of communication, the member requested an immediate escalation to be connected with a manager.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a bill pay transaction claim, addressing the member's dissatisfaction regarding a payment that was reprocessed without prior notification and their subsequent request to speak with a manager. The agent addressed the member's confusion regarding the delivery timeline and stale checks. Delivering a clear update, the agent explained that because the initial bill payment was canceled after the funds had already been deducted, a manual refund was required, resulting in the check being reversed. The agent advised the member that they would need to resubmit the payment and clarified that the reprocessed bill pay would take 8 to 10 business days to be received by the payee.
