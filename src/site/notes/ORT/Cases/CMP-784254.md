## Complaint
- Member contacted to express intense dissatisfaction and frustration regarding the handling of their dispute claims. The member stated that the institution waited until the final, tenth business day to request additional information, only to ultimately deny the claim. Expressing deep exasperation, the member stated, "I've emailed you guys back and forth" and "this is ridiculous," accusing the institution of intentionally giving them a hard time. Due to their extreme dissatisfaction with the resolution process and the communication timeline, the member threatened legal action, stating their intent to take the matter to court.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address their intense dissatisfaction and legal threats regarding the denial and overall handling of their recent dispute claims. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's deep exasperation concerning the communication timeline, specifically their complaint that the institution waited until the tenth business day to request information before ultimately denying the claim. Deliver a clear next step, the agent explained the formal appeal process for the dispute and attempted to navigate the resolution. However, the member expressed that they were closing their account, confirmed they had already redirected their direct deposits, and abruptly ended the call before further assistance could be provided.
