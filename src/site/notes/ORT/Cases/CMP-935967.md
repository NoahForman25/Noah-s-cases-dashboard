## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding blocked access to funds and delays in resolving their ongoing issue. Operational review confirmed that access was temporarily delayed while back-office teams finalized the investigation into their open dispute claim. To resolve the complaint, the specialist successfully finalized the dispute investigation on the customer's behalf and initiated the official creation of a new, fully accessible account to restore full banking capabilities. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute process successfully concluded and the setup of the customer's new account actively underway.
