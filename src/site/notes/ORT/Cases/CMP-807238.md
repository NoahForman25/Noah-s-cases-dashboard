## Complaint
- The member expressed concern regarding a password change message received on her profile and stated she intends to file a police report. The agent advised the member not to click any links contained within the message.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding unauthorized activity on their account. After authenticating the member, the agent discussed recent account activity, during which the member explained they had to change their email due to a scam and verified two transactions of $74.00 and $30.00 that they completed themselves. Addressing further safety and fraud concerns, the agent inquired about disputing additional charges after the member identified a suspicious $289.06 transaction, noted they received a password change alert on their profile, and stated they were filing a police report and contacting Spectrum's fraud team—who successfully canceled the scam-related order. The agent advised the member not to click any links within the password alert message and confirmed the next steps for securing their profile.
