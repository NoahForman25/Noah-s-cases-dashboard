## Complaint
- Member contacted to express significant dissatisfaction and urgency regarding a restriction placed on their account. Although the member successfully completed the required security questionnaire and identity verification (IDV) process, they were advised that the account must remain under review for security purposes for up to one business day. The member stated they could not accommodate this timeframe due to an immediate need to pay rent, and they requested an immediate escalation. A supervisor callback has been formally requested and logged.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their significant dissatisfaction and urgency regarding an account restriction that prevented them from paying rent, following up on their logged supervisor callback request. The specialist delivered a definitive resolution, confirming that although the member had originally been advised of a one-business-day security review timeframe after completing their identity verification and security questionnaire, the comprehensive review is now complete. The specialist verified that all restrictions have been successfully removed, the account is fully active, and the funds are available for immediate use to satisfy their urgent financial needs.
