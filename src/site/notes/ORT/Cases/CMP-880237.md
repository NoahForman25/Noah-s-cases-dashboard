## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding their loan application denial, which was cited as an inability to verify their income. The member stated that they were not given a comprehensive explanation for the decline and emphasized that they would have gladly submitted additional income documentation had they been asked. Additionally, the member requested an escalation to speak with a supervisor to discuss the decision further. Support acknowledged the member's frustration regarding the lack of transparency, clarified standard income verification requirements and adverse action notification policies, and submitted a supervisor call-back request alongside an escalation to Lending Operations to review whether the application can be re-evaluated with supplemental income documents.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her frustration regarding the denial of her loan application due to an inability to verify income. The member had previously expressed dissatisfaction over the lack of a comprehensive explanation and indicated that she would have gladly provided supplemental income documentation had support requested it, while requesting a supervisor escalation to discuss re-evaluating the decision. The specialist apologized for the experience and clarified standard underwriting procedures, explaining that documented income—such as pay stubs, W-2s, or tax returns—must fully substantiate the earnings reported on the application to meet approval criteria. The specialist confirmed that a supervisor callback request was submitted alongside an escalation ticket to Lending Operations to review whether her application can be re-evaluated with additional income documentation, assuring her that a leadership representative will reach out directly to discuss the decision.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
- Dear Makayla,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. We received your complaint regarding your loan application denial and the concerns you raised about the income verification process. We acknowledge your frustration with the lack of a comprehensive explanation for the decline and your indication that you would have provided additional income documentation if you had been asked.

We apologize for the experience you encountered during this process.

Regarding income verification, standard requirements call for documentation that confirms the income reported on your application. This may include recent pay stubs, tax returns, W-2s, or other records that substantiate your earnings. When income cannot be verified with the information initially provided, the application may be declined, and an adverse action notification is issued outlining the basis for the decision.

We have submitted a request for a supervisor call-back and escalated your application to Lending Operations to review whether it can be re-evaluated with supplemental income documents. A supervisor or a representative from Lending Operations will follow up with you to discuss the decision further.

We appreciate the opportunity to assist you. If you have any additional questions, please do not hesitate to reach out.
