## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction that SoFi blocked their funds and placed a restriction on their SoFi Checking and Savings Account, calling the situation "very ridiculous." The member was frustrated that the investigation has taken too long without a resolution, stating SoFi has had sufficient time to complete the investigation and issue their check. Additionally, the member refused to go through PNC Bank to retrieve the money and requested that SoFi return the funds directly.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's complaint regarding prolonged account restrictions and held funds within their checking and savings accounts. Operational review determined that the funds had been subjected to an extended security investigation hold that delayed the return transfer back to the originating institution. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, confirmed that the investigation and transfer processing had successfully concluded with the funds credited back to their external bank account, and issued an apology for the processing delay. The complaint is resolved, with the funds successfully transferred to the customer's bank account and the transaction processing finalized.
