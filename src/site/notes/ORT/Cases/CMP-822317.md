## Complaint
- The member contacted support regarding an extended security hold on a mobile check deposit, expressing severe frustration after receiving conflicting emails with shifting release dates that were not met, leaving them without access to vital funds. The Account Manager reviewed the deposit parameters, clarified the risk triggers causing the prolonged hold, and advised that the current target release date is **July 31, 2026**. During the interaction, the member alleged that SoFi is intentionally withholding their funds and stated an intention to pursue legal action. Due to the explicit legal threats, active hardship, and escalating risk profile across multiple unresolved issues, an escalated high-priority complaint was filed for immediate executive operational review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member to follow up on the extended security hold affecting their mobile check deposit and address their severe frustration regarding conflicting release dates and restricted funds. The agent authenticated the member, acknowledged the delayed timeline—reaffirming the current target release date of July 31, 2026—and explained proper check endorsement protocols to help prevent similar hold delays on future deposits. During the interaction, the member reiterated that SoFi was intentionally withholding their money, stated plans to close their account once the funds are released, and expressed an intention to pursue legal action. Consequently, due to the explicit legal threats, financial hardship, and escalating risk profile, an escalated high-priority complaint was submitted for executive operational review.
