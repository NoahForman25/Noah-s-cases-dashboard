## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding an extended delay in receiving an email from the Internal Review Team detailing the reasons for a dispute denial. Although the agent educated the member on the standard dispute process, the member remained unsatisfied and requested an immediate escalation to a supervisor for further review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and email to follow up on their dissatisfaction regarding an extended delay in receiving the official email detailing their dispute denial reasoning, which had previously prompted a managerial escalation request. During the phone interaction, the agent authenticated the member and successfully submitted an expedited ticket to deliver the denial paperwork within two to three business days, while providing a summary of the denial response via email. Following these communications, the specialist sent a final email notification confirming that because the required supporting documentation for the appeal had not yet been received, the current complaint ticket was closed. The member was advised that the appeal remains eligible for review and that they can contact the support team directly at 855-456-7634 to submit their evidence whenever they are ready to proceed.
