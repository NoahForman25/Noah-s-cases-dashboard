## Complaint
- The member, a SoFi Plus member, expressed dissatisfaction regarding a promotional tracking issue for two banking referrals made on September 30th. Although the terms page stated a 25-day funding window from account creation, a master calendar cutoff ended the promotion at midnight, resulting in a zero-hour funding window for the referees. The following morning, an identical-payout promotion was launched that reduced the ACH requirement from $500 to $50. Because the referees are actively funding within the original 25-day window, the member is requesting that a supervisor open a formal manual review ticket with the marketing operations team to override the expired tracking code and link the referrals to the active campaign.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding conflicting registration window timelines and referral reward eligibility. Operational review confirmed that the referral did not qualify under standard promotional terms because the customer's profile had previously fulfilled a Crypto Referral reward, and program policy limits accounts to a single referral bonus. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided complete transparency regarding referral eligibility guidelines and credited reward points to the customer's account as a gesture of goodwill for their participation. The complaint is resolved, with the courtesy reward points successfully applied to the customer's profile and the account remaining in good standing.
