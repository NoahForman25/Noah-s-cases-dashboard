## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a check rejection on their account for the amount of $22,000.
## Resolution
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a check rejection on their account for the amount of $22,000. SoFi called the member to follow up on the rejected check and verify the account status. After authenticating the member via name and date of birth, the agent informed them that the check was rejected due to a lack of endorsement. The member inquired about the status of a deposit and trading cryptocurrency, as well as expressing confusion about using brokerage funds for trading. The agent confirmed the deposit was pending, provided guidance on future deposits, clarified that brokerage funds could be used for trading, and assisted the member with verifying available funds.
