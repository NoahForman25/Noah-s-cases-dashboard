## Complaint
- Member expressed dissatisfaction with not being able to access funds due to a SoFi debit card system issue.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding a declined $500.00 Coinbase transaction and subsequent authorization hold on their debit account. Operational review confirmed that a debit card system error caused the decline while simultaneously placing a temporary processing hold on the funds. To resolve the complaint, the specialist submitted an expedited hold removal request to the back-office team, prioritized the ticket for urgent review, and provided an updated resolution timeline indicating the hold is expected to clear before October 7. The complaint is resolved, with the hold removal actively pending back-office processing to restore full access to the customer's funds.
