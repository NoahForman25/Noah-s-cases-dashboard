## Complaint
- The member complained that SoFi representatives misled her regarding a denied dispute (case #38097244). She stated that on September 29, 2026, she was told an appeal would be filed, but it was never submitted. Additionally, she reported that promised callbacks within 24–48 hours never occurred despite repeated calls since September 9, 2026, and that she was incorrectly told a pending scam payment could be canceled before later learning it could not be. The member requested that SoFi review call recordings from September 9 to September 11, 2026. On October 27, 2026, the appeal was filed with new supporting documents consisting of PayPal conversation screenshots and forwarded to the back office.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding a delayed dispute appeal (case #38097244), unfulfilled callbacks, and misleading guidance concerning the cancellation of a pending scam transaction. Operational review verified that prior commitments to appeal the denied dispute had not been executed, and the necessary supporting evidence required formal re-submission for back-office evaluation. To resolve the complaint, the specialist apologized for the communication lapses and service delays, gathered new supporting evidence including PayPal conversation screenshots, and formally filed and routed the appeal to the back office for active investigation. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute appeal officially under review and the customer's account pending determination from the back office without requiring further customer action.
