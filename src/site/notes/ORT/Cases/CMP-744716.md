## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding their inability to immediately close their account and open a new one due to an active account restriction. The member expressed severe frustration after being informed they must wait an additional 24 hours, noting that they have already completed multiple identity verifications over consecutive days. The member characterized the prolonged process as exhausting and ridiculous
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on their severe frustration regarding an active account restriction that prevented them from immediately closing their account and opening a new one. After completing authentication, the agent addressed the member's exhaustion over undergoing multiple identity verifications over consecutive days only to face an additional 24-hour wait time. The specialist clarified the account's actual status, explaining that the old account closed. Furthermore, the agent addressed the member's concern regarding their paycheck being sent to this restricted account, informing them that funds cannot remain in a closing account and advising the member to contact their employer directly to reroute the funds.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
- Reason for Call SoFi called the member to follow up on an inquiry regarding the status of their account closure. Key Events The agent authenticated the member. The member expressed confusion about their account status, believing it was closed. The agent clarified that the old account was still open and could not be closed due to negative balance and pending transactions. The member explained that their paycheck was sent to the old account, which they no longer had access to. The agent confirmed that the old account was closed and that funds could not remain in a closed account, suggesting the member contact their employer regarding the paycheck.
