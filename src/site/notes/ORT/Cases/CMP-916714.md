## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding an unconfigured account buffer setting and the chat interface experience. The member complained that the system was approving out-of-network authorizations rather than blocking charges when funds were insufficient, requesting that SoFi stop paying transactions if money is unavailable and asking how to disable the reserve. Additionally, the member voiced frustration with repetitive typos and grammar in the agent's messages. Dissatisfied with the chat's progress and conversational pacing, the member requested to speak with an agent by phone and exited the chat window.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding unexpected out-of-network charge approvals and poor chat communication. Operational review confirmed that the transaction approvals were driven by the account's active Spending Reserve setting, which automatically covers transactions using vault balances when main account funds are insufficient, rather than a technical glitch. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided step-by-step instructions for disabling the Spending Reserve feature directly within the mobile application settings to ensure future charges are declined when funds are unavailable. The complaint is resolved, with the Spending Reserve mechanics clarified and clear self-service steps delivered to prevent future overdraft approvals.

We acknowledge your complaint regarding the unconfigured account buffer setting that resulted in out-of-network authorizations being approved rather than blocked when funds were insufficient. We confirmed that this behavior is tied to the Spending Reserve feature, which allows the account to cover transactions when funds are insufficient from your vault. A technical ticket was reviewed and closed, as this behavior is a feature of the account settings rather than a system error.

To disable the Spending Reserve and prevent transactions from being approved when funds are unavailable, the following steps can be taken:

1. Log in to the SoFi app.
2. Select Money/Banking.
3. Open your Samsung Money by SoFi account.
4. Tap the gear icon in the upper-right corner.
5. Select Vaults.
6. Toggle Spending Reserve off.

Once the Spending Reserve is turned off, out-of-network authorizations will no longer be approved when funds are insufficient in your Samsung account.
