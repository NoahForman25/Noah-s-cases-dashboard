## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction after a debit card dispute for three unauthorized transactions totaling $167.87 was denied. The agent explained the dispute was denied because a PIN was used during the transactions and the card was repeatedly frozen and unfrozen. The agent clarified that the dispute could only be appealed if new documentation, such as a police report, was provided proving the card was lost or stolen. The member noted they froze the account on previous agent advice to prevent further unauthorized activity, confirmed their replacement card was on the way, and was reassured that their upcoming direct deposit would not be impacted.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist reached out to the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the denial of their debit card dispute for three transactions totaling $167.87. Operational review confirmed that the original denial remains in effect because the transactions involved personal identification number (PIN) entry alongside repeated card freezing and unfreezing, which indicated authorized account access. To resolve the complaint, the specialist clearly explained the claim determination and outlined the formal appeal process, advising that the dispute can only be reopened upon receipt of new supporting documentation, such as an official police report. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute remaining closed under its original decision unless new police documentation is provided for re-evaluation.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. We are following up regarding the denial of your debit card dispute for the three transactions totaling $167.87. We acknowledge your dissatisfaction with the outcome and the circumstances that led to the denial.

The original denial remains in effect. The dispute was denied because a personal identification number was used during the transactions and the card was repeatedly frozen and unfrozen, which indicated authorized use. Unless new information is provided for review, the case will remain closed.

If you submit new documentation, such as a police report demonstrating that the card was lost or stolen, the dispute team will review the appeal. If the appeal is reviewed and denied, you will be notified of the decision and the ticket will be resolved. If no new information is provided, no further review will occur.

If provisional credit was issued during the dispute process, it may be reversed in accordance with the applicable timeline following the denial. We are unable to reverse the denial or change the outcome without new supporting documentation.
