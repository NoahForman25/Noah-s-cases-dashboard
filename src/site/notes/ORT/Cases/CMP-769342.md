## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe frustration regarding an active restriction on their account, emphasizing an urgent need to access their funds to pay their mortgage and other bills. The member strongly objected to an interaction with a previous representative, feeling targeted or "marked" by the statement that the account closure would be processed if they stopped making purchases on the card. Consequently, the member requested to escalate the call, asking to speak directly with someone who has the authority to immediately release their funds.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone call and left a voicemail. Then emailed the member to address their severe frustration regarding an active account restriction, an urgent need to access funds for a mortgage and bills, and a previous interaction where they felt targeted by a representative's statements, which prompted an immediate escalation request. The specialist conducted a comprehensive review of the account and delivered a final resolution, confirming that a permanent business decision was made to close the checking and savings account due to activity inconsistent with the Deposit Account Agreement. Furthermore, the member was informed that a check for their remaining balance was officially issued on July 3, 2026, and is expected to arrive via standard mail delivery within 7 to 10 business days, between July 14, 2026, and July 17, 2026.
