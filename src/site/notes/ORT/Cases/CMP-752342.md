## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction with the mobile application layout, alleging that SoFi is intentionally designing the user interface to hide important account information.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone to follow up on an inquiry regarding issues with the cost basis on their account transfers, which coincided with the member's broader dissatisfaction regarding the mobile application layout and their allegation that the user interface is intentionally designed to hide important account information. After completing identity verification, the member confirmed they had reviewed the transfer details previously provided but expressed ongoing confusion regarding their margin balance, requesting a detailed breakdown of the figures. Because a comprehensive margin analysis required advanced support, the agent advised that a transfer to a specialized team member was necessary; the member acknowledged the explanation and agreed to call back at a later time to address their remaining account questions. Main complaint around seeing Margin balance has been addressed and member is satisfied.
