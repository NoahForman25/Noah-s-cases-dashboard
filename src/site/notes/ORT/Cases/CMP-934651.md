## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an indefinite timeline for their account closure and the return of their funds, stating that the delay is unacceptable and that SoFi is tying up their money.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding the timeline for account closure and the return of their remaining funds. Operational review confirmed that the account closure process was completed and the remaining balance was converted into a physical check for disbursement in accordance with standard offboarding procedures. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, provided direct clarity on the account status, and confirmed that the final balance check had been issued and mailed to their address on file. The complaint is resolved, with the account fully closed and the remaining funds in transit to the customer via check.
