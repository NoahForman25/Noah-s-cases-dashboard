## Complaint
- The member submitted a complaint, expressing significant aggravation and threatening to involve law enforcement. The member stated they plan to leave SoFi once the issue is resolved because an on-hold direct payment of -$155.15 to Kraken on November 2, 2026, was processed without their authorization. Additionally, the member expressed frustration that SoFi failed to send a notification when the transaction occurred.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an unauthorized $155.15 direct payment to Kraken, the lack of transaction notifications, and his stated intent to leave the institution. Operational review confirmed that an internal investigation resulted in the decision to permanently close the account and formally deny the dispute filed for the $155.15 transaction. To resolve the complaint, the specialist conducted outreach to confirm the customer was fully informed of the dispute denial and closure outcomes, while offering direct assistance for any outstanding questions from prior interactions. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute formally denied and the customer's account remaining permanently closed following internal review.
