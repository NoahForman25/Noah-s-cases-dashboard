## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a negative credit report from TransUnion related to a Smart Card account opening. The member claimed they did not apply for the Smart Card.
## Resolution
- SoFi contacted the member to follow up on a recent inquiry regarding a Smart Card opened in their name. The agent authenticated the member and explained that the Member Security Team found no signs of account takeover. The member expressed confusion and frustration regarding a decrease in their TransUnion credit score and a minimum payment due, maintaining that they did not apply for the Smart Card. The agent clarified that the Smart Card is a credit charge account that reports to credit bureaus—which can impact credit scores—and explained how using the card could help build credit over time.
