## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding the restriction and closure of their account, which left them unable to access $148.34 — their only remaining funds. The member explained that they are on FMLA leave, disabled, and urgently need access to their funds for food, drink, gas, and medication, but were told they must wait 7 to 10 business days to receive a check by mail. The member requested that the account be reopened or the funds sent electronically, and noted that the closure followed previously denied Regulation E debit card disputes for unauthorized charges they stated were made by someone else. The supervisor explained that the account closure decision is final under SoFi's terms and conditions, the balance has been cleared, and a check has already been issued, leaving no option to reopen the account, transfer funds via Zelle, or stop payment on the check.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the restriction and closure of their account, which restricted access to their remaining $148.34 balance needed for essential living expenses. Operational review confirmed that the account closure decision was executed in compliance with account terms and conditions following prior dispute activity, and confirmed that internal policies prohibit disclosing specific operational closure details. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the remaining $148.34 balance was cleared and disbursed via a physical paper check issued on October 9, 2026, which was mailed to the address on file within a standard 7 to 10 business day delivery window. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining permanently closed and the final balance check currently in transit to the customer.
