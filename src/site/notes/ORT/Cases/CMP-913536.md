## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding two declined transactions—a $100.00 charge from FanDuel on September 26, 2026, and a $75.00 charge from DraftKings on September 27, 2026—that still resulted in pending charges on their SoFi account. The member insisted the issue was caused by SoFi's system rather than the merchants and expressed frustration with SoFi, the agent, and the bank's policies, particularly after waiting a long time to dispute the charges. The agent apologized and explained SoFi's dispute policy, noting that pending charges cannot be disputed until they fully post. The agent advised that the pending charges might drop off if uncompleted, suggested contacting the merchants directly to request cancellation, and informed the member they could initiate a dispute through the app or by contacting SoFi if the charges post. The member disagreed with contacting the merchants, maintaining that SoFi was responsible for the error.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding pending debit transactions for DraftKings and PrizePicks following declined merchant attempts. Operational review confirmed that the authorization holds are currently in a pending state, which, per bank dispute policy, cannot be formally disputed until the transactions fully settle and post to the account balance. To resolve the complaint, the specialist explained the settlement process, advised that pending authorizations typically drop off automatically if uncaptured by the merchant, and outlined how to file a formal dispute through the app or support team if the charges post. The complaint is resolved, with the transactions remaining in pending status and clear instructions provided for initiating a dispute upon final posting.
