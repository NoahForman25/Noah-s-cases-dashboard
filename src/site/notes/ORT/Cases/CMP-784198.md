## Complaint
- Member contacted to express strong dissatisfaction regarding the closure of their account and the withholding of their remaining balance. The member asserted that the funds rightfully belonged to them, disputed the closure decision as incorrect, and expressed frustration that the money was not returned to their external bank. Additionally, the member criticized the internal investigation as flawed and expressed frustration over the lack of a clear explanation regarding the disposition of their funds.

A review of the account confirmed that it was permanently closed and the remaining balance was confiscated following an internal review. In alignment with established policy, the member was advised that no funds would be returned and that specific details regarding the investigation, the closure decision, or the disposition of the funds could not be disclosed.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding the permanent closure of their account and the confiscation of their remaining balance. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's strong dissatisfaction with the internal investigation, their assertion that the funds rightfully belonged to them, and their frustration over the lack of a clear explanation regarding the disposition of the money. Delving into the institutional stance, the agent informed the member that the decision to close the account was final, and in alignment with established policy, re-advised them that no funds would be returned to their external bank. The member shared deep feelings of betrayal, noting they were focusing on gathering evidence against the person responsible for the underlying issues and planning to visit Capital One to handle related account problems; the agent validated their concerns, apologized for the outcome, and concluded the call.
