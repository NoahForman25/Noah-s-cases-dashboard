## Complaint
- The member called to inquire about a restricted account and a previously stopped wire transfer, expressing strong frustration with having to complete the same verification process repeatedly. The member complained that their account is frequently blocked, stated that no one has adequately explained why the funds are held or why the restriction was applied, and demanded the immediate release of their funds. During the call, the member expressed difficulty understanding the representative, criticized the agent's English proficiency, and voiced overall dissatisfaction with SoFi's customer service. After requesting a manager to release the funds and being informed that no manager was currently available, the member stated they would continue calling repeatedly until the issue was resolved. Support reiterated the mandatory nature of the security verification process for wire transfers and documented the account restriction and customer service complaint for managerial follow-up.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong frustration regarding an account restriction, a previously stopped wire transfer, and repeated identity verification requirements. The member had expressed extreme dissatisfaction with frequent account blocks, a lack of clear explanation regarding held funds, and overall service quality, demanding an immediate fund release and requesting a manager escalation after experiencing communication difficulties during a prior call. The specialist apologized for the difficulties encountered throughout the wire transfer and verification process, explaining that security checks are mandatory protocols designed to protect accounts and ensure transaction integrity. Additionally, the specialist confirmed that the wire transfer review has been finalized and the funds have been officially released and made accessible within the account.
