## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi on September 29, 2026, to report potential unauthorized trade activity on their account and requested immediate assistance. Although connected to an Investment Specialist, the member did not respond during the interaction and has not provided further details or follow-up since the initial contact. The member has since withdrawn all funds from their account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding potential unauthorized trade activity reported on September 29, 2026. Operational review confirmed that customer support attempted to transfer the customer to an Investment Specialist to investigate the claim, but the inquiry could not be processed because the customer disconnected without providing transaction details and subsequently withdrew all remaining funds from the account. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct communication explaining why the initial investigation could not proceed and established an open channel for the customer to submit trade specifics should they choose to pursue a formal review. The complaint is resolved, with the account fully liquidated by the customer and the claim closed pending any additional details provided for investigation.
