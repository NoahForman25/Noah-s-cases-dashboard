## Complaint
- The member requested to have the wire fee voided. They were informed that the fee could not be waived, which caused frustration.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone but reached there voicemail. They then left an email to address their frustration regarding a request to have a wire transfer fee voided. The specialist clarified that the wire transfer fee is a standard, required charge for processing this type of transaction, and confirmed that the fee cannot be reversed or waived and will remain on the account.
## Case Intake & Investigation

### First contact
- The resolution specialist attempted to contact the member regarding their complaint. The specialist received the member's voicemail and left a message. The specialist will send a detailed email.
### Second Contact
- Hi Mark,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist, reaching out regarding your recent wire transfer inquiry. We completely understand how important it is to have clarity on account charges, and we are happy to review this for you today.

You shared concerns regarding the fee that would be applied to your wire transfer and requested to have it voided. To provide full transparency on our fee structure, the wire transfer fee is a standard, required charge for processing this type of transaction. Because this is a required fee for completing the transfer, it remains on the account and cannot be reversed or waived.

To recap, we have reviewed the wire transfer charge, and the standard processing fee will remain in place. Please let us know if you have any further questions at all—we are here to help.
