## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding repeated fraud alerts and delays in receiving their new debit card. The agent advised the member to respond directly to suspicious activity alert notifications confirming whether or not transactions are recognized, so appropriate action can be taken to protect the account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding recurring fraud alerts and delays in receiving a replacement debit card. Operational review confirmed that automated security monitoring repeatedly triggered fraud alerts due to frequent freezing and unfreezing of the card, which remains in active use, and verified that digital card capabilities remain fully functional. To resolve the complaint, the specialist instructed the customer to respond directly to automated fraud notifications to confirm legitimate transactions and validated that their digital card is operating without issue while the physical replacement processes. The complaint is resolved, with clear account monitoring instructions provided to ensure ongoing security without unnecessary card interruptions.
