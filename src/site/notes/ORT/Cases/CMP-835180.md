## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction after their account was restricted following flagged activity, learning that the security hold will require at least **1 business day** to resolve. The member asserted that they recognize and authorize the specific transactions that triggered the risk alert, complaining that the restriction is completely unnecessary and leaving them without access to funds for the upcoming weekend. Support explained that automated fraud monitoring protocols require mandatory operational review to protect account security once a high-risk trigger occurs, and logged an urgent account restriction review request to expedite security clearing before the weekend.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to address an account restriction placed following flagged security activity and to apologize for the resulting disruption. After authenticating the member, the agent discussed their strong dissatisfaction over the security hold, which required at least one business day to resolve and left them without access to funds for the upcoming weekend. The member emphasized that they recognized and authorized the flagged transactions, noting that the restriction had caused two external payments to bounce. The agent acknowledged the issue, apologized for the inconvenience, and explained that automated fraud monitoring protocols require mandatory operational review to ensure account security whenever high-risk triggers occur. To compensate the member for the trouble caused, the agent offered 1,500 rewards points and confirmed that an urgent account restriction review request had been submitted to expedite security clearing.
