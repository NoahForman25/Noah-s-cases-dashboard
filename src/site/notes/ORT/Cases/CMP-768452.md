## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction regarding a wire transfer request that was denied by the internal review team, emphasizing that the transaction was explicitly denied rather than canceled and voicing intense frustration when questioned about its intent, which they perceived as a direct invasion of their financial privacy. The member stated they maintain a balance of over two million dollars with the institution and have never been required to justify how they choose to spend their own money, further explaining that the wire was intended as earnest money for a home purchase—serving as a good faith deposit to secure the property—and noting they had already completed the required documentation indicating the purpose of the transfer. Consequently, the member issued a strong warning that if the funds are not promptly transferred to Murray Bank, they will liquidate their entire balance and close their account permanently.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding a denied wire transfer, addressing the member's severe dissatisfaction with the internal review team's decision, perceived invasion of financial privacy, and threat to liquidate their entire balance of over two million dollars and close their account permanently. After completing identity verification, the agent reviewed the transaction history and addressed the member's clarification that the $1,200 transfer was intended as earnest money for a home purchase to secure a property at Murray Bank. Providing an immediate resolution to the transfer delay, the agent added the recipient friend to the call and identified that the pending hold and notification issue were caused by an outdated phone number linked to the friend's account, allowing the agent to clarify the required steps for the recipient to successfully accept the funds.
