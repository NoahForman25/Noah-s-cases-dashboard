## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding the closure of their account, stating they do not understand the reason behind the decision. The member emphasized that they urgently need access to their funds, noting that their child's birthday is this coming Friday and the account restriction is directly impacting their ability to prepare for it.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address their dissatisfaction regarding the closure of their account, noting the member's confusion over the decision and their urgent need to access funds to prepare for their child's upcoming birthday. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the restriction and delivered an update, explaining that the account is currently undergoing a comprehensive review by the security team. The member was informed that no further specific updates or fund releases are available at the moment while the security team completes their investigation.
