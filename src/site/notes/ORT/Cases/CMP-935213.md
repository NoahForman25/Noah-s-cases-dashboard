## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding the denial of a $221.95 dispute for an unauthorized Amazon Marketplace charge posted on July 11, 2026. The member stated that they only maintain one Amazon account, did not authorize the transaction, and believed their debit card information was compromised. The agent filed an appeal on the member's behalf and sent a secure document request email. The member submitted screenshots of their Amazon order history to serve as supporting evidence for the appeal, and the agent attached the documentation to the claim ticket for back-office review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the initial denial of a $221.95 dispute for an unauthorized Amazon Marketplace charge posted on July 11, 2026. Operational review confirmed that while the initial claim was denied, the submission of new order history evidence qualified the transaction for a formal appeal review. To resolve the complaint, the specialist submitted an appeal on the customer's behalf and attached the provided Amazon order history screenshots to the claim ticket for back-office evaluation. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute actively undergoing appeal review and official updates scheduled to be delivered via email once the evaluation is complete.
