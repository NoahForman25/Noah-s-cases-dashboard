## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding fluctuations in their mobile check deposit limits. The member stated they have a $25,000 check that they now have to send back due to their current limits.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding fluctuations in their mobile check deposit limits that prevented them from depositing a $25,000 check. Operational review confirmed that mobile check deposit limits are governed by an automated system that re-evaluates account activity monthly, causing limits to adjust dynamically based on factors like deposit history and overall account standing. To resolve the complaint, the specialist delivered direct transparency regarding the automated nature of these recurring evaluations and explained that deposit thresholds cannot be manually overridden or increased outside of the system process. The complaint is resolved, with the current deposit limits remaining active based on the most recent system evaluation and the customer informed of alternative options for processing the check.
