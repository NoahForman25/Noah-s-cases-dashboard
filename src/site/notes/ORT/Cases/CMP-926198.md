## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding conflicting provisional credit timeframes for a pending 7-Eleven dispute, noting that prior representatives quoted October 6–7 while the app indicates October 9. The member stated that the delay has left their account with a negative balance of -$50, causing financial hardship.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding conflicting provisional credit timeframes for a pending 7-Eleven dispute and the resulting -$50 negative balance. Operational review confirmed that provisional credit eligibility follows standard 10-business-day regulatory guidelines if an investigation remains incomplete, causing minor variance against automated app displays or verbal estimates provided prior to cutoff thresholds. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency on provisional credit timing protocols, clarifying that credits post automatically if the investigation exceeds 10 business days or a final decision is delivered sooner if resolved early. The complaint is resolved, with the 7-Eleven dispute claim actively processing under standard review timelines and clear criteria established for provisional credit issuance.
