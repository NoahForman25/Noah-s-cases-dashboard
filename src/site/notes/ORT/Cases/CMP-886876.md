## Complaint
- The member requested a review of their September 4, 2026 call, where an agent stated an additional application review was submitted and promised a follow-up call. The member never received the callback and is seeking clarification on the status and decline of their application.
## Resolution
- The member called to follow up on a promised callback from a September 4, 2026 call regarding an additional application review. The agent explained that 2024 and 2025 tax documents are required to proceed with the current loan, or the member may reapply with a co-borrower. The member is currently considering the co-borrower option.
