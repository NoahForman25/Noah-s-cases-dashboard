## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an unnotified Miscellaneous Debit of $149.00 posted to their account on November 4, 2026, and requested that a formal complaint be filed along with follow-up communication regarding the issue. The member had previously submitted a dispute on October 13, 2026, for account ending in 1243 and received a provisional credit, with the last communication sent on October 16, 2026, acknowledging additional documents. The dispute case (ZD 8794538) was finalized after the merchant issued a direct refund, making the provisional credit no longer warranted, which led to the batch debit revoking the funds. However, between October 16 and November 4, 2026, the member received no notification via text or email regarding the dispute closure, the merchant's refund, the removal of the provisional credit, or the upcoming debit.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an unnotified $149.00 miscellaneous debit posted to his account following the closure of a dispute against CreditPros. Operational review of dispute case ZD 8794538 confirmed that the merchant had issued a direct refund of $149.00 on October 17, which eliminated the need for the temporary provisional credit previously extended by the institution and triggered a standard debit reversal on November 4. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency explaining that the debit simply offset the duplicative provisional credit after the direct merchant refund, ensuring the customer retained the proper single reimbursement. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute formally closed and the customer's account balanced, open, and in good standing.
