## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction after their loan application was declined due to an inability to verify income. The member stated they provided Schedule C tax documents for tax years 2023 and 2024, but have not yet filed their 2025 tax return, arguing that the tax filing deadline is in October. Support explained that per standard operating procedures for a sole proprietorship, SoFi requires the most recent two years of filed tax returns to verify income. The member alleged that SoFi was "breaking the law" by requesting the 2025 documents and demanded to speak with a manager, noting they had already requested one 15 minutes prior. When support advised that the transfer to a manager was being initiated, the member disconnected the call. Support logged the income verification impasse, documented the member's legal allegations, and submitted an escalation ticket to Lending Operations for a manager review and follow-up.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address his strong dissatisfaction regarding the decline of his personal loan application due to income verification requirements, as well as his frustration over the supervisor transfer process. The member had previously argued that demanding his 2025 tax return was unlawful given the extended October tax filing deadline, emphasizing that he had already submitted Schedule C tax documents for 2023 and 2024 before disconnecting the call while waiting for a manager. The specialist apologized for the difficult experience and addressed the member's legal and procedural concerns. The specialist explained that under standard underwriting guidelines for sole proprietorships, SoFi requires the two most recent filed tax returns—which currently covers tax years 2024 and 2025. Because the 2023 documents fall outside the required two-year lookback window, submission of the 2025 tax return remains a mandatory underwriting requirement to proceed with the application, regardless of IRS filing extensions.
