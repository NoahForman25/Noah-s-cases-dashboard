## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration and requested to speak with a supervisor because they do not have access to the mobile app or their phone to receive a verification code and unfreeze their debit card.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint and escalation request regarding the inability to access the mobile application, receive verification passcodes, and unfreeze their debit card. Operational review determined that restoring access requires an updated phone number on the profile, which strictly mandates identity verification under bank security protocols. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided clear written guidance outlining that a valid state ID is necessary to complete the verification process before contact updates can be applied. The complaint is resolved, with the debit card remaining frozen and profile access pending the customer's submission of a valid state ID to complete identity verification.
