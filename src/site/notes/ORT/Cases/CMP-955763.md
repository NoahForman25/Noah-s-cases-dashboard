## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration, stating that previous agents hung up on her and negatively impacted her life, leaving her stranded with her child. Additionally, the member stated that the agents were supposed to issue 1,700 reward points convertible to $17.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding poor service interactions, disconnected calls, and unissued promotional reward points. Operational review verified the customer's prior attempts to resolve the issue and determined that an adjustment was warranted to fulfill the missing rewards. To resolve the complaint, the specialist issued an apology for the service breakdowns and credited 1,500 reward points directly to her profile, which were made available for immediate redemption. The complaint is resolved, with the reward points successfully deposited into the active account and ready for immediate cash conversion or use.
