## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an account feature lock that prevents them from depositing checks or transferring funds due to a minimum deposit requirement of $500. The member stated they were unaware of this requirement prior to attempting the transaction and did not expect the restriction. Support acknowledged the member's frustration, explained the eligibility criteria and minimum balance requirements associated with Mobile Check Deposit and external transfer features, and submitted an escalation ticket to Deposit Operations to review the account status and evaluate potential feature unlock options.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on a mobile check deposit issue, an unexpected $30.00 returned check fee, and an account feature lock preventing check deposits and external transfers due to a $500.00 minimum deposit requirement. The member expressed confusion regarding a failed scheduled transfer with Citibank and voiced frustration over being unaware of the feature eligibility criteria prior to attempting their transactions. The specialist authenticated the member, explained the eligibility rules and minimum balance requirements associated with Mobile Check Deposit and transfer features, and confirmed that an escalation ticket was submitted to Deposit Operations to evaluate unlocking the account features. Additionally, the specialist apologized for the inconvenience, noted that the transfer issue with Citibank was resolved, and awarded the member 4,500 reward points ($45.00) to cover the returned check fee while walking them through the point redemption process.
