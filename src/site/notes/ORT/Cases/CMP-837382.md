## Complaint
- The non-member expressed strong dissatisfaction after learning that an unauthorized party used her stolen identity to fraudulently open a SoFi account without her knowledge or consent. Confirmed to have no existing relationship with SoFi, she sought urgent clarification on whether her personal funds were compromised and grew deeply frustrated when support could not provide detailed incident specifics or written documentation outlining next steps. Support filed a formal complaint regarding the account opening security gap, escalated the matter to Fraud Operations for immediate account closure and identity theft processing, and committed to issuing formal written confirmation regarding the resolution.
## Resolution
- Closed with no contact due to not leaking sensitive info.
