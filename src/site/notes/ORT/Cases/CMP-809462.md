## Complaint
- The member is expressing dissatisfaction regarding the Direct Deposit (DD) bonus promotion, stating that the terms and conditions are unclear and misleading.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding the Direct Deposit Welcome Bonus promotion and their concerns that the terms and conditions were unclear and misleading. The specialist acknowledged the confusion, apologized for the unhelpful experience, and confirmed that feedback had been submitted to help improve promotional clarity moving forward. Clarifying the promotional criteria, the specialist explained that the bonus is a tiered structure evaluated over a 25-day period starting from the first eligible direct deposit, where total deposits between $1,000.00 and $4,999.99 qualify for a $50.00 bonus, and cumulative deposits of $5,000.00 or more earn a $400.00 bonus, with qualifying payouts deposited within 7 business days following the evaluation period.

### Second Contact
- Dear Anissa,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. Thank you for reaching out regarding the Direct Deposit Welcome Bonus promotion. We acknowledge your dissatisfaction regarding the terms and conditions and your statement that they are unclear and misleading.

A review of the promotional terms was conducted. The Direct Deposit Welcome Bonus is a tiered promotion for SoFi Checking and Savings members who have not previously enrolled in direct deposit with SoFi. To qualify, the first eligible direct deposit must be received during the promotion period. The total amount of qualifying direct deposits received during the 25-day evaluation period determines the payout tier. A total between $1,000.00 and $4,999.99 qualifies for a $50.00 bonus, and a total of $5,000.00 or more qualifies for a $400.00 bonus. Deposits totaling under $1,000.00 do not qualify for a payout. Only one bonus payment is available per member. The applicable bonus is deposited into the Checking and Savings account within 7 business days after the 25-day evaluation period ends.

We are sorry about the confusion behind this promotion and have submitted feedback for providing clearer information regarding our promotions.

To recap, the bonus is determined by the total qualifying direct deposits received during the 25-day evaluation period, with payouts of $50.00 or $400.00 based on the specified tiers. Please let us know if you have any further questions.
