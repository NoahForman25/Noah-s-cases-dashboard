## Complaint
- The member expressed a desire for faster processing timeframes to access funds for trading and to complete withdrawals more quickly.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer to address their complaint regarding account holding periods and restricted trading availability following a $700.00 deposit. Operational review confirmed that deposit holding periods and settlement timelines are governed by standard clearing schedules to ensure secure transaction settlement, which cannot be manually expedited or altered on an individual basis. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding standard deposit availability timeframes and submitted formal customer feedback regarding hold times to internal operations for future platform review. The complaint is resolved, with standard deposit settlement rules maintained for the account and official feedback submitted to the operational team.
