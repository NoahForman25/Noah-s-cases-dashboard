## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding an unresolved **$50.00** discrepancy involving a recurring daily stock purchase (`SCHG`), complaining of being repeatedly transferred between the Banking and Investment teams without a clear resolution. The member explained that on May 08, 2026, a **$50.00** withdrawal posted from their SoFi Savings account intended for SoFi Securities, and a corresponding trade was executed using existing available buying power. However, no record of the $50.00 deposit appeared in either Advisor or Apex clearing systems, leaving the $50.00 missing from their bank account with its disposition unaccounted for. The member voiced extreme frustration over the lack of cross-departmental ownership and stated that if they continue to experience departmental pushback without a resolution, they will escalate the issue to external regulatory bodies. Support filed an official operational complaint and submitted a cross-departmental research ticket between Banking and Investment Operations to track down the missing $50.00 transfer, promising high-priority follow-up to resolve the funds discrepancy.
