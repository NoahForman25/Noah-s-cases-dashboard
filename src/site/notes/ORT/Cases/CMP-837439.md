## Complaint
- The member expressed strong concern regarding misleading information on the website about SoFi Plus membership eligibility, stating their understanding was that establishing a direct deposit into the account would grant them free SoFi Plus status. Support clarified the specific direct deposit requirements and active criteria needed to qualify for complimentary SoFi Plus benefits, and logged the member's feedback regarding website clarity and messaging for product review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on a previous inquiry regarding SoFi Plus membership eligibility and to address confusion caused by misleading information. After authenticating the member, the agent discussed their concerns about website messaging, which led the member to believe that establishing a direct deposit would automatically grant complimentary SoFi Plus status. The agent clarified the specific direct deposit thresholds and active criteria required to qualify for SoFi Plus benefits, and apologized for the confusion. To help rectify the messaging, the agent inquired about where the member encountered the misleading details; while the member did not have the exact source saved, they agreed to share it if located again. The specialist thanked the member for their cooperation and confirmed that their feedback regarding website clarity was logged for internal product review.
