## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding an IRA closure fee assessed against their account following a full balance withdrawal. The member stated that this fee was entirely unexpected and asserted that it was not clearly disclosed to them prior to initiating the withdrawal process.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding an unexpected IRA closure fee assessed after a full balance withdrawal, which the member asserted was not clearly disclosed beforehand. The specialist delivered a clear update, explaining that the fee is a standard assessment applied to full balance withdrawals and cannot be reversed. However, providing a practical resolution to help mitigate the cost, the specialist confirmed that 2,000 rewards points (equivalent to $20.00) have been credited directly to the member's profile.
