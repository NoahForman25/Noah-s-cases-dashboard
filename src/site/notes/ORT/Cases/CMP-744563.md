## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding a previous customer service experience where multiple agents were unable to assist them with updating the phone number on their profile.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on their frustration regarding a previous customer service experience where multiple agents were unable to assist them with updating their profile phone number. After completing identity verification, the agent acknowledged the member's concerns and apologized for the poor experience. The specialist clarified that the previous information provided regarding the update process remains correct, reassured the member that their feedback would be utilized for coaching purposes to improve future agent interactions, and confirmed that a formal coaching request was submitted.
