## Complaint
- Member contacted to report a discrepancy regarding an active "Crypto Welcome Claw" promotion displayed on their in-app dashboard, noting that the offer was still visible despite its official expiration date of May 21, 2026. The member explained that they completed a crypto transaction in good faith, relying entirely on the live dashboard advertisement with the expectation of qualifying for the promotion. Upon subsequently reviewing the promotion's terms and conditions, the member discovered the offer had already expired and expressed concern that displaying an outdated promotion is misleading to consumers. Consequently, the member requested a courtesy credit as remediation, citing that their transaction was made based on the active information presented within the app at the time of purchase.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed the member to address their concern regarding a discrepancy with an expired "Crypto Welcome Claw" promotion that remained visible on their in-app dashboard, which led them to complete a transaction in good faith. The specialist delivered a final resolution, confirming that because the misleading advertisement remained live past its official May 21, 2026 expiration date, a courtesy credit of rewards points was successfully applied to the member's profile by a previous agent. Furthermore, the specialist informed the member that their specific feedback regarding the dashboard display error had been submitted to the technical team to ensure the platform's promotional accuracy is improved moving forward.
