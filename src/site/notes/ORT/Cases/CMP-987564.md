## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with not receiving a promised status update regarding their account restriction, noting they were told they would be contacted by email, text, or phone call but received no communication. The member also voiced frustration over still being unable to access their funds and requested both the reason for the restriction and a timeframe for resolution.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding a missed status update, an ongoing account restriction, and the continued inability to access her funds. Operational review with the member security team confirmed that an active internal security review remains in progress with no predetermined completion date, necessitating that protective account restrictions remain in place until the investigation concludes. To address the member's concerns, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding the status of the review, explained that restrictions cannot be manually lifted prematurely, and assured the customer that the dedicated review team will provide direct notification once a formal determination is reached. The customer's account remains temporarily restricted and under security review, with funds access pending the final outcome of the investigation.
