## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi to address the closure of their joint account, which occurred without prior notification. They reported difficulty communicating with the company and noted that the joint account holder was also impacted. The member expressed frustration about being on hold for an extended period and requested to speak with a security supervisor. They provided both their own contact number and the joint account holder’s contact information.

## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone first but was not able to contact them so they emailed the member to address their frustration regarding the unnotified closure of their joint account, communication difficulties, and extended hold times when attempting to speak with a security supervisor. The specialist apologized for the negative service experience but clarified that the decision to close the joint account is final and cannot be overturned. The member was advised that a separate email containing all disclosable information regarding the decision had been sent, and that no further details could be shared.
