- Member contacted to express severe distress and frustration after being informed that their account is slated for closure following an internal review, with no specific reason provided. The member strongly objected to the timeline for receiving their remaining funds and an active $2,000 dispute, which requires waiting up to 90 days for dispute resolution plus an additional 30 days for paper check delivery. The member emphasized that being denied access to approximately $3,000 to $4,000 for up to 120 days is financially devastating, leaving them unable to cover basic living expenses, pay bills, or meet child support obligations. ## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on an ongoing dispute regarding an unauthorized $2,000 transaction, after completing identity verification, the agent explained that additional documentation was required to reopen the dispute appeal, which the member stated they could not access, while also outlining that any remaining eligible funds would be issued via paper check up to 120 days later due to the required dispute and processing timelines. The member strongly condemned this timeframe as financially devastating—stating it would prevent them from covering basic living expenses, paying bills, and meeting critical child support obligations—and characterized the hold as a hostile withholding of their money. Because the agent clarified that no alternative payout options existed and that the closure decision was final, the highly agitated member rejected the scripted responses, threatened to fly out to a corporate office, and explicitly stated their intent to seek immediate legal counsel, report the situation to regulatory bodies, and publicly escalate the matter across social media. Submitted a Report Member Verbal/Physical Threats Form.

## Notes
- Denial reasons:
	  -  The disputed transactions do not meet the definition of an unauthorized transaction error. Unauthorized electronic funds transactions do not include an EFT initiated with fraudulent intent by the consumer or any person acting in concert with the consumer 
	  - The card is in your possession and there is no other fraudulent activity on the account around the time of the disputed transaction(s).  
	  - The transaction(s) was approved using biometric authentication and/or an established passcode previously set on the registered device.
