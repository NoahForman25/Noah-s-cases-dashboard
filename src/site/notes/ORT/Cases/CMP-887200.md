## Complaint
- The member called to dispute the finalized terms of their refinancing application, stating they intended to increase their loan balance from $91,000.00 to $99,000.00 in order to secure a $7,000.00 cash-out disbursement. The member expressed intense dissatisfaction upon learning that following a hard credit inquiry, the system capped their loan limit at $91,728.20—yielding almost no additional cash out—while simultaneously increasing their interest rate from 14% to 18%. Refusing to accept the revised loan terms that failed to provide the requested capital at a higher rate, the member explicitly demanded an immediate escalation to a supervisor. Support acknowledged the member's strong dissatisfaction regarding the rate increase and reduced cash-out approval, documented the discrepancy between the requested loan amount and final system limits, and submitted an urgent priority escalation ticket to Underwriting Leadership and Credit Risk Operations to review the application decision and arrange a direct supervisor follow-up call.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist followed up with the member via phone call and subsequent email regarding his intense dissatisfaction with the finalized terms of a refinancing application. The member had previously sought to increase his loan balance from $91,000.00 to $99,000.00 for a $7,000.00 cash-out disbursement, but was disappointed when a hard credit pull resulted in the loan being capped at $91,728.20 alongside an interest rate increase from 14% to 18%. Refusing these terms, the member had demanded an escalation to leadership and legal, expressing frustration when informed during the follow-up call that the terms generated post-credit pull could not be adjusted. Following the telephone conversation, the specialist conducted a secondary review of the account records and confirmed that a representative successfully unboarded the recent loan application in accordance with the member's request. The specialist sent a follow-up email confirming the cancellation of the application and clarified that if the member still wishes to seek additional funds or a lower interest rate in the future, he is welcome to submit a new loan application for re-evaluation.
