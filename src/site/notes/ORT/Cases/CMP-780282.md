## Complaint
- Member contacted to express significant dissatisfaction regarding a two-week lack of access to their banking account due to an ongoing restriction. It was explained to the member that the restriction cannot be lifted until the pending dispute investigation is fully completed and new accounts are established; however, the member remained frustrated by the prolonged turnaround time and the continued disruption to their funds.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed the member to address their significant dissatisfaction regarding a two-week lack of access to their banking account due to an ongoing restriction, providing a clear update on the status of their profile and potential disputes. The specialist delivered a clear resolution, confirming that contrary to previous indications that the restriction would remain until a pending investigation concluded, there are currently no restrictions on the account and it is fully accessible. Additionally, the specialist confirmed that three unauthorized debit card charges from [www.1v1me.com](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.1v1me.com) in the amounts of $120.00, $200.00, and $120.00 have officially posted, and advised the member that they can now advance the investigation by filing formal disputes either online through their transaction history or by contacting support directly.
