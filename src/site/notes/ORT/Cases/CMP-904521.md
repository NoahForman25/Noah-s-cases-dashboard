## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration to the AI Chatbot when trying to transfer $25.25 from two unspecified SoFi Active Invest accounts, as they encountered errors attempting to transfer funds to their SoFi Checking account and were unable to link an external bank account. The AI Chatbot indicated the member's SoFi accounts were in a "problem status," causing the transfer rejection. The member expressed severe dissatisfaction, stating they never placed the funds in the Active Invest accounts, and demanded immediate access to their money.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding difficulty transferring funds from their SoFi Active Invest accounts to their SoFi Checking account and problems linking an external bank. Operational review confirmed that the transfer delays stemmed from standard hold requirements, which mandate a 5-business-day waiting period before deposited funds—including redeemed reward points—can be withdrawn or moved to checking. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the hold period had elapsed and successfully completed the $25.25 transfer directly into the customer's checking account. The complaint is resolved, with the funds fully available in checking and clear guidance provided on future reward point withdrawal timelines.

We reviewed the matter and confirmed that the funds have been transferred from your Active Invest accounts to your SoFi Checking account. Regarding the timeframe for invest funds to become available, newly deposited funds in a SoFi Invest account are subject to a 5 business day hold beginning the business day following the deposit date. Funds become available for withdrawal on the 6th business day after they post to the account. Withdrawals can be initiated the day after the hold is released.

We also confirmed that the funds in the Invest account were reward points. We want to advise that when reward points are placed into a SoFi Invest account, they are subject to the same 5 business day hold before they can be withdrawn. If you choose to move reward points into an Invest account in the future, you will need to wait for the hold period to pass before those funds can be accessed.
