## Complaint
- The member expressed intense frustration and anger regarding an overdrawn account balance of **-$307.32**, refusing to listen to support's explanation of how pending transactions and available balance calculations work. The member argued that a prepaid debit card should never allow a negative balance, accused SoFi of using improper and illegal banking practices to miscalculate funds, and threatened to report the institution to the State Attorney General. Dismissing support's guidance as nonsensical, the member stated they would no longer deposit money or use SoFi services, used abusive language, and abruptly disconnected the chat.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their severe dissatisfaction and anger regarding an overdrawn account balance of -$307.32, which had led the member to accuse SoFi of improper banking practices, express an intent to report the institution to the State Attorney General, and state they would discontinue using SoFi services. The specialist acknowledged the member's frustration regarding how a negative balance could occur on a debit card and explained the underlying transaction mechanics. Clarifying that while standard Overdraft Coverage applies up to $50.00 for eligible debit card purchases, the specialist detailed that an account balance can exceed that limit if previously authorized pending transactions settle for a final amount that surpasses the available funds. The specialist reaffirmed that this standard settlement process caused the -$307.32 balance in accordance with banking regulations, and offered further assistance to address any remaining questions.
