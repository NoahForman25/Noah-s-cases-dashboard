## Complaint
- Member contacted regarding funds they believed were withdrawn to a linked bank account last week. Review confirmed the funds were available for withdrawal, but no transfer had been initiated; the member expressed concern that the funds were being intentionally held to accrue interest, and the explanation that the withdrawal was never initiated was provided before transferring the member to Investment Support (IS) for further assistance.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding a failed withdrawal and concerns about purchasing SpaceX stock. After authenticating the member, the agent addressed their frustration over funds they believed were withdrawn to a linked bank account the previous week, which the member suspected were being intentionally held to accrue interest; the agent clarified that while the funds were fully available, no transfer had actually been initiated, and transferred the member to Investment Support for further assistance. Additionally, the agent acknowledged the member's dissatisfaction regarding limitations with the AI chatbot and misleading information in an advertisement about purchasing SpaceX stock, promising to pass their feedback along to the appropriate teams.
