## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration with SoFi, stating that all their issues stemmed from the SoFi debit card due to multiple debit transactions on their account that they did not autherized.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding multiple unauthorized debit card transactions and overall card functionality concerns. Operational review confirmed that debit card functionality had been successfully restored and verified that pending transactions cannot be formally disputed under standard banking protocols until they complete posting. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, confirmed their debit card is actively working, and provided clear guidance on the dispute process and timeframes to submit claims once the unauthorized charges post. The complaint is resolved, with the debit card fully operational and clear next steps established for the customer to dispute the transactions once posted.
