## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with SoFi's investigation team closing their disputes and denying their appeal regarding a merchant that failed to deliver as promised. The member questioned why SoFi found no error despite the member submitting photo/video evidence, screenshots, and noting the merchant issued bonus credits due to outages. The member requested to speak directly with the investigation team and asked to reopen the cases, but was informed that closed disputes cannot be reopened and a new appeal requires new supporting documentation.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the closure of their disputes and the denial of their appeal despite submitted photo and video evidence. Operational review confirmed that the investigation team thoroughly evaluated all previously provided evidence and determined no processing error occurred, leading to the permanent closure of the initial claims under standard dispute guidelines. To resolve the complaint, the specialist explained that direct contact with the investigation team is unavailable and provided clear guidance on the requirement to submit entirely new, unreviewed supporting evidence in order to initiate a new appeal. The complaint is resolved, with the disputes remaining closed under the original denial determination pending any new documentation provided for review.
