## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi via chat inquiring why they had waited three weeks to receive an email regarding a cancellation request for a $1,800.00 transaction (DK*DRAFTKINGSQ9CB on September 29, 2026), which had already been resolved directly with the merchant. The member expressed strong anger, dissatisfaction, and insulted the agent regarding the delay and their need to use the funds to make a payment. The agent explained that the back office is the team responsible for providing final resolutions and determining whether funds are returned. Noting multiple prior cancellation requests on the transaction, the agent initiated a follow-up request and filed a formal complaint to have the interaction and prolonged timeframe reviewed.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a three-week processing delay for a cancellation request on an active $1,800.00 DraftKings transaction dispute that had already been settled with the merchant. Operational review confirmed that previous requests had stalled across departmental handoffs, leaving the dispute active and preventing the customer from accessing their funds. To resolve the complaint, the specialist escalated the ticket directly to the specialized dispute closure team to formally cancel the claim and release the transaction, providing direct phone contact information for expedited follow-up if the dispute did not close within one business day. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute closure submitted for final processing and the account remaining open in good standing pending confirmation of the released funds.
