## Complaint
- The member expressed confusion and frustration regarding a security hold placed on their newly opened account following fund transfers from Morgan Stanley and JPMC accounts, noting that all setup information had been provided and no clear reason was given for the restriction despite depositing significant funds. The member was further upset by the lack of an immediate resolution, the unavailability of a supervisor during the call, and the one-business-day review timeline, stating a readiness to withdraw all funds and close the account based on this initial experience with SoFi. The agent acknowledged the member's frustration, asked required security questions to verify the deposits were legitimate and in the member's name, and committed to escalating the restriction internally for potential removal. Additionally, the agent kept the member updated during holds, offered a supervisor callback within 24 to 48 hours, assured the member that an internal specialist would follow up within one business day, and respected the member's decision regarding the account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding a security hold placed on her newly opened account following external transfers from Morgan Stanley and JPMorgan Chase. Operational review confirmed that the restriction was temporarily instituted as a standard fraud prevention measure to verify the legitimacy and ownership of the high-value incoming transfers. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the security investigation had concluded successfully, issued an apology for the delay and initial service experience, and confirmed the removal of the administrative hold. The complaint is resolved, with the security restriction dropped on November 4 and full access restored to the customer's account in good standing.
