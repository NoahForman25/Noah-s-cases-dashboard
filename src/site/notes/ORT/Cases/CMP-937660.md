## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a $276.66 dispute dated September 28, 2026, related to Intuit. After the agent explained that SoFi could not specify the required documents for filing an appeal, the member stated they had no receipts or emails because they never used Intuit's services or held a subscription. The member stated that suggestions to contact Intuit for proof or file a police report were unhelpful, noting that Intuit had been unreachable and unhelpful when contacted previously.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the handling and denial of a $276.66 dispute for an unauthorized Intuit charge. Operational review confirmed that the claim was thoroughly evaluated and denied based on available transaction records, and that standard claim guidelines require new supporting documentation or evidence to initiate a secondary appeal. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding the appeal requirements, explained that specific document types cannot be prescribed in advance, and outlined the necessary steps to submit new information for consideration. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute remaining closed under the initial denial determination pending any new supporting documentation provided by the customer.
