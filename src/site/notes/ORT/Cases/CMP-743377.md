## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe frustration regarding ongoing login issues with their online banking that have persisted for a couple of weeks, stating: "I AM VERY ANGRY WITH THE BANK JERKING AROUND FOR A COUPLE OF WEEKS I AM FRUSTRATED". The member requested to speak with an account manager, characterized the institution as unfriendly and abusive, and expressed that they feel entirely unheard regarding their concerns.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to talk about their frustration about the long-standing problems with online banking. They felt like their problems were being ignored. After verifying their identity, the agent talked about technical problems. The member said they were because of a new phone that didn't have biometric fingerprint support and problems with facial recognition. The member said they could log in using their thumbprint on their iPad. The agent gave more tips for text verification codes. Finally, the specialist recognized the member's frustration with platform security measures and submitted formal feedback regarding their difficulty ordering new checks.

The agent provided tips on how to find the correct six-digit code sent via text for future logins. The agent acknowledged the member's concerns and submitted feedback regarding the check ordering process.
