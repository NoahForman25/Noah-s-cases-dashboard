## Complaint
- Member contacted to express intense frustration regarding restrictions placed on their account, characterizing the institution's decision to place their funds on hold following an attempted wire transfer as "frustrating and disgusting." The member strongly objected to the restriction, emphasizing that there was absolutely no valid reason to hold their money.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on an inquiry regarding a wire transfer hold, addressing the member's intense frustration and objections over their funds being restricted following the transfer attempt. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's concerns regarding the delay in accessing their money. The agent delivered an update on the situation, apologizing for the inconvenience caused by the hold and reassuring the member that their case is actively being worked on by the appropriate team. Additionally, the agent provided the member with a direct contact number for any further inquiries while the review is finalized.
