## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction after being informed they are ineligible to apply for an account, asserting that the institution is covering up fraud by withholding information regarding the unauthorized use of their personal data. The member further stated that they intend to file a lawsuit against the institution due to this lack of disclosure.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction and allegations of a fraud cover-up regarding their account ineligibility and the unauthorized use of their personal data. The specialist provided a reassuring update, clarifying that a profile associated with the member's Social Security Number was located and proactively closed to prevent further misuse, and confirmed that unauthorized profiles would be deleted. Furthermore, the member was assured that they are not liable for the unauthorized activity and was provided with a comprehensive list of actionable identity protection resources, including contact details for major credit bureaus and identity theft reporting guidelines, to help secure their personal information moving forward.
