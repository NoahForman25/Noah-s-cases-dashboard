## Complaint
- Member contacted expressing frustration after attempting to complete a peer-to-peer (P2P) transaction that was unsuccessful.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding a failed peer-to-peer (P2P) payment transfer on their new account. After authenticating the member, the agent addressed their frustration over the unsuccessful transaction, noting the member had only attempted the transfer once since opening the account. Delivering account insights and next steps, the agent explained potential causes for the disruption—such as initial security controls for new accounts or temporary internal technical issues—and confirmed that a ticket had been submitted for further investigation, with a resolution expected within a couple of business days. The member noted their new card should be arriving soon and expressed overall satisfaction with the remainder of their account experience.
