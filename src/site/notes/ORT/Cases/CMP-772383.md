## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding having to undergo an identity verification process to access their account online, noting that they had just completed the verification steps with a previous specialist. During the interaction, the line disconnected while the member was in the process of completing the Secondary Identity Verification (SSIDV).
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on an inquiry regarding their account and identity verification process, addressing their previous dissatisfaction and the accidental disconnection that occurred during Secondary Identity Verification (SSIDV). After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's core concern regarding unauthorized withdrawals totaling $800.00, confirming that a dispute was filed and that official denial documents would be sent soon. To address further issues with transferring funds to Chime and a missing $10.70, the agent successfully assisted the member in setting up two-factor authentication, which revealed an unauthorized $10.00 Zelle transaction that the agent promptly disputed. Providing an immediate resolution to secure the account and assist the member, the agent advised a password change, applied a $20.00 temporary courtesy credit to meet immediate needs, and noted the member's request to permanently remove Zelle once all active disputes are fully resolved.
