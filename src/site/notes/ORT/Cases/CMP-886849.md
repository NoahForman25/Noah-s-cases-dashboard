## Complaint
- The member expressed intense dissatisfaction regarding the unannounced closure of their Checking and Savings accounts and the lack of specific details provided, calling the situation "insane" after being informed that the closure was due to a general violation of SoFi's terms and conditions with no further explanation available. The member was deeply frustrated by the lack of prior notification and the requirement to wait several weeks for remaining funds to arrive via a mailed check, stating, "you literally took all my money and you are saying I can not get it until in a few weeks, that is insane if you think you can take all somebody's money without telling them." Support acknowledged the member's distress, explained standard compliance and account agreement policies regarding closures, and verified the primary mailing address on file for check issuance. Support then submitted an urgent priority escalation ticket to Risk Operations, Account Restructure, and Executive Complaints to audit the remaining balance liquidation, expedite the check processing timeline, and ensure updated tracking information is provided.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her intense dissatisfaction regarding the unexpected closure of her Checking and Savings accounts, the lack of detailed explanation provided beyond general terms and conditions, and the delay in receiving her remaining funds. The member had previously criticized the lack of advance notice and voiced severe frustration over having her funds held while waiting for a mailed check. The specialist acknowledged her distress, explained that account closures are executed in accordance with standard compliance and account agreement policies, and confirmed that an internal escalation ticket was submitted to audit the balance liquidation and expedite the payout timeline. The specialist verified that the final balance check was officially issued and mailed on September 2, 2026, advising her that while delivery can take up to 30 days from the issue date, the check is actively en route to her primary address on file.
