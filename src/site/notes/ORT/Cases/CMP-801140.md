## Complaint
- Member contacted via AI Chatbot expressing dissatisfaction regarding an unposted withdrawal, stating the transaction had not completed or appeared in their account activity after seven days. The AI Chatbot acknowledged the concern, advised that Invest withdrawals typically take 3 to 5 business days, and requested clarification on which Invest account was involved; the interaction ended after the member stopped responding.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint originally submitted via the AI Chatbot regarding a delayed Invest withdrawal, where the member had expressed strong dissatisfaction after waiting seven days for a withdrawal that was not displaying in their transaction activity. Although the AI Chatbot had clarified that Invest withdrawals typically take 3–5 business days and asked which account was affected before the chat closed due to inactivity, the agent successfully authenticated the member on the phone, at which point the member confirmed that the withdrawal issue had already been resolved on their end, allowing the agent to confirm no further action was needed and conclude the call.
