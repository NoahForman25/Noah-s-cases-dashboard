## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding how transaction postings are displayed and processed on their account. The member declined to initiate or proceed with a formal dispute for the charges.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding potential unauthorized transactions and confusion over how account postings were being displayed. After authenticating the member, the agent addressed their confusion regarding their balance, which had turned negative following a Zelle payment and several subsequent transactions. Delivering clear account details, the agent reviewed the transaction history and explained that the funds were temporarily on hold until the pending charges fully settled. The member confirmed that they recognized all the transactions as authorized—explaining why they previously declined to initiate a formal dispute—and expressed full understanding of the posting process and current balance status.
