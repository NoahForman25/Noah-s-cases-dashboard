## Complaint
- The member expressed severe frustration regarding an inability to transfer funds from an external ACH account into their SoFi Checking account in order to pay their SoFi Credit Card. As a direct result of these transfer failures, the member was unable to make timely payments and incurred late fees on their credit card account.

### Second Contact
- Dear Ryan,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. Thank you for reaching out regarding the inability to transfer funds from your external account to your SoFi Checking account to pay your SoFi Credit Card, and the resulting late fees. We acknowledge your severe frustration regarding these transfer failures and the financial impact of the late fees.

A review of your account and the transfer attempts was conducted. We apologize for the issues experienced with the Mobile Check Deposit (MCD) process. According to our system records, there are no documented attempts on our end for an MCD. Once you contact the other bank and receive confirmation to proceed, you may reattempt the MCD. The current limit for this feature is $50,000.00. Any valid check below this limit will be able to process.

To recap, there are no MCD attempts visible in our system, and you may reattempt the deposit up to the $50,000.00 limit once cleared by the other bank. Please let us know if you have any further questions.
