## Complaint
- The member expressed extreme frustration and dissatisfaction after being transferred in circles between the Dispute Team and the Retail/Met Banking Team without receiving assistance. The member highlighted that the previous representative (Rusette Ilagan) failed to review the transfer notes before routing them back to Disputes, forcing them to repeat their situation after spending approximately two hours in chat. Explaining that they experienced a job loss in June, filed for bankruptcy, and are now facing an overdrawn balance, the member requested a courtesy credit to cover the overdraft amounts caused by the dispute outcome. Support apologized for the misdirection and poor handling, logged a formal service complaint regarding the agent loop, and escalated the file to Operations Leadership and Customer Advocacy to review the overdraft fees for a potential courtesy credit waiver.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her extreme frustration after being repeatedly transferred in circles between the Dispute Team and the Retail/Banking Team over a two-hour chat session without receiving assistance. The member had reported that representatives failed to read transfer notes, forcing her to repeatedly explain her situation during a period of severe financial hardship involving job loss, a bankruptcy filing, and an overdrawn account balance following an adverse dispute outcome. The specialist apologized for the severe service friction and agent looping, confirming that a formal service complaint was logged for internal review. The specialist verified that her account was escalated to Operations Leadership and Customer Advocacy to evaluate a courtesy waiver for the overdraft fees. Additionally, the specialist credited 1,500 reward points ($15.00) directly to her profile as a gesture of goodwill for the misdirection and poor handling.
