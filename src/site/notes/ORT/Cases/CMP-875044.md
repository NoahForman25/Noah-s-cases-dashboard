## Complaint
- The member called to dispute the decision on a **$50,000.00** loan application, which had been declined due to excessive debt obligations relative to income. Although the representative explained the reason for the decline and advised the member to contact the credit bureaus directly to address any errors, the member insisted on disputing the credit report information directly with SoFi. Support re-explained the role of credit bureau data in the automated underwriting process and advised the member on their right to submit a formal direct dispute. Support documented the specific liabilities the member claimed were inaccurate, logged the member's dissatisfaction regarding the dispute handling process, and submitted a direct credit dispute ticket to Lending Operations and Credit Risk for review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding the denial of a $50,000.00 personal loan application due to excessive debt obligations relative to income, as well as their frustration with how their credit dispute was handled. The member had previously called to challenge the credit report data used in the automated underwriting decision, insisting on disputing inaccurate liabilities directly through SoFi rather than contacting the credit bureaus independently. The specialist explained that during the application review, an income discrepancy was identified: the original submission listed a household income of $266,000.00, whereas the member's individual income is $116,000.00. The specialist advised the member that a new application using their individual income is required for accurate prequalification. Additionally, the specialist confirmed that while underwriting relies on credit bureau data, a direct credit dispute ticket detailing the specific inaccurate liabilities was formally submitted to Lending Operations and Credit Risk for review on the member's behalf.

While the automated underwriting process relies on data provided by the credit bureaus, you have the right to submit a formal direct dispute, and that process has been initiated on your behalf.
