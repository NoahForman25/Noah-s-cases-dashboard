## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction after experiencing an error that prevented them from receiving funds through Zelle. Support acknowledged the member's frustration, verified the status of the Zelle integration on the account, and guided them through initial troubleshooting steps. Support then documented the technical error details and escalated the transaction issue to Deposit Operations and Technical Support for investigation and resolution.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her dissatisfaction regarding a technical error that prevented her from receiving incoming funds through Zelle. The member had previously reached out to support after encountering the transaction failure, expressing frustration over the unexpected service disruption and its impact on her access to funds. While support had initially guided the member through basic troubleshooting steps and escalated the transaction failure to Deposit Operations and Technical Support, the specialist confirmed that Zelle functionality remains temporarily disabled on her account while the investigation is ongoing. The specialist apologized for the disruption, recommended using alternative transfer methods such as ACH transfers or direct deposits in the interim, and noted that the member may attempt to use Zelle again once the technical investigation is complete and full integration is restored.
