## Complaint
- Member contacted to express strong dissatisfaction regarding severe delays encountered while attempting to withdraw funds, noting it took one hour and twenty-five minutes to resolve the issue. Due to the poor experience, the member stated they intend to close their account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding severe delays encountered while attempting to withdraw funds, which took one hour and twenty-five minutes to resolve and led the member to state an intent to close their account. After authenticating the member, the agent addressed their frustration over the extended time required to access their money and apologized for the delay. Delivering a partial resolution, the agent confirmed that a withdrawal of $611.85 had been successfully processed and informed the member that the remaining funds would become available on the 24th. The member acknowledged the update and indicated they would complete the withdrawal of the remaining balance once those funds unlock on the 24th.
