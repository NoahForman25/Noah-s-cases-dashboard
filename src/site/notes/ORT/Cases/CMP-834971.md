## Complaint
- The member expressed strong frustration regarding a delay in receiving follow-up communication from Fraud/Investigative Operations, stating they were initially told an investigator would contact them within 1 business day. Upon being informed by support to wait an additional business day, the member criticized the delay as unacceptable and requested an immediate escalation to a supervisor or manager to address the situation. Support logged the operational callback delay and submitted a supervisor call-back request to address the member's escalation.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an ongoing account restriction and ensure their concerns regarding delayed support communication were addressed. After authenticating the member, the agent discussed their strong frustration over a delay in receiving a callback from Fraud/Investigative Operations, which had exceeded the initially promised one-business-day timeframe. The member criticized being told to wait an additional business day as unacceptable and requested an immediate escalation to a supervisor or manager. The agent confirmed that the investigative review process remains ongoing and verified that a supervisor call-back request, along with an operational callback delay log, was formally submitted so a representative can reach out directly within the specified escalation window.
