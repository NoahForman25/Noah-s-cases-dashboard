## Complaint
- The member contacted customer service regarding an incoming Social Security direct deposit following the closure of their account due to security flags from numerous transactions. The member expressed dissatisfaction that no advance notification of the closure was provided. The member stated they contacted the Social Security Administration to provide new account details, but was informed that paper checks are no longer issued and the updated direct deposit information will not take effect until December. Consequently, the member requested to have the closed account reopened temporarily to receive their Social Security funds while the update is pending.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding support ticket A-200161, missing incoming funds transfers, and concerns regarding potential identity fraud. Operational review confirmed that the customer's banking accounts were already closed, meaning the system cannot accept incoming transfers or keep accounts active to receive incoming funds. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational guidance explaining that any attempted direct deposits will automatically bounce back to the originating sender, advising the customer to coordinate directly with the sender to route deposits to an alternative institution. The complaint is resolved, with the banking accounts remaining permanently closed and no incoming funds accepted.
