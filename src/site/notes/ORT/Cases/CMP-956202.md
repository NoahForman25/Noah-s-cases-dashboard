## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction that his individual and joint savings accounts are earning lower APY rates (4.0% and 4.2%) than the 4.4% APY currently advertised to new customers, stating it is unfair to well-established members with substantial balances. He stated that he has $1.5 million deposited and threatened to move his funds to another bank if SoFi does not match the 4.4% rate on both accounts. After the initial representative was unable to adjust the rate and transferred the call, the account manager informed the member that an override request would be submitted to the internal account review team, who will notify him of their decision via phone call or email within three to five business days.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint requesting an APY adjustment on their individual and joint savings accounts to match promotional rates offered to new customers. Operational review confirmed that promotional APY boosts are governed by specific eligibility criteria and cannot be manually overridden or matched for established accounts, regardless of deposit balance size. To resolve the complaint, the specialist reviewed the formal rate-adjustment request ticket, delivered transparent written notice explaining that the requested APY adjustments could not be applied, and acknowledged the customer's expectations. The complaint is resolved, with the savings accounts remaining open in good standing at their existing respective rates of 4.0% and 4.2% APY.
