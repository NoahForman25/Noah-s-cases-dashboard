## Complaint
- The member expressed severe dissatisfaction regarding SoFi's handling of an ongoing dispute, asserting that SoFi should have been aware of the issue and claiming that two involved entities merged illegally with SoFi bearing responsibility. The member reported making approximately 30 calls over the past year without resolution—highlighting multiple call disconnects, including a two-hour call where an agent hung up—and accused representatives of engaging in unfair practices, suggesting agents receive commissions for brushing customers off. The member attributed severe personal life disruptions and financial losses directly to the unresolved matter, detailing a ruined credit standing, accumulated late fees, loss of employment, auto repossession, loss of insurance, and periods of homelessness. Support documented the member's extensive grievance history, logged an urgent service complaint regarding agent behavior and call disconnections, and escalated the case to Executive Management and Risk Operations for priority manual review.
