## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration and dissatisfaction with the account closure process, stating they were tired of "jumping through hoops" to close their accounts. Desiring to end their relationship with SoFi due to a disappointing overall experience, the member formally requested the closure of all their SoFi accounts.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration with the account closure process and their formal request to end their relationship with SoFi due to a disappointing overall experience. Acknowledging that a previous phone call to sell investment shares and discuss the member's Robo account had concluded prematurely, the specialist outlined the mandatory steps to close the SoFi Invest account. The specialist explained that the account must reach a $0.00 balance, which requires the member to either sell their remaining holdings and withdraw the settled funds or transfer the account to another brokerage firm. Additionally, the specialist provided specific navigation instructions for withdrawing funds and closing the account via the website and mobile app, offering further phone support if needed to finalize the process.

### Second Contact
- Dear Michael,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. Thank you for reaching out regarding the closure of your SoFi accounts. We acknowledge your dissatisfaction with the account closure process and your frustration regarding the required steps to end your relationship with SoFi.

A review of your account and our recent phone interaction was conducted. During our previous call, we began the process of selling your investment shares and discussing your Robo account before the call concluded prematurely.

To successfully close your SoFi Invest account, the account must first reflect a $0.00 balance. Currently, there are still shares and holdings in the account. To proceed, you must either sell the remaining investments and withdraw the proceeds once the funds are settled, or request a transfer of the Invest account to another brokerage firm.

To withdraw the funds after selling your shares, select your Invest account, choose "More," and then select "Withdraw" to complete the request. If the full cash balance is not immediately available, it is due to pending investment sales or recent deposits that are still processing.

Once the account balance reaches $0.00, you can close the account on the website by selecting the Invest account from the homepage, clicking "More," and choosing "Close Account." In the mobile application, tap "Invest," select the account, tap the gear icon in the top right corner, and choose "Close Account."

To recap, the Invest account must have a $0.00 balance to be closed, which requires selling your remaining shares and withdrawing the settled funds. Please let us know if you have any further questions or if you would like to continue our phone conversation to complete this process.
