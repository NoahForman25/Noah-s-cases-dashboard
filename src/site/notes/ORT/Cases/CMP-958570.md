## Complaint
- The member was transferred to a live agent regarding their checking account to receive assistance with support ticket A-200161, which concerned a missing incoming funds transfer and potential identity fraud. The member expressed frustration that once the ticket was forwarded to the bank, SoFi claimed it was no longer liable and that the issue was between the member and their bank. Additionally, the member expressed distress regarding identity theft, noted having approximately $30 remaining in their account, and indicated they may close their SoFi account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding support ticket A-200161, missing incoming direct deposit funds, and concerns surrounding potential identity fraud. Operational review confirmed that an internal research ticket was completed using the provided trace ID, but no incoming funds could be located within the institution's clearing network. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding fund routing protocols and directed the customer to coordinate directly with the originating institution to initiate an official sender-side trace and recovery process. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's checking account remaining active and open while external trace efforts proceed through the sending bank.
