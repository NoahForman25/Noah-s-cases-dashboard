## Complaint
- Member contacted expressing frustration regarding a missing referral reward from a link used in March 2026. The member noted that a previous representative promised courtesy points for the delay but was unable to issue them, advising a one-month follow-up. Upon following up, system restrictions again prevented the representative from manually adding the points; the ongoing issue, combined with personal hardship, resulted in significant distress during the interaction.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their distress and dissatisfaction regarding a missing referral reward from March 2026 and the repeated system restrictions that prevented representatives from manually issuing promised courtesy points. The specialist acknowledged the frustration caused by the delayed resolution and the miscommunication, particularly during a period of personal hardship. Delivering confirmation of resolution, the specialist clarified that system records show the issue has been addressed, confirming that 1,500 rewards points were successfully credited to the member's profile on July 3, 2026, to fulfill the courtesy credit for the referral.
