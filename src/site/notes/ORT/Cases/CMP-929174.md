## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding SoFi's inability to immediately and fully block a merchant named Grant. The member noted that other financial institutions block merchants upon customer request and expressed concern that Grant will again withdraw funds set aside for a bill. Due to feeling that their account is not adequately protected, the member stated they are considering closing their account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding SoFi's inability to immediately block a specific merchant from attempting transaction debits and the customer's resulting consideration to close the account. Operational review confirmed that the recent transactions from the merchant entered the system as direct payments and were currently in a pending state, while a stop payment order was successfully placed on October 9, 2026, to block all future posted transactions. To resolve the complaint, the specialist clarified the distinction between pending authorization holds and posted ledger charges, outlined how to submit an advance dispute in the mobile application while transactions remain pending, and confirmed that active stop payment protections are in effect for future debit attempts. The complaint is resolved, with active stop payment controls established on the merchant and clear dispute submission procedures provided for pending holds.
