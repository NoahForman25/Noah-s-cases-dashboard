## Complaint
- The member expressed extreme distress and severe personal hardship regarding open complaint **CMP-835159** and an uncorrected dispute appeal, stating that they and their daughter have spent two nights without shelter due to restricted or unavailable funds. The member voiced intense exhaustion and frustration over spending the last three days navigating hours of chats, dropped calls, continuous transfers, and contradictory information from support. Emphasizing that they refuse to restart the dispute or verification process from scratch, the member explicitly requested that the assigned handling team perform an immediate, comprehensive review of the entire documented dispute history and grant an urgent decision regarding provisional credit or immediate fund release today. Due to severe personal hardship and escalating risk, this matter requires immediate, high-priority escalation to Management and Risk Operations for expedited review under complaint **CMP-835159**.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their extreme distress and severe personal hardship regarding open complaint CMP-835159 and two active dispute appeals, which had left the member and their daughter without shelter for two nights due to restricted funds. Acknowledging the member's intense frustration over days of dropped calls, continuous transfers, conflicting information, and their request for an immediate review without restarting the process, the specialist confirmed that both dispute appeals are actively under review by the assigned team following the submission of new documentation. The specialist clarified that the original dispute denials remain in effect while the active investigation is underway, confirming that an update will be provided as soon as the review concludes and noting that any provisional credits previously issued could be reversed if the final determination is upheld.
