## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding a hold on their funds and requested clarification on when they would become available. A review of the account confirmed an available balance of $14.78 and showed that a cash advance is currently pending as a pre-authorization hold. The agent explained that the hold is expected to be released automatically on October 18, 2026, at which point the funds should become available if the transaction is not finalized by the merchant, apologized for the inconvenience, and reassured the member.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding a temporary hold on their account balance related to an expected GRID cash advance. Operational review confirmed that the $50.00 cash advance exists as a pending pre-authorization hold on the account, which temporarily restricts the available balance until the transaction settles or expires. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, explained the nature of pre-authorization holds, verified the current available balance of $14.78, and confirmed that the held funds will automatically release back to the account on October 18, 2026, if the merchant does not finalize the transaction. The complaint is resolved, with the operational timing of the authorization hold fully clarified and the pending transaction set to automatically clear by the specified date.
