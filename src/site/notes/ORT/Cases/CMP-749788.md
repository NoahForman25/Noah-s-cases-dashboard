## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding their inability to complete the required identity verification process because they do not currently possess any acceptable official documentation.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding an inability to complete the required identity verification process due to a lack of standard, acceptable official documentation. The specialist apologized for the inconvenience and provided an alternative solution, clarifying that the institution can accept an official identification document that has been expired for 90 days or less. The member was advised that as long as they have access to a smartphone, this document can be used to easily complete the verification process and restore full access to their account.
