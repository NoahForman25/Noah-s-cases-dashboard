## Complaint
- The member expressed severe frustration after SoFi processed a $29,300 check deposit on July 17th—following days of repeated system error messages—which led the member to deposit the check at Schwab on July 16th instead and initiate an electronic transfer to SoFi. This duplicate processing caused the member's Schwab account to go negative by $19,000, leaving them caught between two financial institutions unable to resolve the issue directly with each other. Holding SoFi fully responsible for the processing failure, the member warned they would liquidate and transfer all remaining funds back to Schwab if the matter is not properly resolved.
