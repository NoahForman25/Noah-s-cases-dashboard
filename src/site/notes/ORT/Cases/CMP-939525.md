## Complaint
- The member called in frustrated and used profanity regarding a pending Grammarly charge on their account. The member stated they had responded to a text from SoFi confirming the transaction was unauthorized, but the charge still appeared on the account as pending.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a pending unauthorized Grammarly charge after responding to a text alert. Operational review of the automated fraud messaging system confirmed that two text verification alerts were dispatched to the customer's device, but no response was recorded in the system, allowing the authorization to proceed and post to the account under standard fraud monitoring protocols. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided transparency regarding the text alert records and explained how responding directly to future transaction alerts will automatically block unauthorized charges from posting. The complaint is resolved, with the Grammarly charge confirmed posted to the account and instructions provided on using text alerts to manage future card security.

- Dear Chelbie,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. We are writing to acknowledge your complaint regarding the pending Grammarly charge on your account and the frustration caused by the charge appearing despite your response to the text message confirming the transaction was unauthorized.

We have reviewed your account and can confirm that the Grammarly transaction has posted. The dispute process was not initiated on your account. Two text messages were sent to your device asking you to approve or deny the transaction, but no response was received from either message. When no response is received, the transaction is permitted to proceed and post to the account, which is what occurred in this case.

For future transactions, when a text message is sent asking you to approve or deny a charge, responding to the text will determine whether the transaction is allowed to post. Selecting the option to deny the transaction will prevent the merchant from posting the charge to your account.

We appreciate you working with us on this matter. Please reach out if you have any further questions or issues.
