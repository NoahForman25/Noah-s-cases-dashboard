## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after contacting SoFi to update the phone number on their profile, as their current phone is broken and their driver's license is expired. The member requested that a security code be sent to their email instead, but was informed that email two-factor authentication is no longer supported. They were further advised that their phone number cannot be updated without a valid ID, adding to their frustration. The case was subsequently escalated to a higher authority for further assistance.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the inability to update their account phone number while possessing an expired driver's license and a broken phone. Operational review confirmed that email-based two-factor authentication has been decommissioned for profile security, and security policies strictly mandate a valid, unexpired government-issued identification to process profile contact updates. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding identity verification protocols, explaining that the identification requirement cannot be bypassed or waived under compliance standards. The complaint is resolved, with clear instructions provided for the customer to recontact support with a valid form of identification to finalize the phone number update.
