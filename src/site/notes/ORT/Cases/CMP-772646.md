## Complaint
- Member contacted to express intense frustration regarding two debit card charges from METAPAY*Tracy Jay on July 4, 2026, totaling -$125.00 and -$60.00, which posted to an account they claimed should have been closed, stating verbatim, "I think it's ridiculous that I have to go through this after I've been contacting you people for 2 days telling you this is a deactivated account." The member had anticipated placing a stop payment on the transactions—though advised it was not guaranteed—and intended to handle the debit card replacement on their own. After an apology was extended, the member was informed that system records showed their last documented contact was actually in April, and a formal dispute was subsequently submitted for both transactions under ticket **#8206199** with assurances that their feedback is taken seriously to help improve services.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their intense frustration regarding two unauthorized METAPAY*Tracy Jay debit card charges totaling $185.00 that posted on July 4, 2026, to an account the member believed was deactivated. The specialist addressed the member's objections regarding their recent communication difficulties and delivered a clear update, confirming that a formal dispute for the $125.00 and $60.00 transactions was successfully submitted under ticket #8206199 and is actively being investigated. Additionally, the specialist acknowledged the member's feedback regarding their contact history—noting system records showed the last documented interaction occurred in April—and documented their choice to handle the debit card replacement independently after being advised that a stop payment could not be guaranteed.
