## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after being unable to click on their virtual debit card in the app, stating they believe they are being hacked. The agent connected the member to the technical support team, but due to high call volume, the member requested to be connected with an account manager.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding technical difficulties accessing his virtual debit card within the mobile app, concerns of unauthorized account compromise, and delays reaching technical support. Operational review indicated that the application glitch had been addressed and cleared during subsequent customer service interactions. To resolve the complaint, the specialist conducted outreach to verify that virtual debit card access had been fully restored and offered direct follow-up assistance should any persistent technical issues remain. The complaint is resolved, with the digital debit card functional and the customer's account remaining secure, open, and active in good standing.
