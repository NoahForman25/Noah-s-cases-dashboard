## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration because their direct deposit was not received in their SoFi account. They stated that their employer informed them a prenote was applied to the account, leading the member to believe SoFi was holding or delaying the deposit. The agent provided empathy and explained that SoFi does not hold or block direct deposits as long as the correct routing and account numbers are provided. Per the member's request to speak with a manager, the interaction was escalated for further assistance.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a missing direct deposit and concerns that an account prenote was causing SoFi to hold their funds. Operational review confirmed that SoFi does not hold or delay incoming direct deposits provided the correct routing and account numbers are used, and verified that the transmission has not yet reached SoFi's network. To resolve the complaint, the specialist clarified bank deposit handling policies and instructed the customer to obtain a trace ID from their employer's payroll provider so the deposit can be located across banking networks. The complaint is resolved, with clear diagnostic steps provided for the customer to help locate the missing funds through their employer.
