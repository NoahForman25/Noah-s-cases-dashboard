## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after observing that a transaction they wish to dispute shows as posted in the SoFi app, whereas it still reflects a pending status in the agent system.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding a disputed transaction that appeared posted in the mobile application while displaying as pending in the internal banking system. Operational review confirmed that a backend system processing delay caused a status synchronization discrepancy between the customer-facing application and internal records. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, submitted a ticket to remove the hold on the account, and instructed the customer to check their account balance on the twenty-fourth or twenty-fifth for full resolution updates. The complaint is resolved, with the system hold removal actively in progress and the account monitored for final balance posting.
