## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding receiving repeated automated or outbound phone calls from SoFi without any accompanying voicemails or clear interaction notes explaining the reason for the outreach. The member noted that when calling back, support representatives were unable to locate notes detailing why the outreach occurred. Support acknowledged the member's annoyance with the unnecessary calls and lack of documentation, reviewed recent outbound contact logs, and submitted a technical and operational ticket to Account Operations and Communications Management to suppress non-essential automated calls and update contact preferences.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to address her financial difficulties, account closures, and frustration regarding repeated automated collection calls received without accompanying voicemails or clear representative notes. The member explained that her checking account was closed without explanation and that she was struggling financially, while expressing annoyance over receiving mystery calls from SoFi that left no record when she called back. The specialist authenticated the member, clarified that the call was intended to provide support rather than collect payments, and acknowledged a scheduled $35.00 payment set for the upcoming Friday to help bring the account current. Reassuring the member regarding her account situation, the specialist reviewed recent outbound contact logs and submitted an operational ticket to Account Operations and Communications Management to suppress non-essential automated calls and update her contact preferences.
