## Complaint
- Member contacted to inquire about persistent account login failures and to confirm the current status of their account. The member expressed concern regarding the security of their remaining funds, noting they are encountering a verification error when attempting to log in, and requested to speak with a manager due to apprehensions about potential account closure.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed the member to address inquiries regarding persistent login failures, verification errors, and concerns over the security of their remaining funds. The specialist confirmed that the member previously connected with the team to address the account closure and clarified the process for returning the remaining balance. A final resolution was outlined, informing the member that a check will be processed and mailed to the address on file within 7 to 10 business days, with an expected arrival window between July 6, 2026, and July 9, 2026.
