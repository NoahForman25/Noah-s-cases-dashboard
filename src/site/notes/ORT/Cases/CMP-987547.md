## Complaint
- The member expressed extreme frustration and anger after their account was restricted for security reasons while attempting to pay for groceries at a Walmart checkout, preventing access to their funds. The member experienced further inconvenience when prompted for identity verification requiring a physical ID or passport; although a passport was used, the verification link failed to capture the photo, leaving the process incomplete and the member unable to purchase groceries. After being informed the issue would take one business day to resolve with no same-day option, the member stated intentions to cancel direct deposits and stop using SoFi. The agent apologized for the inconvenience, attempted internal escalation to lift the restriction, sent the verification link via text, accepted the passport as an alternate ID, and attempted to locate an available supervisor upon request. The agent explained that procedures prevented manual removal of the restriction, documented the member's concerns, confirmed follow-up by an internal specialist within one business day, and acknowledged the member's decision to discontinue direct deposits.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding an abrupt account restriction experienced during a point-of-sale transaction at Walmart, along with associated document verification failures and funds access delays. Operational review determined that automated security protocols initially flagged the profile for potential account takeover, triggering protective holds to safeguard funds while identity verification was reviewed. To resolve the complaint, the security review team verified that no unauthorized takeover occurred, issued an apology for the service disruption, and promptly lifted all administrative restrictions that same day. The customer's account is now fully unrestricted, accessible, and active in good standing.
