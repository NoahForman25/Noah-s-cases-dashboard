## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding conflicting information provided by support concerning a stop payment request. The member noted that after submitting the initial stop payment request, they received no confirmation of its acceptance and were subsequently advised by an agent that the request had been removed. However, upon following up on a later call, the member was informed that the transaction was declined specifically because the stop payment request had actually been accepted and was active. Support documented the contradictory agent guidance, explained the actual status of the stop payment on the account, and logged an operational service complaint regarding the misinformation for representative review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an inquiry regarding a stop payment issue and to address significant frustration caused by conflicting information from previous representatives. The member reported receiving no initial confirmation after submitting a stop payment request, later being told by an agent that the request had been removed, and subsequently discovering on a follow-up call that a transaction was declined because the stop payment was actively in place. After authenticating the member, the specialist apologized for the repeated misinformation and clarified the actual active status of the stop payment on the account. To acknowledge the inconvenience and resolve the logged operational service complaint, the specialist added 1,500 reward points (valued at $15.00) to the member's account as a goodwill gesture. The member expressed appreciation for the adjustment and confirmed that all outstanding concerns had been addressed.
