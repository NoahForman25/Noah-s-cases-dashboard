## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction after an automatic loan payment was drafted on their August 21 due date, despite a previous representative incorrectly assuring them no payment would process. Although the member had deactivated auto-pay on August 20, the current month's draft was already in motion, meaning the cancellation only applied to future billing cycles. Currently between jobs, the member incurred over $100.00 in bank overdraft fees due to this miscommunication and rejected a 1,000 reward point gesture as inadequate compensation. Support instructed the member to submit screenshot proof of their August 20 auto-pay cancellation via secure message and escalated the request to Loan Operations and Customer Advocacy for priority review and fee reimbursement consideration.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong dissatisfaction regarding an automatic loan payment drafted on their August 21, 2026 due date despite prior assurances from a representative that no draft would process. The member, who was currently between jobs, incurred over $100.00 in external bank overdraft fees due to the miscommunication and rejected an initial 1,000 reward point offer as inadequate compensation for the financial strain caused. The specialist apologized for the representative's error and explained that while auto-pay was turned off on August 20, the current month's payment was already in motion, meaning the deactivation applied only to subsequent billing cycles. To address the member's concerns, the specialist confirmed receipt of the screenshot proof showing the August 20 cancellation and credited an updated total of 1,500 reward points to the member's profile. Finally, the specialist verified that the case remains under active priority review with Loan Operations and Customer Advocacy for full fee reimbursement consideration.
