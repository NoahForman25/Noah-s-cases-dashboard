## Complaint
- Member contacted to express confusion regarding a fifteen-day hold placed on their funds, as they were under the impression that the standard hold duration was only three business days. The member stated: "I mean, I didn't rob a bank or anything. I just put the money to to my use. I was hoping for my use, but oh my goodness. Well, I won't encourage anyone else to do this."
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their confusion and frustration regarding an unexpected fifteen-day hold placed on their recent wire transfer funds, which the member believed would only be held for three business days. The specialist provided a positive update, confirming that the hold on the wire transfer has been successfully released sooner than originally outlined. Additionally, the specialist apologized for the extended delay and the lack of proactive communication regarding the initial hold timeframe, ensuring the member's funds are now fully available for use.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
- The resolution specialist attempted to contact the member regarding their complaint. The specialist received the member's voicemail and left a message. The specialist will send a detailed email.
