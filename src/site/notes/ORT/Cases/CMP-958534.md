## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi expressing high frustration regarding the denial of disputes and subsequent appeals for three unauthorized debit card charges totaling approximately $2,900 ($970.24 each) at an Orlando Apple Store on October 22. The member stated she did not authorize the charges, does not live near Orlando, and was unable to access the investigation documents emailed to her. Additionally, the member mentioned she is nine months pregnant, that the charges nearly drained her account, and threatened to file a police report, consult an attorney, and submit complaints to credit bureaus and the Consumer Finance Department. The representative submitted a formal complaint regarding the situation, emailed the investigation team concerning the inaccessible documents, and informed the member to monitor her phone and email for follow-up from higher-level review teams regarding the appeal.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding the denial of disputes and subsequent appeals for three unauthorized Apple Store charges totaling approximately $2,900, as well as difficulties accessing the associated investigation documents. Operational review determined that the dispute investigations and appeal evaluations were formally completed, with the findings supporting the original denial decisions and leaving no further internal recourse available under standard review procedures. To address the complaint, the specialist provided direct written notice of the final claim outcomes, communicated the toll-free support line for ongoing inquiries, and clarified that reconsiderations strictly require the submission of new, previously unreviewed supporting evidence. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute denials upheld and the customer's account remaining in active standing pending the submission of any new documentation.
