## Complaint
- The member was charged a $10 monthly fee that they were not expecting, leading them to close their account. They feel this fee is discriminatory as other members do not have the same charge.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding an unexpected $10.00 monthly fee, which led to their decision to close their account and raise concerns that the charge was discriminatory compared to other accounts. The specialist acknowledged the member's concerns and confirmed that an account review showed no $10.00 monthly fee had been assessed by SoFi on their profile records. To resolve the discrepancy, the specialist invited the member to call 855-456-7634 directly if the charge still appeared on their end so it could be investigated further.
