## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding an unauthorized **$7.99** debit card transaction from `PP*GOOGLE OFFERUP INC` posted on August 8, 2026, which was debited from their savings account as an unapproved subscription charge. The member voiced intense frustration that other concurrent suspicious transactions were declined while this specific charge was approved, criticizing SoFi's fraud monitoring systems for failing to protect their account. Support initiated a debit card dispute for the $7.99 transaction, recommended card reissuance to prevent future unauthorized recurring charges, and documented the security concern for fraud investigation.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong dissatisfaction regarding an unauthorized $7.99 debit card charge from PP*GOOGLE OFFERUP INC on August 8, 2026, which posted to their savings account as an unapproved subscription. The member expressed intense frustration that SoFi's fraud monitoring systems approved this specific charge while declining other concurrent suspicious transactions. The specialist apologized for the security breach, confirmed that the $7.99 transaction had officially posted, and requested that the member call customer support directly to file a formal dispute and initiate a debit card reissuance to prevent future unauthorized charges.
