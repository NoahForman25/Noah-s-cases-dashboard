## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding a security compromise after discovering that an unauthorized individual was able to access their account and update the email address and phone number on file. The member was informed that a caller had previously accessed the profile after successfully completing automated verification via the Interactive Voice Response (IVR) system to inquire about the account balance, though no changes were made during that specific interaction. The member expressed significant dissatisfaction with the institution's reliance on automated system verification rather than manual identity validation to confirm the caller's true identity. The agent provided empathy, offered an apology for the situation, and initiated a formal complaint on the member's behalf.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their severe frustration and safety concerns regarding a security compromise where an unauthorized individual updated the profile's email address and phone number, following a separate incident where a caller bypassed security via the Interactive Voice Response (IVR) system to check the account balance. The specialist validated the member's dissatisfaction with automated verification systems, issued an apology, and confirmed that internal coaching was delivered to the involved agent to reinforce security protocols. Furthermore, the specialist outlined the active recovery steps being taken, including a current protective account restriction, an ongoing investigation under a dedicated ticket to address the unauthorized activity and the resulting $99.00 negative balance, and the requirement for a successful identity verification once the member's camera issues are resolved to safely restore full access.
