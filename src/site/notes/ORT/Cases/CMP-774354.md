## Complaint
- Member contacted to express extreme frustration regarding excessively long hold times, stating they had been on the phone for two hours—and nearly the entire day—attempting to reach the dispute team without success. The member inquired about the optimal time to call back to secure an immediate connection. They were provided with accurate, realistic scheduling information regarding department availability, ensuring no unrealistic expectations were set. Additionally, the member requested to speak with a supervisor, and this escalation request was noted and processed.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their extreme frustration regarding excessively long hold times, noting the member had been waiting on the phone for two hours to reach the dispute team without success, and addressed their supervisor escalation request and inquiry regarding optimal call times. The specialist processed the escalation request and provided realistic scheduling information regarding department availability to set accurate expectations for future contacts. Delivering a clear resolution regarding their broader account access concerns, the specialist confirmed that while separate issues had impacted their joint account, a comprehensive review verified that the member's individual checking and savings account, along with their associated Zelle profile, remain fully active with no restrictions.
