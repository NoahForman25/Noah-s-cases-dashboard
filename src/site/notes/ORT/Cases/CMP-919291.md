## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding the denial of their personal loan application due to insufficient income. The member explained that they were self-employed, had provided a 1099 form showing an income over $130,000, and had requested extra time to submit their 1040 tax form, but the application was closed and denied before they could upload it. The member questioned the reason for denial, alleged discrimination, and expressed frustration over the representative's suggestion to add a co-borrower before deciding to reapply with all required tax documents.

- Dear Ashley.

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. We acknowledge your complaint regarding the denial of your Personal Loan application and your concern that the decision was based on discriminatory factors. We recognize the frustration caused by the denial, particularly given that your application was closed before you were able to upload your 1040 tax form.
