## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding delayed access to $200 from a declined Food Lion cash-back transaction under dispute ticket #8835852 and the resulting financial hardship. To address the complaint, the specialist apologized for the extended timeframe, submitted an urgent hardship escalation on the customer's behalf, and provided transparent written guidance regarding the mandatory review procedures.
