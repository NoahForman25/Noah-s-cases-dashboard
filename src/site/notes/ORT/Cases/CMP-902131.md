## Complaint
- The member requested a manager due to deep frustration over their personal loan application expiring while waiting for a signature. The member stated they spent two weeks trying to sign, but an incorrect email address blocked their access despite contacting support multiple times and following instructions without resolution. Although the member's husband completed his portion, system errors prevented the member from signing. After being told the application expired and could not be reopened, the member protested the unfairness, insisted the issue was SoFi's fault, and demanded a reconsideration of their application.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist reached out to the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the expiration of their personal loan application caused by an incorrect email address and login errors during the signature phase. Operational review confirmed that the application expired before the signing process could be finalized due to unresolved delivery and access blockers, and system constraints prevent expired applications from being reopened. To resolve the complaint, the specialist acknowledged the service delays, apologized for the communication issues, and informed the customer of the option to submit a new application. The complaint is resolved, with the expired application remaining closed and any future loan request requiring a new application subject to standard underwriting review.
