## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding a pending pre-authorization debit card hold of **$69.85** from a gas station transaction (`529 GENEVA AVE`, dated August 06, 2026) that caused their account balance to go negative.

  The member was frustrated upon learning that the pending status cannot be manually removed or expedited, that the negative balance cannot be cleared preemptively, and that they must wait for the standard authorization hold release process, which is estimated to complete by August 15, 2026.

  Support empathized with the situation, explained that pre-authorization hold durations are governed by standard card network processing rules, and submitted an official complaint for higher-level review, advising the member that a response would be provided within 24 to 48 business hours.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an inquiry regarding a pending $69.85 pre-authorization debit card hold from a gas station transaction (529 GENEVA AVE, dated August 06, 2026) that caused their account balance to go negative. After authenticating the member, the agent listened to their strong dissatisfaction regarding visits to the station without resolution, as well as their concerns regarding their overall balance and an upcoming unemployment deposit.

  

The agent reassured the member that no funds would be lost once the hold expires and clarified that pre-authorization hold durations are governed by standard card network processing rules, meaning the pending status cannot be manually removed, expedited, or preemptively cleared before its scheduled expiration on August 15, 2026. To address the member's frustration and balance impact, the specialist submitted a formal complaint for higher-level operational review, advising the member that a follow-up response would be provided within 24 to 48 business hours.
