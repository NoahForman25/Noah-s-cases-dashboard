## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration and requested to speak with a supervisor regarding a review that determined they are no longer eligible to apply for any SoFi products in the future.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an account review that deemed him ineligible for future products, as well as his request to speak with a supervisor. Operational review determined that the administrative restrictions previously placed on the member's profile were eligible for removal, allowing full access to be reinstated. To resolve the complaint, the specialist confirmed that the restrictions were dropped, restored login access across his accounts, and provided a direct phone number for further technical assistance if needed. The customer's account is now fully accessible, unrestricted, and in good standing.
