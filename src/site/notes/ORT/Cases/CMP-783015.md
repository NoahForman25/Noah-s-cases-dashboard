## Complaint
- Member contacted to express confusion and dissatisfaction regarding conflicting communications about a withdrawal request. The member noted they were initially advised that the transaction was approved, but subsequently received an automated email stating the request had been denied, leaving them unclear on the actual timeline and approval process.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding conflicting communications about a withdrawal from their Robo IRA. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's confusion and dissatisfaction regarding automated notices that indicated the request was denied after they were initially told it was approved. Delivering a clear update on the transaction status, the agent explained that the IRA shares had been successfully sold and that the funds would be sent out shortly, while detailing the underlying reasons for the processing delays. The member provided critical feedback regarding the institution's communication gaps and stated they would not pursue future account rollovers due to this experience; the agent validated these concerns and assured the member that their feedback would be formally submitted for future improvements.
