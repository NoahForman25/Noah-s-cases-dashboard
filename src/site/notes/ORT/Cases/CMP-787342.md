## Complaint
- Member initiated a chat session to dispute an automatic $11.99 transfer from their Vault to cover a transaction, stating that their Spending Reserve feature had been intentionally turned off to prevent such actions. The member expressed serious concerns regarding what they perceived as unfair or deceptive practices by the institution. Consequently, the member requested an immediate escalation to management, indicated an intent to file formal regulatory complaints, and requested a complete copy of the chat transcript for their records.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address a complaint regarding an automatic $11.99 transfer from their Vault to cover a transaction, which occurred despite the member explicitly turning off the Spending Reserve feature. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's initial concerns regarding deceptive practices, their request for a management escalation and chat transcript, and their intent to file regulatory complaints. The member provided screenshot evidence showing the setting was disabled, prompting the agent to acknowledge the system error, submit a technical support ticket, and credit $20.00 in promotional points to the account as compensation. Upon following up after the technical issue was resolved, the member confirmed the feature was working correctly and clarified that the original transfer was triggered by an authorized Adobe transaction they had forgotten about; the agent subsequently advised the member to request a refund directly from Adobe and call back to file a formal dispute if necessary.
