## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding the denial of their student loan application due to an inability to verify income, despite having exceptional credit. The member explained that they experienced a 404 error when attempting to upload income documents directly into the system, which led an agent to provide a secure email link to submit the documentation prior to a hard credit pull. The member voiced frustration over being instructed to reapply, stating it was unfair to undergo the process again due to a technical error on SoFi's end and expressing concern that the system error would recur.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding the denial of their student loan application after encountering a system 404 error when uploading income documentation. Operational review confirmed that the application was declined under credit policy guidelines because required income verification documents were not successfully processed through the portal prior to evaluation. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, clarified that technical submission gaps prevented verification during the initial review, and provided guidance on alternative document submission methods for future applications. The complaint is resolved, with the original decision maintained under credit policy rules and clear submission channels established should the customer choose to submit a new application.
