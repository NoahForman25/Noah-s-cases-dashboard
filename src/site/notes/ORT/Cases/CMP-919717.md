## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction and frustration, stating they had been on the phone with SoFi for two days attempting to get their money back. They requested an immediate escalation to speak with a supervisor, agreed to receive a supervisor callback, and scheduled a supervisor callback.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding delayed access to retrieved funds and incurred late fees. Operational review confirmed that retrieving the misrouted funds requires a formal Hold Harmless Letter directly from the originating institution, Global Credit Union, before SoFi can process the release. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided the customer with the designated email address where Global Credit Union must submit the documentation and scheduled an escalated supervisor callback to ensure ongoing oversight. The complaint is resolved, with clear documentation requirements established and a supervisor callback scheduled to monitor the retrieval process once the credit union submits the letter.
