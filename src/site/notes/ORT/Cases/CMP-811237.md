## Complaint
- The member was frustrated because they could not access the funds in their checking account due to a pending transaction hold, despite believing the transfer had already completed. Expressing dissatisfaction with the delay, the member questioned the hold and complained about having to wait longer to access their money.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding a pending transaction hold on a $100.00 deposit that prevented them from accessing funds in their checking account. The specialist acknowledged the member's frustration over the delay and wait time, addressing their belief that the transfer had already completed. Explaining standard banking protocols, the specialist clarified that processing timeframes are required for deposits to fully clear and confirmed that the pending hold is scheduled to drop on July 28, 2026, at which point the $100.00 will become fully accessible in their account.
