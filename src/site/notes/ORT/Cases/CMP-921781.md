## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding receiving a lower APY than expected and stated that the qualification requirements were not clearly understood. The member voiced frustration with needing a direct deposit or qualifying monthly deposits to earn the highest APY rate and considered closing the account due to the discrepancy between expected and actual rates.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email following a disconnected phone call to address their complaint regarding earning a lower annual percentage yield (APY) than expected and confusion surrounding deposit requirements. Operational review confirmed that the account did not receive the boosted yield because the required qualifying direct deposit or monthly deposit thresholds were not met, which stemmed from unclear qualification criteria during initial evaluation. To resolve the complaint, the specialist applied a complimentary 90-day APY override to automatically grant the maximum 3.3% rate regardless of deposit activity and provided information on SoFi Plus subscription options for future yield benefits. The complaint is resolved, with the boosted interest rate active on the account for 90 days and clear promotional guidelines outlined for long-term eligibility.
