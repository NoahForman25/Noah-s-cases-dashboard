## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an inability to complete account creation with SoFi, as they were not receiving the required verification email to set up their account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding an inability to complete account creation due to non-receipt of the required verification email. An internal investigation confirmed that delivery failures occurred because the customer's email provider blocked incoming messages from SoFi or lacked sufficient inbox storage space. To resolve the issue, the specialist advised the customer to check their inbox capacity and spam filters, or restart the application process using an alternative email address. The complaint is resolved with clear next steps provided to enable successful account setup upon updating their email settings or details.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact

If the verification email is still not received after completing these steps, you may attempt the account creation process again using a different email address.
