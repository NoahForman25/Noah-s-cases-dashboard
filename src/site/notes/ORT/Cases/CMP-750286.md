## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction regarding the handling of their dispute appeal, demanding an immediate escalation to a supervisor. The member expressed frustration over the review process and the perceived lack of coordination, stating:

"I made another appeal yesterday and submitted documentation that proves that I was inpatient in the hospital when the charges were made. That is unacceptable. I submitted the documentation you’re referring to yesterday. To the appeal team."
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their severe dissatisfaction regarding the handling of their dispute appeal and their demand for an immediate supervisor escalation due to a perceived lack of operational coordination. The specialist validated the member's frustration concerning the time-sensitive hospital documentation submitted the prior day, which proved they were an inpatient when the contested charges occurred. To resolve the issue promptly, the specialist confirmed that the appeal request and supporting medical records are currently active and have been requested to be fast-tracked under an expedited review, reassuring the member that a status update will be provided as soon as the investigation is finalized.
