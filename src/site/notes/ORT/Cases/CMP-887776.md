## Complaint
- The member called to dispute the denial of their SLR application, stating they checked with an agent beforehand to verify that their educational institution and loan servicer were eligible before applying. They noted that friends with loans serviced by MPower were approved, leading them to believe their application was declined in error or unfairly. The member requested that a manager re-evaluate the application and call them back as soon as possible.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist followed up with the customer via phone call to address their dispute regarding the denial of their student loan refinance application and allegations of misleading eligibility information. Operational review confirmed that loans originating from ineligible international banking institutions cannot be refinanced under policy guidelines, regardless of the current loan servicer or external social media claims. To resolve the member's dissatisfaction and process their explicit request, the specialist submitted a formal personal data deletion request for all information associated with the application. The complaint is resolved, with the application remaining declined and the data deletion request currently processing according to standard regulatory timelines.
