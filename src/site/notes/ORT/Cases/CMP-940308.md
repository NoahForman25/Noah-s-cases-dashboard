## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction and frustration regarding the closure of their account without prior notification. The agent explained that the account was closed due to an unpaid negative balance that began on August 20, 2026, noting that the member was given 57 days to make a payment and that the single deposit received during that time was insufficient to clear the balance. In response to the member's mention of a $60 deposit collected by SoFi, the agent verified that because the account was closed, the deposit was not accepted and would be returned to the sender. The member stated they would file a complaint and ended the chat.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the unnotified closure of their account and the handling of a recent deposit. Operational review confirmed that email notifications were dispatched prior to action and that the account was closed following a 57-day period with an unpaid negative balance that was not cleared by a partial deposit. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided transparency regarding the closure timeline and confirmed that the pending $60 deposit was rejected due to the closed status and is being returned to the original sender. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining permanently closed and ineligible for re-opening under standard policy guidelines.
