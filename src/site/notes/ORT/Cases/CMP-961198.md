## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding an unexpected $154 overdraft, recurring subscription charges from DoorDash and MoneyLion, the lack of proactive fraud alerts, and extended waiting periods for dispute investigations.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's complaint regarding recurring unauthorized transactions from DoorDash and MoneyLion, as well as dissatisfaction with overdraft fee notifications. Operational review confirmed that four previous disputes had been approved with a $40 refund credited, though ongoing merchant debits persisted due to active billing tokens and limitations with standard stop-payment controls. To resolve the complaint, the specialist reviewed preventative measures including un-enrolling the card from the Mastercard automatic billing updater program, formally initiated dispute claims for three unauthorized MoneyLion transactions under an estimated 7 to 10 business day timeline, and submitted operational feedback regarding account overdraft alerts. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's checking account remaining open and active while the newly submitted dispute claims proceed through standard investigation.

### First contact
- Reason for Call

SoFi called the member to address unauthorized transactions on their account related to DoorDash and MoneyLion.
Key Events

The member agreed to dispute three specific transactions with MoneyLion that they did not authorize.
