## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration and dissatisfaction regarding ongoing transaction declines. The member emphasized that this is the fourth consecutive day they have encountered these issues, and they are highly dissatisfied that the underlying problem remains unresolved.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and could not reach there voicemail. So, emailed the member to address their frustration and dissatisfaction regarding ongoing transaction declines experienced over four consecutive days. The specialist delivered a clear resolution, explaining that the recent declines were triggered by standard security verifications designed to protect the account, which will naturally decrease as the system recognizes the member's regular spending patterns over time. To resolve any persistent card issues, the specialist outlined the next steps, informing the member that they can request a replacement debit card directly through their online account or have the resolution team process the order immediately upon request.
