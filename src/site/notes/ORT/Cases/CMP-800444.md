## Complaint
- Member contacted expressing frustration over a missing referral bonus payout. The agent explained that the referral did not qualify due to the specific deposit type used for funding and outlined the eligible funding options for the promotion. When the agent directed the member to the terms and conditions on the referral website, the member stated "No one reads that," expressed further dissatisfaction with accusations regarding the agent's attention and care, and disconnected the call.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding a missing referral bonus payout for her husband's account, following a previous call where she expressed frustration after learning the deposit type used did not qualify under promotion terms and disconnected. After authenticating the member, the agent addressed her dissatisfaction with the referral process and delivered a partial resolution by manually adding $25 worth of points (2,500 points) to her profile. The agent then discussed the member's concerns regarding fund transfer limitations and local transportation hurdles affecting her money access, explaining available transfer options and offering checkbooks. The member agreed to order checks and expressed appreciation for the rewards points added to her account.
