## Complaint
- The member expressed deep sadness and frustration after being declined for a new SoFi loan due to too many recent credit inquiries. The member explained that they were recently a victim of identity theft and fraud; although all fraudulent accounts were successfully caught, declined, or rectified, the resulting credit inquiries continue to negatively impact their credit profile. Emphasizing their long-standing membership and loyalty with SoFi, the member requested a manual reconsideration of their loan application. Support expressed sincere empathy regarding the fraud incident and explained the automated credit bureau evaluation rules that drive inquiry-based declines. Support guided the member on how to dispute and remove fraudulent inquiries directly with the major credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion) and submitted an escalation request to Lending Operations and Fraud Appeals to review the application context and determine if a manual reconsideration or re-evaluation is possible once bureau updates are initiated.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding technical issues when attempting to access the Invest section of the mobile app. The member had previously expressed frustration to the AI chatbot after the app repeatedly froze during investment management, demanding that technical support fix the performance issues before the chat interaction ended. The specialist apologized for the disruption, acknowledging that the Invest platform should operate seamlessly, and confirmed that the report regarding the app freezing behavior was formally documented for the technical team to investigate. Additionally, the specialist requested that the member reach out directly if the issues persist, noting that providing specific device details and error messages would help technical support diagnose and resolve the issue as quickly as possible.
