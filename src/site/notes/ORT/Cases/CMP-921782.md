## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding the 10-business-day review timeframe required to receive provisional credit. The member noted that their other bank only takes 24 hours to provide provisional credit.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the 10-business-day timeframe required to receive provisional credit compared to faster timelines at other financial institutions. Operational review confirmed that the dispute claim remains under active investigation within standard regulatory evaluation windows. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided transparency regarding dispute guidelines, clarifying that provisional credit is automatically issued if the investigation extends past 10 business days, or a final determination is communicated sooner if completed early. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute claim remaining actively under review and clear conditional timelines established for provisional credit issuance.
