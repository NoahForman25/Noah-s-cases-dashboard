## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding the pending closure of their account due to fraud flags. They explicitly disagreed with the decision and requested a more detailed explanation of the specific reasons for the closure.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding the pending closure of their account following disputed Fanatics charges and the lack of communication surrounding the claim denials. Operational review confirmed that the dispute was evaluated and denied in accordance with fraud review protocols, which subsequently triggered a mandatory account restriction and pending closure due to policy violations. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding the closure determination, sent official claim denial documentation via email, and clarified the operational grounds for the decision. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute investigation finalized and the account remaining restricted in pending closure status.
