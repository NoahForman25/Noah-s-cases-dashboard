## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding a negative balance in their SoFi Checking account resulting from a returned check they had requested to cancel. The member stated they did not realize the funds spent were from the deposited check and felt SoFi was responsible for making the funds available before subsequently returning the check.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a negative balance of $463.31 in their checking account resulting from a revoked, cancelled check deposit. Operational review determined that provisional funds were made partially available while standard check processing was underway, and the subsequent reversal triggered by the customer's own cancellation request appropriately revoked those funds, causing the overdrawn balance. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided clear operational transparency regarding check clearing and funds availability rules, formally advising the customer that they remain responsible for depositing funds to cover the negative balance. The complaint is resolved, with the checking account remaining open in a negative balance status pending the customer's deposit to cure the deficit.
