## Complaint
- Member contacted to express significant frustration while attempting to purchase an airline ticket from American Airlines. The member explained that the institution's security system successfully generated and sent a verification code to complete the transaction; however, the merchant's website provided no corresponding field or option to input the code, completely blocking the purchase. As a result of this technical impasse and the inability to process the transaction, the member requested an immediate escalation to speak with a supervisor.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on an escalation request regarding their significant frustration over a blocked American Airlines ticket purchase, which was caused by a technical impasse where the merchant's site lacked a field to input the security verification code. Upon receiving the call, the member expressed discomfort with providing their full name and date of birth for phone authentication. The agent validated the member's security concerns and agreed to transition the communication. Before concluding the call, the member noted that they had already resolved the ticket issue by contacting the airline directly, and the agent assured them that a detailed follow-up email would be sent to their address on file.
