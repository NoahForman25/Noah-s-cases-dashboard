## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding account access issues, noting that they are unable to take incoming support calls due to their work schedule and providing their email address as the preferred method for assistance. Support acknowledged the communication constraint and documented the login error details. Support then escalated the login blocker to Technical Support and Account Operations for review, requesting that all updates and resolution steps be communicated directly to the member via email.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding account access issues and a persistent login blocker, honoring their request to conduct all communications via email. Technical Support and Account Operations conducted an investigation into the login error and successfully resolved the blocker once necessary visual details were reviewed. To resolve the matter, the specialist confirmed that full account access had been restored and verified that the customer's loan remains in active repayment status. The complaint is resolved, with the technical support ticket officially closed and clear support contact channels provided for any future needs.
