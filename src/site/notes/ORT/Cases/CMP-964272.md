## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration that a charge was allowed to process and requested a thorough review of the situation, stating they feel the bank's security is inadequate as a result.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an unauthorized $50.00 transaction from EPOCH.COM and concerns regarding account security controls. Operational review confirmed that the charge had posted to the account and required formal claims processing through fraud investigation workflows. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that a dispute was officially initiated for the $50.00 charge, provided reassurance regarding ongoing security oversight, and extended an apology for the negative experience. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute actively in progress under standard investigation and the customer's account remaining open and monitored in good standing.
