## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration that prior disputes for three unauthorized transactions were declined, stating he felt accused of lying. Out of frustration with SoFi for not reimbursing him following an account takeover (ATO) incident, the member stated he cut up his debit card and deleted the mobile app. Additionally, the member was upset by an email claiming the transactions were consistent with his typical activity, affirming that this was untrue and that he did not process the Truist Bank deposit or the subsequent unauthorized transactions.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding the denial of dispute #885657 for $2,095, allegations of account takeover, and frustration over prior messaging stating the transactions matched his typical activity. Operational review confirmed that the original dispute determination stands based on available account history and that the claim remains closed under standard review criteria. To address the complaint, the specialist apologized for the customer's negative experience, provided the main customer support phone number, and clearly outlined the formal appeals process, clarifying that reconsideration strictly requires obtaining and submitting new supporting documentation. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute denial upheld and the customer's account pending the submission of any new evidence to initiate a formal re-investigation.
