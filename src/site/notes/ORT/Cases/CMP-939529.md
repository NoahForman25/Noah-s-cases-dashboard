## Complaint
- The member expressed strong frustration and used profanity regarding a $35 Planet Fitness charge that resulted in a negative account balance. The member stated they did not authorize an overdraft on their account, insisted the bank was legally required to decline the transaction if funds were unavailable, and demanded the charge be reversed. The agent explained that the bank does not enroll members in fitness plans, offered to file a dispute if requested, and noted that recurring merchant charges must be addressed with the merchant directly or routed to a higher-level support team. The member refused to answer the agent's dispute intake questions, used derogatory language toward the agent, and stated they will no longer do business with SoFi.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a $35 Planet Fitness charge that resulted in a negative account balance and their assertion that the transaction should have been declined. Operational review confirmed that recurring Planet Fitness transactions have posted to the account since February 2026 under pre-authorized debit terms, and that no formal claim was initiated due to incomplete intake questions during prior contact. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided instructions on how to cancel the recurring membership directly with the merchant to prevent future postings and offered to complete the dispute intake process for the charges already posted. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining open in good standing pending the customer's decision to complete the formal dispute intake.
