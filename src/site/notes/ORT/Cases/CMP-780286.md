## Complaint
- Member initiated a formal complaint against SoFi regarding a canceled DoorDash order, expressing shock and intense frustration that the initial dispute was resolved in the merchant's favor without clear justification. The member explicitly accused SoFi of theft, stating that the institution "stole" his money by retaining the charge even though DoorDash fulfilled its obligation by issuing a refund. As supporting documentation to challenge the initial outcome, the member provided the official DoorDash cancellation and refund confirmation email. An appeal has been formally submitted on behalf of the member, who is demanding a prompt resolution and immediate reimbursement of the $50.18 balance.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their formal complaint, intense frustration, and accusation of theft regarding a denied $50.18 DoorDash transaction dispute that was initially resolved in the merchant's favor. The specialist delivered a clear update, confirming that an official appeal has been successfully submitted on the member's behalf to challenge the initial outcome. Additionally, the specialist verified that the DoorDash cancellation and refund confirmation email provided by the member has been included as supporting documentation, and noted that the dispute team is actively conducting a secondary review and will provide official updates directly via email once a final determination is reached.
