## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a $72.37 pending authorization hold on their checking account from Stop A Minute after purchasing $15.00 in gas on October 11, 2026. The member voiced frustration that the hold resulted in a negative account balance, preventing them from completing other intended transactions and forcing them to use credit cards instead. The agent explained that pre-authorization holds at gas stations are determined by the merchant to verify sufficient funds, confirmed that SoFi cannot release pending holds or process a dispute until the transaction posts, and advised that the hold is expected to expire on October 17, 2026.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a $72.37 pending authorization hold from Stop A Minute following a $15.00 fuel purchase that temporarily created a negative account balance. Operational review confirmed that fuel merchants automatically place temporary pre-authorization holds to verify fund availability before the final fuel transaction amount is calculated and settled. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided clarity on how merchant pre-authorization holds function and confirmed that the $72.37 pending hold has been successfully released from the customer's checking account. The complaint is resolved, with the authorization hold cleared and the account balance returned to normal operating status.

The hold amount is set by the merchant and typically exceeds the purchase amount, as the actual fuel total is not known until the pump session is complete.
