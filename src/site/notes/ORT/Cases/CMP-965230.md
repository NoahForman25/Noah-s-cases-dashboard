## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a $50,000.00 check deposit that has been rejected multiple times over the course of a week, despite troubleshooting by using different devices, turning off the camera flash, and using a suitable surface. The member noted that an email from tech support stated the matter is under investigation with no resolution yet, and referenced a prior ticket case number. After an initial disconnected call and an unanswered follow-up, the member called back, reiterated frustration over the ongoing delay, and requested to speak with a supervisor. The agent apologized, explained that because troubleshooting was complete they must wait for the tech ticket to process, and stated the situation would be escalated further.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding the repeated technical rejections of a $50,000.00 check deposit over a one-week period. Operational review revealed that previous technical support ticket MTS-125002 had been prematurely closed despite ongoing mobile capture failures caused by camera contrast issues with the blue check background. To resolve the complaint, the specialist reopened the technical support ticket in the priority escalations queue with the customer's new screenshots and provided specific operational guidance to capture the blue check against a solid, dark-colored, non-reflective backdrop in a well-lit environment. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's account remaining active and in good standing pending a re-attempt of the mobile check deposit or further technical escalation if the rejections persist.
