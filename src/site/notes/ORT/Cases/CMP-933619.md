## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a interaction with a previous representative who discussed their transactions specifically with the merchant ONLYFANS.COM*A, stating that the representative shamed them. The member requested compensation, stating that 3,000 Reward Points was insufficient for the shame experienced, and indicated an intent to raise the issue with an attorney.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding inappropriate comments made by a previous representative concerning merchant transactions and a declined debit card purchase. Operational review confirmed that the card transaction decline at CTLP*VIVO PORT ROYAL was caused by a token authentication requirement for card-present transactions rather than an account restriction, and verified that the customer's debit card remains fully active and operational. To resolve the complaint, the specialist apologized for the unprofessional tone experienced during the prior call, provided clear context regarding transaction security questions, and verified card functionality across other merchants. The complaint is resolved, with the debit card confirmed active and operational guidelines reinforced for future customer interactions.
