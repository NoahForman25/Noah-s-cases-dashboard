## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction after unrecognized debit card transactions were allowed to process, driving their account into a negative balance and causing direct financial distress, while also voicing frustration over having to repeat details previously provided in chat. The member formally reported three unauthorized charges: a posted **$5.00** transaction from `AURA.STAROLY` on August 22, 2026, alongside pending transactions from `AURA.STAROLY` for **$14.50** on August 23, 2026, and `DM29*PFIVERY.COM` for **$35.93** on August 27, 2026. Support secured the account against further unauthorized activity, initiated fraud disputes for the unrecognized charges, and logged an operational service complaint regarding repeated customer communications and the negative balance impact for priority review by Fraud Operations.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong dissatisfaction regarding unauthorized debit card transactions that drove their account into a negative balance, as well as their frustration over having to repeat account details previously shared in chat. The member had formally reported three unrecognized charges: a posted $5.00 transaction from AURA.STAROLY on August 22, 2026, and two pending charges—a $14.50 transaction from AURA.STAROLY on August 23, 2026, and a $35.93 transaction from DM29*PFIVERY.COM on August 27, 2026. The specialist confirmed that support secured the account against further unauthorized activity and filed a formal fraud dispute for the posted $5.00 AURA.STAROLY charge. Regarding the pending transactions, the specialist apologized for the negative balance impact and explained that pending charges cannot be formally disputed until they fully post to the account. The specialist instructed the member to call support once the $14.50 and $35.93 charges transition to posted status so that formal fraud disputes can be submitted immediately for those remaining items, while confirming that an operational service complaint regarding repeated communications was logged for internal review.
