## Complaint
- Member contacted to express significant dissatisfaction regarding a two-week lack of access to their banking account due to an ongoing restriction. It was explained to the member that the restriction cannot be lifted until the pending dispute investigation is fully completed and new accounts are established; however, the member remained frustrated by the prolonged turnaround time and the continued disruption to their funds.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding a two-week lack of access to their banking account due to an ongoing restriction. After completing identity verification using the member's name and date of birth, the agent addressed the member's significant dissatisfaction regarding the prolonged turnaround time and the continued disruption to their funds. Delivering a clear update on the resolution process, the agent explained that the restriction must remain in place until the pending dispute investigation is fully completed. The agent reassured the member that an representative would reach out later to assist with establishing new accounts and officially reopening their profile once the "all-clear" is given.
