## Complaint
- Support educated the member on the requirements for the promotional direct deposit bonus. The agent reviewed the 25-day evaluation period following the initial qualifying direct deposit, explained the required cumulative direct deposit thresholds ($1,000–$4,999.99 for $50; $5,000+ for $400), and confirmed that the member met the threshold to earn the $50 bonus.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding the payout amount received for the Direct Deposit Welcome Bonus. The specialist apologized for the experience and clarified the terms of the offer, explaining that bonus amounts are calculated based on cumulative qualifying direct deposits received during a strict 25-day evaluation period starting from the first eligible deposit. The specialist outlined the promotional tiers—$50 for total deposits between $1,000.00 and $4,999.99, and $400 for deposits totaling $5,000.00 or more—and confirmed that because the member's account received $3,000.00 during the evaluation window, it correctly qualified for the $50 tier.
