## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding the loan agreement terms and payment schedule. Although the member signed the loan agreement on September 16 and the co-borrower signed on September 29, the agreement reflected an extended date of October 16, and a request was made today to restart the agreement date. When informed that her first payment would be due on October 17, the member complained that having the due date so close to the signing date was unfair. The agent advised that the complaint would remain open and that a manager would follow up with her within one day.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding loan agreement terms and dissatisfaction with the initial payment due date. Operational review confirmed that while the loan agreement was signed on September 16, 2026, and the co-borrower signed on September 29, 2026, the application remains in the funding stage, preventing manual adjustments to the established October 17, 2026 first payment date. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding loan origination guidelines and outlined the process for requesting a due-date modification after the initial payment successfully clears. The complaint is resolved, with the loan actively proceeding through the funding stage and a clear path established to evaluate a payment due-date adjustment once the first payment settles.
