## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a partial hold placed on their account following a wire transaction. The member stated that after completing verification, SoFi held not only the $60,000 wire amount but also an additional $12,600, leaving them with insufficient funds for urgent business needs. The member noted that no follow-up call was received after initial verification, previous escalations went unanswered, and a promised escalation email was never delivered. The member expressed significant distress and frustration over the delay and lack of communication, and requested to be connected directly with the internal review team to override or expedite the hold, stating that the October 15 release date was too late for their needs.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding temporary security holds placed on their account following a wire transaction and delays in communication regarding the hold duration. Operational review confirmed that a review hold was applied to the funds in accordance with standard risk policies and established verification timelines, which could not be expedited or manually overridden. To resolve the complaint, the specialist apologized for the communication gaps and unreceived escalation updates, provided clear transparency regarding verification review schedules, and verified that the security holds were set to release in full on October 15, 2026. The complaint is resolved, with the security holds expiring as scheduled and complete access to the held funds restored to the customer's account.
