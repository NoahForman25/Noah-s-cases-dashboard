## Complaint
- The member called to follow up on their student loan refinance application, expressing frustration that a UAS loan was rejected from the refinance and that they received no notification regarding the rejection after uploading requested documentation. The agent confirmed the documents were reviewed and rejected on September 16, noting a discrepancy with a 2025 date on the co-signer information. The agent informed the member that the application requires a co-signer release or takeover to proceed with UAS loans, submitted a complaint regarding the lack of clear communication, and requested that the personal loan reviewer reach out to the member directly within a few business days with further clarification.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the customer via email to address their grievance regarding the silent rejection of a UAS loan from their student loan refinance application and the missing status notification. Operational review confirmed that uploaded documents were rejected due to a date discrepancy on the co-signer documentation, and that refinancing UAS loans under standard guidelines mandates an active co-signer release or takeover. To resolve the complaint and address the communication gap, the specialist logged a formal grievance regarding the lack of customer notification and established a direct pathway for the customer to connect with a personal loan review agent to update the co-signer details. The complaint is resolved, with the refinance application remaining active and awaiting updated documentation to proceed.
