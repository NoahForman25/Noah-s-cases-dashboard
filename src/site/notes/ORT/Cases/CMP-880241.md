## Complaint
- The member expressed concern and frustration regarding a delayed loan disbursement, noting they had submitted a voided check over a week ago but were only now informed that it must be signed by a bank representative. The member explained that a medical condition prevents them from visiting a branch to obtain the signature, despite being an employee of that bank, and raised frustration over the lack of prior communication regarding this requirement. After support explained that failing to provide the signed document could prevent loan funding and suggested opening a SoFi Money account as an alternative disbursement method, the member requested an escalation. Support agreed to transfer the member to an Account Manager for further assistance and documented the communication gap regarding disbursement documentation requirements.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on a delayed loan disbursement and address their strong frustration regarding uncommunicated documentation requirements. The member had previously submitted a voided check over a week prior, only to be later informed that it required a bank representative's signature—a requirement that presented a severe hardship due to a medical condition preventing branch visits, despite the member being an employee of that financial institution. The specialist authenticated the member, acknowledged the communication gaps that caused the funding delay, and apologized for the inconvenience experienced throughout the process. After discussing the required paperwork and alternative funding options, the specialist awarded 1,500 reward points ($15.00) to the member's profile as a gesture of goodwill for the service friction while continuing to assist them with completing the disbursement requirements.
