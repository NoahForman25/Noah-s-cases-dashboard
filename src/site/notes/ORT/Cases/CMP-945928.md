## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an inability to dispute a pending transaction. The agent advised that pending transactions cannot be disputed directly and explained that the option may be available in the app or once posted, while also noting that the debit card would need to be canceled if the transaction was unauthorized. The member declined to cancel the card, requested to speak with another representative, and voiced disbelief about the process before hanging up the call. The agent logged a complaint regarding the interaction.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding a pending unauthorized transaction from King Size Rewards and dissatisfaction with the dispute process. Operational review confirmed that pending transactions must complete posting before a formal claim can be initiated, requiring identity verification prior to taking account-level action. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer using a one-time passcode, formally initiated the dispute process for the posted transaction, explained the claim resolution timeframes, and confirmed that a replacement debit card was issued to protect against future unauthorized charges. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute actively under review and the new replacement card issued to ensure account security.
