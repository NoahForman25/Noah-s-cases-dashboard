## Complaint
- The member expressed strong frustration after a scheduled autopay processed despite receiving previous assurances that the cancellation would take effect immediately and prevent the debit. Citing the unexpected withdrawal, the member demanded to be transferred to the Banking department to address the processed payment and address the account balance. Support acknowledged the discrepancy in communication regarding the autopay processing window, apologized for the unexpected transaction, and initiated a warm transfer to Banking Operations and Payments Support to review the payment status, explore reversal or return options, and confirm the permanent cancellation of the autopay schedule.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her strong frustration regarding an unexpected autopay withdrawal that processed after she was previously assured the payment schedule had been canceled. The member had previously demanded an immediate transfer to Banking Operations after the debit went through, seeking a payment reversal and an account balance review due to the conflicting agent guidance. The specialist reviewed the transaction timeline and clarified that autopay arrangements must be disabled at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled debit date to prevent processing. The specialist explained that because her cancellation request was submitted approximately 30 minutes before the payment posted, the system could not halt the queued transaction in time, but verified that the autopay schedule is now fully disabled to prevent any future automated debits.
