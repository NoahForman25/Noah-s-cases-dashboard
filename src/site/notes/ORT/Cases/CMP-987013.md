## Complaint
- The member expressed anger and frustration regarding a prolonged delay in withdrawing Solana from their crypto account due to a review for a crypto transfer. The member stated that this delay caused them to lose their apartment and indicated that they will be contacting the Better Business Bureau (BBB).
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an extended delay in processing a Solana withdrawal from his crypto account and the subsequent personal impact. Operational review confirmed that the transaction was subjected to a mandatory internal security review by the crypto team to verify transfer legitimacy and safeguard against unauthorized activity. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the review concluded successfully, issued an apology for the processing delay, and confirmed that the transaction was finalized and funds were made available. The complaint is resolved, with the cryptocurrency withdrawal completed on November 5 and the customer's account active and in good standing.
