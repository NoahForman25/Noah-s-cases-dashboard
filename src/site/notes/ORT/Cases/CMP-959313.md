## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi to regain access to his account, cancel his stolen debit card, and obtain his funds via a wire transfer (requesting MoneyGram or Western Union) to leave an unsafe living situation. The member explained that he is a disabled veteran without a phone, device, or login credentials due to repeated thefts, head injuries, and trauma. Representatives informed him that wire transfers can only be initiated by the member through the app or website to an external bank account, and that representatives cannot process wire transfers or wire funds to services like Western Union. Consequently, the only available method to disburse his funds upon account closure was a paper check sent via standard mail within 7 to 14 business days, with no expedited delivery option available. The member expressed strong dissatisfaction, stating that mailing a check to an unsecure location in 7 to 10 days was unacceptable, dangerous, and a failure to accommodate a disabled veteran, while criticizing SoFi's limitations as a virtual bank.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding account access difficulties, cancelling a stolen debit card, and the inability to disburse funds via third-party wire transfer to leave an unsafe environment. Operational review determined that institution policies restrict outbound wire transfers to self-directed online or in-app requests to external bank accounts, precluding representative-initiated wires to cash-pickup services such as MoneyGram or Western Union, while outdated contact details on file posed security risks for mailed disbursements. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding fund transfer rules and offered direct assistance to securely update the customer's mailing address and email on file to facilitate proper check delivery upon account closure. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's account pending profile address updates and final closure instructions to ensure safe disbursement of the remaining funds.
