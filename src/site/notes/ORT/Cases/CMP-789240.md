## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction with the institution's platform, characterizing it as "the worst system he has ever used." The member expressed intense frustration regarding difficulties in accessing his funds, asserting his belief that the system's design and operational barriers felt intentionally structured to prevent him from making withdrawals.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their severe dissatisfaction with the institution's platform, which the member characterized as "the worst system he has ever used." The specialist addressed the member's intense frustration and belief that the system's design and operational barriers were intentionally structured to prevent him from making withdrawals. Delivering a clear update on the account status, the specialist apologized for the inconvenience and confirmed that a comprehensive review of system records shows the withdrawal of the member's funds was already successfully processed and completed with the assistance of a previous agent.
