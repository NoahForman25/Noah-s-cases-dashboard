## Complaint
- The member called SoFi to request updates to their personal loan disbursement details following recent direct payments they made to their credit cards. They explained that their current card balances no longer match the initial debt consolidation payoff amounts listed on the application and requested that the disbursement schedule be modified accordingly to avoid overpaying those creditors. 
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on a previous inquiry regarding personal loan disbursement details and credit card payoff distributions. The member had previously contacted support to request updates to their debt consolidation payoff amounts, explaining that recent direct payments to their credit card accounts reduced the balances and required adjustments to the disbursement schedule to prevent overpaying those creditors. The specialist authenticated the member and reviewed the updated disbursement numbers. During the call, the member acknowledged the loan distribution details, confirmed their understanding of the refund process for any potential credit card overpayments, and accepted the final disbursement structure.
