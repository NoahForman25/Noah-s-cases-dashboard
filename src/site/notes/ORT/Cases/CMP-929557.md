## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a denied $50,000 wire transfer intended for an investment with a long-term friend, stating they have called multiple times over three days without resolution. The member voiced frustration over excessive hold times, unanswered call-back promises, and the delay in releasing the funds back to their account, which are currently held until October 14. They expressed concern that the funds cannot be re-transferred immediately, noted they would not have opened an account with SoFi had they anticipated these issues, and stated they may report the situation to the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau. In response, the agent explained that the wire was denied and placed on security hold, informed the member that the funds will be released by October 14 (or earlier if the review finishes sooner), provided the direct phone number for the Member Security Team, and filed a complaint on the member's behalf.
## Resolution
-  A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call and email correspondence with the customer to address their complaint regarding involuntary account closure, delayed disbursement of a $239,005.04 balance, and an uncredited $318.00 merchant dispute with Foot Locker. Operational review confirmed that a temporary $50,000 security hold on the account was scheduled to expire, allowing the full remaining balance of $239,005.04 to clear, while internal policies restricted disclosure of specific account closure drivers. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer's mailing address, verified that a total balance check would be issued and mailed within standard processing timelines, and provided compensation points while addressing the pending dispute status. The complaint is resolved, with the account permanently closed and the complete balance check issued to the customer's verified address.
