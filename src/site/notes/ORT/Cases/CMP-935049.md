## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after their physical debit card was deactivated following a dispute claim. The member requested immediate access to their funds and card, as well as phone support. The agent advised that direct transfers from chat to phone are unavailable, provided the support phone number (855-456-7634), and instructed the member to say "fraud" to the automated system to reach an agent.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the deactivation of their physical debit card following a filed dispute claim. Operational review confirmed that debit card deactivation and immediate card replacement are mandatory security protocols triggered upon filing a fraud dispute to protect the account from further unauthorized activity. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided transparency regarding card security policies and confirmed that a replacement debit card was successfully ordered and dispatched to the customer's address on file. The complaint is resolved, with the compromised card deactivated for security and the replacement debit card actively in transit.
