## Complaint
- Member contacted to express significant frustration regarding their inability to cancel their debit card. The agent offered to order a replacement card; however, this option could not be processed because the member's mailing address on file is outdated, and the member currently lacks a valid government-issued ID to update it due to recent incarceration. To secure the account in the interim, the agent proposed placing a temporary freeze on the card, but this security measure was also blocked because the member no longer has access to the registered phone number on file and cannot complete the identity verification process without a valid ID. The member requested to speak with an account manager to resolve these administrative barriers, but they ultimately disconnected the call while placed on hold waiting for the escalation team.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their significant frustration regarding an inability to cancel their debit card, update their account information, and the fact that they were disconnected while on hold waiting for an account manager. The specialist delivered a clear update regarding the administrative barriers on the profile, confirming that ordering a replacement card requires an updated mailing address and placing a temporary freeze on the card requires verified phone access. Because the member recently experienced incarceration and currently lacks both a valid government-issued ID and access to the phone number on file, the specialist clarified that standard banking protocols strictly require a valid ID to update personal information or implement security measures. Ultimately, the specialist advised the member to reach out once a new, valid ID is obtained so the institution can safely complete the verification process and secure the account.
