## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding a perceived hold on their funds, explaining they feel that an expected refund from Walgreens is being intentionally delayed or withheld by the institution. The member referenced multiple recent debit card transactions at Walgreens #17156, including charges on July 7 ($64.31), July 9 ($13.70 and $14.76), and July 12, 2026 ($43.22), for which they are awaiting the returned funds.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding a perceived hold on their funds and the feeling that expected refunds from Walgreens were being intentionally delayed or withheld. The specialist delivered a clear resolution after conducting a comprehensive review of the four specific transactions from July 7, July 9, and July 12, 2026. The specialist confirmed that all the requested refund amounts have been successfully processed, credited, and attributed to the account balance appropriately, with no active institutional holds remaining on those funds.
