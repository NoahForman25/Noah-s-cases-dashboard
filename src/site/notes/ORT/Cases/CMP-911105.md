## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after being unable to complete a $450 Pay a Friend transfer to his wife, stating the app indicated a $0 daily limit. After initial troubleshooting and a transfer to tech support, the agent clarified that the member had reached the $3,000 monthly Pay a Friend limit. When informed the limit could not be raised and offered a transfer to the banking department, the member declined, criticized SoFi as a low-class bank, and stated he would switch banks.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding an inability to complete a $450 Pay a Friend transfer due to app limit errors. Operational review confirmed that the transfer failed because the customer had reached the hard $3,000 monthly feature threshold, which is fixed by policy and cannot be manually raised. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding the hard system limits, explained how monthly caps calculate, and reviewed alternative transfer options to move funds between accounts. The complaint is resolved, with the Pay a Friend limits remaining subject to system parameters and normal reset schedules.
