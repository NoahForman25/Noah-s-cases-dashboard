## Complaint
- Member initiated a chat session to file an unauthorized dispute for a transaction from FUNMERCHANT.COM totaling $19.71 on July 10, 2026, stating they did not authorize the charge. The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding the dispute process, explicitly stating they did not want to cancel and replace their current debit card. It was explained to the member that card replacement is a standard security procedure for unauthorized claims to prevent further compromised activity. The dispute was successfully filed, and clear expectations were set regarding both the investigation timeframe and the delivery schedule for the new debit card.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address a complex complaint involving a dispute for an unauthorized $19.71 FUNMERCHANT.COM transaction, a mandatory debit card replacement, and technical difficulties linking the card to Google Wallet. After completing identity verification, the agent confirmed the dispute was filed and explained that replacing the debit card is a mandatory institutional safety protocol to prevent further fraud, despite the member's opposition to canceling it. The agent also addressed the member's identity theft concerns by advising them to update passwords, provided troubleshooting for the missing Google Wallet button, and submitted a technical ticket—which subsequently determined the member's Google Wallet and SoFi profile addresses must match exactly to successfully link the card.
