## Complaint
- The member is angry after failing to receive a scheduled callback from a supervisor. They requested to speak with a supervisor regarding income verification for a loan, which requires additional documentation or the addition of a co-borrower.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the customer via email to address their grievance regarding a missed supervisor callback and clarification on income verification requirements for their loan application. The specialist explained that additional documentation, specifically a K-1 form alongside the W-2, is standardly required for applicants with business ownership to verify that overall company financial performance supports ongoing wage amounts. To resolve the complaint and move the loan application forward, the customer was advised to either submit the requested K-1 documentation or elect to add a co-borrower to the application. The complaint is resolved with clear next steps provided to fulfill the underwriting guidelines.

### Second Contact
- Dear Jude,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. We acknowledge your complaint regarding the failure to receive a scheduled callback from a supervisor and your request to speak with a supervisor regarding income verification for your loan application.

We reviewed the matter and confirmed the reason additional documentation is required. Because you have ownership in the business, we are required to review the business income as a whole, not only the W-2 wage amount. Your K-1 form shows the portion of the business's profit or loss allocated to you. Reviewing both the W-2 and the K-1 helps us confirm that the wages paid by the business are supported by the business's overall financial performance and are likely to continue. This review is a required step in the income verification process for applicants with business ownership.

To move your application forward, please provide the requested K-1 documentation. Alternatively, adding a co-borrower remains an available option if you prefer not to submit the additional documentation.
