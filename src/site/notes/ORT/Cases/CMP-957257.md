## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi regarding two previously denied dispute claims of $50 each from University of Phoenix, stating that she is neither a student nor affiliated with the institution, has never conducted business with them, and felt SoFi should take her word rather than require impossible proof for unrecognized charges. The representative submitted an appeal for the two denied transactions, advising the member to expect an email update within 10 business days. After reviewing the account history and identifying additional related transactions, the representative also gathered details to file a new dispute for the remaining unauthorized University of Phoenix charges, while clarifying and excluding legitimate transactions from the University of Alabama and UAB Callahan Eye Hospital.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding two previously denied unauthorized transaction disputes of $50 each involving the University of Phoenix. Operational review confirmed that the original dispute denial stands unless an appeal is formally initiated with new supporting documentation, while the specific disputes requested for cancellation were successfully processed. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided transparency regarding the 10-business-day timeline for appeal decisions, outlined instructions for disputing any remaining unauthorized transactions directly through the mobile application, and cancelled the requested dispute claims. The complaint is resolved, with the prior claim denials standing and the account remaining open in good standing pending the customer's submission of additional documentation or new dispute filings.
