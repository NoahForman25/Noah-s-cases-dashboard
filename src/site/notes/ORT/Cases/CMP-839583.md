## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding unauthorized transactions on their account, consisting of a $1,000.00 Pay A Friend transfer sent to "Om So" and an unauthorized $500.00 Mobile Check Deposit. Support initiated immediate account security protocols to restrict further unauthorized access, guided the member through securing their credentials, and submitted fraud disputes for both the transfer and the mobile deposit for priority review by Fraud Operations.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong dissatisfaction regarding unauthorized transactions on their account, which included a $1,000.00 Pay A Friend transfer sent to "Om So" and an unauthorized $500.00 Mobile Check Deposit. While support had previously initiated immediate account security protocols to restrict further compromise, guided the member on securing their login credentials, and submitted fraud disputes for priority review, the member remained concerned about the handling and resolution of the claims. The specialist acknowledged the significant impact of these unauthorized transactions and confirmed that the member's appeal is actively under review by the dispute team. The specialist reassured the member that all submitted documentation is currently being evaluated and clarified that no further action is required from them at this time, as the team will reach out directly if additional information is needed once the review concludes.
