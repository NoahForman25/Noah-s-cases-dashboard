## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction with the extended processing timelines required to withdraw or transfer funds out of SoFi. Support explained the standard clearing schedules, security verification checks, and ACH processing windows that govern withdrawal timelines, while documenting the member's feedback regarding account liquidity and transfer speed for operational tracking.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong dissatisfaction regarding extended processing timelines for withdrawing and transferring funds out of SoFi, as well as the resulting impact on their account liquidity. The specialist detailed the standard clearing schedules and ACH processing windows, clarifying that SoFi Invest IRA ACH transfers submitted before the 3:30 PM ET cutoff on a business day are sent standard same-day and typically arrive at the external bank on the following business day, whereas requests submitted after the cutoff process the next business day. Additionally, the specialist explained that IRA withdrawals are subject to specialized security and administrative checks since they are classified as distributions, noting that incorrect distribution selections can lead to rejections and that mandatory state tax withholding rules may require direct phone support rather than self-service processing. The specialist also provided transparency regarding potential account closure costs, noting that a $100.00 fee applies if an IRA is fully closed, and confirmed that the member's feedback regarding withdrawal speeds has been documented for operational tracking.
