## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with SoFi restricting access to his accounts and funds for an estimated one business day during an internal review, stating it is unacceptable to be cut off from purchasing groceries, gasoline, and other necessities. The member requested a transfer to the investigation team or a supervisor to escalate and expedite the review, noting he had previously spent 30 minutes explaining the situation to investigations and remained unclear about subsequent updates.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding repeated account restrictions and the process for managing account number changes. Operational review confirmed that the account restrictions were automated security safeguards deployed during an ongoing internal investigation to protect funds prior to payment receipt. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, clarified that such security holds are rare, and outlined accessible support pathways—including utilizing secure chat support and submitting formal review requests while traveling—to bypass the need for phone calls. The complaint is resolved, with the security review process clarified, the agreed-upon payment timeline confirmed, and the customer equipped with alternative support channels should future account reviews occur.
