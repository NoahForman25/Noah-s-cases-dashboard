## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding the inability to access approximately $3,200 in cryptocurrency funds, which were won on an online gambling platform and routed directly to their crypto wallet. The member noted they had previously converted gambling winnings to USD externally before transferring without issue, but sending the funds directly triggered an unexpected verification requirement. Emphasizing extreme urgency, the member stated they had spent their entire paycheck on gambling, leaving them without funds for basic family expenses, and voiced broader frustration with the lack of physical branch locations, transaction fees, and the difficulty of resolving issues via phone support.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding a delayed $3,200.00 cryptocurrency transfer, strict verification requirements, and broader concerns regarding phone support, transaction fees, and the lack of physical branches. The specialist acknowledged the member's intense frustration and financial hardship after transferring online gambling winnings directly to their crypto wallet. Delivering clarification and confirming resolution, the specialist explained that direct crypto transfers trigger standard, mandatory security verifications designed to protect account safety. Finally, the specialist confirmed that system records show the verification was completed and the $3,200.00 transfer successfully processed on July 18, 2026, with the funds unlocked and already utilized by the member.
