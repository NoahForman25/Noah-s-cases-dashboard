## Complaint
- The member expressed concern regarding a government-issued check made out to their former legal name and requested a manual review, stating that supporting documentation had already been provided. The agent explained that the check would be declined due to the name mismatch and advised that the name on file must be updated first. The agent escalated the issue to higher management and filed a complaint for further review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding rejected mobile check deposits resulting from a legal name discrepancy on a government-issued check. Operational review confirmed that bank policy strictly requires the payee name on deposited checks to match the legal name on the account profile, preventing manual overrides for check deposits made out to former names. To resolve the complaint, the specialist explained the name matching policy requirements, advised the customer on initiating a profile legal name update, and escalated the complaint to executive management for secondary administrative review. The complaint is resolved, with the check deposit guidelines clarified and the customer directed to update their profile name prior to re-submitting the deposit.
