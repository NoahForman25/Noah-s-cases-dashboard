## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to address an unauthorized transaction dispute and assist with app-related technical issues. After authenticating the member, the agent logged a manual dispute for a $440.00 unauthorized debit card charge from QUINZERLIE SHAMER DU dated July 08, 2026, bypassing repeated app crashes and session timeouts that had previously prevented the member from filing. The agent detailed the provisional credit process and, upon reviewing the account, discovered the member was also due a separate bonus credit resulting from a previous system error, confirming that a follow-up email would be sent regarding this payout. Additionally, the agent addressed the member's inquiry regarding direct deposit timing, clarifying that while direct deposits may arrive up to two days early, early delivery is not guaranteed.
