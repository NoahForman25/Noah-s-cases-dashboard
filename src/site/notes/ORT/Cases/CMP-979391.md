## Complaint
- The member contacted support to dispute two unauthorized ACH charges on her account, expressing frustration that she did not receive the transaction verification messages she usually receives. During the reading of disclosures, the member stopped responding and the call disconnected. A callback was attempted, but the member did not answer.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email following a disconnected phone call to address her complaint regarding unauthorized ACH charges from GRANT, missing transaction verification alerts, and the interruption of her initial claim intake. Operational review confirmed that the transactions had been attempting to clear since October 28 and posted once the card was unfrozen, while also explaining that automated security verification alerts are activity-dependent and not triggered by every transaction. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding the alert system, outlined the requirement for verbal disclosures to formally submit the claims, and provided a direct phone number to finalize both the dispute and a recurring stop payment on the merchant. The customer's account remains active and open in good standing, pending inbound verbal contact to complete the dispute disclosures and place the requested stop payment.
