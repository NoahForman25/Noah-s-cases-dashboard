## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration and dissatisfaction regarding unauthorized transactions within their SoFi account. The member reported that unauthorized transfers were made from their checking account to their crypto account, followed by unauthorized debit card charges that they did not authorize, consisting of $899.57 to Fahd Abdulaziz Al Hazo on October 26, 2026, and $5.99 to KAVA MEDIA CORP.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding unauthorized transfers between their checking and crypto accounts, as well as unauthorized debit card charges totaling $899.57 and $5.99 from October 26, 2026. Operational review determined that because the customer's accounts had already been closed, the institution is systemically unable to initiate or process dispute claims on their behalf. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided formal written notice explaining the procedural limitations associated with closed accounts and directed the customer to contact the respective merchants directly to dispute and recover the unauthorized funds. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute claims unable to be processed internally and the customer's accounts remaining permanently closed.
