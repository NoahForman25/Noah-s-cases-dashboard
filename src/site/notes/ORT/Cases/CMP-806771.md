## Complaint
- The member is strongly disputing the denial of her claim, insisting that she never authorized or entered her SoFi debit card details for the Amigo Energy transaction and emphasizing that she exclusively uses her UnitedHealthcare UCard for this expense. Expressing extreme frustration and refusing to let the matter drop, the member stated that her submitted supporting documentation was completely disregarded in the final determination, identifying the dismissal of this evidence as a primary driver of her complaint.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong dissatisfaction regarding the denied dispute for a $392.67 transaction with Amigo Energy on 05/29/2026. The specialist acknowledged the member's extreme frustration over the outcome, specifically addressing her statements that she never authorized or entered her SoFi debit card for the charge—as she exclusively uses her UnitedHealthcare UCard for this expense—and her concern that her submitted supporting documentation was disregarded. Reaffirming the investigation's findings, the specialist explained that while the member believes the provided evidence constitutes valid proof, it is insufficient to approve the claim; therefore, the original denial remains in effect, any associated provisional credit may be reversed, and the dispute will remain closed unless new supporting documentation is submitted for further review.
