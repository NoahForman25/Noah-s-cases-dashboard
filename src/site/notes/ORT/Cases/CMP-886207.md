## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi to report that two personal loans held solely in their name are incorrectly reflecting a bankruptcy status. The member stated that their son, who shares a similar name but has a different Social Security number and date of birth, recently filed bankruptcy. The member confirmed they did not file bankruptcy and requested removal of the bankruptcy coding from their accounts so they can resume normal payments.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an issue where two personal loans held solely under the member's name were incorrectly flagged with a bankruptcy status. The member had previously contacted support to report the error, explaining that while their son shares a similar name, they have distinct Social Security numbers and dates of birth, and only the son had filed for bankruptcy.

During the call, the member reiterated that they have not filed for bankruptcy and requested the immediate removal of the bankruptcy restrictions so they can resume normal loan payments. The specialist documented the misapplied bankruptcy coding caused by the shared name and submitted a Collections Escalation Specialists Request (Ticket C-184317) to conduct a manual review, detach the bankruptcy flag from the member's loans, and follow up directly with the member to resolve the account status.
