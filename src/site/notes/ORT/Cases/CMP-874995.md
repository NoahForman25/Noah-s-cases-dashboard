## Complaint
- The member expressed extreme frustration and dissatisfaction regarding their experience over the past two weeks, stating that a hard inquiry was prematurely placed on their credit report without proper due diligence or prior verification of information. The member alleged that an unauthorized third party representing "Guys Up Source, Inc." acted on their behalf by creating a fraudulent email address to access the SoFi portal and misrepresenting the loan's interest rates. Describing the process as unethical and stating that the two-week ordeal caused significant delays for their business, the member requested that SoFi conduct a thorough investigation into the third-party's unauthorized actions and submit a dispute to the credit bureaus to remove the hard pull from their credit report. Support logged the detailed allegations of third-party fraud and unauthorized portal access, escalated the identity theft and unauthorized application claims to Fraud Operations and Member Security for immediate investigation, and submitted a credit bureau dispute ticket to Credit Risk and Operations to evaluate the removal of the hard credit inquiry.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their extreme frustration regarding a hard credit inquiry placed on their credit report, as well as alleged unauthorized activities conducted by a third party representing "Guys Up Source, Inc." Over a two-week period, the third party allegedly created a fraudulent email address to access the SoFi portal, misrepresented loan interest rates, and initiated a loan application without proper verification, causing significant disruption to the member's business operations. The specialist confirmed that the allegations of third-party fraud and unauthorized portal access were formally escalated to Fraud Operations and Member Security for immediate investigation. Regarding the hard credit inquiry, the specialist explained that because the application's required confirmation questionnaire was completed, the inquiry was automatically authorized by system protocols, preventing SoFi from removing the hard pull directly. However, the specialist noted that a credit bureau dispute ticket was submitted to Credit Risk and Operations, and offered to initiate direct outreach to the credit bureaus on the member's behalf to formally challenge the inquiry.
