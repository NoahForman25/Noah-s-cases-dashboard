## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding an inability to apply for a checking and savings account due to an existing profile registered under their mother's name that uses the member's only email address. The member previously attempted to remove their mother's name, but their mother declined to provide the requested personal information. The member requested that the entire profile be closed and deleted so they could reuse their email address, and was frustrated that the agent advised having their mother call in to close it. The member explained that their mother has no knowledge of the account, does not recognize the profile, and cannot provide the verification details on file because all information—aside from the name—belongs to the member. Believing it made no sense for their mother to verify information that is not hers and that they should be permitted to close the account using their own details, the member became increasingly upset and requested to speak with a manager or higher-level representative.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding an inability to open a new checking and savings account due to an existing profile registered under her mother's name linked to her personal email address. Operational review confirmed that because the profile was formally established under the mother's name, regulatory and security protocols strictly require identity authentication from the registered account holder before any administrative modifications, account closures, or email dissociations can be processed. To address the issue, the specialist provided operational transparency explaining why verification remains tied to the registered individual rather than the associated contact information, outlining that the mother must first complete identity authentication to authorize the closure and release the email address for new application onboarding. The profile remains open under the mother's name, pending inbound authentication to proceed with the requested closure and allow the customer to apply independently.
