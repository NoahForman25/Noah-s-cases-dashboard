## Complaint
- Member expressed dissatisfaction because of the hold on his funds.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding an ongoing hold on their funds. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's intense frustration over their treatment as a new customer, which had prompted them to threaten to close their account. Delivering an immediate next step to resolve the issue, the agent assured the member they would receive the highest level of assistance and placed the line on hold to facilitate a warm transfer. The agent successfully connected the member with the specific fraud agent who was originally working on their case to officially release the restriction on their checking account and restore access to their funds.
