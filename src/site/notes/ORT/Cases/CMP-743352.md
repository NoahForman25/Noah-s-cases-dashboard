## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding ongoing unauthorized charges from a merchant, while explicitly declining a replacement debit card due to the delivery timeframe. They also expressed dissatisfaction with the processing timeframe required to block the specific merchant. The agent acknowledged the member's frustration, explained the administrative and processing steps required to implement a merchant block, and created Zendesk ticket 8089723 to track the issue.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone regarding Zendesk ticket 8089723 to follow up on their frustration with ongoing unauthorized charges from a Cleo AI subscription, the delivery timeframe for a replacement debit card, and the processing time required to implement a merchant block. After completing authentication, the agent provided information on how to utilize mobile card details via the app for secure transactions while waiting for a physical card to arrive. The member confirmed that the subscription issue was resolved and no longer a concern, resulting in the cancellation of the merchant block request and the closure of the tracking ticket. This was notified to the member via email.
