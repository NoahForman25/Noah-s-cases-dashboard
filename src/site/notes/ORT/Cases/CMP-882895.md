## Complaint
- The member expressed severe dissatisfaction regarding an unauthorized withdrawal of $223.96 from their SoFi Savings Account to the merchant "MOPAR" on September 6, 2026. The member objected to having to contact support a second time after previously detailing the issue to a live representative, and formally demanded an immediate fraud investigation, a case reference number, and escalation to compliance under federal Electronic Fund Transfer Act regulations (Regulation E) after the previous agent failed to provide one. The member subsequently went idle before the interaction concluded. Support acknowledged the severity of the unauthorized transaction, documented the service breakdown and missing case number, and submitted an urgent priority escalation ticket to Fraud Operations, Dispute Advocacy, and Compliance Leadership to immediately initiate a formal Regulation E dispute, assign a tracking case number, and follow up directly with the member.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her severe dissatisfaction regarding an unauthorized $223.96 charge from the merchant "MOPAR" on September 6, 2026, and the lack of a tracking case number during her initial support interaction. The member had previously demanded an immediate fraud investigation, a case reference number, and an escalation under Regulation E after having to contact support a second time regarding the unauthorized transaction. The specialist acknowledged the service breakdown regarding the missing case reference number and reviewed the transaction details. The specialist clarified that the $223.96 charge posted to her SoFi Smart Card rather than her SoFi Savings Account, requiring the investigation to be handled by the specialized Smart Card Dispute team. The specialist directed the member to contact the Smart Card Dispute team directly so they can formally initiate the Regulation E dispute, assign the requested tracking case number, and conduct the full fraud investigation on her behalf.
