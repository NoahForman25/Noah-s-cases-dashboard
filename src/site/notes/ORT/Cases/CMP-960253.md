## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction and frustration regarding conflicting information about their SoFi smart card application after not receiving an expected status email. A previous representative had assured the member that the request was escalated, but the current agent contradicted this, stating no escalation process exists and advising the member to check their account for eligibility instead. The agent acknowledged the member's frustration and promised to relay their feedback to the internal team.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's complaint regarding confusing and conflicting information surrounding the smart card application process. Operational review established that holding both a joint account and an individual account renders a customer systemically ineligible for the smart card product under current program rules, with no available operational workarounds or escalation paths. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, delivered clear operational transparency regarding these strict account eligibility constraints, and officially submitted the customer's feedback to the internal product team for review. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's existing accounts remaining open and active, while the smart card application remains unavailable due to account structure requirements.
