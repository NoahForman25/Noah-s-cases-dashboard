## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding a missing $40 credit from a Zelle transaction that had not posted to their account. The member described the situation as "shady" and noted that based on their prior experience working at banks, they understand how banking operations function.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding confusion and a perceived missing $40 credit from a canceled Zelle transaction. Operational review confirmed that Zelle transfers place an immediate hold on available funds, meaning no separate debit or credit line items post to the ledger unless the transaction fully completes or finalizes. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, clarified how authorization holds operate upon cancellation, verified that the held funds had successfully released back into the available balance, and credited $20.00 worth of reward points as compensation for the confusion. The complaint is resolved, with the held Zelle funds fully released, reward points applied to the account, and ledger balance accuracy confirmed.
