## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding multiple account issues, including the inability to withdraw $55.13 from a recent investment trade settlement, an unexplained decline on their banking application, and a perceived runaround caused by support's inability to disclose specific underwriting decline reasons alongside the unavailability of live Invest department support. Support acknowledged the member's frustration with the compounding issues, explained standard trade settlement hold timelines for investment withdrawals, and clarified privacy and regulatory restrictions around credit decisioning disclosures. Support then submitted an urgent escalation ticket to Invest Operations and Banking Operations for manual review of the withdrawal hold and application status.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on a bank application decline, an Invest account withdrawal timeline, and a missing referral bonus. The member had previously expressed strong dissatisfaction after being unable to withdraw $55.13 from a recent investment trade settlement, receiving an unexplained banking application decline, and experiencing frustration over support's inability to disclose specific underwriting decline reasons or connect them with live Invest agents. The specialist authenticated the member, clarified that an official email containing the specific bank application decline reasons was sent, and provided a direct contact number for further assistance while explaining regulatory privacy restrictions surrounding credit decisioning. The specialist clarified that the $55.13 in Invest funds would be available for withdrawal on September 9th following standard trade settlement hold timelines, addressed the missing referral bonus by checking the profile, and credited 1,000 reward points ($10.00) to the member's profile as a gesture of appreciation for their referral efforts.
