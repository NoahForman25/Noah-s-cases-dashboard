## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction and frustration regarding an unauthorized $42.11 charge from "CCBill.com *OnlyFans" posted on July 31, 2026. The member stated they have never used OnlyFans, noted that a similar fraudulent charge was previously overturned on their account, and urgently requested an immediate credit return, threatening to close their account and switch financial institutions if the issue is not resolved promptly. Support initiated a formal transaction dispute for the $42.11 debit, issued a temporary provisional credit request based on the previous fraud history, deactivated the compromised card to prevent further unauthorized billing, and escalated the case to Dispute Operations and Fraud Prevention for priority investigation.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address his strong dissatisfaction regarding a disputed $42.11 charge from "CCBill.com *OnlyFans" posted on July 31, 2026. The member had previously demanded an immediate credit return, stating he had never used the service, noting that a similar fraudulent charge was previously overturned, and threatening to close his account if the matter was not promptly resolved. The specialist apologized for the ongoing financial concern and reviewed the status of the investigation. While support had previously submitted a provisional credit request, deactivated the compromised card, and escalated the claim to Fraud Prevention, the specialist confirmed that the initial formal dispute was denied. However, the specialist explained that an appeal process is open and urged the member to call support directly to connect with a dispute agent and submit any additional supporting documentation to re-evaluate the claim.
