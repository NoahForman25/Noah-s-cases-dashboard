## Complaint
- The member called to dispute the denial of a $240.52 claim regarding an unauthorized charge from Adobe, expressing frustration that SoFi denied the claim on the grounds that merchant records matched account records on file. The member stated that she did not authorize the charge, cancelled her Adobe service a year prior, had her previous card replaced, and questioned how an old card could be charged or how records matched when Adobe previously stated they could not locate her account or the transaction. The member expressed concern that the upcoming reversal of $240.52 on November 16 would cause her account to become negative, repeatedly requested the specific evidence and records SoFi used to match the transaction with the merchant, and asked for assistance contacting Adobe directly or speaking with the back-office team. The agent explained that the back office determined the claim lacked sufficient evidence, confirmed the back-office team does not have a direct phone line, and stated company policy prevents agents from contacting merchants directly. The agent advised that an appeal requires additional documentation, such as screenshots or confirmation from Adobe stating they cannot find the charge, which the member agreed to attempt despite her dissatisfaction.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding the denial of a $240.52 dispute for an unauthorized Adobe charge, the scheduled provisional credit reversal, and requests for merchant matching evidence. Operational review confirmed that the dispute determination remains upheld because the transaction matched account records and the claim lacked sufficient counter-evidence to overturn merchant documentation. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency explaining the necessity of new supporting information to challenge the outcome and outlined the formal appeal process through the dispute team, specifically advising that written confirmation or records from Adobe would be required to reopen the case. The customer's account remains active and open, with the dispute denial in effect and the $240.52 credit reversal scheduled for November 16 pending the submission of new appeal documentation.
