## Complaint
- Member contacted to escalate a denied dispute regarding five Apple Cash transfers executed on June 19, 2026, totaling $1,500.00, claiming the initial investigation was inadequate. The member expressed great dissatisfaction with how their claim was handled and maintained that the transactions were completely unauthorized. The member stated that they received an official transfer verification text message and explicitly replied "No" to deny the transfers, but the transactions were approved regardless of their response. Due to the situation, the member stated they intend to file formal complaints with both the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) and the Better Business Bureau (BBB).
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to deliver the results of the escalated appeal for the five denied Apple Cash disputes totaling $1,500.00 from June 19, 2026. The specialist informed the member that the specialized team completed their secondary investigation into the fund authorization and verification responses, and the initial denial decision has been upheld. To move the case forward, the specialist clarified that the member can submit new or additional documentation regarding the transactions, which will allow the team to reopen and re-evaluate the investigation.
