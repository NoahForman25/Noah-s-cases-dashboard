## Complaint
- The member filed a formal complaint regarding unresolved, unauthorized wire transfers totaling $116,500 that occurred between February 18th and February 23rd, expressing intense frustration over a prolonged lack of resolution and poor internal communication. The member reported that scammers systematically drained savings account funds through multiple wire transfers—$15,000 and $9,000 on the 18th, four $14,000 transfers on the 19th, two $14,000 transfers on the 20th, and an $8,500 transfer on the 23rd—all executed without security verification from the institution. Despite speaking with at least eight representatives who repeatedly promised callbacks, the member received no updates and issued an ultimatum to escalate the investigation directly to the FDIC.
