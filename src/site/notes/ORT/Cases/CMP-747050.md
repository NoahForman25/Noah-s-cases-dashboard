## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding the review timeline for their restricted account after being informed they would need to wait one business day. The member questioned the processing delay and emphasized the urgency of the situation, stating that they need the pending wire transfer to go through today.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on their dissatisfaction regarding a restricted account review timeline and their urgent need for a pending wire transfer to clear. After completing identity verification, the specialist addressed the member's initial frustration with being told to wait one business day for the processing delay to resolve. The member confirmed that the wire transfer had since successfully cleared, and the agent apologized for the poor service experience and ensured all outstanding account issues were fully resolved.
