## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction after being advised that a dispute cannot be initiated for a transaction that is still in a pending status. The member strongly objected to the wait period required for the transaction to post before an investigation can begin, stating verbatim:

"Somebody is using my money and you're telling me that I have to wait for you guys to decide whether or not you're going to give it back. That's genius."
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and did not reach there voicemail so emailed the member to address their severe dissatisfaction regarding the standard banking protocol that prevents a dispute from being initiated while a transaction is still in a pending status. The specialist addressed the member's strong objections and sarcastic remarks concerning the required waiting period before an investigation can begin. Providing a path to resolution, the specialist clarified that funds are not permanently transferred while pending and confirmed that the resolution team stands ready to immediately file a formal dispute on the member's behalf as soon as the transaction officially posts to the account.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
- The resolution specialist attempted to contact the member regarding their complaint. The specialist did not receive the member's voicemail. The specialist will send a detailed email.
### Second Contact
- Dear Corinne,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. Thank you for reaching out regarding the pending transaction on your account and the dispute process. We completely understand the frustration and urgency caused when unauthorized activity occurs, and we appreciate the opportunity to provide clarity on our procedures today.

To ensure your account is secure, a review of the transaction in question was conducted. We recognize your concerns regarding the requirement to wait before initiating a formal investigation. In accordance with standard banking protocols, a transaction must transition from a pending status to a fully posted status before a formal dispute can be submitted. While a transaction remains pending, the funds have not yet been permanently transferred to the merchant, which prevents the official dispute process from beginning.

To resolve this for you, we are absolutely ready to assist you as soon as this transaction officially posts to your account. Once the status updates to posted, please contact us back, and we will immediately file the dispute on your behalf to begin the investigation.

To recap, standard banking procedures require the transaction to post before a dispute can be filed, and we are standing by to submit the dispute for you as soon as that status updates. Please let us know if you have any further questions at all—we are here to help.
