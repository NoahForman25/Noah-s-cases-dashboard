## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction because their account was closed and representatives were unable to explain the reason for the closure. The member stated, "I'm gonna make a case and report to the SEC commissions for discrimination." Additionally, the member was dissatisfied that they had not received an expected callback from a manager, which prompted them to call back.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the unexplained closure of their account and the failure to receive a promised callback from management. Operational review confirmed that the account closure decision was final under bank discretion guidelines and validated that a communication lapse occurred regarding the delayed callback. To resolve the complaint, the specialist formally apologized for the delayed outreach and confirmed that all required disclosures regarding the closure process had been furnished. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining in active closure processing status and no further administrative actions pending.
