## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration about the declined wire transfer. The member also expressed discomfort with providing sensitive information over a call they did not initiate after the agent advised to resubmit the wire. The member refused to receive a call from us and requested to speak with a manager.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding a canceled wire transfer and their discomfort with providing sensitive personal details during an uninitiated outbound phone call. After being advised to resubmit the wire, the member had refused further phone outreach, raised security concerns about verifying information on outbound calls, and requested a manager escalation. The specialist apologized for the transfer cancellation and acknowledged the member's security preferences, confirming that support was shifting to email communication to accommodate their comfort level. The specialist provided instructions for the member to safely resubmit the wire transfer at their convenience—either by calling customer support directly using an official phone number or by responding directly to the email—and offered continued assistance to resolve the matter.
