## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with the handling and denial of their claim, which requires new information or additional documentation to file an appeal. The member indicated they plan to contact a banking regulatory body regarding the process followed.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the handling and subsequent denial of their dispute claim. Operational review confirmed that the claim was thoroughly evaluated and denied based on all available transaction data, and that formal dispute guidelines require new supporting evidence to initiate an appeal. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided complete transparency regarding the claim investigation findings and outlined the exact process for submitting new documentation should the customer choose to reopen the case. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute remaining closed under the initial denial determination pending any new documentation provided for review.
