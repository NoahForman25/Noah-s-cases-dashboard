## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding their account closure, stating that they cannot wait 30 calendar days for a check to be delivered due to financial hardship and an inability to pay rent. The member is requesting to speak with a manager.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's complaint regarding an unexplained account closure and the urgent need for their funds to avoid financial hardship. Operational review established that the account was finalized for permanent closure following an internal evaluation, with policy restricting frontline disclosure of specific review criteria. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, clarified that the remaining balance disbursement had already been expedited via a check mailed to the address on file, and updated the delivery estimate to arrive within three to six business days. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining permanently closed pending the customer's receipt of the disbursement check.
