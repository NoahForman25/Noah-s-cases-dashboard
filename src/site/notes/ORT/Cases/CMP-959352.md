## Complaint
- The member documented ongoing dissatisfaction with prior customer service attempts, noting that repeated efforts through front-line channels resulted in being passed back and forth between SoFi and Zelle without a resolution. While the overall issue is resolved, the member demands that SoFi determine whether the restriction stems from internal systems or an old network-level block from Wells Fargo, coordinate with Zelle or Early Warning Services to remove the block, and provide a written determination outlining the steps required to restore access.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding restricted Zelle access, repetitive transfers between customer support channels, and her request for a formal written determination identifying the root cause of the limitation. Operational review established that the block is maintained externally at the Early Warning Services (EWS) and Zelle network token level, which systemically prevents the institution from manually overriding, clearing, or reinstating access. To resolve the complaint, the specialist delivered operational transparency in writing explaining that the institution cannot view proprietary third-party criteria or intervene with external entities, directing the customer to work directly with Zelle and EWS to appeal the network restriction. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's core banking accounts remaining active and in good standing, while Zelle services remain unavailable pending external network clearance.
