## Complaint
- Member contacted to express deep dissatisfaction upon discovering that their SoFi account had been unexpectedly closed that morning without prior warning or explanation. The member stated that a direct deposit of $718.29 and a $50.00 peer-to-peer transfer from a friend—totaling $768.29—had posted to the account the same day, leaving them entirely unable to access their funds. While accepting the closure itself, the member expressed strong frustration regarding the funding restriction, repeatedly stating that the institution was "holding their money hostage." The member requested to either wire the balance to a newly opened external account or transfer it to a friend, and sought clarity on the reason for the account closure.

A profile review confirmed that the account was pending closure following a comprehensive internal review; however, specific details regarding the decision could not be disclosed. The member was advised that an active $149.00 dispute was currently in progress, which prevented any outbound electronic transfers, leaving a physical check mailed to the address on file as the only available method for releasing the remaining balance. The member strongly pushed back on this option due to the lengthy processing time and requested an immediate escalation. The agent acknowledged the request and transferred the line to an available supervisor for further assistance, with the issue remaining unresolved at the time of transfer.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on an unexpected account closure and resulting funding restrictions, addressing the member's deep dissatisfaction, claims of identity theft, and subsequent escalation request. Following identity verification, the agent addressed the member's frustration over losing immediate access to $768.29 in fresh deposits and clarified that the account was permanently closed following an internal review, though specific reasons could not be disclosed. The agent further explained that an active $149.00 dispute blocked outbound electronic transfers, leaving a physical check as the only method to release the remaining balance. While the member noted they could still access certain funds via PayPal, they strongly rejected the check option due to processing delays. To address the impasse, the agent apologized for the experience and transferred the line to a supervisor for immediate escalation.
