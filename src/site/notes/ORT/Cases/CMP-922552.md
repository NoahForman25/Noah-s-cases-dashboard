## Complaint
- The member expressed significant frustration after being charged twice for recurring payments on their SoFi personal loan, noting they have photographic evidence of the duplicate charges. The member inquired about the reimbursement process and timeline, described the situation as "ridiculous," and stated that the unexpected charges may cause financial difficulties and potential billing problems.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding a duplicate recurring payment deduction of $1,300.00 on their personal loan. Operational review confirmed that two identical payment transactions were processed for the single due date, causing an unauthorized duplicate debit from the customer's account balance. To resolve the complaint, the specialist submitted a formal refund request to reverse the extra transaction and reinstate the correct ledger balance before financial hardship could occur. The complaint is resolved, with the duplicate payment refund actively processing for return to the customer.
