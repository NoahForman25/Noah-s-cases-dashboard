## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction after being unable to fund their account using a debit card. Support explained the eligible funding methods and debit card transfer limits/restrictions, guided the member on alternative options for depositing funds (such as ACH transfer or direct deposit), and logged the member's feedback regarding account funding channel limitations.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding the inability to fund their SoFi account using a debit card. The specialist clarified platform capabilities, explaining that debit card transfers are not currently supported as an available account funding method on the SoFi platform and apologizing for any inconvenience this channel restriction caused. To assist the member with depositing funds, the specialist outlined eligible alternative funding methods, including ACH transfers, wire transfers, and direct deposits. The specialist provided guidance on how to initiate these transactions within the account profile by linking an external bank account or setting up direct deposit details with an employer or benefits provider. Finally, the specialist confirmed that the member's feedback requesting debit card funding functionality was formally logged and submitted to the internal product team for consideration in future platform enhancements.
