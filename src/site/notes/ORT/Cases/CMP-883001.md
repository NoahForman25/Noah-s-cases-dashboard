## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction after waiting 45 minutes in the queue and being misrouted to the incorrect department by a previous representative. The member clarified that their call specifically pertained to reported fraudulent activity associated with a Home Loan Line of Credit (HELOC) application. Support apologized for the long wait and transfer error, logged a formal routing complaint, and initiated an urgent warm transfer to the Home Loans Fraud and Risk Operations team for immediate handling of the unauthorized application activity.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her strong dissatisfaction regarding a 45-minute queue wait time and misrouting by a previous agent, as well as reported fraudulent activity associated with an unauthorized Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC) application on her profile. The specialist apologized for the extended wait time and departmental misdirection, confirming that a formal service complaint was logged regarding the call routing failure. The specialist reviewed the account profile and explained that while the member requested account deactivation, the profile cannot be deactivated until the unauthorized HELOC application officially expires—a third-party process that may take up to 30 days. The specialist confirmed that the dispute was routed to the Home Loans Fraud team, provided contact details for the third-party HELOC servicer (Cenlar at 855-622-3198), and recommended that the member monitor her credit report for any unauthorized inquiry impact.
