## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi regarding the $400 direct deposit bonus they did not receive after falling approximately $25 short of the $5,000 requirement, and requested a one-time goodwill adjustment due to financial hardship. The member expressed dissatisfaction that an exception could not be made for the $400 bonus and requested to receive the qualifying $50 bonus immediately that day. An apology was extended, and the member was informed that adjustments cannot be made because the $400 tier strictly requires the full $5,000 deposit threshold within the 25-day evaluation period. Per the member's request, 5,000 reward points were added to the account immediately, and the member was advised that bonuses are credited within 7 business days following the evaluation period.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's complaint regarding direct deposit bonus eligibility and missing reward points after falling short of the qualifying threshold. Operational review confirmed that the promotion strictly requires meeting the full deposit criteria within the designated evaluation period to qualify for the higher bonus tier, though qualifying tier rewards are fulfilled within standard processing timelines. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified the account, apologized for the experience, confirmed receipt of the $50 reward bonus, and issued an additional $25 in reward points to the customer's profile. The complaint is resolved, with the promotional bonus credited and the additional points processing with an expected posting window of up to seven business days.
