## Complaint
- Member contacted to express deep dissatisfaction regarding a four-day inability to access their account, noting this was the fourth consecutive day they had reached out to support. The member explained that despite successfully completing two-factor authentication via both Google Authenticator and text message, the mobile application continuously redirected them back to the login screen. They emphasized their growing frustration over having already spoken to approximately ten representatives—including technical support and managers—without reaching a resolution. The member also detailed a highly stressful experience attempting to update their email address, reporting that the identity verification link repeatedly failed due to unexpected system errors and incoming call interruptions. This forced them to restart the process multiple times while pulled over on the side of the road during heavy rain.


## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their deep dissatisfaction regarding a four-day loss of account access, during which the member had reached out to support on four consecutive days and spoken with approximately ten representatives without resolution. The specialist acknowledged the member's frustration over repeated two-factor authentication redirects back to the login screen, as well as the stressful system errors and call interruptions experienced while attempting to complete identity verification to update their email address. Delivering technical guidance and subsequent resolution, the specialist initially provided comprehensive device and browser troubleshooting steps—such as adjusting camera permissions, clearing cache, disabling active VPNs, and closing app overlays—to address the verification glitches. A follow-up review of system records confirmed that a successful login to the profile was subsequently completed, successfully restoring account access and allowing the technical support ticket to be closed.

### Second Contact
- Dear Theodore,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. Thank you for reaching out regarding the four-day inability to access your account and the difficulties experienced during the identity verification process. We recognize the inconvenience caused by the repeated login redirects after completing two-factor authentication and the system errors encountered while attempting to update your email address.

To resolve the ongoing login and verification errors, we recommend completing the following technical troubleshooting steps for your device:

Confirm camera permissions: For Android devices, navigate to Settings > Apps > select the application or browser in use > Permissions > ensure Camera is allowed. Select an allowed setting such as "Allow only while using the app" or "Ask every time."
Check browser settings: If using Chrome, confirm the browser site settings allow camera access for the Persona verification page.
Restart and retry: Restart the application or browser and the device, then retry the verification link.
Close conflicting applications: Ensure no other application, website, phone call, or video chat is actively using the camera or microphone.
Check network connections: Disable any active VPN or firewall that may interfere with the network connection, and attempt the process on a different network.
Update software: Check the application store for updates, or reinstall the application. If using a browser, ensure it is updated to the latest version.
Clear browser data: Clear the browser cache and cookies, confirm pop-ups are allowed, close the browser, and retry the link.
Close overlays: If an error message regarding "bubbles" or overlays appears, close any active chat bubbles or overlay applications and retry.
To recap, we have provided specific device troubleshooting steps to address the login redirects and camera permission errors. If the technical issues persist after completing these steps, please contact us directly at the number below, and we will assist you further.
