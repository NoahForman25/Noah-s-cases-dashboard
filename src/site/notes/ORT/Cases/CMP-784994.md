## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction after being unable to successfully verify the full name exactly as it appears on their account profile. Due to the resulting verification failure and their frustration with the authentication process, the member requested an immediate escalation to speak with a supervisor.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address their dissatisfaction and escalation request following a failed identity verification attempt, which occurred because the member was unable to provide their full name exactly as it appears on their account profile. The member, who had originally called to regain login access to their account, explained that they had already verified their information through another channel and had received a link to reset their password. Upon receiving this update, the agent confirmed that no further assistance was required if the password reset link was functioning correctly, and the call was successfully concluded with the member's agreement.
