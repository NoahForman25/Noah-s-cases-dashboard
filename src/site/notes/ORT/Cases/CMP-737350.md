YOUR NO USE AND NO HELP". The member is requesting an immediate review of their account history to verify that they only receive income from Lyft rather than paying them, and they are requesting to close their account if these funds are not returned.
## Resolution
-A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dispute regarding two unauthorized Lyft charges totaling $362.00 ($300.00 and $62.00) and their request to review their transaction history to verify they only receive income from the merchant. The specialist apologized for the negative experience but clarified that because the member's bank account is now officially closed, the dispute process has been finalized, no adjustments or refunds will be issued, and the review of the disputed charges is concluded since the account is now closed. No actions can be taken now.
