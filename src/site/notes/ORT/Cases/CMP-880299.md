## Complaint
- The member expressed strong frustration after their original loan application with a co-borrower was denied due to an inability to verify income, leading them to immediately submit a solo application based on advice from a previous agent. However, the second application was automatically declined due to SoFi's policy restricting new applications within 30 days of a prior decline. The member criticized the conflicting guidance that resulted in an immediate second denial and demanded to speak with a supervisor. Support acknowledged the conflicting advice and application frustration, documented the agent's instructions, and submitted an urgent supervisor callback ticket alongside an escalation to Lending Operations to review the application sequence.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address his strong frustration regarding the immediate denial of his second loan application and the conflicting guidance provided by a previous representative. The member had previously applied with a co-borrower and was declined due to income verification constraints, but was subsequently advised by support to reapply as a solo applicant without being warned about SoFi's policy restricting new submissions within 30 days of a prior decline, resulting in an automatic second denial. The specialist apologized for the incorrect instructions and acknowledged the friction caused by failing to communicate the mandatory 30-day waiting period, which applies universally to all new applications following a decline. The specialist confirmed that the conflicting agent advice and application history were documented, and verified that both a supervisor callback ticket and a Lending Operations escalation were submitted to review the agent's guidance and evaluate the application sequence.
