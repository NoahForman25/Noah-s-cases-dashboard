## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi to check the status of a traditional IRA withdrawal initiated on Friday morning and was informed that it would take two business days to process. The member expressed dissatisfaction that holiday closures were not communicated upfront prior to explaining their issue.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding communication transparency surrounding the processing timeline for a traditional IRA withdrawal initiated over the weekend. Operational review confirmed that processing was extended by an additional business day due to the Monday Columbus Day bank holiday, leading to the funds being disbursed on October 13, 2026. To resolve the complaint, the specialist apologized for the initial failure to proactively disclose holiday processing schedules and confirmed that the transaction had successfully completed. The complaint is resolved, with the requested IRA funds fully withdrawn and processed to the customer's account.
