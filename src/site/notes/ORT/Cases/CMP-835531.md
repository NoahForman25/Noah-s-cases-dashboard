## Complaint
- The member contacted support via chat reporting that the mobile app was freezing and causing execution delays when attempting to place trades. However, the member provided no specific stock symbols or technical details regarding the error, and after going idle, the chat disconnected automatically. Support documented the reported app performance issue and closed the interaction due to member inactivity.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their report that the mobile application was freezing and causing execution delays during trade attempts. Acknowledging that the member's previous chat interaction had disconnected automatically due to inactivity before specific details could be collected, the specialist apologized for the technical issues affecting their trading activity. To allow the technical team to investigate the root cause, the specialist requested that the member provide the specific stock ticker symbol involved. Additionally, the specialist advised the member to contact support by phone with details of the behavior and any visible error messages should the performance issues persist.
