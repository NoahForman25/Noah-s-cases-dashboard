## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding the upcoming closure of their account, stating that SoFi made the decision to close it without their request, and asked for a specific timeframe for the closure. The member also requested information on hiring a lawyer to recover their funds, stating they did not want SoFi keeping their money for personal use. Additionally, the member requested to speak with a supervisor, and a supervisor callback was scheduled.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's complaint regarding an unrequested account restriction, an upcoming account closure, and the resulting delay in accessing funds for urgent payments. Operational review indicated that the account was placed under restriction and routed for permanent closure pursuant to internal review protocols, which require specialized back-office handling and restrict frontline disclosure of proprietary review details. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, clarified that the account closure and funds disbursement had been formally escalated to the appropriate processing team, and scheduled a supervisor callback to address the customer's timeline concerns and legal inquiries. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's account actively restricted and proceeding through final closure workflows pending supervisor follow-up and the subsequent return of remaining funds.
