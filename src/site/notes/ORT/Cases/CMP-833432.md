## Complaint
- The member expressed strong frustration regarding a delayed **$200.00** Mobile Check Deposit, accusing a previous agent of misrepresentation after being initially told funds would be available by August 6, 2026, only to see the date pushed to August 11, 2026.

  Support clarified that hold timelines can be extended due to automated risk evaluations, confirmed that a notification email detailing the extended hold had been sent, and verified the final availability date of August 11, 2026. The member confirmed receipt of the hold notification email before ending the call, and a QA agent filed a formal complaint to track the communication discrepancy.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an inquiry regarding a extended hold on a $200.00 mobile check deposit. After authenticating the member, the agent addressed their strong frustration over a delayed availability date, which was pushed from August 6, 2026, to August 11, 2026, leading the member to accuse a prior representative of misrepresentation. The agent apologized for the inconvenience and clarified that standard deposit holds can be extended automatically following automated risk evaluations. The specialist confirmed that the final $200.00 funds release was scheduled for August 11, 2026, and verified that the member had received the hold notification email explaining the timeline adjustment. Additionally, a QA agent filed a formal complaint to document and track the initial communication discrepancy.
