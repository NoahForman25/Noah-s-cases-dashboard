## Complaint
- The member contacted us expressing extreme dissatisfaction regarding the balance on their profile. The member stated that they had made a deposit and believed they were charged twice by a certain merchant, resulting in what they believe to be an incorrect account balance. The member provided the following statement verbatim: "You guys are lying about the records, and you guys are making it seem like it's accurate when it's really not." The agent attempted to break down the transactions on the member's profile to provide a clear and transparent explanation of the balance. However, the member remained insistent that the balance was incorrect and was not receptive to the explanation. The member requested to be connected to the Security Team to address the matter further. Before the interaction could be completed or the transfer could be made, the call disconnected.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone regarding there complaint to follow up on their extreme dissatisfaction with their account balance and their belief that they were double-charged by a merchant. Although a previous agent had broken down the transactions to provide a transparent explanation before a call disconnection occurred, the member indicated during this follow-up call that they had changed their mind about pursuing the complaint and would handle the matter on their own, resulting in no further action being taken.
