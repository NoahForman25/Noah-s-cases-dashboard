## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding an unauthorized transaction conducted by an unknown party, claiming that the incident was the fault of the institution. The member asserted that the unauthorized access resulted directly from a data breach on the institution's end.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding an unauthorized transaction the member believed was caused by an institutional data breach. After completing identity verification, the member explained that their phone had been hacked, requiring a factory reset. Delivering a clear resolution, the agent confirmed that an account takeover had occurred and informed the member that their accounts were being safely reopened, with new account numbers and a new debit card being issued. Finally, addressing the member's confusion regarding conflicting system messages, the agent clarified that their underlying profile remains intact and advised them to update their password immediately for security.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
- Reason for Call

SoFi called the member to follow up on a complaint regarding a transaction that was not supposed to happen due to a potential data breach.
Key Events

The agent authenticated the member.
The member explained their phone was hacked and they had to perform a factory reset.
The agent informed the member that there was an account takeover and that the member's accounts were being reopened.
The agent advised the member that they would receive new account information and a new debit card.
The member expressed confusion about conflicting messages received regarding their account status.
The agent clarified that the member's profile would remain the same, but they should change their password for security.
