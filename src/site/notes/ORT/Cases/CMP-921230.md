## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an inability to access their SoFi profile and SoFi's failure to assist them in regaining access, noting they no longer have access to the phone number or email address on file. Additionally, the member expressed dissatisfaction with their interaction with the previous agent and was transferred to Escalations, despite stating they never requested a manager.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding account access recovery after losing access to their registered phone number and email address, as well as dissatisfaction with unwanted manager transfers and work-hour calls. Operational review confirmed that account security protocols were successfully applied to verify the customer's identity and update their contact credentials without requiring further managerial intervention. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that all profile updates were set up correctly, confirmed that account access was fully restored, and apologized for the inconvenience caused by workplace phone calls and the previous representative's handling. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's profile contact information updated and full account access re-established.
