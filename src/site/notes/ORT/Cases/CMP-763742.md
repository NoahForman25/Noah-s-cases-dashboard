The member described the treatment as completely unprofessional and objected to an ongoing failure to address their situation in a timely manner. Due to these issues, the member stated they are officially preparing to file a formal report with the Attorney General's Office.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and did not reach there phone. Then emailed the member to address their severe dissatisfaction regarding a previous phone interaction, their claim that a representative violated their First Amendment rights, and their stated intent to file a report with the Attorney General's Office. The specialist addressed the member's objections to the representative's professionalism and the ongoing account verification timeline, documenting the feedback for internal review. Providing a final resolution, the specialist clarified that because the institution operates as a private financial entity rather than a government body, First Amendment protections do not apply to its corporate interaction policies.
