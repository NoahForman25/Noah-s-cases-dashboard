## Complaint
- The member reported that they previously requested SoFi to block the merchant Albert, but they were still charged $39.99.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding unauthorized merchant charges totaling $79.98 and a failure to block recurring transactions from Albert. Operational review confirmed that despite a previous merchant block request, recurring card updates allowed the merchant to successfully process a subsequent $39.99 transaction. To resolve the complaint, the specialist filed a dispute for the unauthorized charges, permanently re-blocked the merchant, and issued a new debit card to prevent further unauthorized access. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute processing under active fraud review and the replacement debit card dispatched to the customer.
