## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding fund availability timelines after initiating an ACH transfer on Friday, July 24, 2026. The member stated an urgent need for the money and was frustrated that the funds would not become available until Wednesday.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to address their strong dissatisfaction regarding ACH fund availability hold times after initiating a transfer on Friday, July 24, 2026, which delayed urgently needed funds until Wednesday. After authenticating the member, the agent acknowledged their concerns and apologized for the confusion surrounding transfer processing timelines. During the call, the member also reported duplicate Apple charges on their account and noted a submitted dispute; upon reviewing the account, the agent confirmed that additional information would be required to proceed with processing the duplicate transaction claim.
