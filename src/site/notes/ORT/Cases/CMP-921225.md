## Complaint
- The member expressed significant frustration regarding conflicting information about whether their dispute was under reinvestigation and whether additional documentation was required, as well as a lack of response to their inquiries. The member questioned when the reinvestigation actually began and stated they intend to file a complaint with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, noting they have kept recorded documentation of all communications and procedures followed.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding conflicting information about their dispute reinvestigation status and unanswered inquiries. Operational review confirmed that customer service representatives provided inconsistent updates regarding documentation requirements and failed to respond to timeline inquiries in a timely manner, though the dispute team had ultimately closed the appeal after determining the submitted documents contained no new evidence. To resolve the complaint, the specialist conducted a full audit of the case history, acknowledged the communication breakdowns, and provided direct transparency regarding the final appeal decision. The complaint is resolved, with the appeal remaining closed based on existing documentation and the customer informed of the final determination.
