## Complaint
- The member is upset about a transaction they already reported. They asked for the merchant to be blocked so no more charges would show up. The member said they were told someone would fix this when they called before, so they want to know if they will get their refund. They were told it would take two or three days. Now, after waiting, they want to know if they will get their money back. It looks like no dispute was made, and the member feels their time was wasted.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address their frustration regarding a previously unreported dispute, unfulfilled refund timelines, and an unblocked merchant after subscription cancellation. After completing authentication, the agent explained how the processing methods allowed the charges to clear and recommended a new debit card for security. To resolve the issue, the specialist officially filed a new dispute for the $20.00 transaction, established a 10-business-day investigation timeline, and issued the member 1,000 courtesy points ($10.00) for immediate redemption to help offset the financial strain.
## Case Intake & Investigation

### First contact
- Reason for Call SoFi called the member to follow up on a complaint regarding unauthorized transactions from their account. 
	Key Events The agent authenticated the member using their date of birth. The member explained that unauthorized transactions were taken from their account despite having canceled their subscription. The agent apologized and explained that the transactions may have gone through due to the merchant's processing methods. The agent suggested ordering a new debit card for security. The member expressed urgency for the return of their $20, stating they were out of work and needed the funds. The agent informed the member that ordering a new card would not return the $20 but offered to file a dispute for the transaction. The member confirmed that a dispute had already been filed previously but was not processed. The agent filed a new dispute for the member and provided them with a timeline of 10 business days for the investigation. The agent offered the member 1,000 points as a temporary solution, equating to $10, which the member could redeem immediately.
