## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding three failed attempts to initiate a funds transfer to their linked Capital One account. The member noted that each attempted transaction was systematically canceled, with subsequent automated email notifications stating the transfers were blocked due to security concerns.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding three systematically canceled funds transfers to a linked Capital One account and the subsequent automated security alerts. The specialist delivered a definitive resolution, confirming that the transfers were blocked because the profile was temporarily placed under a brief security review. The specialist verified that the security review is now complete, the account restrictions have been cleared, and advised the member to disconnect and reconnect the Capital One account within their profile settings to refresh the link and resolve any lingering connection issues for future transfers.
