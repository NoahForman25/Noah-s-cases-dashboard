## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding the correction of the settlement amount for dispute number 8001495. The member objected to the adjustment, which occurred because a previous agent incorrectly filed the dispute for the full transaction amount rather than the requested partial amount.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed the member to address their frustration regarding a settlement amount correction for dispute number 8001495, which stemmed from a previous agent incorrectly filing the claim for the full transaction amount instead of the requested partial amount. The specialist clarified that the system automatically processes claims for the full amount listed in the transaction history—in this case, $995—and advised that any partial adjustments or refunds, such as $400, must be coordinated directly with the merchant. Additionally, the member was informed that while a stop payment had been placed on the merchant, the standard dispute process would continue to proceed normally.

- Hi Maeva,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. We are reaching out regarding the concerns with the settlement amount for dispute number 8001495. We completely understand the frustration regarding how the transaction amount was filed, and we are here to provide clear details on how this process works.

When a transaction is disputed, our system processes the claim for the full amount listed in the transaction history, which is $995. To recap, disputes are filed for the full transaction amount, and any partial refunds will need to be arranged directly with the merchant. Please let us know if there are any further questions at all—we are here to help.
