## Complaint
- The member was transferred to support after being unable to complete their application because a commercial address was submitted instead of a residential address, causing the system to automatically reject the application. Support explained to the member that SoFi policy does not permit commercial addresses for account applications and advised that the address would need to be updated with Experian before the application process could proceed. Unsatisfied with this requirement, the member requested to speak with a higher authority. Support filed an official complaint on the member's behalf, advising that a representative from management would follow up within 24 to 48 business hours, which the member agreed to.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on issues encountered during their online banking account application. After authenticating the member, the agent discussed two primary verification barriers: a name format discrepancy between the member's photo ID and Experian profile, and the system rejection triggered by the physical address provided. The agent clarified that account verification requires the name on Experian to match the ID, and confirmed that the main reason for the application rejection was the submission of a non-USPS commercial address instead of an eligible residential address. Support explained that SoFi policy strictly prohibits commercial addresses for account applications and advised that the residential address must be updated directly with Experian before reapplying. Unsatisfied with this requirement, the member requested a supervisor escalation. Support logged a formal complaint and informed the member that a management representative would follow up within 24 to 48 business hours, which the member accepted.
