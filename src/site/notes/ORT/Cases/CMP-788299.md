## Complaint
- Member contacted via chat to request an update on their current account restrictions. Upon review of the account, it was identified that the Member Security team completed an investigation and made the decision to terminate the business relationship. After being informed of this decision and advised that any eligible remaining funds would typically be returned via a check mailed to the address on file, the member expressed strong frustration. Specifically, the member was highly dissatisfied that the institution could not disclose the reason for the account closure, was unable to provide a specific timeline for when the check would be mailed, and expressed deep concern that their funds might not be returned to them.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong frustration and concern regarding the restriction of their account, the termination of the business relationship, and the lack of a specific timeline or explanation for the return of their funds via a mailed check. Delivering a clear update following a review of the account status, the specialist confirmed that the account restriction has been successfully lifted. Providing an immediate resolution, the specialist advised the member that they are now able to directly access their account and transfer their funds themselves, bypassing the need for a mailed check and resolving their concerns over the availability of their money.
