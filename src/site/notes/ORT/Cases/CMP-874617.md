## Complaint
- The member contacted support to request a last-name update on their account and was sent a Persona verification link to complete identity validation. During the attempt, the member encountered camera permission issues on their device. Support provided device troubleshooting steps and offered an email submission alternative, but the member refused both options, expressed strong frustration with the technical friction, and chose to discontinue the process. Support apologized for the inconvenience, and the name update request was left incomplete per the member's decision.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong frustration regarding technical friction experienced while attempting to update the last name on their account. The member had previously reached out to initiate the name change and was sent a Persona identity verification link, but encountered camera permission issues on their device. Although support offered device troubleshooting steps and an email submission alternative, the member declined both options, expressed frustration with the process, and chose to discontinue the interaction, leaving the request uncompleted. The specialist apologized for the technical difficulties experienced during the identity verification attempt and confirmed that the last-name update remains incomplete per the member's choice to halt the process. The specialist explained that Persona links require active camera permissions to safely validate identity, and instructed the member to call support directly whenever they are ready to proceed so an agent can guide them through the verification steps and complete the name change.
