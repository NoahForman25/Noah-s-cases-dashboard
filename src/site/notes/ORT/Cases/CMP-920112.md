## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding multiple issues with their student loan and customer service experience. They reported receiving a text message regarding their account, but when calling customer service, a representative initially informed them that their account was fine. Shortly after, they received a call from a rude agent seeking to collect a past-due debt resulting from a returned automatic withdrawal. The member logged in and paid the past-due balance, but noted that after making an additional principal payment, their next payment due date was advanced to November. They expressed strong dissatisfaction with SoFi changing payment due dates when paying ahead, calling the practice predatory. Additionally, the member voiced frustration with the AI phone system and requested a formal complaint to be filed. The agent agreed to file a complaint and initiated a transfer to the Student Loan Servicing team.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding collection outreach following a returned automated withdrawal and dissatisfaction with payment due-date advancements. Operational review confirmed that a returned automatic payment triggered standard collection notifications, but verified that no late fees were assessed to the account. To resolve the complaint, the specialist clarified how extra payments advance the scheduled due-date under loan servicing guidelines, confirmed the past-due balance was successfully paid, and credited reward points to the customer's profile as goodwill for the rude collection call and service frustration. The complaint is resolved, with the account updated to current status, no active late fees present, and the loan servicing team made available to assist with future payment schedule management.
