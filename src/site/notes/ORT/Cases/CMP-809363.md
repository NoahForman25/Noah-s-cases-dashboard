## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction after encountering a profile lockout error that prevented them from logging in, and they hung up the call while the agent was attempting to transfer them to the technical support team.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding a profile lockout error that prevented them from logging into their account, as well as the disconnection that occurred while being transferred to technical support. The specialist acknowledged the frustration caused by the access restriction and the dropped call, and provided troubleshooting steps to restore profile access. To resolve the lockout, the specialist advised the member to perform a password reset directly on the login page and assured them that if login difficulties persist, they can call back to be reconnected with a technical support agent for further assistance.
