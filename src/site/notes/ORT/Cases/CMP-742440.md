## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration and requested that a pending transaction be canceled and the funds returned to the account immediately. They also requested an immediate escalation to a supervisor to address the matter.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on their frustration regarding an unnotified hold on a recent $6,800.00 transfer into their savings account, where only $1,500.00 was initially made available. After completing authentication, the agent clarified that the remaining funds were subject to a standard, required security holding period and explained the processing delay. To address the member's dissatisfaction and their request to cancel the pending transaction immediately, the specialist issued a $15.00 courtesy credit as an apology for the inconvenience adn submitted feedback in the feedback mony bank channel; however, the member remained dissatisfied with the explanation and stated their intention to close the account.
