## Complaint
- The client expressed frustration regarding the closure of their crypto account and the fact that they have not yet received a check for the remaining proceeds, noting it has been over 30 days without delivery. The client demanded that the check be issued immediately without fail and stated, "SoFi is great at delivering excuses and poor customer service." Additionally, the client threatened to report SoFi to the "U.S. Treasury Department for Bank Fraud and file a civil lawsuit to recovery the funds plus interest and attorney fee."
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's complaint regarding delayed disbursement of remaining funds following the closure of their crypto account. Operational review confirmed that account liquidation and fund release procedures require a multi-step settlement process before physical disbursement instruments can be generated. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, confirmed that the fund transfer process had been formally initiated the previous day, and verified that a cashier's check was officially ordered for fulfillment. The complaint is resolved, with the crypto account closed and the final balance scheduled for delivery via cashier's check within seven to ten business days.
