## Complaint
- The member explained they were informed that the account restriction would be lifted, but the issue remained unresolved. The member expressed frustration regarding the ongoing delay and the resulting impact on their ability to pay bills.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding delays in lifting an account restriction that disrupted his ability to pay bills. Operational review confirmed that the restriction had been maintained pending the formal completion and clearance of an internal investigation. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the review concluded favorably, issued an apology for the processing timeline, and confirmed that full account access had been restored on October 29. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's accounts fully unrestricted, active, and returned to normal standing.
