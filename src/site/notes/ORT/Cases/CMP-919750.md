## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with SoFi's overdraft feature, which allows recurring transactions to process and drive the account balance negative when funds are insufficient. The member specifically noted a -$15.98 debit card transaction from POSDW DEFERIT* SEP FEE 5AADE on September 29, 2026.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a recurring debit card charge that drove their account balance negative, which they attributed to an unrequested overdraft feature. Operational review confirmed that Samsung Money by SoFi accounts do not offer overdraft protection, and the negative balance was caused by posting transaction timing relative to pending account activity for a $15.98 POSDW DEFERIT charge on September 29, 2026. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding account clearing mechanics and offered to initiate a formal dispute if the charge was unrecognized or unauthorized. The complaint is resolved, with clear account ledger mechanics explained and an open invitation extended to file a transaction dispute if requested.
