## Complaint
- The member expressed strong frustration regarding the extended processing time and overall delays in getting their loan approved. Unwilling to wait further without clear answers, the member explicitly requested an immediate warm transfer to a manager for further assistance and expedited resolution. Support acknowledged the member's dissatisfaction with the underwriting timeline, documented the application delay, and submitted an urgent priority escalation ticket to Lending Operations and Management to review the application status and initiate a manager callback.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an inquiry regarding personal loan processing delays and a misrouted prior escalation. The member had previously expressed strong frustration over extended underwriting timelines and demanded a manager escalation to expedite approval. The specialist authenticated the member and reviewed the application status, confirming that all prior processing hurdles—including ID verification—were fully resolved. The member acknowledged that the verification steps were complete and noted they were advised funding would occur within two to three business days, and the specialist confirmed that the loan was moving forward as expected.
