## Complaint

## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong frustration regarding inaccurate navigation guidance provided by the AI chat assistant when trying to locate the "Manage Account" section in the mobile app. The specialist apologized for the inconvenience and confirmed that a representative had successfully walked the member through the correct step-by-step navigation path during their previous interaction. Additionally, the specialist confirmed that a feedback ticket was submitted for internal review to address the AI assistant's routing errors and improve the accuracy of automated support responses.
