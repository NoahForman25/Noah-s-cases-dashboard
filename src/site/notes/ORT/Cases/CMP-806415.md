## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an unfulfilled third-party promotional offer through Branded Surveys, which promised 10,000 points upon investing $40. The member stated SoFi previously informed Branded Surveys that the offer requirements were not met, and requested that SoFi re-contact the partner site to confirm completion. Additionally, the member requested to submit screenshots of the promotional terms from the Branded Surveys website to be noted on their file.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address an ongoing complaint regarding an unfulfilled promotional offer from a third-party partner, Branded Surveys, which promised 10,000 points upon investing $40. After authenticating the member using their full name and date of birth, the agent reviewed the issue where the member invested required funds and submitted proof, but the vendor refused to award the owed points. To resolve the matter, the agent credited 2,500 points ($25.00) directly to the member's profile, agreed to note their file with promotional screenshots provided by the member, and addressed the member's frustration regarding previously dropped calls. The member expressed gratitude for the credit and shared plans to invest the funds, after which the agent provided general guidance on available investment options while clarifying that specific financial advice could not be offered.
