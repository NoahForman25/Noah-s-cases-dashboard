## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a $27,600 wire transfer lost to an FBI impersonation scam involving their life savings, stating they felt unprotected and that SoFi failed to take their situation seriously. The member voiced frustration over extended hold times, repeated transfers, and a lack of updates on existing tickets (FR-79187 and SS-47098). They raised concerns about the recall process with the receiving bank, noted that scam victims are being directed to SoFi, and offered to provide evidence—including information on another victim. In response, the agent submitted an open escalation/complaint (CMP-922886) for review by a resolution specialist within 24 to 48 hours.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the loss of $27,600 to an FBI impersonation scam, expressed frustration over extended hold times, and concern over a perceived lack of urgency from support. Operational review confirmed that account takeover had occurred and verified that a formal wire recall request was previously submitted to the recipient institution. To resolve the complaint, the specialist apologized for the communication delays and clarified that fund recovery relies strictly on the receiving bank's willingness and ability to return the recalled wire. The complaint is resolved, with the wire recall actively pending with the receiving bank and clear terms established for mailing any returned funds upon receipt.
