## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding the funding process to pay off their existing lender. The member stated they were under a binding contract with SoFi to completely pay off their lender, but are now left with a remaining balance with College Ave and are expected to pay back both loans.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the funding process to pay off their existing student loan lender, College Ave, and their dissatisfaction over an unpaid remaining balance. Operational review confirmed that the payoff amount disbursed strictly matched the payoff figure outlined in the customer's signed Final Disclosure, and that the residual balance resulted from interest and fees that accrued during the application processing window prior to agreement finalization. To resolve the complaint, the specialist explained that additional standalone funds cannot be disbursed under the closed transaction, provided transparency regarding loan payoff mechanics, and outlined alternative paths forward, including paying College Ave directly or applying for a separate refinancing loan for the remaining balance after making their first scheduled payment. The complaint is resolved, with the refinance loan actively funded as structured and clear options established to manage the residual balance with College Ave.
