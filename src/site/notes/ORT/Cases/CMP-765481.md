## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding a lack of transparency surrounding their account closure, noting they were not provided with specific details or reasons for the action. The member described the policy of withholding specific closure information as "ridiculous" and requested to be contacted by an upper-management representative or corporate official who can provide a comprehensive explanation for the closure.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding their account closure, addressing the member's frustration over a lack of transparency and their previous request to speak with upper management. After completing identity verification via the member's full name and date of birth, the agent addressed the member's confusion regarding the vague rule violation email they received. The agent clarified that specific details about the violation could not be disclosed due to policy restrictions, but delivered a path forward by advising the member to wait one month before attempting to reapply for a new account. Additionally, the agent informed the member about the process and timeline for receiving a check in the mail for any remaining funds left in the closed account.
