## Complaint
- The member made a loan payment online that was scheduled for the 24th instead of the intended 23rd. The agent apologized, advised the member that a system issue may have occurred, and assured them that they would not be penalized for the error.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the customer via email to address their complaint regarding an online loan payment that was scheduled for the 24th instead of their intended date of the 23rd. Operational review confirmed that a temporary online system processing error caused the payment date discrepancy. To resolve the complaint, the specialist assured the customer that no late penalties, interest adjustments, or negative account impacts would occur as a result of the system error, and attempted a follow-up phone call to ensure complete satisfaction. The complaint is resolved with the loan account remaining current and penalty-free under its normal terms.
