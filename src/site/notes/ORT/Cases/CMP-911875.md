## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration over not receiving their debit card, which caused an inconvenience and delayed their ability to access their funds.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding repeated delays in receiving a replacement physical debit card following an address update. Operational review confirmed that while a new debit card had been ordered, standard processing and fulfillment workflows had delayed its shipment. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified the updated shipping address, confirmed that an expedited card request had already been submitted to accelerate delivery, and guided the customer on activating their digital card through the mobile app for immediate account access. The complaint is resolved, with the replacement debit card actively expedited for shipment and temporary digital card access fully restored.
