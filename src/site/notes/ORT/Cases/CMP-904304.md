## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration with having to replace their debit card again and noted that it takes too long to receive the physical card. Fraud QA filed the complaint on behalf of the agent who received it on September 15, 2026.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding an ongoing transaction dispute and delays in receiving a replacement physical debit card. Operational review confirmed that the charge investigation remains actively processing within standard review timeframes, while physical card delivery times follow standard postal routing schedules. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency on the dispute progress, confirmed that automated status updates will continue to be sent via email, and submitted a formal operational feedback ticket regarding delivery turnarounds. The complaint is resolved, with the charge dispute actively undergoing review and the replacement card in transit to the customer.
