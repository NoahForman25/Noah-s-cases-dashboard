## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding the requirement to withdraw a recent deposit in their Invest account back to the original funding bank. The member requested instead to transfer the funds directly to their internal Checking/Savings account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding the requirement to return a recent Invest account deposit back to its original external funding bank rather than transferring it directly to their internal Checking and Savings account. The specialist acknowledged the member's dissatisfaction with this limitation and delivered clear procedural context, explaining that Invest and Checking/Savings operate under distinct money-movement rules. To ensure security and allow recently deposited funds to fully clear, standard protocol mandates that recent Invest deposits must be withdrawn back to the originating financial institution before being redirected. Finally, the specialist noted system records showing that the member had already successfully initiated the withdrawal to the original bank, confirming the movement was underway.
