## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an active restriction on their account, which prevented them from completing transactions and moving their funds. The member objected to SoFi's security procedures and the requirement to provide a verified phone number to lift the hold. Although the member ultimately decided to wait until they could furnish the necessary information, they voiced strong dissatisfaction with the process and demanded an immediate account unrestricting. Support documented the member's grievance, explained the account verification requirements, and submitted a review ticket to Risk and Account Operations to monitor the account status.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist reached out to the customer via email to address their complaint regarding an active hold on their account and their request to immediately restore access to their funds. A review confirmed that SoFi made the decision to close the account following a security and risk evaluation, which prevents the restriction from being lifted. To resolve the matter, the specialist communicated that all eligible funds will be disbursed via check to the mailing address on file once the remaining pending transaction fully posts. The account remains restricted and queued for final closure upon the completion of the pending transaction processing.
