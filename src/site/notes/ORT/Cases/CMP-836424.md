## Complaint
- The member expressed strong frustration after a transaction posted to their account despite believing their debit card was locked at the time. Support informed the member that records indicate the card was unlocked when the authorization occurred, allowing the transaction to process successfully. The member also voiced significant dissatisfaction with the standard pending status timeline, explaining that they urgently need the transaction to clear/post so they can formally initiate a charge dispute to recover their funds. Support clarified card locking rules, detailed the dispute eligibility criteria for pending versus posted transactions, and documented the issue for dispute follow-up once the charge clears.
