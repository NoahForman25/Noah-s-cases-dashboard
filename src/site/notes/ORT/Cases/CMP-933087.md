## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration that an instant internal transfer (ICT) withdrawal did not immediately deposit into their checking account after selling stock the previous Wednesday. The member accused SoFi of being dishonest and engaging in deceptive advertising for labeling the transaction as "instant" when funds remained unavailable, stating they plan to leave SoFi as a result.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a delay in receiving funds from an instant internal transfer (ICT) following a stock sale. Operational review confirmed that while internal transfers between investment and deposit accounts are designed for quick processing, standard clearing cycles still require a brief processing window after stock liquidation before funds fully settle into the available balance. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the $245.00 transfer was successfully processed, apologized for the delay and initial confusion around immediate fund availability, and explained the required clearing timeline. The complaint is resolved, with the $245.00 transfer fully completed and posted to the customer's checking account.
