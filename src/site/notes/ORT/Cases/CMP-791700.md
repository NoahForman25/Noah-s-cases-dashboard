## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding an unexpected fee charged to their investment account and the lack of prior communication regarding the charge. The member stated that due to these ongoing communication lapses and fee issues, they are considering completely closing their account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding an unexpected fee charged to their investment account and the lack of prior communication about the charge, which had led the member to consider closing their account entirely. The specialist apologized for the communication lapses and delivered a resolution confirming that the fee was successfully reversed and credited back to the account. Additionally, the specialist explained that document delivery preferences (paper mail vs. paperless electronic delivery) can be updated by phone, and credited 2,000 rewards points (equivalent to $20.00) to the member's profile as goodwill to encourage an ongoing relationship.
