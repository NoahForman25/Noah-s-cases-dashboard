## Complaint
- The member expressed aggravation regarding having their card replaced due to unauthorized charges. The member stated they should not have to go through the trouble of replacing the card and reconnecting it to all their services when they were not the person who made the unauthorized charges.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's complaint regarding repeated card replacements resulting from recurring unauthorized transactions, specifically an unrecognized Amazon charge. Operational review explained that standard security protocols mandate card reissuance to protect compromise points, and merchant token updates via the Mastercard Automatic Billing Updater can inadvertently allow recurring unauthorized debits to transition across new card numbers. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, provided transparency regarding these protective measures, and offered to opt the account out of the automatic billing updater to prevent future merchant-level rollover charges. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's account remaining active while they evaluate the provided security options prior to requesting further profile updates.
