## Complaint
- The member expressed disappointment that an ACH debit posted to their account despite having placed a stop payment. The member stated that a debit card stop payment was useless and ineffective, emphasizing that the stop payment requested was specifically for ACH transactions.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a $20.00 ACH debit from Allstate Ind Co Direct Payment that posted despite an active ACH stop payment order on the account. Operational review confirmed that the transaction bypassed the existing stop payment instructions due to a processing error, allowing the unauthorized debit to clear. To resolve the complaint, the specialist formally acknowledged the failure, issued an apology, and initiated an internal dispute that was promptly approved to credit the funds back to the customer. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute completed in the customer's favor and the account returned to normal standing.
