## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding misinformation provided about their account's charge-off status following a failure to adhere to the agreed-upon payment schedule, and requested to speak with a manager.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding conflicting information about their personal loan charge-off status and concerns over upcoming payment deadlines while awaiting bank documentation. Operational review confirmed that the account faced potential default escalation due to missed scheduled payments, while required financial documents remained pending from the customer's primary bank. To resolve the complaint, the specialist clarified the true status of the account, verified the documentation delay with the customer, and addressed their managerial escalation request by routing the file for immediate supervisor oversight and payment schedule coordination. The complaint is resolved, with the loan account remaining under active manager review to prevent premature charge-off while pending bank documentation is received.
