## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding the pending status of a wire transfer initiated on October 27, 2025, which was urgently required that same day to finalize a vehicle purchase started two days prior. The member became frustrated when informed that the transfer was undergoing an internal review that could not be overridden, and requested to speak with someone who could immediately approve the transfer or to be escalated to a supervisor.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding delays with a pending wire transfer initiated on October 27, 2026, intended for a vehicle purchase. Operational review confirmed that the transfer had been placed on a temporary hold for standard evaluation by the member security team, preventing immediate frontline approval or override. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the security team completed its review and authorized the transaction on October 29, allowing the funds to successfully disburse, and provided an apology for the processing delay. The complaint is resolved, with the wire transfer fully executed and the customer's account in active, good standing with no further action required.
