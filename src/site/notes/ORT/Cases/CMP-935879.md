## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding the personal loan verification of employment process and conflicting information from agents. The applicant explained that their employer requires verification requests to be submitted through a third-party portal (VerificationManager.com) rather than completing standard forms directly, and expressed concern over a lack of proactive communication and delays during the application review. An account manager clarified the process, confirmed receipt of the signed consent form, and updated the request so the verification team can reach out via the employer's preferred third-party portal within one to two business days.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding conflicting information and communication delays during the personal loan verification of employment process. Operational review confirmed that the initial friction stemmed from a miscommunication regarding the employer's requirement for employment verification via a specialized third-party portal (VerificationManager.com) rather than standard direct forms. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, apologized for the communication gaps during prior agent interactions, and verified that an account manager had successfully updated the application file to process the verification through the employer's preferred portal. The complaint is resolved, with the personal loan application fully reviewed, approved, and disbursed to the customer.
