## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi to request the removal of an account restriction that was placed due to unusual activity. The member confirmed that all requested security actions—including verifying the suspicious transactions and enabling two-factor authentication—had been fully completed. The member expressed strong dissatisfaction with the resolution delay, highlighting that more than 24 hours had elapsed since they were told the restriction would be lifted. Support verified the completion of the security requirements, documented the service turnaround delay, and escalated the case to Fraud Operations for immediate priority review to remove the account restriction.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an inquiry regarding a canceled wire transfer and an account restriction triggered by unusual activity. After authenticating the member, the agent addressed the member's previous strong dissatisfaction regarding a delayed resolution, noting that the member had waited over 24 hours for the restriction to be lifted despite successfully verifying suspicious transactions and enabling two-factor authentication. While support had previously documented the service turnaround delay and escalated the case to Fraud Operations for an immediate priority review, the member confirmed during the call that another agent had already resolved the issue and that everything was now in good standing. The specialist acknowledged the member's prior frustration, verified that there are no longer any restrictions on the account, and advised the member that they are clear to safely retry their wire transfer.
