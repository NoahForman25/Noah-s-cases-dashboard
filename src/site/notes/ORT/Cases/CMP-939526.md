## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction upon learning that their SoFi account was selected for closure following an internal review, noting that no prior notification or explanation was provided. They stated they rarely used the account—primarily for crypto purchases and occasional transfers to family—and maintained that they had done nothing wrong. Criticizing SoFi's lack of transparency, the member threatened to report the company to the Better Business Bureau and discourage referred friends from using the platform. Additionally, the member requested that their remaining $0.39 balance be transferred to their linked external bank account rather than issued via check, characterizing any failure to return the funds as thievery.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the unnotified closure of their SoFi account following an internal review and a request to transfer their remaining balance to an external bank account. Operational review confirmed that the account was officially closed on October 16, 2026, and verified that no external bank account was linked to the profile to perform an electronic funds transfer. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided transparency regarding the closure status and arranged for the remaining $0.39 balance to be disbursed via a physical check mailed to the customer's verified address on file. The complaint is resolved, with the account fully closed and the check scheduled to arrive within 7 to 10 business days.
