## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction upon learning that their SoFi account was selected for closure following an internal review, noting that no prior notification or explanation was provided. They stated they rarely used the account—primarily for crypto purchases and occasional transfers to family—and maintained that they had done nothing wrong. Criticizing SoFi's lack of transparency, the member threatened to report the company to the Better Business Bureau and discourage referred friends from using the platform. Additionally, the member requested that their remaining $0.39 balance be transferred to their linked external bank account rather than issued via check, characterizing any failure to return the funds as thievery.
