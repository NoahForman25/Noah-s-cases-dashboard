## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding an inability to log into their SoFi account after updating their email and phone number with the security team. Despite having completed identity verification—including submitting photos of the front and back of their ID and a facial scan—the login system repeatedly prompted for re-verification and rejected the attempts with errors. The member voiced frustration over extended hold times, being transferred repeatedly between security and technical support without resolution, and being locked out while needing to transfer funds from a vault to checking, questioning why they could not access the account after updating their contact information. The agent apologized, clarified that the visual verification error was a technical issue requiring escalation, and placed the member on hold to transfer them to technical support for continued troubleshooting, leaving the issue unresolved on the call.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding recurring identity verification errors, extended hold times across departmental transfers, and an inability to access funds after updating his contact information. Operational review identified that the repeated document verification prompts were caused by an internal technical glitch, while account activity confirmed a successful login was recorded on November 3. To resolve the complaint, the specialist conducted outreach to verify if login functionality remains stable, provided a direct telephone contact line for immediate live troubleshooting, and offered to submit a formal technical support ticket should verification errors recur. The customer's account remains active, open, and in good standing, pending customer confirmation of unrestricted access.
