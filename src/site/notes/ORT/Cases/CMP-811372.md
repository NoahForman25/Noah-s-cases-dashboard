## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration as they were trying to transfer $50 to their Chime account, but they accidentally initiated a wire transfer and got charged a $30 wire fee. They're requesting to reverse the $30 fee.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration after accidentally initiating a wire transfer when attempting to send $50.00 to their Chime account, which resulted in an automatic $30.00 wire fee. The specialist acknowledged the member's request for a fee reversal and explained that wire transfers incur a standard, non-refundable fee due to network processing. However, as a one-time goodwill exception, the specialist credited 3,000 rewards points (equivalent to $30.00) to the member's profile to offset the charge, while advising them to carefully review chosen transfer methods in the future as subsequent accidental wire fees cannot be remediated.
