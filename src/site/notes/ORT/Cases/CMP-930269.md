## Complaint
- The member expressed urgency regarding a dispute for $45.00 with Klover, stating that they need the funds returned immediately for personal reasons.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding the processing timeline and request for an expedited refund on a pending $45.00 dispute with Klover. Operational review confirmed that provisional credit evaluation strictly follows a standard ten-business-day processing window and cannot be manually accelerated under dispute policy guidelines. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, clarified the mandatory investigation timeframes, and credited 1,500 reward points to the customer's account as immediate goodwill compensation while the claim processes. The complaint is resolved, with the $45.00 dispute actively undergoing standard review and reward points applied to the profile.
