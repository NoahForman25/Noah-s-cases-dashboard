## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction regarding the access requirements for a third-party payment service (Zelle). They disputed previous communication indicating they could receive client payments, arguing that the unadvertised requirement to establish a direct deposit first constitutes false advertising. Also stating "will be contacting Forbes regarding their #1 ranking - as well as the FDIC - FTC - and as attorneys who specialize is class action lawsuits"
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and could not reach there voicemail. Then emailed to address their severe dissatisfaction and claims of false advertising regarding unadvertised direct deposit requirements to access Zelle for client payments. The specialist apologized for any previous lack of clarity but explained that an active direct deposit is a standard platform requirement to unlock this payment feature. The member was advised that once a qualifying direct deposit is received, Zelle access will automatically be enabled on the account to allow for the sending and receiving of funds.
## Case Intake & Investigation

### First contact
- The resolution specialist attempted to contact the member regarding their complaint. The specialist did not receive the member's voicemail. The specialist will send a detailed email.
### Second Contact
- Hi James, Thank you for reaching out regarding your access to Zelle and your concerns about receiving client payments. We completely understand how important it is to have clear expectations for your banking tools, and we are here to provide clarity on this feature today. To ensure the highest level of security and functionality for our members, access to Zelle requires an active direct deposit set up on your account. This is a standard requirement across our platform to unlock this specific payment feature. Once a qualifying direct deposit is received, your account will automatically gain access to send and receive funds through Zelle. We apologize that this information was not clear from the beginning. To recap, establishing a direct deposit is the required step to enable Zelle on your account. Please let us know if you have any further questions at all—we are here to help.
