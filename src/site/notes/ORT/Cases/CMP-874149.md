## Complaint
- The member called to discuss issues regarding their personal loan and inquired about the documentation required to request an increase in their total loan amount. Support reviewed the existing loan details, outlined the necessary verification documents and underwriting criteria for a loan modification, and guided the member on the formal application process for additional funding.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an inquiry regarding their personal loan application, required documentation, and recent difficulties reaching customer service. After authenticating the member, the agent addressed their frustration with previous contact attempts and listened to details regarding recent employment changes that impacted their active loan application. The member explained their desire to request an increase in their total loan amount and asked to restart the application process, requesting that the file remain open while they gather updated documentation. The specialist outlined the necessary verification documents and underwriting criteria required for a loan modification, guided the member on the formal re-application process for additional funding, and updated the account notes to ensure the application stays open until the required materials are submitted.
