## Complaint
- The member requested to file a formal complaint regarding a previous interaction with a customer service representative, expressing concerns about perceived unequal treatment and possible discrimination during the call.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint and formal grievance regarding perceived unequal treatment and discrimination during a previous representative interaction. Operational review determined that the specific call recording could not be located in internal records without supplementary interaction details, such as the exact timestamp or originating phone number. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct phone contact information and requested additional call details to enable the quality review team to retrieve the recording and evaluate the representative's conduct. The complaint is resolved, with the formal investigation into the representative interaction paused pending the customer's provision of the necessary contact details to locate the call.
