## Complaint
- Member contacted to request assistance with resolving a charged-off account to prevent further negative credit impact. They expressed significant frustration that the account was closed without prior notice, though records indicate notification emails were sent in February and March. The member offered to pay the negative balance immediately but was informed the debt has been transferred to a third-party collection agency; however, the agent was unable to provide the agency's contact details. The member requested a callback with this information and was advised that paying the debt would not change the recorded reason for the account closure. Although offered the option for the primary holder to open an individual account, the member remains focused on paying the debt to mitigate credit impact and expects a follow-up call with the third-party collection details to resolve the matter.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed the member to address their concerns regarding a charged-off account, its impact on their credit, and their request for third-party collection agency details to pay the negative balance. The specialist clarified that a charge-off on a checking or savings account does not impact the member's credit score, as credit reporting for charge-offs applies exclusively to credit cards and loan products. Furthermore, the member was advised that the institution does not pursue collections after a deposit account charge-off is initiated, meaning no outstanding balance remains to be settled and no third-party collection agency involvement or further payment is required.
