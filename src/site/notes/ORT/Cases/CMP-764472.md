## Complaint
- Member contacted to express concern regarding ongoing difficulties completing an application for a new checking and savings account. The member expressed apprehension that if their application is being denied, it may be due to fraudulent activity involving someone applying under their name without their knowledge.

During the interaction, the member requested to speak with a supervisor but was disconnected while the representative was attempting to transfer the call to the Escalations team. A supervisor callback has been scheduled to follow up with the member.

> **Internal Note:** Per previous ticket #7505600, the member is ineligible to open an account. The application request has been completed and cleared.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their concerns regarding ongoing difficulties completing a new checking and savings account application, their apprehension about potential identity fraud, and the accidental disconnection that occurred during an attempted transfer to the Escalations team for a supervisor review. The specialist delivered a final resolution, confirming that the application review under ticket #7505600 has been fully processed and cleared, and that the profile remains ineligible to open a new account. The member was informed that to maintain compliance with security protocols, further specific details regarding the final decision cannot be disclosed.
