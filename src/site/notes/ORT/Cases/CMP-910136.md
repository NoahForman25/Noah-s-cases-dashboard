## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding difficulty navigating the mobile app due to two-factor authentication loops and dissatisfaction with an agent's demeanor on a prior call, requesting an apology and manager callback. The member was also dissatisfied that their mobile check deposit limits ($2,500 daily / $5,000 monthly) could not be manually increased, noting the limits are insufficient for their weekly paycheck amounts. The agent assisted the member in switching their two-factor authentication method to phone calls to allow app access. After transferring the call to an account manager, the manager explained that deposit limits are subject to automated monthly evaluations based on account activity and cannot be adjusted manually. The manager filed a complaint regarding the previous agent's conduct and escalated the limit increase request to the Resolutions team for follow-up within 24 to 48 hours. The member stated they would look for another bank.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist addressed the customer's complaint regarding two-factor authentication access issues, agent demeanor during a previous contact, and the inability to manually increase mobile check deposit limits. Operational review confirmed that mobile check deposit limits of $2,500 daily and $5,000 monthly are evaluated automatically on a monthly basis based on account history and cannot be overridden manually. To resolve the issue, the specialist assisted in updating the customer's two-factor authentication method to phone calls to restore app access, delivered an official apology for the previous agent interaction, and logged internal feedback regarding the service experience. The complaint is resolved, with two-factor authentication functionality restored and deposit limits remaining subject to automated monthly profile evaluations.
