## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding their restricted account and inability to access their funds. After being informed of a one-business-day turnaround time for the escalated case, the member described the timeline as ridiculous and demanded a supervisor, despite being advised that a manager would provide the same information, and subsequently disconnected the call.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a temporary account restriction and dissatisfaction over the one-business-day turnaround time for case resolution. Operational review confirmed that the restriction was fully resolved the following business day and all administrative holds were removed from the account. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that the restriction had been lifted and provided clear instructions for the customer to perform a password reset via the mobile app or website to release the security lock on their profile. The complaint is resolved, with account restrictions removed and password recovery steps established for the customer to regain immediate access.
