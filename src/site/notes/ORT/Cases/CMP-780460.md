## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding their inability to deposit a check, noting that the system blocked the transaction due to account eligibility requirements.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding an inability to deposit a check due to a system block related to account eligibility requirements. The specialist delivered a clear update, confirming that the mobile check deposit transaction was systematically blocked because the account does not currently meet the mandatory eligibility criteria required to utilize the feature.
