## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration and persistent confusion regarding the Verification of Employment (VOE) process. Despite support providing detailed explanations of the procedure and the specific underwriting requirements, the member adhered to her initial misunderstanding, remained unreceptive to the clarification, and voiced dissatisfaction with the documentation demands. Support re-explained the VOE guidelines, documented the member's feedback regarding process clarity, and logged the interaction for underwriting review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her frustration and persistent confusion regarding the Verification of Employment (VOE) documentation requirements for her application. The member had previously expressed dissatisfaction with the demands of the verification process and remained unreceptive despite support providing detailed explanations of underwriting guidelines and procedures. While support had previously re-explained the VOE requirements and logged the interaction for underwriting review, the specialist confirmed that the Verification of Employment has now been officially completed and cleared. The specialist apologized for the confusion experienced throughout the process, confirmed that the submitted documents are actively being reviewed to advance her application, and assured the member that her feedback regarding process clarity was documented for internal quality review.
