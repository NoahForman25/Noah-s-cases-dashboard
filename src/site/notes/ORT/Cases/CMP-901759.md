## Complaint

The agent attempted to send the statement via a zip file, but the member remained dissatisfied. A ticket was created for the back-office team to provide a compiled document.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist reached out to the customer via email to address their complaint regarding receiving their May 2025 checking account statement as individual files rather than a single compiled document. Operational review confirmed that initial delivery via a compressed zip file did not meet the customer's formatting needs, requiring direct back-office document generation. To resolve the issue, the specialist submitted a high-priority fulfillment ticket for the back-office team to compile and deliver the unified checking statement. The complaint is resolved, with the back-office team actively processing the document request for final delivery via secure email.
