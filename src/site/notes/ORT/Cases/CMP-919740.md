## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding the denial of their claim despite submitting proof of delivery for an order that was never received. The member disputed the claim denial rationale—which stated the merchant received a valid authorization—clarifying that their claim was for non-receipt of goods rather than an unauthorized transaction. The member requested to appeal the decision, noted they had no further documents to submit, and requested a temporary credit while the dispute is under review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the initial denial of a non-receipt of goods dispute and a request for provisional credit during the appeal. Operational review confirmed that the original denial incorrectly relied on valid merchant authorization data rather than evaluating non-receipt of merchandise, prompting an immediate appeal submission. To resolve the complaint, the specialist formally resubmitted the claim under the correct non-receipt criteria using the previously provided delivery documentation and clarified that provisional credit will be issued within 10 business days if the appeal investigation remains pending. The complaint is resolved, with the formal appeal actively under review by the dispute team and clear timeline expectations set for provisional credit.
