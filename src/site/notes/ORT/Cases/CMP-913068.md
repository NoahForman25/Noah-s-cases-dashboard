## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with SoFi's decision to close their account and restrict access to their funds, describing it as the strangest action they had ever seen a bank take. They voiced frustration over being unable to transfer or access their money immediately, particularly following a dispute they had filed. The member repeatedly demanded an explanation for why the account was locked and who could provide further details regarding the restriction.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding account closure, restricted access to funds, and the handling of a $14,000.00 dispute alongside a $4,285.12 direct deposit. Operational review confirmed that the account was permanently closed following an internal risk evaluation, and that external originators maintain full authority to initiate reversals or adjustments on direct deposit transfers. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding the finality of the account closure, addressed the status of the disputed charges, and clarified that discrepancies regarding direct deposit amounts must be resolved directly with the originating employer. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining permanently closed and the customer informed of the proper channels to handle deposit adjustments externally.
