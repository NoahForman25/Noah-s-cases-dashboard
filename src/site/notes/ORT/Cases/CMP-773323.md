## Complaint
- The initial agent requested assistance regarding a member who asked to be connected with an Account Manager to follow up on a previously opened appeal, asserting that they had already submitted new information which the initial agent was unable to locate within the system records. Upon review, it was confirmed and explained to the member that they had indeed provided new documentation, specifically located in previous follow-up tickets **ZD #8186180** and **ZD #8194275**; however, the member was ultimately advised that while the receipt of this additional information was confirmed, the documentation has already been thoroughly reviewed and the original decision stands as no error was found.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed the member to address their dispute appeal, the submission of additional documentation, and their previous request to speak with an Account Manager after an initial agent was unable to locate their files. The specialist delivered a final resolution, confirming that the new documentation was successfully received and reviewed under tickets #8186180 and #8194275; however, based on the findings of the escalated review, no error was found and the original dispute decision stands as final. Additionally, the specialist confirmed that the official denial documents had been previously sent via email and remain accessible to the member for their records.
