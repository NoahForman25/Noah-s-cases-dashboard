## Complaint
- The client expressed dissatisfaction regarding confusion and delays surrounding the sale of ARKVX, stating the asset should have been sold by now. She noted that a previous representative, Chris Webley, advised her it would be sold by today, October 2, 2026. The client believes she is being taken advantage of.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding delays and confusion surrounding the sale of ARKVX shares. Operational review confirmed that the order was executed on October 3, 2026, which fell within the expected processing window outlined by the previous representative. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided full transparency regarding asset liquidation timelines, apologized for the miscommunication regarding execution speed, and verified that the transaction successfully completed. The complaint is resolved, with the security sale fully finalized and no further action required on the account.
