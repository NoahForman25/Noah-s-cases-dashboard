## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding the prolonged delay in receiving their remaining balance following an account closure. Having already waited approximately two weeks, the member questioned why the disbursement process could take up to 30 days and voiced intense frustration over receiving scripted responses, inconsistent information from different representatives, and a lack of a clear explanation or specific delivery timeline. The member explicitly stated their intent to file a formal regulatory complaint with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB). Support detailed the standard post-closure reconciliation and disbursement procedures, logged an official high-priority service complaint regarding agent communication inconsistencies, and escalated the case to Account Operations to expedite the manual review and release of the remaining funds.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on their strong dissatisfaction regarding a prolonged delay in receiving their remaining balance after an account closure. Having waited approximately two weeks, the member expressed intense frustration with standard 30-day disbursement timelines, scripted support responses, contradictory information from representatives, and a lack of clear delivery updates, stating an intent to file a formal regulatory complaint with the CFPB. After authenticating the member, the specialist investigated the account balance and clarified that the funds were not held for a mailed check, but had been returned directly to the originating source, which the member identified as their Coinbase account. The specialist provided the exact transaction ID and time the funds were transferred back to Coinbase to assist the member in locating them. While support previously logged an official high-priority complaint regarding communication inconsistencies and escalated the case to Account Operations, the specialist accommodated the member's request to keep the ticket open for ongoing tracking and provided direct contact information for future reference.
