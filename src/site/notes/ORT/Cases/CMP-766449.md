## Complaint
- Member contacted via chat to express strong dissatisfaction and a lack of security regarding their experience with the institution, stating that they have never encountered so many issues with a bank before. Due to these ongoing issues and their safety concerns, the member indicated their intention to close their account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding unauthorized transactions that had drained the member's account balance. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the fraudulent activity and placed formal disputes for the unauthorized merchant transactions. Providing an immediate resolution to secure the account, the agent ordered a new card and placed a freeze on the card, and confirmed the member's mailing address. Additionally, the agent discussed how the member can securely manage their new card information directly through the mobile application.
