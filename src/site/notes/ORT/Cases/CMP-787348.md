## Complaint
- Stated that SoFi promotes bonuses at a certain time frame, but doesn't complete on their end. Has been over a month and doesn't know why is not being provided what was advertised.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed the member to address their dissatisfaction regarding an advertised direct deposit welcome bonus that they believed was delayed by over a month. The specialist delivered a clear update on the account's promotional status, explaining that the program includes a mandatory 25-day evaluation window starting from the first qualifying deposit to track cumulative amounts. The specialist confirmed that the member's first deposit posted on July 6, 2026, and verified that their current qualifying total of $2,826.38 currently positions them for a $50.00 payout tier. Finally, the specialist clarified that the tracking period will officially conclude on July 31, 2026, giving the member until that date to reach the $5,000.00 threshold required to qualify for the higher promotional bonus payout.
