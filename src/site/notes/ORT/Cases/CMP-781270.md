## Complaint
- Member contacted to express significant frustration after noticing transactions from their external Bank of America account incorrectly displaying within their SoFi account interface. The member also reported a secondary technical issue regarding the mobile application logging them out too quickly, which has severely disrupted their overall user experience. Highlighting the data overlap, the member insisted that the appearance of external transactions within their SoFi profile constitutes a serious privacy breach, raising urgent concerns regarding the security, privacy, and integrity of their financial data.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address privacy concerns regarding external Bank of America and Chase accounts visible within their profile, explaining it was caused by the SoFi Relay product and successfully deleting those connections per the member's request. Additionally, the agent addressed an app timeout issue and provided an update on a missing $15,000.00 wire transfer previously sent to Golden Road Cars, noting that formal recovery inquiries were sent to the receiving bank on July 8 and July 10, 2026. The agent confirmed a separate $98,000.00 wire was successful and promised to submit an escalated investigation ticket for the lost funds with a follow-up via email.
