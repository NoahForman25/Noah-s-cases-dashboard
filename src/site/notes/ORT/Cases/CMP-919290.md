## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding an issue with an autopayment attempt that resulted in NSF fees assessed to their account. The member stated they contacted SoFi on September 15, 2026, and were advised they would receive clarification within a few days, but received no further communication. The agent apologized for the lack of communication and connected the member with an account manager for assistance.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding non-sufficient funds (NSF) fees triggered by an automated payment attempt and the lack of subsequent follow-up. Operational review confirmed that notification emails detailing the NSF event were sent and an outbound call attempt was made, though timely direct communication was delayed. To resolve the complaint, an account manager previously intervened and credited reward points to the customer's account to fully offset the assessed NSF fees. The complaint is resolved, with the fee reimbursement issued and the account returned to good standing with no further action required.
