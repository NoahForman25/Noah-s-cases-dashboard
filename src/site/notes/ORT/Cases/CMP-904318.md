## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with the delay in accessing their funds, noting they had called multiple times over several days without resolution. They were frustrated that a new account could not be opened while a pending $2 parking transaction remained unsettled on their compromised old account, questioning why the old account could not remain open with the small balance rather than requiring full closure first. The agent apologized for the delay and explained that all pending transactions and disputes must be settled before closing a compromised account and issuing a new one. The member felt this process was unreasonable and lacked a clear timeline, and requested to speak with a manager to bypass it; after expectations were set that a manager would provide the same guidance, the member did not insist. The agent reassured the member that the new account request was submitted and instructed them to monitor their email for updates once the pending transaction clears and the new account becomes available.
