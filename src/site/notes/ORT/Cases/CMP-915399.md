## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding an account restriction and conflicting advice from a previous representative, who had advised them to close the account and open a new one due to suspicious login activity and a phone number update. The member voiced extreme urgency and frustration as they were stranded out of town with a flat tire and unable to access their funds, while confirming that their logins on two devices and a $200 House of Sport transaction were legitimate. Additionally, the member was frustrated by having to repeat the enhanced identity verification process, long hold times, the inability to immediately remove the restriction or speak with a live supervisor, and mentioned consulting a lawyer. The agent advised that the review would take one business day to complete and that a manager was currently unavailable, prompting the member to request a callback.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding account restrictions, conflicting guidance to close the account, and repeated identity verification delays while stranded away from home. Operational review confirmed that security protocols triggered the temporary restriction following suspicious login activity and a primary phone number update, but verified that all logins and the $200 House of Sport charge were legitimate. To resolve the complaint, the specialist removed the account restriction to restore immediate access to funds, confirmed that the legitimate $200 transaction and an additional pending Aspen Davenport charge were processing toward posting, and provided instructions for disputing the latter if unrecognized. The complaint is resolved, with full account access restored and pending transactions processing normally.
