## Complaint
- Member expressed frustration while demanding provisional credit and was advised that an update regarding the credit decision would be sent via email within 10 business days. The member threatened legal action and requested to speak with a supervisor. A manager callback was created and scheduled accordingly (Atlas Ticket #8301848).
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding a provisional credit request, the associated 10-business-day processing timeframe, and an escalated supervisor callback (Atlas Ticket #8301848) following threats of legal action. The specialist acknowledged the member's frustration over the review period and confirmed an unsuccessful attempt to reach them by phone to discuss the escalation. Delivering clarification on the dispute process, the specialist explained that standard procedures mandate up to 10 business days for the team to complete its review, during which time provisional funds will either be issued to the account or a formal determination notice will be sent once the evaluation period concludes.
