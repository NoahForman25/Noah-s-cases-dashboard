## Complaint
- Member contacted expressing frustration over being unable to activate Zelle, noting that expected direct deposit funds have not hit the account. The member voiced concerns regarding feature eligibility and internal policies, feeling treated unfairly compared to other financial institutions where direct deposit is not a mandatory prerequisite for Zelle enrollment. Additionally, the member reported severe difficulty funding the account and expressed strong dissatisfaction after incurring Non-Sufficient Funds (NSF) charges as a result.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding the inability to activate Zelle, delayed direct deposit funds, and incurred Non-Sufficient Funds (NSF) charges. The specialist acknowledged the member's frustration with Zelle eligibility requirements and internal policies, particularly their feeling of unfair treatment compared to institutions where direct deposit is not required for Zelle access. Delivering policy clarification and guidance, the specialist explained that the account currently does not meet qualifications for Zelle enrollment and advised that a recurring ACH direct deposit of regular income (such as payroll, pension, or benefits) is required to move toward eligibility once any account restrictions are clear. Addressing the funding issues, the specialist clarified that NSF fees occur when transactions process without sufficient available funds and cautioned the member to ensure funds are not being held by their Smart Card before attempting future transfers.

We understand that it is frustrating getting this so make sure that their are available funds in account that is not being held by your Smart Card before attempting again.
