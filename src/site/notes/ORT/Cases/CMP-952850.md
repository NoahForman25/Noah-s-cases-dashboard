## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with being unable to cash or deposit checks from their SoFi account. The agent intended to transfer the member to the Banking Team, but the call was disconnected.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the inability to cash or deposit a check and frustration over a disconnected transfer to the Banking Team. Operational review confirmed that the check received was unfunded because the member security team had initiated the creation of a new, secure account to house the customer's balance. To resolve the complaint, the specialist coordinated with the security team, assigned a dedicated agent to complete the account setup within one to two days, and assured the customer that the funds would be fully accessible once the new account goes live. The complaint is resolved, with the new account setup actively in progress and the funds scheduled for immediate release upon completion.
