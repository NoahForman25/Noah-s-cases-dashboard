## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding unauthorized changes made to their profile information, stating that unauthorized individuals were able to update the account details while they were unable to do so. They characterized the security discrepancy as ridiculous and requested an immediate review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding unauthorized updates made to their profile information and their concern over account security. The specialist clarified that a comprehensive account review indicated the changes were made directly through the member's profile login, and recommended an immediate password update to prevent further unauthorized access and secure the profile. Additionally, the specialist acknowledged the member's decision to close their accounts due to this experience and expressed regret over their departure.
