## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration while attempting to update the phone number on file without a valid state ID. The member requested that verification be conducted via email or through other required details, stating they need access to their profile to purchase a new ID and arguing there should be a workaround. Additionally, the member asked to escalate the call and remarked that Navy Federal is a better bank.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the requirement of a valid state ID to update their account phone number and their request for alternative verification via email. Operational review confirmed that identity verification cannot be bypassed or handled through email, as strict security protocols require a real-time interaction to confirm identity before high-risk profile changes are authorized. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided transparency regarding security guidelines, offered direct personal assistance during specified office hours to walk the customer through the verification process efficiently, and explained the next steps needed to apply the phone number update. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining secure and the phone number update pending the completion of real-time identity verification.
