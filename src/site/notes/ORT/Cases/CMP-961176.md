## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with an ongoing account restriction and strict identity verification protocols that prevent access to funds needed for essential expenses like food and bills. The member strongly objected to submitting sensitive information and legal identification without reciprocation, demanding that the agent and supervisor provide their own legal IDs via email or FaceTime to prove they are real individuals and not AI. Furthermore, the member expressed extreme frustration with the agent's repetitive responses, stating the representative sounded like a robot, and contested the verification requirements by noting they previously opened the account without having to complete these identification steps.
