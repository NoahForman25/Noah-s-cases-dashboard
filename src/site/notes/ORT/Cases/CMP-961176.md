## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with an ongoing account restriction and strict identity verification protocols that prevent access to funds needed for essential expenses like food and bills. The member strongly objected to submitting sensitive information and legal identification without reciprocation, demanding that the agent and supervisor provide their own legal IDs via email or FaceTime to prove they are real individuals and not AI. Furthermore, the member expressed extreme frustration with the agent's repetitive responses, stating the representative sounded like a robot, and contested the verification requirements by noting they previously opened the account without having to complete these identification steps.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding an account restriction, identity verification protocols, and the resulting disruption to accessing funds for essential expenses. Operational review confirmed that identity verification is a mandatory, non-bypassable security protocol required to protect account integrity, and established that the restriction had been fully cleared following the completion of the internal investigation. To resolve the complaint, the specialist issued an apology for the customer's experience, provided concrete troubleshooting steps for mobile document capture, and offered to submit an internal technical support ticket if further verification issues occur. The complaint is resolved, with the account officially unrestricted as of October 30 and returned to active standing with full fund access restored.
