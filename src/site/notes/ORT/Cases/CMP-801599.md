## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding the inability to access their funds and a lack of specific details surrounding their account closure. The member stated they performed no unusual activity on the account and questioned why it was flagged or identified for closure.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding a recent account restriction, the inability to access their funds, and the lack of specific details surrounding their account closure. The specialist acknowledged the member's distress regarding the disruption to their direct deposits, especially as the member maintained they performed no unusual activity. Delivering a final decision, the specialist explained that following a comprehensive review, the account was identified for closure in accordance with standard banking procedures—a decision that is final and irreversible, with specific internal closure criteria remaining confidential. The specialist reassured the member that any eligible remaining balance will be issued via check and mailed to the address on file after the official account closure email is sent.
