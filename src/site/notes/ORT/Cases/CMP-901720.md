## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding their inability to access or update a bank account opened by their ex-boyfriend in 2022, citing inconsistent information from representatives on whether the main account was closed. They requested to either close the existing account or update their account details, but were unable to complete the required enhanced verification because their only form of identification is expired. The agent explained that valid, unexpired identification is required to update account information or open a new account, confirmed the profile shows as closed, and placed the member on hold to transfer them to a manager.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding conflicting information about account status and the inability to update contact details on a long-inactive account. Operational review confirmed that the customer's profile is fully closed, and security policy strictly mandates a valid, unexpired government-issued identification to complete enhanced verification or update profile records. To resolve the matter, the specialist clarified the account's closed status, acknowledged the service friction, and advised the customer on the necessity of obtaining valid identification prior to attempting any profile updates or new account creation. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining closed until unexpired identification can be provided.
