## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after a previous representative advised them to sign a check four times, which messed up the check.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding a mobile check deposit endorsement issue and concerns over fund availability schedules. Operational review confirmed that the initial $500.00 portion of the September 25 check had processed correctly according to standard hold schedules, and despite prior conflicting representative instructions regarding check endorsements, the item remained valid and un-cancelled. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct transparency regarding the hold release schedule for the remaining funds, reassured the customer that the deposit would process without cancellation, and clarified proper check endorsement guidelines for future deposits. The complaint is resolved, with the remaining check funds actively processing toward scheduled availability and no further deposit interruptions.
