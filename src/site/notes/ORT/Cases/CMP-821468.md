## Complaint
- The member expressed strong frustration regarding persistent Face ID authentication failures on the app. When support offered a standard password reset to bypass the issue, the member refused the troubleshooting step and requested an immediate supervisor escalation to address the technical functionality of their account login.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an inquiry regarding recent account access difficulties. After initiating identity verification, the agent addressed the member's inquiry about the timing of the call, as the member noted the issue had been resolved the previous day. The member explained that the disruption was caused by persistent Face ID authentication failures on the mobile app, which had prompted strong frustration and a request for a supervisor escalation when standard password reset troubleshooting was previously offered. The agent confirmed that because the member was now able to access their account and considered the login issue resolved, no further action or escalation was required at that time.
