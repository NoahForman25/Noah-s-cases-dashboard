## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after learning that SoFi could not expedite their $2,500.00 mobile check deposit. After an account manager informed them that the standard 3 to 5 business day timeframe applies, the member requested to escalate the matter to a representative above the account manager level. The account manager apologized for the inconvenience and advised the member to expect a response from a resolution specialist within 24 to 48 hours.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding the inability to expedite a $2,500.00 mobile check deposit. Operational review confirmed that mobile check deposits are subject to standard bank clearing schedules and funds availability policies, which require a processing timeframe of three to five business days and cannot be manually accelerated. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, acknowledged their frustration, and provided clear transparency regarding standard deposit processing windows and funds release timelines. The complaint is resolved, with the $2,500.00 mobile deposit processing under standard clearing timelines and expected to post to the account upon full settlement.
