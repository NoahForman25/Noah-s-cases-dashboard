## Complaint
- The member expressed extreme dissatisfaction with the level of service and professionalism experienced across multiple channels, stating they prefer to discontinue their relationship with SoFi due to employees treating their concerns as irrelevant and a supervisor being "beyond rude" during a previous conversation. Highlighting significant service friction, the member noted that despite initiating eight separate chat sessions and making two phone calls, their underlying issue remains completely unresolved. Support acknowledged the unacceptable service experience, logged a formal complaint against the supervisory interaction, and escalated the unresolved issue to Executive Customer Advocacy and Operations Leadership for immediate review and resolution.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her extreme dissatisfaction with the service and professionalism experienced across eight chat sessions and two phone calls, which led her to express a desire to discontinue her relationship with SoFi. The member reported that representatives treated her concerns as irrelevant, described a previous supervisory interaction as "beyond rude," and emphasized that her underlying technical issue remained completely unresolved despite extensive follow-up. The specialist apologized for the severe service friction and confirmed that a formal complaint regarding the supervisory conduct was logged and escalated to Executive Customer Advocacy and Operations Leadership for immediate executive review. Additionally, to address previous errors where rewards were incorrectly directed to her Invest account, the specialist credited 1,500 reward points ($15.00) directly to her profile with specific functionality allowing redemption into her Checking and Savings account, while reassuring her that the unresolved issue is under active investigation.
