## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration that they did not want to provide the reason for the call and insisted on being transferred to the fraud department. The member mentioned "I don't give a f***" and the member doesn't want to talk to representative overseas.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding a disputed $564.00 Verta transaction and dissatisfaction with prior customer service handling. Operational review of the account's transaction history confirmed that no charge or authorization attempt for $564.00 from Verizon was received or processed by the bank. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, provided clear transparency regarding the absence of the transaction on the account logs, and explained that a dispute cannot be initiated without a posted or pending charge reaching the system. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining fully operational and standard dispute options available should the transaction appear on the account in the future.
