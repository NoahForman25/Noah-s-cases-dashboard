## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding a **$286.45** Collection Order Hold applied to their account on August 14, 2026. The member requested that the held funds be returned immediately and stated that they have decided to close their SoFi account as a result of the incident. Support explained the legal and compliance requirements surrounding legal collection orders, documented the account closure request, and escalated the case to Operations to review the hold status and handle the account closure process.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on an inquiry regarding an account restriction, held funds, and the overall status of their account. After authenticating the member by verifying their name and date of birth, the agent addressed the member's strong dissatisfaction concerning a $286.45 Collection Order Hold applied to their account. The member requested an immediate return of the held funds and expressed a desire to close their SoFi account due to the incident. The agent explained the legal and compliance requirements governing collection order holds, discussed options for linking an external Discover account to facilitate fund transfers, and outlined potential steps to delay or manage account closure by adding funds. To address the member's requests, support documented their desire to keep funds accessible where possible while honoring the account closure request, escalating the case to Operations for a comprehensive review of the hold status and execution of the closure process.
