## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding an unauthorized transaction in the amount of $750.00 that successfully processed through their account. The member noted that the transaction went through before they received a fraud verification message from SoFi asking if they recognized the activity. The member emphasized that these funds consist of their disability benefits and represent the only money they currently have, demanding that the $750.00 be returned to them as soon as possible.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding a disputed transaction that was approved without their consent. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's dissatisfaction and urgency regarding the $750.00 charge, noting that the transaction processed before they could respond to a fraud alert, and that these funds represent their essential disability benefits. The agent explained how the automatic security system handles transactions and acknowledged that the member had already submitted all necessary documentation. Ultimately, the agent reassured the member that the team is actively investigating the dispute, while documenting their concerns regarding previous resolution delays and the immediate financial impact.
