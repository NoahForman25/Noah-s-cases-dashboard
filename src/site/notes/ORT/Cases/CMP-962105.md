## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction after forgetting to refer her joint account holder through her referral link, and requested to receive the referral bonus despite not having used the link.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's complaint regarding missing rewards points after she failed to use her referral link when setting up her husband's account. Operational review confirmed that the referral connection had not been attached during account opening and customer service onboarding had not prompted the referral link. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, provided a one-time goodwill compensation of 5,000 points ($50.00) to the customer and 2,500 points ($25.00) to her husband, and walked through redemption instructions via the mobile app and website. The complaint is resolved, with both members' accounts remaining active, in good standing, and successfully credited with the promotional points.
