## Complaint
- The member called after being locked out of the SoFi app due to a forgotten password and losing access to the email address on file, urgently needing to view recent funds deposited into their account. While attempting the required identity verification process to update the email address, the member experienced multiple technical difficulties, including delays receiving the link, camera capture issues, persistent uploading screens, and repeated "unexpected error" messages. The member expressed strong frustration, stating that banking with SoFi has been a hassle since opening the account and mentioning they may stop using the bank. The agent offered to transfer the member to technical support to resolve the verification issue.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding multiple technical failures during the identity verification process, which prevented her from recovering app access to view urgent funds. Operational review indicated that the identity verification workflow encountered repeated mobile camera capture and upload errors required to update her inaccessible email address, and an attempted live transfer to technical support was cut off when the call dropped. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided dedicated direct support contact details and offered to either facilitate an immediate connection with technical support or submit a technical troubleshooting ticket on her behalf to guide her through the verification steps. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's account remaining open and active under access lockout pending direct technical troubleshooting to successfully verify her identity and restore online login credentials.
