## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration, accusing SoFi of misleading them regarding a credit card referral bonus. The member disputed the timeline, stating that they believed the processing period had already exceeded the required 7 business days.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their concerns regarding a credit card referral bonus and its processing timeline. The specialist validated the importance of receiving promotional points promptly but clarified that, upon review, the system indicates the referred individuals have not yet completed the necessary criteria to trigger the bonus. To resolve the issue, the specialist recommended that the referees contact the institution directly so their specific account statuses can be reviewed and any missing requirements can be identified to help complete the process.
## Case Intake & Investigation
