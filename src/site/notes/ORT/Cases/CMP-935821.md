## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration that their account remains unclosed and that a check has not been issued as previously advised after being unable to link a bank account, noting multiple calls to SoFi regarding this request. The member could not be verified during the call because they were unable to provide the last four digits of their Social Security number, receive a one-time passcode, or complete automated identity verification. The agent emailed the member and requested that they call back when they have the necessary information to complete verification.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the delayed closure of four Invest accounts and the issuance of remaining balances via physical check. Operational review confirmed that telephone account discussions were restricted due to incomplete identity verification when the customer was unable to provide required security credentials or receive a one-time passcode during prior calls. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided written confirmation that all four Invest accounts were officially closed on October 16, 2026, and that a check for the remaining balances is being issued and mailed. The complaint is resolved, with the four Invest accounts fully closed and funds in transit via check.
