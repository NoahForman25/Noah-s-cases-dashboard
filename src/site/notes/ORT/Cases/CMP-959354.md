## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction that their checking and savings accounts were restricted during a fraud review, preventing access to funds needed for rent, bills, food, and gas, and raising concerns about potential eviction. The member requested that SoFi reject an incoming direct deposit scheduled for that night or the following morning, explaining that their employer advised having it rejected so a live paper check could be issued instead of being held in the restricted account. Additionally, the member expressed frustration with receiving conflicting information from multiple representatives and stated that a prior agent refused to assist them with moving their money.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding account restrictions during a fraud review, conflicting representative guidance, and the urgent request to reject an incoming direct deposit to prevent funds from being held. Operational review confirmed that the restriction protocol successfully prevented the pending direct deposit from posting to the restricted profile, allowing the customer's employer to reissue payment directly, while associated Zelle limitations were cleared. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that clean replacement checking and savings accounts were established to restore full banking access and invited the customer to reach back out if any secondary service issues arise. The complaint is resolved, with new accounts successfully opened and active in good standing, while the direct deposit was kept from posting to the restricted profiles.
