## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding suspected fraudulent activity on their debit card after receiving a fraud alert for denied transactions from Citibank and Bank of America. The member voiced frustration over recurring security issues, noting this is the third time their card has been compromised and stating that SoFi fails to protect their funds. Additionally, the member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a previously denied dispute for $847.03 in FGT Baha charges from August, reiterating that they did not authorize the transactions. In response, the agent submitted an appeal for the $847.03 dispute, deactivated the compromised card, issued a replacement debit card, and provided contact details for the banking and member security teams.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding recurring debit card compromise, fraud alerts from external institutions, and dissatisfaction over a previously denied $847.03 dispute. Operational review confirmed that a secondary appeal was submitted for the $847.03 FGT Baha transaction and verified that the compromised card was deactivated to prevent further unauthorized activity. To resolve the complaint, the specialist apologized for the security disruptions, confirmed that a replacement debit card was dispatched to the customer's address, and established immediate reporting protocols in the event of future unauthorized attempts. The complaint is resolved, with the $847.03 dispute actively undergoing secondary appeal review and the replacement debit card in transit.
