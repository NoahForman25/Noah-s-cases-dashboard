After account validation, the agent confirmed the funds were sent via ACH transfer rather than an instant network transfer, which standardly requires up to three business days to clear. The member remained highly dissatisfied with this timeframe, and an idle warning was sent during the interaction after the member stopped responding.

- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on their dissatisfaction regarding the processing timeframe for a $170.00 incoming Zelle transaction, which had previously been confirmed as an ACH transfer requiring up to three business days to clear. Before identity verification could be completed, the member informed the specialist that the delayed transaction had since been resolved and disconnected the call without further action being taken.
