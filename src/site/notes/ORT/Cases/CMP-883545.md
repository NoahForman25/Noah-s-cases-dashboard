## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding their accepted personal loan terms, emphasizing that the interest rate on the five-year option was far too high and stating they intended to select a different loan option before signing the agreement without a full review. Pointing out that the final loan terms did not align with their expectations, the member stated an intent to file a complaint with the Better Business Bureau (BBB) and expressed frustration over the inability to modify the contract terms post-origination, noting they now plan to pay off the balance within two years to mitigate interest costs. Support acknowledged the member's concerns regarding the interest rate and binding agreement terms, explained that executed loan contracts cannot be altered once funded, and highlighted that SoFi personal loans carry no prepayment penalties for early payoff. Additionally, support documented the member's formal feedback and dissatisfaction regarding the rate transparency and submitted a complaint log to Lending Operations and Compliance.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to follow up on concerns regarding the interest rate on their $15,000 personal loan. The member had previously expressed strong dissatisfaction over the interest rate on the five-year option—stating it was higher than expected—and voiced frustration that post-origination terms could not be altered after signing the contract, noting plans to pay off the balance within two years to avoid excess interest and stating an intent to file a complaint with the Better Business Bureau. The specialist authenticated the member, acknowledged their prior conversation from September 6th, and apologized for the experience. The specialist validated the member's concerns, confirming the agreed-upon terms for the $15,000 loan at approximately 12% interest, and added 1,500 reward points ($15.00) to the member's profile as a goodwill gesture for the experience while ensuring they felt fully heard and supported.
