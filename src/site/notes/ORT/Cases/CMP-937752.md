## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration and described the situation as "ridiculous," alleging that SoFi declined a $20.00 payment to AirtalkWireless and stating that the agent lacked basic comprehension. Although the agent explained that the transaction was not showing in the account's authorization history and advised using an alternative payment method, the member maintained that SoFi Bank caused the issue.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a declined $20.00 payment to AirtalkWireless. Operational review confirmed that no transaction attempt from the merchant was ever received or processed by SoFi, indicating that the failure occurred on the merchant's payment portal prior to reaching the bank's processing system. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided transparency regarding account authorization logs and recommended that the customer use an alternative payment method to complete the transaction. The complaint is resolved, with the checking account confirmed fully operational and verified to have no active restrictions or processing declines.
