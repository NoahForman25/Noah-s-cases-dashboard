## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding a missing $109.89 debit card refund from O'Reilly Auto Parts, stating the merchant processed the return the prior day and promised immediate funds. The member explained they could not wait the standard five business days because they needed the money for work-related expenses, noting the delay could cost them $1,000 to $1,400. The member requested a provisional credit or threatened to close their account, criticizing SoFi's refund timeline as shady compared to another bank. The chat ended due to inactivity after the agent asked for the member's full name.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a delayed $109.89 debit card refund from O'Reilly Auto Parts and the financial strain caused by the processing delay. Operational review confirmed that the transaction was subject to standard merchant settlement timelines and posted to the account the day following the complaint submission. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified the successful arrival and posting of the full refund, offering an apology for the delay and frustration experienced. The complaint is resolved, with the refund posted and all funds fully accessible in the customer's account.
