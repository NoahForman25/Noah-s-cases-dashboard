## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding restrictions placed on their account and to request financial assistance with covering late fees incurred as a direct result of those restrictions. After completing authentication via their full name and the last four digits of their Social Security number, the member voiced strong frustration over the lack of prior notification regarding the account lockdown and the negative financial impact it has caused.

During the interaction, the representative placed the call on hold multiple times to review the account status. The member was subsequently informed that their account had been compromised and that it would be necessary for them to create a new profile.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed the member to address their frustration regarding restrictions placed on their account, a lack of prior notification, and resulting late fees due to an account compromise. The specialist delivered a final resolution, confirming that a new secure profile with updated account information was successfully created for the member on July 6, 2026, and is ready for immediate use. To alleviate the financial impact of the late fees and the inconvenience caused by multiple holds during the account review, the specialist credited 2,500 rewards points directly to the member's profile for them to redeem into their new banking account.
