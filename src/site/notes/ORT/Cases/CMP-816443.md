## Complaint
- The member expressed extreme frustration upon discovering that a newly posted cash deposit was immediately depleted by an unauthorized debit card charge of **$118.24** to `APPLE.COM/BILL` on July 29, 2026. Stating that their funds are effectively inaccessible due to ongoing unauthorized activity, the member demanded immediate access to their money and requested an urgent manager escalation to address the security compromise and account drainage.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their extreme frustration regarding an unauthorized $118.24 debit card charge from APPLE.COM/BILL on July 29, 2026, which immediately depleted a newly posted cash deposit. The specialist acknowledged the member's urgent concerns regarding account security and inaccessible funds, apologizing for the impact of the transaction despite automated security controls. To secure the account and begin recovering the funds, the specialist urged the member to call customer support directly so the dispute team can formally file a claim, clarifying that SoFi's internal dispute process can proceed simultaneously alongside any review conducted by Apple.
