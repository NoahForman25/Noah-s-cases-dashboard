## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding a missing $40 payment, noting that the funds were absorbed by a pre-existing negative balance of the same amount. Although the agent explained the cause of the negative balance and provided a $1 reward point courtesy to fully satisfy the remaining deficit and facilitate the requested account closure, the member remained unsatisfied. The member requested to speak with a supervisor to further address the $40 deduction before proceeding.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction and request for a supervisor review regarding a $40 payment that was absorbed by a pre-existing negative balance of the same amount. The specialist validated the member's need for clarity but confirmed that a careful review of the account's transaction history proved the recent activity was accurate, with the $40 payment properly applied to clear the prior deficit. The member was reminded that a $1 reward point courtesy had also been previously applied to fully satisfy the remaining balance mismatch, ensuring that the account is now clear and the requested account closure process can proceed.
