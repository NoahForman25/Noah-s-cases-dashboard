## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi regarding a discrepancy between an expected $400 promotion bonus and the $50 bonus credited to her account. The member stated this was her third time reaching out and reported that a previous representative had confirmed approximately three weeks earlier that she met the deposit requirements for the $400 bonus, which was expected within a week. Expressing frustration over receiving misinformation and having to reach out multiple times, the member stated that taking a chance on a virtual bank had not been a great start, questioned if the $400 offer would be honored, and noted she might move to another bank if the issue was not resolved. Upon review, the agent explained that the promotion required at least $5,000 in qualifying Direct Deposits during the 25-day evaluation period (September 30 through October 25). 
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding receiving a $50 promotional bonus instead of the expected $400 bonus after previously receiving conflicting information from customer support. Operational review confirmed that while a prior representative incorrectly stated that a $6,894.60 deposit from a Scout Account qualified for the promotion, that transaction was classified as a standard transfer rather than an eligible direct deposit, leaving qualifying direct deposits during the evaluation period at $3,820.10 and correctly qualifying only for the $50 tier. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding direct deposit criteria, issued an apology for the previous agent's misguidance, verified the initial $50 bonus credit, and issued an additional $50 courtesy credit to acknowledge the misinformation. The complaint is resolved, with both promotional and courtesy credits applied and the customer's account remaining active and in good standing.
