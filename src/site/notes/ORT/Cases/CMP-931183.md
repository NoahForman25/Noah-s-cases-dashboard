## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding an ongoing issue with Zelle and requested to speak with a manager, stating that a previous manager promised a call back that was never received. The member requested a prompt resolution as they need to make a payment using the Zelle feature.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding ongoing Zelle functionality issues and an unfulfilled callback request from management. Operational review confirmed that the customer's Zelle service remains fully active, showing successful incoming transfers posted shortly prior to contact and no failed or pending outgoing transaction logs in the system. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified account functionality, requested confirmation on whether any expected inbound transfers remain outstanding, and established a direct channel for real-time troubleshooting should any further transfer delays occur. The complaint is resolved, with the Zelle feature confirmed operational and explicit verification guidelines provided to ensure all expected payments are accounted for.
