## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi regarding an open dispute for a $91.00 transaction, expressing frustration that the process has taken nearly a month and that the refund had not posted to their account despite receiving confirmation from the merchant that the funds were returned. The member was dissatisfied that SoFi did not provide a provisional credit, stating they felt penalized for the merchant's mistake and that other banks would have credited the account. The member stated that if the issue was not resolved that day, they would stop using SoFi, and the agent offered to file a formal complaint on their behalf and recommended keeping the dispute open.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an open $91.00 transaction dispute with COINFLOWPlay Bracco, the extended investigation timeframe, and the lack of provisional credit. Operational review confirmed that merchant refunds typically require 5 to 10 business days to clear institutional processing channels, and the dispute remained actively pending standard investigation workflows without eligibility for early provisional credit. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational guidance explaining standard processing timelines and recommended submitting merchant-issued refund confirmation documents directly to the dispute file to facilitate an expedited review. The complaint is resolved, with the formal dispute remaining actively open under investigation and the customer's account in good standing pending documentation review or merchant settlement.
