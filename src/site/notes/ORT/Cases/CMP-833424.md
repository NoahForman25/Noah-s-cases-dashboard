## Complaint
- The member called to request an early release on a delayed mobile check deposit, expressing frustration over the standard holding period. Despite support explaining that deposit holds are automated and cannot be manually released—and providing an expected availability date of Monday—the member became combative, repeatedly accusing the agent of lying and intentionally withholding funds while claiming other institutions process checks much faster. Support documented the interaction and re-confirmed the standard processing timelines.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction and combativeness regarding an automated security hold on a mobile check deposit, during which the member had accused a previous representative of lying and intentionally withholding funds. The specialist apologized for the hold duration, but confirmed that deposit holds are automated system processes that cannot be manually bypassed. Furthermore, the specialist verified that the information provided during the prior call was accurate, noting that the funds had since become fully available in the account according to the original timeframe shared by support.
