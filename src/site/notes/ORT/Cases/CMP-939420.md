## Complaint
- The user expressed dissatisfaction after encountering conflicting timeline information regarding a registration window that promised a 25-day clock but applied a 0-day cutoff on September 30th, while frontline staff cited a 21-day timeline. The user requested that their file be cross-referenced with active master complaint folder CMP-939374 so both accounts can be reviewed together by a back-office resolutions specialist.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding conflicting registration timelines and referral reward eligibility. Operational review confirmed that program terms restrict profiles to a single fulfilled referral bonus, rendering the additional referral non-qualifying under standard terms. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, provided full transparency by reading the official referral policy wording, and issued $100.00 worth of reward points as a goodwill gesture for their participation. The complaint is resolved, with the courtesy reward points applied to the customer's account and the referral terms clearly established.
