## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding a referral bonus, stating that they have not received the expected rewards points for participating in the credit score monitoring referral program.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone and left a voicemail. Then emailed to address their dissatisfaction regarding a missing referral bonus for the credit score monitoring referral program. The specialist conducted a comprehensive review of the profile and provided a final resolution, confirming that there is currently no referral attached to the account within the system. The member was informed that because a completed referral link was not detected, the rewards points cannot be credited, but they remain eligible to earn points through future referral program sign-ups.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
- Reason for Call

- SoFi called the member to inform them that their inquiry has been received and is under review.

Key Events

- The agent left a message for the member stating that they will receive a detailed email shortly with more information and next steps.
