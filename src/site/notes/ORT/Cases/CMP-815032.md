## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi to report a billing discrepancy regarding an $18.70 transaction listed under "CHISK EDITION." The member stated their card was charged three times, triggering a fraud alert to review the transactions. While two charges were from an unrecognized merchant name ("CHISK EDITION"), the member intended to purchase from "Digital Edition." The member expressed frustration that SoFi recorded the incorrect merchant name during the fraud alert process, voicing concern that funds were directed to the wrong entity due to a bank-side processing error rather than a standard fraud scenario.

## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their concerns regarding three separate $18.70 charges billed under "CHISK EDITION" instead of "Digital Edition," which triggered a fraud alert and raised concerns that a bank processing error sent funds to the wrong entity. The specialist acknowledged the member's frustration over the naming discrepancy and clarified that SoFi displays transaction descriptors exactly as transmitted by the merchant's payment processor without manual alteration. Explaining that official billing descriptors often differ from a business's public or store name, the specialist advised the member to contact the merchant directly to confirm their official billing entity.

### Second Contact
- Dear Robin,

This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist. Thank you for reaching out regarding the $18.70 transactions listed under "CHISK EDITION" and the associated fraud alert. We acknowledge your dissatisfaction regarding the merchant name displayed on your account and your concern that a processing error directed funds to the incorrect entity instead of "Digital Edition."

A review of your account and the transactions in question was conducted. When transactions are processed, our system displays the merchant name exactly as it is transmitted to us by the merchant's payment processor. We do not alter or manually record merchant names. It is common for the official billing name provided by a merchant to not fully align with their public business name. We advise verifying the billing name with the merchant directly to ensure you are working with the correct entity.

To recap, the merchant name displayed on your account is provided directly by the merchant's payment processor, and we recommend confirming this billing name with the merchant. Please let us know if you have any further questions.
