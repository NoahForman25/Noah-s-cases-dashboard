## Complaint
- The member expressed severe frustration regarding a balance hold placed on their funds, which left them with a zero available balance and caused debit card declines. The member stated that the held funds stem from a $6,570 paycheck issued to their company name (Integrity CNA Services), explained that it was a simple mistake as a self-employed individual, and demanded the immediate release of the remaining $437 balance.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding a balance hold that restricted funds and caused debit card declines after a business paycheck was deposited into a personal account. Operational review confirmed that account security restrictions were placed because commercial checks made payable to a company name cannot be processed under personal account agreements. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that account review protocols were completed, successfully removed the security restrictions, and released the remaining $437 balance back to the customer's available funds while clarifying that business entity names cannot be added to personal profiles. The complaint is resolved, with the security hold fully lifted and the account restored to normal active status.
