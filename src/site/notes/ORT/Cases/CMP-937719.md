## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction after being misled twice by SoFi representatives regarding the mobile check deposit cancellation process. Additionally, the member voiced frustration over the discrepancy between the advertised "fast and easy" mobile deposit and the actual 5 to 7 business day processing timeframe, and expressed concern that a check issued by SoFi would take 7 days to clear despite their account tenure and direct deposit history.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding misleading representative guidance on cancelling a mobile check deposit, extended deposit hold timeframes, and delays in funds availability. Operational review confirmed that security holds of five to seven business days are standard risk management protocols applied to verify funds regardless of account tenure, and the requested cancellation could not be executed because the deposit had already processed into posting status. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding standard check clearing cycles, issued a formal apology for the conflicting cancellation instructions, and credited 1,500 reward points to the customer's profile as a courtesy accommodation for the misinformation. The complaint is resolved, with the funds hold expiring on October 19 and the customer's account remaining active, fully funded, and in good standing.
