## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration with SoFi's communication regarding their personal loan application after not receiving updates on their submitted verification of employment (VOE). After the agent advised that notes showed the VOE was expired and that agents cannot transfer calls directly to the assigned team member, the member criticized how SoFi handles communication with members and disconnected the call.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding a lack of updates and processing delays for their personal loan verification of employment (VOE). Operational review confirmed that the submitted verification documentation had expired, which prevented processing, and that direct phone transfers to individual underwriting assigned team members are restricted under standard operational procedures. To attempt to resolve the complaint, the specialist explained the requirement for an updated VOE, clarified that submitting an offer letter alone would not satisfy the expired verification requirement, and accepted the customer's agreement to provide updated employer verification documents. The complaint is resolved, with the application awaiting a current verification of employment submission to proceed with the loan review.
