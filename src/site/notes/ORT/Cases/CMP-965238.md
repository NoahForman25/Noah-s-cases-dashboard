## Complaint
- The member expressed confusion because additional information provided for an appeal was not updated properly. The member resubmitted the tickets, clarified that the service was not provided, and requested that it be classified as "service not received." Ticket #8799698 was updated, and the member was advised they would receive an update within 10 business days of the appeal request. Additionally, the member may apply for a PC, which requires approval from the back office team; no further assistance was needed, and the interaction concluded.
## Resolution
-  A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address her complaint regarding appeal documentation not updating properly for a disputed $100.35 charge at Park Motel. Operational review confirmed that the dispute ticket required reclassification to reflect that services were not received, while determining that the transaction did not qualify for immediate provisional credit under standard eligibility guidelines. To resolve the complaint, the specialist updated ticket #8799698 with the resubmitted documentation, properly classified the claim as "service not received," and established an expected appeal review timeframe of 10 business days. The complaint is resolved, with the formal dispute appeal actively pending investigation and the customer's account remaining open and in good standing.
