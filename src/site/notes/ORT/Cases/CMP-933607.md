## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi to check the status of a $100.00 dispute filed on October 7, 2026, regarding a charge from ANTHROPIC* CLAUDE SUB. Upon learning the dispute was denied, the member expressed dissatisfaction, stating, "I am very disappointed that SoFi would stand on the side of Claude." The agent offered to submit an appeal and requested any new supporting documentation. The member agreed to reopen the dispute, provided a summary for the appeal, and noted they would submit an additional PDF once contacted by the back-office team. The agent submitted the appeal and advised the member that the information would be reviewed, with an update provided via email within 10 business days.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the initial denial of a $100.00 dispute for an ANTHROPIC* CLAUDE SUB charge. Operational review confirmed that the initial claim was denied based on the evidence initially evaluated, but qualified for a secondary review upon the submission of new context. To resolve the complaint, the specialist formally reopened the claim by filing an appeal on the customer's behalf and coordinated with the back-office team to collect additional supporting PDF documentation for evaluation. The complaint is resolved, with the appeal actively processing under a 10-business-day review window and official status updates set to be delivered via email.
