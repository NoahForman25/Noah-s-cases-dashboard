## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding a rejected direct deposit despite previous assurances from the security team and multiple emails confirming that account restrictions would not affect the transaction. They stated this rejection breached their agreement, resulting in delayed payroll funds and severe financial distress, and expressed dissatisfaction with the lack of notifications and internal communication regarding the block.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a rejected direct deposit and delayed payroll funds following security account restrictions. Operational review confirmed that account restrictions temporarily blocked incoming payroll processing despite prior communications, but the profile has since been fully verified and restored. To resolve the complaint, the specialist verified that all account features are fully operational, confirmed the requested explanatory email detailing the breakdown was delivered, and verified that future direct deposits will post without interference. The complaint is resolved, with the account fully operational, restrictions lifted, and clear confirmation provided to the customer.
