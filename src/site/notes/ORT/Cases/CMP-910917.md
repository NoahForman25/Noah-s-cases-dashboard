## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding the denial of a $9,200 dispute (ticket #8548829) and a lack of communication and resolution. The member explained that the funds are currently being held by HawaiiUSA Federal Credit Union due to an account number missing two digits. The receiving credit union confirmed they hold the money but require an Indemnification/Recall Letter from SoFi faxed to 808-844-8717 before releasing or returning the funds. The agent verified the dispute was opened on September 4 and confirmed a request was submitted to the Disputes Team, though the status of the recall letter remained unavailable. The agent escalated the issue to the Resolution Specialist team under complaint ticket CMP-910917 with a 24- to 48-hour timeline and created a follow-up ticket (FR-79295) requesting the recall letter be faxed as instructed, with a copy provided to the member and notification once the funds are returned.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email and follow-up phone calls to address their complaint regarding the initial denial of a $9,200 dispute and delays in retrieving misrouted funds held by HawaiiUSA Federal Credit Union. Operational review confirmed that the transfer was held by the receiving institution due to an invalid account number missing two digits, requiring formal indemnification documentation from SoFi to execute a fund recall. To resolve the complaint, the specialist escalated the case, successfully submitted a Hold Harmless letter directly to HawaiiUSA Federal Credit Union to authorize the release of the funds, and closed the complaint with the correct retrieval process underway. The complaint is resolved, with the formal recall letter sent to the receiving credit union and the $9,200 set to post directly to the customer's account upon final processing.
