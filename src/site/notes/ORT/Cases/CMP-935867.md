## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an unauthorized payment of $113.30 taken from their bank account for their personal loan on October 13, 2026. The member stated they had already made an early payment on September 30 (which cleared on October 1), and a subsequent automatic payment on October 5 had previously been reversed upon request. The agent explained that because the October 5 payment was reversed, the automated system triggered a second attempt on October 13. The member requested a refund/reversal of the October 13 charge, overdraft protection/reversal, and confirmation that no further unauthorized attempts would occur. The agent agreed to process the reversal request and follow up with the member via phone or email due to the member's time constraints.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding an duplicate loan payment and confusion surrounding their automatic payment settings. Operational review confirmed that the extra payment occurred due to the timing of manual and scheduled automated processing, while system security restrictions prevent representatives from manually modifying or enabling recurring payment arrangements on behalf of account holders. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, clarified how automated payment timing caused the double processing, and provided step-by-step guidance on how to re-enable and set their preferred payment date to the fifth of the month directly within the mobile application. The complaint is resolved, with the extra loan payment applied directly toward the loan balance and clear instructions provided to the customer to re-establish their preferred automatic payment schedule.
