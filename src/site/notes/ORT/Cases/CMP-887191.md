## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction upon learning that the resolution timeframe for a point-of-sale transaction dispute can take up to 90 days. Unwilling to accept the extended timeline, the member explicitly demanded an immediate supervisor escalation before disconnecting the call. Fraud Quality Assurance subsequently submitted a formal service complaint on behalf of the agent who took the interaction on September 1, 2026. Support documented the member's escalation request, logged the disconnected call, and routed the grievance to Fraud Operations and Customer Care Leadership to review the dispute status and schedule a supervisor outreach.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to address there report of an unauthorized $47.74 point-of-sale transaction through PayPal on her account. The member had previously expressed strong dissatisfaction upon learning that POS dispute investigations can take up to 90 days to resolve, demanding an immediate supervisor escalation before disconnecting the call, which prompted Fraud Quality Assurance to log a formal service complaint regarding the interaction. During the outreach, the specialist authenticated the member, confirmed that she remained in physical possession of her card, and formally filed the unauthorized $47.74 dispute on her behalf. The specialist reassured the member regarding the claim, issued a replacement card with an estimated arrival window of 7 to 10 business days, and documented her concerns to ensure proper oversight by Fraud Operations and Customer Care Leadership.
