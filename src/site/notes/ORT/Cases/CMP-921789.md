## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding account takeover fraud in which their SoFi account was hacked, resulting in unauthorized transactions, a Bitcoin purchase, and an unauthorized $20,000 personal loan application. The member reported a negative checking balance of $28 and $250 still missing from savings, expressing concern that upcoming automatic payments for electric and telephone bills would be declined or incur overdraft fees. Additionally, the member voiced frustration with delays in returning the funds, stating they expected resolution within 24 to 48 hours, and threatened to notify the Federal Trade Commission if the issue is not resolved immediately.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding account takeover fraud, which involved unauthorized transactions, a Bitcoin purchase, a $20,000 loan application, and missing funds causing concern over impending bill payments. Operational review confirmed that unauthorized access occurred on the account and established that identity verification is required before full account restoration can be finalized. To resolve the complaint, the specialist apologized for the response delay, detailed the security investigation process, and scheduled an outbound call from the specialized fraud security team to complete the required identity verification. The complaint is resolved, with the security team actively coordinating identity verification to complete the investigation and process the return of stolen funds.
