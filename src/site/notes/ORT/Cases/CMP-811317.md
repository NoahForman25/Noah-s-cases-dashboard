## Complaint
- The member expressed severe frustration over an open $42.11 dispute with Planet Fitness, stating they are left without funds for basic needs like food and gas, while reporting that a previous representative was disrespectful by failing to address them by their preferred name ("K") instead of their legal name ("K****"). Additionally, they expressed strong dissatisfaction with SoFi's lack of overdraft protection and stated a complete loss of confidence in the institution, threatening to switch to a new bank due to these ongoing issues.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address severe frustration regarding an open $42.11 dispute with Planet Fitness, financial hardship, and a previous representative failing to address them by their preferred name ("K" / Ken). After authenticating the member and acknowledging the name issue with a $20.00 goodwill credit (2,000 points), the agent discussed disputed charges from Planet Fitness and IPVanish, placed stop payments, and verified the member's address to order a replacement debit card. Additionally, the agent investigated the member's concerns regarding unavailable overdraft protection, identified a coding error preventing direct deposits from being recognized, submitted a ticket to fix the system issue, and issued temporary 60-day overdraft coverage to restore confidence and alleviate their financial strain.
