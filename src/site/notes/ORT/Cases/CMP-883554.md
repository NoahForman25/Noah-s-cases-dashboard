## Complaint
- The member expressed intense frustration and anger upon discovering a remaining balance on their personal loan after believing they had already submitted the full payoff amount, calling the situation "ridiculous." Demanding that SoFi waive the residual interest and remaining principal balance, the member stated an intent to file a formal complaint with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) due to the unexpected balance. Support acknowledged the member's dissatisfaction, explained how daily interest accrual and payoff quote validity windows can impact final settlement amounts, and documented the member's regulatory complaint notice. Support then submitted an urgent priority escalation ticket to Loan Servicing Leadership and Compliance for an immediate review and audit of the account payoff calculations.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her intense frustration and anger regarding an unexpected residual balance remaining on her personal loan following what she believed to be a full payoff. The member had previously criticized the surprise balance, demanded that SoFi waive the remaining interest and principal, and stated an intent to file a formal complaint with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB). The specialist acknowledged her feedback, documented the regulatory complaint notice, and explained how daily simple interest accrues on a per-diem basis alongside how payoff quote validity windows dictate final settlement amounts. The specialist noted the audit of the account calculations and confirmed that she subsequently worked with a representative to pay the remaining balance in full, officially settling the loan and resolving the account.
