## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with having Zelle active without direct deposit set up, or with the process for making instant money transfers outside of SoFi.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding Zelle eligibility and the process for making instant money transfers outside of the institution without having direct deposit set up. The specialist clarified that access to Zelle is based on receiving a direct deposit, and establishing a direct deposit is required to open up eligibility. The member was advised that once their first direct deposit posts to the account, they will be able to enroll in Zelle for instant transfers.
## Case Intake & Investigation

### First contact
- Hi Richard, This is Noah, a Resolution Specialist, reaching out regarding your recent feedback. We completely understand how important it is to have convenient options for instant money transfers outside of SoFi, and we are happy to provide clarity on Zelle eligibility today. You shared concerns about accessing Zelle without having direct deposit set up. To ensure a secure experience, Zelle eligibility is based on receiving a direct deposit into your checking and savings account. Setting up direct deposit is the best way to secure access to this feature. Once the first direct deposit posts to the account, you will be eligible to enroll in Zelle for instant transfers. To recap, we review several account factors for Zelle access, and establishing a direct deposit will open up your eligibility to enroll. Please let us know if you have any further questions at all—we are here to help. This is an attempt to collect a debt and any information obtained will be used for that purpose.
