## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding a pending transaction, characterizing the processing delay as "crazy" and stating that they need the funds made available immediately.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their frustration regarding a pending transaction delay and their request to have the funds made available immediately.
