## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with Zelle being unavailable due to an account restriction, noting they had contacted SoFi multiple times without resolution. The agent explained that the restriction was tied to a security review requiring phone number verification. After the member mentioned considering leaving SoFi over the experience, their Zelle enrollment was removed, and they were advised to re-enroll using the current profile phone number. The member then ended the call to catch an Uber for work, stating they would call back later.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist called the member to address a long-standing complaint regarding Zelle access issues, which had previously blocked the member from using the service for nearly a year and resulted in back-and-forth referrals between SoFi and Zelle. After authenticating the member, the agent reviewed the access issues and security restrictions tied to phone number verification, apologizing for the multi-contact friction. As goodwill compensation for the prolonged inconvenience, the agent credited 2,000 points to the member's profile. During the call, the member successfully restored Zelle access by re-enrolling with their email address. The agent also addressed the member's inquiry regarding an APY decrease by outlining the updated SoFi Plus requirements and current rate structure. Before ending the call to commute to work, the member noted previous thoughts of leaving SoFi over the ordeal, so the agent removed the legacy Zelle enrollment and instructed them to re-enroll using their current profile phone number when convenient.
