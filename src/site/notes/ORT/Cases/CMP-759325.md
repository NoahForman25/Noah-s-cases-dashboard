## Complaint
- Member contacted to express extreme dissatisfaction and a significant loss of confidence regarding a provisional credit applied to their account. The member expressed strong frustration and distrust toward the institution's accounting practices, suggesting that funds are being manipulated or improperly managed. Additionally, the member noted a recent change in their account behavior, stating that beginning in May, they transitioned to depositing cash directly into the account rather than receiving their Supplemental Security Income (SSI) check, and questioned if this change triggered the current issue. The member stated verbatim:

"Whoever your accountant for this app is, is doing a lot of pencil whooping. And all of a sudden, recently, in May, I started depositing money, cash into the SoFi account instead of getting an SSI check. Does anybody wanna know that? Y'all aren't the only bank that's doing it, though, so don't feel bad."
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding active disputes and the provisional credits applied to their account. After completing identity verification, the agent apologized for the difficulties experienced and addressed the member's extreme dissatisfaction and distrust toward the accounting practices. The agent delivered a clear resolution, confirming that the underlying disputes remain pending and verifying that the provisional credits have been properly credited to the profile. Additionally, the agent read a necessary disclosure regarding the account currently holding a negative balance, and the member concluded the call with no further questions or concerns.
