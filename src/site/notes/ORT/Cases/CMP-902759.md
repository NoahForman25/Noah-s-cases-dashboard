## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction after receiving an unprompted 2FA code in the middle of the night, which raised suspicion and resulted in losing out on a $7 transfer and additional funds. The member was further frustrated by having to navigate through AI and IVR prompts—including requests for their SSN—before reaching an agent, increasing their security concerns. Additionally, the member was dissatisfied with the previous agent's explanation, felt SoFi was unsafe due to the lack of a clear answer regarding the unprompted code, and requested due diligence to determine what occurred.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their safety concerns regarding an unprompted two-factor authentication code received overnight, automated system prompts requesting sensitive information, and unauthorized merchant charges totaling $79.98. Operational investigation confirmed that the merchant recurring billing triggered the unauthorized charges despite a prior block, while the unprompted authentication attempt and security prompts were evaluated to ensure profile integrity. To resolve the complaint, the specialist reassured the customer regarding account security protocols, filed formal disputes for the unauthorized charges, permanently re-blocked the merchant, and issued a replacement debit card. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute processing under active fraud review and the new debit card dispatched to the customer.
