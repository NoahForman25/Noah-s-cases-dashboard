## Complaint
- Outbound call was placed to the member to discuss recent account activity regarding a returned check, which the member initially confirmed. However, toward the end of the interaction, the member expressed sudden doubt, questioning whether the interaction was a scam call. The member then began screaming that she did not understand the situation and abruptly disconnected the line.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to provide clear, written documentation following an outbound call regarding a returned check, addressing the member's sudden confusion, suspicion of a scam, and subsequent disconnection. The specialist delivered a clear update, confirming that a recently deposited check in the amount of $700.00 was returned and failed to process, and recommended that the member contact the original remitter directly to determine why the funds did not clear. Additionally, the specialist advised the member to exercise caution with future deposits, explaining that processing multiple returned checks can result in standard account restrictions, and reassured her of the institution's legitimacy to resolve her concerns.
