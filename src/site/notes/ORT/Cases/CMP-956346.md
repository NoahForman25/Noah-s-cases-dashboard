## Complaint
- The member expressed disappointment and frustration regarding a wire transfer request that was delayed and subsequently denied, stating this is a recurring issue with their wire transfers. The member noted that they received a verification call 10 minutes prior and called back immediately, but the transaction still experienced immediate delays. Additionally, the member expressed overall dissatisfaction with the process.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding recurring delays and the denial of a wire transfer request following an immediate verification callback. Operational review confirmed that the transaction was held and denied due to pending security questions required by the member security team to clear fraud prevention protocols. To resolve the complaint, the specialist coordinated directly with the member security department to establish an expedited review pathway and provided the customer with direct contact details to complete the required security questionnaire. The complaint is resolved, with the wire transfer review pending the customer's direct phone contact with the member security team to finalize verification and process future transfers.
