## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding an unauthorized account opened using their mother's personal information, following a $100,000 fraudulent transfer from external institutions. They were frustrated by a previous hold time of over an hour and ten minutes that ended in a disconnection, as well as inconsistent information provided between departments. Additionally, the non-member expressed dissatisfaction that the agent could not immediately disclose the unauthorized SoFi account number needed for fraud and identity theft reports, nor provide immediate confirmation that the unauthorized profile had been frozen. The non-member requested to speak with someone else to obtain reporting information and was scheduled for a supervisor callback.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist reviewed the complaint details regarding an inquiry from a non-member, electing to close the case without direct contact after an initial outreach attempt via phone went unanswered. Operational review determined that because the individual is a non-member without an active account or profile, email follow-up would provide no operational benefit or actionable resolution. To complete the administrative process, the specialist documented the outreach attempt and closed the internal complaint ticket. The complaint is resolved and officially closed with no further operational actions required.
