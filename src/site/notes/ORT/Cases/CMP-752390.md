## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding an unauthorized charge on their account and requested to expedite the investigation process. The member emphasized that they are currently experiencing financial hardship, making the immediate recovery of these funds critical.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a denied dispute regarding unauthorized transactions, which the member had previously requested to expedite due to critical financial hardship. After completing identity verification, the member expressed deep frustration over the denial after having their money stolen and emphasized the immediate need to recover the funds. The agent explained the required verification steps and offered to request supporting documentation from the dispute team, which the member declined. To move the case forward, the member agreed to gather and provide new supporting evidence, such as a police report, to appeal the decision and support their ongoing claim.
