## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding a repeatedly denied dispute for an unauthorized $55.59 Uber transaction on their debit card. The member explained that the charge occurred on a different person's Uber account while their wallet was briefly lost, stating that both they and Uber confirmed the transaction was not theirs. The member voiced severe frustration that SoFi repeatedly denied the dispute without directly contacting the merchant or conducting a thorough investigation, and criticized customer service for providing generic denial letters and automated responses. In response, the manager explained that because the appeal was denied, the member would need to submit new supporting documentation to reopen the claim. The manager noted that a previous request was submitted to email the member the documents used in the claim decision (taking 2–3 business days) and confirmed a complaint had been filed on the member's behalf for follow-up by a resolution specialist within 24 to 48 business hours.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the repeated denial of a dispute for an unauthorized $55.59 Uber transaction and dissatisfaction with generic claim responses. Operational review confirmed that the secondary appeal review was completed and the denial decision was upheld based on the evidence evaluated during the investigation. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided transparency regarding the appeal outcome, explained that new supporting documentation is required to reopen the claim, and verified that a request was placed to send the customer the underlying decision documents within 2 to 3 business days. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute remaining closed under the appeal determination pending any new supporting evidence submitted by the customer.
