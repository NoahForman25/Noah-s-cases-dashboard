## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding SoFi's policy that prevents negotiating interest rates on a Personal Loan Refinance application, stating that it feels disingenuous given their long-standing membership. The member criticized the bank for failing to assist loyal members seeking payment relief or offer flexible rate-reduction options during financial strain. Support explained that personal loan refinancing rates are strictly determined by automated underwriting models and fixed risk-based pricing rules without manual negotiation. Support documented the member's feedback regarding member-loyalty discounts and pricing flexibility, and escalated the sentiment to Credit Risk and Lending Product Operations for review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding SoFi's policy against negotiating interest rates on a Personal Loan Refinance application. The member had expressed frustration that the bank does not offer flexible rate-reduction options or pricing adjustments for long-standing members seeking payment relief during financial strain, describing the rigid pricing structure as disingenuous to loyal customers. The specialist explained that Personal Loan Refinance interest rates are determined strictly by automated underwriting models and fixed risk-based pricing guidelines, meaning agents lack the authority to manually adjust or negotiate rates. Additionally, the specialist clarified that SoFi no longer accepts rate-match requests and cannot submit custom pricing adjustments. To address the member's broader concerns regarding payment relief and customer retention, the specialist confirmed that their feedback advocate for member-loyalty discounts and interest rate flexibility was formally documented and escalated to Credit Risk and Lending Product Operations for executive review.
