## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction after being advised that a provisional credit cannot be issued for a pending Cash App debit card transaction from June 26, 2026, in the amount of -$700.00. The representative informed the member that a dispute cannot be formally submitted until the transaction transitions from pending to posted. The member explained that the unauthorized transaction occurred because they lost their phone, which did not have a passcode lock. Although the member requested to speak with a supervisor, they did not press the issue further after being advised that management would be available at 8:00 AM ET. The member stated verbatim:

"All my money is gone can I get a credit to my account for now. A bank is supposed to cover me when fraud happens I need to switch banks if yall can’t help. Can I be transferred to a supervisor."
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their severe dissatisfaction regarding the inability to receive immediate provisional credit for a pending -$700.00 Cash App transaction from June 26, 2026. The member—who explained the fraud resulted from a lost, unlocked phone and had previously requested a supervisor—was reminded that transactions must transition to a fully posted status before a formal dispute can be evaluated. Providing a resolution, the specialist confirmed that the transaction has now officially posted and outlined the next steps for the member to file a formal dispute, either directly through their online account or by contacting the support team via phone or chat.
