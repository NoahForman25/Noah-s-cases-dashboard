## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding an unauthorized pending charge of $715.00 drawn from their bank account despite receiving prior verbal assurances and system confirmation that their next payment was not due until October 22nd. The member noted they had previously made a manual payment on their personal loan and attempted to disable automatic payments, but stopped after the system indicated doing so would increase their interest rate by 0.25%. Because the pending charge led to insufficient funds and concerns over a potential $25 late fee, the member called for assistance. During the call, an agent from the dispute team attempted to transfer the member to the personal loan department, but the line disconnected before the transfer completed. The member expressed intense frustration with the transfer process and SoFi's policy, as the agent apologized but stated they could not reconnect or dial the department back, forcing the member to call back and repeat the process.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding an unauthorized automated payment withdrawal of $715.00 that caused an overdraft on their external account. Operative review confirmed that the system triggered the automatic payment because disabling AutoPay would void the 0.25% interest rate discount, while automated system messaging had generated conflicting next-due dates following a manual payment. To resolve the complaint and rectify the member experience issue, the specialist reassured the customer regarding payment timing, confirmed the returned payment would not be re-attempted until the next scheduled date, and issued rewards points to fully cover the external overdraft fee. The complaint is resolved, with AutoPay properly configured, no late fees assessed, and the loan account remaining current.
