## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration during a call with member security regarding an inability to access their SoFi profile after updating their phone number and email address. They reported that the password reset process failed with "invalid or expired link" and "internal server error" messages, and noted that the login page did not display typed characters clearly. Additionally, the member experienced two-factor authentication issues, as the system displayed only six digits of their phone number and failed to send verification codes via text or call. The member became agitated, used profanity, and demanded a supervisor, leading the agent to disconnect the call due to abusive behavior.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding complete profile lockout and repeated system errors following an update to their contact details. Operational review confirmed that updating primary phone and email information triggered two-factor authentication loops and invalid verification links, preventing secure account access. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided direct instructions for the customer to connect with the specialized Member Technical Support team to perform manual identity re-authentication and clear the technical server errors. The complaint is resolved, with a clear technical path established to restore profile access upon customer contact.
