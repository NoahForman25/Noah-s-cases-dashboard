## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration, stating that the policy is discriminatory towards individuals on fixed incomes.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint alleging that direct deposit bonus qualification requirements discriminate against individuals on fixed incomes. Operational review confirmed that Social Security Administration deposits post once monthly, making it more challenging to hit total promotional volume thresholds within specific evaluation windows compared to multi-period payroll cycles, which reflects deposit frequency rules rather than discrimination based on income source. To resolve the complaint, the specialist clarified the promotional criteria and credited 1,500 reward points to the customer's account as a goodwill gesture for the experience. The complaint is resolved, with the reward points added to the customer's profile and the promotional policy criteria fully explained.
