## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after incurring a negative balance on their account despite having overdraft coverage explicitly disabled.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to address a complaint regarding overdraft coverage being unexpectedly activated and causing a negative balance, despite the member having explicitly disabled the feature. During the call, the agent authenticated the member, acknowledged their frustration over the unexpected activation, and apologized for the experience. To resolve the issue and offset the negative balance, the agent credited $25.00 in rewards points to the member's profile. When the member inquired about managing their overdraft preferences, the agent explained how to toggle the feature on and off in the app once the account returns to a positive balance following the redemption of the promotional points.
