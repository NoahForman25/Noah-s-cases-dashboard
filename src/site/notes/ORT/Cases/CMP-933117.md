## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding frequent transaction verifications required every time they use their debit card.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the repeated denial of a dispute for an unauthorized $55.59 Uber transaction and dissatisfaction with generic claim responses. Operational review confirmed that the secondary appeal review was completed and the denial decision was upheld based on the evidence evaluated during the investigation. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided transparency regarding the appeal outcome, explained that new supporting documentation is required to reopen the claim, and verified that a request was placed to send the customer the underlying decision documents within 2 to 3 business days. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute remaining closed under the appeal determination pending any new supporting evidence submitted by the customer.
