## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe dissatisfaction regarding the closure of their account, accusing the institution of withholding their funds. The member stated:

"You guys stole my money"
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their severe dissatisfaction regarding the closure of their account and concerns over their remaining balance. The specialist conducted a comprehensive review of the account and the recent interaction to address the missing documentation regarding where the funds were routed. A final resolution was provided, confirming that the funds in question were successfully returned to the originator. The specialist recommended that the member contact the originating institution directly to track the returned deposit on their end.
