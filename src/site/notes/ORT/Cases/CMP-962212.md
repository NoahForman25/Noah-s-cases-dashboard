## Complaint
- The member expressed significant frustration regarding an ongoing investigation, noting that they had been dealing with the situation for several days without receiving any disclosure as to why it was initiated. The member questioned the necessity of providing requested information, stated they did not understand the nature of the process, and argued that the lack of transparency was unacceptable compared to official investigations where a rationale is provided. Additionally, the member expressed dissatisfaction with an account restriction preventing access to their funds, demanded a prompt resolution, and indicated they would likely close the account if the matter was not resolved quickly.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted an outbound phone call to address the customer's complaint regarding an uncommunicated account investigation, fund access restrictions, and the subsequent account closure. Operational review confirmed that the account had been finalized for closure following the internal review, prompting standard residual balance liquidation protocols. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer via passcode, provided clear operational transparency regarding the disbursement timeline, and confirmed that a remaining balance check of $16.82 was processed on October 30 with an expected delivery window of November 10 to 13. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining permanently closed pending the customer's receipt of the disbursement check.
