## Complaint
- The member called to request reopening a previously denied dispute for merchandise paid for but never received, which had been denied due to a lack of supporting documentation such as proof of purchase, order confirmation, or merchant communication. The member expressed strong frustration, stating they only possessed the original receipt and lacked merchant contact information or any new details required by SoFi guidelines to reopen the case. The member noted that a similar dispute was resolved in their favor by Chase, expressed dissatisfaction with SoFi's policy explanations and claim denial, and ultimately requested to close their SoFi account due to handling of the dispute.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding the denial of a dispute for merchandise paid for but never received, the inability to reopen the claim without specific merchant records, and his resulting request to close the account. Operational review confirmed that the denial was correctly upheld due to the absence of required documentation—such as an order confirmation, proof of purchase, or merchant communication—as mandated by dispute network standards. To address the complaint, the specialist provided operational transparency regarding documentation requirements and outlined the formal appeal process, noting that any reconsideration requires the submission of new supporting evidence while detailing potential provisional credit reversal timelines. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute denial remaining in effect and the account pending formal closure processing per the customer's request.
