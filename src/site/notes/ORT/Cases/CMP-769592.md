## Complaint
- Member contacted regarding their QQQ call options expiring on July 2, 2026, expressing frustration that they were unable to execute a sale between 4:00 PM and 4:15 PM. The member claimed they suffered a financial loss because they were under the impression that the trading window for these contracts extended until 4:15 PM. The member was advised that options trading on the platform concludes strictly at 4:00 PM.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on an inquiry regarding options trading policies and operational hours, addressing the member's frustration over financial losses sustained when they were unable to sell their expiring QQQ call options between 4:00 PM and 4:15 PM on July 2, 2026. After completing identity verification, the agent apologized for any confusion surrounding trading hours and addressed the member's belief that the trading window extended past the standard close. The agent delivered a clear policy clarification, informing the member that the platform does not allow options contracts to be credited or traded after market close and that trading concludes strictly at 4:00 PM. To address the member's experience, the agent submitted formal internal feedback regarding the need for clearer communication on this policy moving forward.
