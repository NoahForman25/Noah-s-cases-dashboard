## Complaint
- Tthe member expressed deep frustration over an overdraft issue on their joint account, explaining that as a long-term customer, they assumed overdraft protection was enabled automatically. The member highlighted the financial impact of this misunderstanding, which included a $30 NSF fee and $153 in accumulated interest caused by a bounced check. While the agent offered 3,000 rewards points ($30 value) to offset the NSF fee, the member remained dissatisfied with the lack of clear communication and explicit disclosure surrounding the manual opt-in requirement. The agent confirmed the setting must be turned on manually, provided direct links to the relevant website disclosures, and submitted a formal complaint regarding the misleading guidance provided by a previous representative, promising a follow-up once Account Operations reviews the case.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted follow-up phone calls with the customer to address their grievance concerning a bounced check, non-sufficient funds (NSF) charges, and total accrued penalties resulting from unenabled overdraft protection. Operative review confirmed that overdraft protection requires a manual opt-in by the account holder, though previous agent miscommunication had contributed to the customer's misunderstanding regarding automatic activation. To resolve the complaint and address the negative member experience, the specialist provided direct links to SoFi's feature disclosures and credited 16,000 rewards points ($160 equivalent value) to the customer's profile to offset the incurred fees. The complaint is resolved, with the fee credit applied directly to the account by the end of the day.
