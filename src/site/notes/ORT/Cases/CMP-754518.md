## Complaint
- Member contacted to express strong dissatisfaction with the automated phone menu, stating that the system "stinks to high heavens." The member expressed frustration over being required to interact with an AI voice response system every time they call in order to reach a live representative.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong dissatisfaction with the automated phone menu, noting the member's frustration that the voice response system "stinks to high heavens" and their irritation over being required to interact with an AI system to reach a live representative. The specialist validated the importance of connecting with a live person quickly and explained that the automated menu functions to accurately route calls to specialized departments on the first attempt. Finally, the specialist confirmed that the member's blunt feedback regarding the AI interaction has been officially documented to help improve future system configurations and the overall phone support experience.
