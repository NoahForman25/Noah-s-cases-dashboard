## Complaint
- Member contacted to express continued dissatisfaction regarding their inability to replay the claw game feature. Despite the agent issuing a $10.00 goodwill credit as compensation for the technical limitation, the member remained upset that the game usage could not be manually re-prompted or reset.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on a complaint regarding technical issues with the promotional claw game feature. After completing identity verification, the agent addressed the member's continued dissatisfaction regarding their inability to replay the game, noting that the member remained upset because the system could not manually reset or re-prompt the game usage. To provide a definitive resolution and make up for the experience, the agent issued an additional $25.00 in rewards points as compensation, building upon the initial $10.00 goodwill credit previously offered.
