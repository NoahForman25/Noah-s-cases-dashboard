## Complaint
- The member reached out via chat to request a $75 reimbursement for an ACAT transfer fee, providing a Webull statement showing the fee and outgoing assets. The agent explained that they needed to identify the specific transfer and destination brokerage account to process the reimbursement memo, noting an initial rejected ACAT from August. The member expressed strong frustration with the repeated questions, stating that the statement had already been provided, the assets were visible in their SoFi brokerage account, and they would not have transferred the funds had they known they would incur the fee. Dissatisfied with the agent's inability to locate the successful transfer from the provided documents, the member requested a phone number and stated they would call in to speak with someone else.
