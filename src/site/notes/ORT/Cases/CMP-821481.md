## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction regarding the requirement to complete verification to add a middle name to their profile, arguing that the request exceeds legal requirements ("beyond the law") and refusing to complete the verification process. Support explained the identity verification rules and compliance obligations governing profile updates, noted the member's refusal to perform the required verification, and documented the interaction on the account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong dissatisfaction regarding identity verification requirements needed to add a middle name to their profile—a process the member refused to complete, arguing it exceeded legal requirements. Apologizing for a 10-day delay in follow-up, the specialist explained that name updates are treated as high-security matters governed by mandatory compliance obligations designed to protect the account and verify authorization. The specialist noted the member's previous refusal and confirmed that the mandatory identity verification requirement remains in effect for any future profile updates.
