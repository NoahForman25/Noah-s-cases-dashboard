## Complaint
- The member requested to submit a formal complaint after their application was denied due to not meeting the FICO score eligibility criteria. The member expressed frustration that prior agents failed to inform them of the FICO score requirements beforehand, stating they would not have applied had they been given this information.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding the denial of their personal loan application and previous agent miscommunication regarding credit score requirements. Operational review confirmed that the application was declined for failing to meet baseline FICO score eligibility criteria, and prior representatives omitted this requirement during pre-application inquiries. To resolve the complaint, the specialist delivered an official apology for the miscommunication, provided clear instructions on how to formally dispute the hard credit inquiry with credit reporting bureaus, and submitted internal feedback regarding agent training. The complaint is resolved, with the loan application remaining denied and the customer equipped to dispute the credit inquiry externally.
