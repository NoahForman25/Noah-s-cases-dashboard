## Complaint
- Member contacted to express dissatisfaction regarding a check issued by SoFi following their account closure, stating they are unable to deposit it because it is not being accepted by their other financial institution. The member requested that a member of the team contact them directly to either facilitate an alternative method to reimburse the funds or issue a replacement check that can be successfully processed.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding an inability to deposit a remaining balance check at their other bank due to an account closure. The specialist provided a resolution, clarifying that while a new check can be reissued upon request, it will contain the exact same information and formatting as the original document. Consequently, the specialist recommended that the member attempt to deposit the current check at a different financial institution or utilize a check-cashing service to successfully access their funds.
## Case Intake & Investigation
### First contact
- The resolution specialist attempted to contact the member regarding their complaint. The specialist did not receive the member's voicemail. The specialist will send a detailed email.
