## Complaint
- The member expressed strong frustration that her external IBC bank account failed to link automatically and that support resources provided no assistance over the weekend. She reported that both the Coach feature and the AI assistant continually directed her to the service phone line **(855) 456-7634**, which went unanswered, while the AI incorrectly insisted the line was open until 5:00 PM PT and repeatedly attempted to connect her. The member stated that the system provided inaccurate information, resulting in a significant waste of time, so support documented the technical linking failure and submitted a complaint regarding the automated system's incorrect routing and hours of operation.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her strong frustration regarding an automated linking failure with her external IBC bank account, as well as inaccurate guidance provided by support resources over the weekend. The member reported that both the Coach feature and AI assistant repeatedly directed her to an unanswered telephone line (855) 456-7634 while falsely assuring her the line was open until 5:00 PM PT, causing significant inconvenience and lost time. The specialist apologized for the incorrect system routing and inaccurate operating hours, confirming that a formal complaint was documented and submitted for internal review. Additionally, the specialist verified that the linking issue had since been resolved with another representative, confirming that the external IBC bank account is now successfully linked to the profile and ready for use.
