## Complaint
- The member contacted SoFi on August 12, 2026, expressing significant dissatisfaction and distress regarding an unresolved merchant dispute (Ticket IDs: 8169419, 8142689) that has been pending for approximately four months. The member explained that after paying $406.00 for a Walmart purchase, only a partial credit of $203.00 was returned to their account, leaving an unadjusted discrepancy of $203.00. Despite calling on multiple occasions, the member reported receiving no definitive resolution or explanation for the missing funds. The member highlighted severe personal financial hardship—stating they urgently need the money to buy food for their children—and voiced a complete loss of trust in SoFi's ability to keep their funds safe. Support escalated the open dispute tickets to Dispute Operations for immediate priority review to resolve the partial credit discrepancy and expedite the release of the remaining funds.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their significant dissatisfaction and distress regarding an unresolved merchant dispute (Ticket IDs: 8169419, 8142689) involving a $406.00 Walmart purchase. The member had previously reported that only a partial credit of $203.00 was returned to their account, leaving an unadjusted $203.39 discrepancy over a four-month period. The member highlighted severe personal financial hardship—explaining an urgent need for the funds to buy food for their children—and expressed a total loss of trust due to receiving no definitive updates despite calling on multiple occasions. While support had previously escalated the case to Dispute Operations for an immediate priority review, the specialist confirmed that the investigation into the appeal under ticket #8338769 has been completed. The specialist apologized for the negative experience but explained that after evaluating the previously provided details, the investigation determined no error occurred, meaning the original denial stands and no provisional credits will be issued. Additionally, the specialist advised the member that they may submit a new appeal if they possess new supporting documentation or evidence not included in the prior review, though without new materials, the current decision remains final.
