## Complaint
- The member requested a transfer to a new representative after experiencing frustration with a previous agent who repeatedly interrupted and prevented them from explaining their issue. The member stated they had previously submitted a due date change request through the SoFi mobile app and expressed confusion as to why the updated date had not taken effect.\ The agent reviewed the account records, which still reflected the original due date on the fifth of every month, and explained that a system error during the app request likely prevented the change from processing. The agent clarified that once the account returns to good standing, the option to update the payment due date online will become available again. Support documented the member's feedback regarding the service experience with the prior agent, provided clear steps for re-attempting the due date adjustment, and logged a technical notes report on the app update failure.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address her complaint regarding a negative agent interaction involving frequent interruptions, as well as her frustration concerning a payment due date adjustment that failed to process through the SoFi mobile app. The member had previously requested a new agent after receiving unhelpful service and sought clarification on why her account continued to display the original due date on the fifth of every month. The specialist apologized for the poor representative interaction, documented her service feedback, and logged a technical report regarding the app failure that initially prevented the update. The specialist reviewed her account records and confirmed that the technical issue was resolved, verifying that her monthly payment due date has now been officially updated to the 20th of every month.
