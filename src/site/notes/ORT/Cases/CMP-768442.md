## Complaint
- Member contacted to express severe frustration regarding an ongoing lockout, noting they are unable to access account updates because both the email address and phone number on their profile have been changed. The member stated they have made multiple attempts to verify their identity but have been unsuccessful, which has directly impacted their ability to pay their credit card and other essential bills. The member voiced specific concern over their inability to successfully pass the Persona identity verification check required to update their contact information. Because the member was unable to pass the standard SSIDV and Persona verification steps, the case was escalated yesterday with an advised wait time of one business day. The member was reminded that they can expect to receive updates once the review is complete, but they must continue to wait as the investigation remains within the designated processing timeframe.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone call and left a voicemail. Then emailed the member to address their severe frustration regarding an ongoing account lockout, which has prevented them from paying their credit card and essential bills due to unauthorized changes to their email and phone number. The specialist acknowledged the member's multiple unsuccessful attempts to pass standard SSIDV and Persona identity verification checks, which had prompted an escalation on July 5, 2026. Providing a resolution based on the security review, the specialist confirmed that unauthorized access to the account had occurred and that there is currently a pending dispute on the profile. The member was informed that while standard security protocols require the pending dispute to be fully processed before a new account can be established, the resolution team stands ready to assist them in creating a new, secure account once the dispute is officially resolved.
