## Complaint
- The member called support to inquire about a declined card transaction and report a discrepancy between the available balance displayed in the mobile app versus the figure provided by the agent. The member expressed strong frustration, explaining that after depositing a **$400.00 check**, the app reflected **$100.00** in available funds, yet their purchase was declined. Support clarified the deposit hold schedule and funds availability rules, explaining how pending transactions, active holds, and authorization checks affect the real-time card processing balance.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their strong frustration regarding a declined card transaction and a balance discrepancy between the mobile app and card processing system following a $400.00 mobile check deposit. The member voiced confusion after a purchase was declined despite the mobile app displaying $100.00 in available funds. The specialist apologized for the experience and explained standard deposit hold protocols, clarifying how active check holds affect the real-time balance available for debit card authorizations regardless of the displayed amount. Additionally, the specialist confirmed that the holding period on the $400.00 check has since concluded and the funds are now fully available in the account for use.
