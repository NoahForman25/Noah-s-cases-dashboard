## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding an urgent need for funds due to traveling overseas the following day. The agent advised the member that a ticket would be submitted concerning the Zelle transactions.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address his complaint regarding an urgent need for funds while traveling overseas and ongoing difficulties completing incoming Zelle transactions. Operational review of the submitted investigation tickets confirmed that no internal blocks, restrictions, or platform-side failures were impacting the account, indicating that the processing failures likely originated from external constraints on the sender's account or their financial institution. To resolve the complaint, the specialist advised re-attempting the transfers, recommended having the sender troubleshoot directly with their own bank, and provided direct phone contact details should further support be required. The complaint is resolved, with the customer's account remaining fully active and unrestricted in good standing while external sender troubleshooting takes place.
