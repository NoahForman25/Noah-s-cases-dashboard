## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration after not receiving their direct deposit on its expected date of the 26th, requesting to expedite the funds or receive a cash advance. Additionally, the member alleged discrimination against individuals receiving Social Security income, citing SoFi's minimum personal loan limit of $5,000.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their dissatisfaction regarding a delayed direct deposit expected on the 26th, their request for expedited funds or a cash advance, and their allegation of discrimination against Social Security recipients due to SoFi's $5,000.00 minimum personal loan limit. The specialist acknowledged the frustration surrounding the deposit timing and confirmed that system records show the direct deposit was successfully credited to the account on July 27, 2026. Addressing the loan concerns, the specialist explained that the $5,000.00 threshold is a standard institutional application requirement and confirmed that the member's feedback regarding loan limits and eligibility was formally documented for internal review.
