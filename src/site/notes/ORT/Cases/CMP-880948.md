## Complaint
- The member expressed strong dissatisfaction after having to call multiple times to explain the same ongoing situation, requesting direct contact with their assigned application reviewer to clarify the specific documentation requirements. Highlighting the frustration caused by repeating details to multiple representatives without progress, the member insisted on speaking with the decision-maker handling their file. Support acknowledged the member's frustration regarding the repetitive interactions, documented the specific clarification requests, and submitted an urgent escalation ticket to Underwriting Operations to facilitate direct reviewer contact or supervisor follow-up.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via phone to discuss missing benefit income documentation required for their loan application, while also addressing the member's frustration regarding receiving repeated automated calls without voicemails or clear representative notes. The member clarified that while they are actively employed, their co-borrower is retired and will receive the required benefit income documentation on September 30th upon receiving their next distribution, at which point they will call back to upload the updated paperwork. During the interaction, the specialist acknowledged the member's annoyance regarding the non-essential outbound calls and the lack of representative contact logs during prior callbacks. The specialist reviewed recent contact history, noted the member's timeline to submit the co-borrower's income documents on September 30th, and submitted an operational escalation ticket to Communications Management to update contact preferences and suppress non-essential automated outreach.
