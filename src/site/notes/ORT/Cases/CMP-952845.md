## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction with the enhanced verification process, stating, "This is absolutely ridiculous." The agent sent a Persona link, but the member was unhappy because the process continually kept loading.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding technical issues encountered during the enhanced identity verification process and the Persona link failing to load. Operational review confirmed that completing enhanced verification via Persona is a mandatory security requirement before requested updates to the account's phone number and email address can be processed. To resolve the complaint, the specialist apologized for the technical disruption, advised the customer to reattempt the process at a later time, and offered direct assistance with identifying alternative verification methods should the loading errors persist. The complaint is resolved, with the account remaining secure and the contact information updates pending successful completion of identity verification.
