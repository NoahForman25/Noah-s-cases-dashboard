## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding notifications of unauthorized transactions and raised concerns about the handling of their account security. The member stated that SoFi is failing to provide adequate protection and security to ensure their account remains secure.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding unauthorized debit card charges and concerns over account security protocols. Operational review confirmed that potential fraud flags triggered security alerts on the account, which required blocking the compromised debit card and issuing a replacement to prevent further unapproved activity. To resolve the complaint, the specialist authenticated the customer, confirmed the cancellation of the fraudulent $44.19 and $1.11 charges, and guided the customer on proactive security steps, including updating their online account password. The complaint is resolved, with the compromised transactions removed, the new replacement debit card issued, and security recommendations provided to help protect the account going forward.
