## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a deleted $150.00 hold for "HARRAHS AK - CHIN HOTE," stating that they did not see these funds added back to their account and arguing that it did not make sense based on their transaction history. The agent apologized and explained that the $150.00 hold had already been released on October 9, 2026. At the time of the release, the account had a negative balance of $18.76, which left a remaining balance of $131.24 after the funds were applied. The agent set expectations that released holds automatically adjust the available balance without showing up as a separate line item or triggering a notification. Although the member requested to dispute the transaction, they were advised that a dispute could not be filed because the charge was only a security hold and was never officially posted to the account.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding a released $150.00 authorization hold from Harrah's Ak-Chin Hotel that the customer believed was not returned to their account balance. Operational review confirmed that the $150.00 temporary security hold was successfully released on October 9, 2026, and automatically applied toward a pre-existing negative account balance of $18.76, leaving an available balance of $131.24. To resolve the complaint, the specialist explained that released pre-authorization holds automatically adjust available ledger balances without generating distinct line-item entries or user notifications, and clarified that unposted holds cannot be submitted for formal transaction disputes. The complaint is resolved, with the authorization hold fully cleared, the funds accurately reflected in the account balance, and the transaction mechanics explained.
