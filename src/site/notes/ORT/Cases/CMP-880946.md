## Complaint
- The member expressed significant frustration regarding their recent loan application denial and the resulting hard credit inquiry on their credit profile. The member emphasized that they have valid paystubs from W-2 employment as well as a 10-year history of business ownership to demonstrate income stability, expressing dissatisfaction over the 30-day waiting period to reapply or the requirement to add a co-borrower. Support acknowledged the member's concerns regarding income verification and credit impact, documented their income sources, and submitted an escalation ticket to Lending Operations and Credit Risk for a supervisor review of the application decision and credit pull handling.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address his significant frustration regarding the denial of his recent loan application, the resulting hard credit inquiry, and the policy requiring either a 30-day waiting period or adding a co-borrower to reapply. The member had previously emphasized that his W-2 paystubs and 10-year history of business ownership demonstrated clear financial stability, challenging the basis for the application decline and its impact on his credit profile. The specialist reviewed the application and clarified that the loan was declined because his 2025 business tax returns are currently on an extension, explaining that filed tax returns are mandatory to verify self-employment or business income and that a W-2 alone cannot satisfy those underwriting requirements. The specialist noted that the previous representative offered adding a co-borrower as an alternate path to bypass the standard 30-day reapplication wait time, and confirmed that an escalation ticket was submitted to Lending Operations and Credit Risk for a supervisor review of the credit decision and hard inquiry handling.
