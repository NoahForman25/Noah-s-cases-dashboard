## Complaint
- The member expressed significant concern and frustration upon discovering that a debit card dispute for a **$249.48** pending charge from `AGODA.COM DA VINCI H` was never actually filed, despite being previously assured it would be processed within 24 to 48 hours. The member voiced strong dissatisfaction after learning that pending transactions cannot be formally disputed until posted, criticizing the lack of follow-through and stating that the dispute team failed to handle the matter with the required urgency. Additionally, the member expressed frustration over the standard 10-business-day timeline to receive provisional credit, stating a complete loss of confidence in SoFi's support services and requesting an immediate transfer to a supervisor. Support clarified the dispute eligibility rules for pending versus posted charges, documented the agent error regarding the initial submission promise, and initiated a priority supervisor callback request.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member via email to address their significant frustration regarding an unfiled debit card dispute for a $249.48 pending charge from AGODA.COM DA VINCI H, as well as misinformation provided during a prior interaction. The member had expressed a total loss of confidence in support services after discovering that the dispute was never initiated—despite previous assurances that it would be processed within 24 to 48 hours—and voiced strong dissatisfaction with the standard 10-business-day processing timeline required to receive provisional credit, demanding an immediate supervisor escalation. The specialist apologized for the previous representative's error and explained that pending transactions cannot be formally disputed until they fully post to the account. The specialist verified that the agent error was documented for internal quality review and clarified that the 10-business-day timeframe for provisional credit evaluation is a mandatory review period required for the dispute team to investigate the claim. Finally, the specialist confirmed that a priority supervisor callback request was successfully initiated to follow up directly with the member.
