## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration regarding recurring transaction declines, noting that the issues persist even after they have explicitly approved the transaction via the platform's security alerts.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the member by phone to follow up on an inquiry regarding recurring debit card transaction declines, addressing the member's frustration that these issues persisted even after explicitly approving the security alerts via the platform. After completing identity verification and accessing the account details using a one-time passcode, the agent investigated the two attempted $250.00 transfers intended for One Finance and the member's credit union. Providing a clear resolution, the agent explained that the declines were primarily caused by a low account balance, while noting that automated security measures also impacted the transfers due to the account being newly used. The member acknowledged the explanation and expressed appreciation for the follow-up call.
