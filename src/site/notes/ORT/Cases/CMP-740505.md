## Complaint
- Member contacted to express frustration that nearly all of her transactions are being flagged and denied, which is making her nervous about using her account, especially ahead of an upcoming trip to Canada. She noted that manually authorizing each transaction is highly inconvenient and requested that the fraud flagging feature be turned off. After the agent requested verification for multiple pending transactions, the member became further frustrated, stated she would be transferring her funds to another financial institution, and ended the interaction without a resolution.

- Hi Kimberly,

Thank you for reaching out about your recent card transactions and your upcoming trip to Canada. We completely understand how important it is to have seamless access to your funds, especially when traveling, and we are here to help clarify this for you.

To ensure your account remains fully secure, our system is currently requesting additional verifications. Because you have ordered two new cards within the past few months, the system is taking extra care to learn your spending patterns. As you continue to use your current card, these verification requests will naturally decrease.

To best protect your account, our standard security features remain active at all times. To support your upcoming travel to Canada.

To recap, the recent verifications are a standard security measure linked to your new cards, and this will improve as you build a history with your current card. Please let us know if you have any further questions at all—we are here to help.
