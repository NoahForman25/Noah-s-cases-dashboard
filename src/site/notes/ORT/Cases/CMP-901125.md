## Complaint
- The member expressed frustration regarding the denial of their application despite completing multiple identity verifications, but the co-borrower is the one that is still needing to do the identity verification. They reported repeatedly attempting to contact the specialist without success and complained about the lengthy, demanding application process resulting in a decline.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist contacted the customer via phone call to address their complaint regarding the decline of their initial loan application and difficulties surrounding identity verification. Review of the application confirmed that the initial decline occurred due to incomplete identity verification specifically on the co-borrower's side, rather than a failure on the primary applicant's submission. To resolve the complaint and address the friction experienced during the process, the specialist apologized for the communication delays and credited 1,500 rewards points to the customer's profile. The complaint is resolved, with the customer actively progressing through a new joint application alongside the co-borrower.
