## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding multiple unauthorized charges on their debit card and Smart Card accounts—including duplicate Marriott charges, Cash App transactions to Emmanuel Sia, and a $3,400 charge—which were previously disputed and denied. The member explained that they were hospitalized from September 4 to September 16 with no access to their cards, and noted that police and detectives are investigating the stolen funds. Frustrated with the lack of resolution, delayed refunds, and denied disputes, the member submitted hospital discharge documentation and a police report, and requested an escalation, prompting the agent to submit an appeal for all denied disputes for further review.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist communicated with the customer via email to address their complaint regarding the denial of multiple unauthorized card charges—including duplicate Marriott fees, Cash App transfers, and a $3,400 transaction—incurred while hospitalized. Operational review confirmed that formal appeals were submitted for all denied claims, incorporating the newly provided police report and hospital discharge records to establish card non-access during the fraud window. To resolve the complaint, the specialist submitted all supplemental documentation to the disputes team for comprehensive secondary re-investigation and outlined the potential outcomes, including full fund restoration upon appeal approval. The complaint is resolved, with all disputed card transactions actively undergoing secondary appeal review based on the submitted official documentation.
