## Complaint
- The member expressed dissatisfaction regarding a miscellaneous debit on their account, stating SoFi previously removed funds after providing dispute credits. The member received three temporary credits for disputed transactions, but after the merchant issued a direct refund two days later, the back-office team debited one temporary credit. The member stated they will change banks due to this experience.
## Resolution
- A resolution specialist conducted a follow-up phone call with the customer to address their complaint regarding a miscellaneous debit that debited a previously issued provisional credit. Operational review confirmed that three temporary credits were originally applied during a merchant dispute investigation, but when the merchant processed a direct $15.98 refund two days later, the system debited one provisional credit to prevent duplicate merchant reimbursement. To resolve the complaint, the specialist provided transparency regarding dispute accounting mechanics, explained that duplicate merchant refunds trigger automatic temporary credit adjustments, and filed a secondary dispute for an unreceived Uber Eats order. The complaint is resolved, with the dispute accounting mechanics fully clarified and the new transaction claim actively processing under standard review workflows.
